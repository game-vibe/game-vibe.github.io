Game Description#

Flick Shot Rogues places heroes on a desktop field, where the outcome of each course depends on the laws of physics. Players plan, aim and start their characters, like balls in billiards or pinball. The task is to clean the location from opponents, causing chain reactions and thoughtful clashes. Success requires a combination of tactical insight with an understanding of inertia to conquer various biomes of the island, including the full trap of the jungle.

Tactics of victory on the board in Flick Shot Rogues:

Drive and accuracy: check each launch for the maximum effect. Chain reactions: Place enemies with each other and with the environment. Adaptation: Change the strategy depending on the relics and curses received.

Step -by -step battles make you carefully verify the perfect blow. Sending the hero to rotation towards enemies initiates explosive combinations that break the battlefield into pieces. Hasty actions carry a great risk, because the mistake can turn out for the detachment with fatal consequences. The entire gameplay turns into a calculated solution that requires to weigh potential damage and the likelihood of an expensive tactical error.

The defeat drops progress, however, each new trip to the island offers an altered arrangement of enemies and awards. Gamer collects a team from available heroes and must adapt his strategy at every new attempt. Throughout the race, powerful objects, talents and relics will have to be found, giving unique and changing effects effects. Curses add another level of choice, allowing you to strengthen the passage of powerful interactions at the cost of taking serious risks.
