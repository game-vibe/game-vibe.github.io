Game Description#

Stronghold Warlords aims to play online with a friend or team up online with players from all over the world, taking part in bloody medieval battles. Representing the interests of one of the warring factions, on a pirate ship you can take command of an entire army with its own unique abilities and tactics. The castle simulator Stronghold Warlords recreates the culture and life of East Asia. Players are given a completely free excursion into the most interesting and at the same time controversial Asian era.

The developers allow you to take command of Mongol hordes, imperial warriors, Japanese samurai and Chinese troops. The possibility of free online play implies not only full-scale battles with the participation of a huge number of units, but also offers sieges with the help of massive towers and fortified guns. The online game Stronghold Warlords introduces many historical military leaders, the period of the first appearance of gunpowder weapons, etc.

Despite the phantasmagoria, the gameplay is aimed at realism and is based on historical information. Each faction allows you to use its unique abilities and advantages. Military commanders increase valor and allow for greater control of the battlefield. In addition to military battles, there are trade and diplomacy mechanics. The single-player campaign features random battles and a full storyline. Multiplayer battles provide an opportunity to experience the full strategic depth of the project. Gameplay Features: Defeating warlords unlocks new castles, armies and opportunities; 4 unique cultures represented by Mongol archers, imperial spear throwers, cold-blooded samurai and laddermen; historical campaign spanning the period from the 3rd century BC to the rise of the shogunate; numerous weapons: fire spears, siege towers, gunpowder cannons, etc.; the opportunity to become the greatest commander, providing his soldiers with entertainment, plenty of food and much more.
