Game Description#

Everhood 2 is an unconventional role-playing game (ARPG) that throws traditional combat out the window. Instead of standard battles, users encounter enemies accompanied by music. You are guided by the celestial Raven, who promises to help you regain your voice if you can defeat the fearsome Dragon of the Mind. This quest will take you through a surreal world where the line between life and death is blurred, and every victory seems like a step deeper into the unknown. The title invites gamers to explore not only the physical, but also the metaphysical, revealing secrets about the color of the soul and the true nature of existence.

In Everhood 2, battles take place in a variety of musical genres - more than 100 songs to keep things interesting. It’s all about mastering rhythm and timing, challenging your reflexes and absorbing the musical flow. Don’t forget the wild visuals - this world is unlike any you’ve seen before, filled with whimsical and mind-blowing designs that add to the otherworldly feel of the project. This journey lasts 10-12 hours, and each moment draws you deeper into the unique rhythm-based combat system. The adventure will also bring unexpected companions. Along the way, you’ll meet quirky and humorous characters that add a light touch to the tense and surreal tone of this title.
