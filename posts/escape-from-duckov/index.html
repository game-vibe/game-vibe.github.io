Game Description#

Escape from Duckov is an action-adventure game that immerses you in a world where the main character, a little duck, has to survive and fight for his life. Having awakened from a strange dream, the protagonist finds himself in a harsh reality full of dangers and hostile creatures. To survive, he needs to explore the surrounding areas, constantly in search of useful supplies and resources. Every foray outside the shelter turns into a risky undertaking, because in the event of death, all the equipment found will be irretrievably lost. This aspect forces you to carefully plan your actions and avoid unnecessary risks, using covert tactics or engaging in open battle only when absolutely necessary.

The central element of the gameplay is the development of your own base, located in an underground bunker. Materials collected during forays are used to build and upgrade various modules, such as medical stations, weapon racks and even a cozy duck’s nest. Developed infrastructure allows you to recover faster from defeats and provides access to the creation of more powerful equipment. The Escape from Duckov project features a deep weapon customization system that allows you to turn found objects into effective means of defense and attack. Gradually revealing the secrets of this world through dialogues with other characters, the feathered soldier must find out the truth about what is happening.
