Game Description#

The cooperative action gameSourceWorlds allows you to play online with a friend for free or fight in PvP arenas with random players online. Actions take place in the world of the Architect. SourceWorlds multiplayer allows you to collaborate on quests, hunt for rare items, fight a huge number of enemies, and much more. The goal is to find a way out of the mad architect’s world and return to your real world.

You can create an army of gnomes or use the grappling hook for tactical purposes. Fights take place on land using ships or vehicles. You can even ride a dragon to incinerate all your opponents. Free online play allows you to join forces and cross swords in intense combat. Multiplayer is available for 4 people. You can go through the storyline with your partners. Gold is needed to buy new equipment and upgrades.

Locations and dungeons are randomly generated. There are a number of challenges available that challenge your skills and ability to interact with the world around you. You can find legendary items from Giacomo that bestow enormous power. There are 170 weapons available, including swords, daggers, axes, hammers, stop shields, and miniguns. You can be both a warrior and a magician. It is possible to get behind the wheel of absolutely any vehicle that is present at the locations. You can also change and customize the appearance of your character.
