Game Description#

Prey is an immersive sim with a first-person view. The style is taken from science fiction works about space. The game engine is modern and provides high-quality images - CryEngine 4. The universe has several significant differences from the real world: the Soviet Union did not collapse, the President of the United States of America John Kennedy did not die due to an assassination attempt, as well as a serious leap in technology in the field of space research, as a result of which humanity came into contact with an alien race - the Typhons.

The aliens turned out to be unfriendly towards people, because of this, the largest countries in the world, including the USSR and the USA, joined forces to create a common space fleet. The “Cage” station in the distant orbit of the Moon becomes an important object in space; its purpose is to obtain information from captured Typhons through experiments, to study the structure of the aliens and determine weak points. It will become the place where the gameplay takes place.

The plot is not linear, the player is responsible for all decisions made by the protagonist (at the beginning of the passage you will be asked to choose the gender of the main character). In the process of leveling up a character with the surname Yu, you can even gain the abilities of typhons, for example, the ability to transform into many surrounding objects such as cups or balls. Also in Prey you constantly need to search the levels for the necessary resources for crafting, new firearms, and key cards. They can open areas of the station that were previously closed. You can also reach previously inaccessible locations by going into outer space in special spacesuits.
