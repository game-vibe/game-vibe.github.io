Game Description#

MARVEL vs. CAPCOM Fighting Collection: Arcade Classics includes seven exciting games that will not leave any fan of the genre indifferent. Among them are such hits as X-MEN VS. STREET FIGHTER and MARVEL vs. CAPCOM 2 New Age of Heroes, as well as the rare THE PUNISHER game, which will especially delight collectors. You can play online with a friend or fight with other players online online. In addition, the online game is available for free on the pirate market, which makes this collection especially attractive for fans of retro action films.

Do you consider yourself an exceptional button pusher? Well, you’re in luck! The collection is designed for both beginners and experienced professionals. Beginners can easily get into the game with customizable difficulty levels and dedicated one-button missions, while veterans can dive headfirst into online ranked battles. As for online play, thanks to the rollback netcode, matches run smoother than oil on a hot frying pan.

But hold your horses, friends—there’s more to this package than just throwing punches and fireballs. History buffs can gawk at over 500 illustrations and never-before-seen development documents. And for those with itchy ears, there’s a jukebox with over 200 tracks from the original arcade machines. Talk about a trip down memory lane!

So whether you’re looking to relive the glory days of putting quarters in the arcade, or you’re a fresh fighter ready to test your mettle, MARVEL vs. CAPCOM Fighting Collection: Arcade Classics is what you need. It’s a smorgasbord of superhero and Street Fighter action that will keep you glued to your screen for hours. Are you ready to compete?
