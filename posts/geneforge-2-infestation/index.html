Game Description#

GeneForge 2 - Infestation invites players to a unique role -playing adventure, developing concepts presented in its predecessor. In a world where shepers have the ability to create life forms and manipulate them, in the center of the narrative - the search for the protagonist who is trying to find out the reason for an incomprehensible infection. This infection is jealous of environmental stability and the balance of forces between the factions.

Gamers have to go through complex scenarios, solving the storyline that unfolds depending on their solutions. The gameplay is built on research and interaction. Gamers have to go through many environment, each of which has its own characteristics and challenges. From the terrible dungeons, which seem to whisper the secrets of the past, to the bright fields in which life boils - there is something to offer every area. Along the way, gamers are faced with various fractions and characters, each of which brings depth to the general narrative.

The world is inhabited by creatures who have its own motives, so each interaction can become key. The battle in Geneforge 2 - Infestation focuses on the strategy and development of the character. Users can participate in battles that require reflection and planning, and not rely only on gross power. Mechanics allows you to use a wide range of combat styles, encouraging players to an individual approach.
