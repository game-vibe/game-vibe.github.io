Game Description#

GOD EATER Resurrection is a re-release of a role-playing adventure with the subtitle Burst. For free, you can play pirate online with a friend, going through a rich story campaign together. By connecting online via the Internet, players have the opportunity to team up against powerful monsters and giant bosses, saving the planet from an alien threat. Innovations in GOD EATER Resurrection: updated soul devouring - Predator style; all types of weapons from the second part of the adventure have been added; new Aragami attacks have been introduced; network play that allows you to go through the story campaign together; improved graphics, updated effects and much more.

The gameplay allows you to assemble a team of heroes to go to battle with Arag monsters. Each enemy and all heroes have their own unique abilities. Characters are represented by different characteristics and skills. The possibility of free online play makes each hero very important for the team. Events begin three years after the end of the previous part.

The fighters belong to the Blood squad. The Fenrir Corporation sends heroes to investigate an unusual case that draws the protagonists into a global conspiracy. There are 3 players in the squad. All three can attack the giant monster. Each enemy has its own weak point: head, tail, paws or other point. Once an Aragami’s health reaches a critical level, it enters the second phase and becomes much more aggressive. A defeated enemy drops valuable loot. After completing the mission, you can teleport to the base to sell unnecessary items and upgrade your abilities.
