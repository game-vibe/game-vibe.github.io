Game Description#

Rogue Glitch Ultra is a fun online game where you can play online with friends or random partners. You find yourself in a colorful world where various adventures and challenges await you. Your task is to collect weapons, improve your character and fight enemies. The game has free online play option so you can enjoy it for free. In addition, you can play on a pirated version if you do not have a licensed copy of the game. Thus, you can play online online anytime and anywhere. If you want to go through the game from beginning to end with a friend, you can choose co-op mode and face all the difficulties together.

Rogue Glitch Ultra eliminates the need for precise aiming with its auto-aim feature. This allows players to focus on mastering platforming skills and creating the perfect gear for their playstyle. The game offers a variety of weapons, from throwing sponges at enemies to using a minion pistol to summon helpful companions.

As players delve deeper into the hostile universe, they collect malbytes, a valuable currency that is used to transform existing characters into powerful glitched evolutions. These evolutions will allow players to advance further and meet a mysterious creature who will make a tempting offer. Key features of Rogue Glitch Ultra: Solo and online co-op gameplay for 6 players; 150+ unique weapons and bonuses for different play styles; 18 character options with updated stats and skins; Auto-aim, allowing you to focus on the platform and optimize equipment; Unfold the narrative through the events of a glitched video game world; Improved graphics, new content, items and boss fights in RGU compared to the original.

Rogue Glitch Ultra is a thrilling, action-packed experience perfect for fans of challenging platformers with unique gameplay mechanics. Featuring a vast arsenal, varied character options, and engaging storytelling elements
