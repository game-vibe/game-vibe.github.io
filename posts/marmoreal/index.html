Game Description#

In Marmoreal, you need to do everything possible to stop the evil army of the undead, led by the Necromancer. His goal is to take over the world, exterminate all good warriors and the common population, and then, controlling his army, become a completely immortal Lich. This cannot be allowed under any circumstances. Therefore, it is necessary to organize powerful resistance that will plunge the enemy forces into oblivion. To do this you need weapons, as well as a sufficient amount of strength that can be accumulated.

In Marmoreal, the gamer will control heroes, sorceress girls who are brave warriors. They join the Mages of Arcanum, as well as the Warriors of Merkyrion, and together with them they will resist the evil forces. Moreover, the game offers 2 separate campaigns for these armies, which, although connected by one idea, confront each other at different time intervals, fulfilling a specific role. It will also be possible to select heroes for whom the passage will take place. In total there are 6 different Persians, among whom you can find wizards, knights, living armor, as well as other units. Each has a set of unique qualities.

The development of heroes is tied to their character traits. Thus, as you progress, you can discover new features. It is with their help that abilities are strengthened and new skills are acquired. In addition, there will be different factions of ill-wishers. These are the already mentioned undead, and also forest spirits, the remnants of an ancient civilization, and they are all fighting for dominance. There is loot from enemies that will drop; these can be unique items, weapons, armor and other ammunition.
