Game Description#

Verho - Curse of Faces offers a brutal challenge in a world ravaged by an ancient curse. The journey begins with the choice of one of the masks, which determines the initial characteristics and sets the vector of character development. However, this class system is flexible, allowing you to invest points into any stats over time, be it Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence, creating a unique warrior. The combat system requires careful attention: each weapon, from the fast rapier to the heavy axe, has its own fighting style. Studying the habits of your opponents and using your arsenal wisely becomes the key to survival in dark dungeons filled with deadly creatures.

The action takes place in the cursed lands of Yariv, which became the source of the disaster known as the Age of Solitude. According to legend, anyone who sees someone else’s face is doomed to instant death along with what they saw. That is why masks have become not just an accessory, but the only way to survive. The Verho - Curse of Faces project is deliberately devoid of modern conveniences: there is no world map or quest log. To progress through the plot, you need to carefully listen to the few characters, memorize the routes and independently explore every corner in search of hidden passages and valuable artifacts.

The main goal of traveling around Yariv is to uncover the secret of the Curse of Faces. The hero faces a difficult choice: try to rid the world of this scourge or subjugate its dark power to his own will. The decisions made during the passage influence the fates of the characters encountered and the outcome of the entire story. Success depends on the player’s ability to piece together pieces of information found in item descriptions and ancient ruins, gradually reconstructing the full picture of the disaster that occurred and finding his own answer to the main question of this world.
