Game Description#

SURV1V3 is a cooperative shooter designed for playing online online with a friend or other participants from around the world. You can play the pirate game online for free, fighting hordes of zombies in virtual reality. The gameplay is aimed at cooperative survival. Multiplayer and single player campaign available. Events start on day 407 of infection with the V1V3 virus.

The possibility of free online play invites up to four people to team up and try to overcome all the obstacles of the infected city together. Participants will have to engage in an unequal battle with an army of the living dead. The maps in SURV1V3 are huge and interactive, fully exploreable. There are many main and secondary tasks presented. A total of 3 modes are available: Story campaign, including 18 hours of gameplay, Trials, which are battles with the horde, escape and defense, and Survival - a mode in which you need to level up, craft useful items, etc.

Weapons include rifles, machine guns, snipers, shotguns, pistols, bows, etc. Each barrel has its own characteristics and physics. You can personalize guns and install various modifications. A feature of the gameplay is complete immersion. There is no HUD, allowing you to completely immerse yourself in what is happening on the screen. Friendly fire is also available, so you need to be as careful as possible in critical situations. Skills are tested on maps such as The Last Garden, Hell Street and Big Bird. The project includes: a menu in the form of a military tablet; weapons with unique physics; the ability to disguise as zombies; more than 40 types of enemies; full dismemberment of bodies; huge levels for exploration; co-op for up to 4 players; 18 hours of storyline.
