Game Description#

Roboquest is a multiplayer shooter designed to play online with a friend or compete with players from all over the world online. By choosing one of the characters, the pirate allows you to take part in a variety of competitive modes for free. The gameplay is a fast-paced first-person shooter with simplified roguelike elements. You will play as the Iron Guardian. There are many different classes presented, where each character owns unique types of weapons and uses individual combat tactics.

You can complete a campaign or a set of challenges either alone or with partners - the possibility of free online play includes both cooperation and PvP competitions. The gameplay of RoboQuest implies verticality. You can jump on walls and platforms, cling to ledges and much more using steel boots or jetpacks. The environment, like the location of loot or enemies, is randomly generated. Multiplayer allows you to fight powerful bosses together. By collecting unique guns, you need to destroy all the robots in your path. Customize weapons, upgrade your character, etc. possible within the camp.

The plot of Roboquest starts in 2700. A robopocalypse occurred, after which people began to live in deserts in order to survive. Scavenger girl Max stumbles upon a robot guard lying in a landfill. Having fixed the bot, he takes the side of people and gives humanity a chance to survive. There are canyons, farms, deserts, etc. to be explored. The locations contain many secrets and dangers. Acting as a defender of people, it is necessary to return humanity to its former greatness!
