Game Description#

Aviassembly is dedicated to creating and flying your own aircraft. Players assemble aircraft from individual parts, taking into account tank volume, weight distribution, possible speed and cargo compartment capacity. The selection and installation of spare parts directly affects the behavior of the vehicle in the air and its flight performance in accordance with the laws of physics. Gamers will have to control the takeoff, maneuvers in the sky and landing of their vehicles.

The gameplay in Aviassembly is based on completing tasks to earn money within the world of the project. Orders include the delivery of a variety of cargo, each with its own transportation conditions and difficulties. Pilots need to be careful about their fuel consumption on flights to ensure they arrive at their destination. The funds received go towards further improvement of aircraft and open access to new horizons.

Development involves researching new components to improve aircraft designs to increase range, speed or lift. Upgraded vehicles allow you to take on more difficult assignments and explore previously closed regions. You can discover new airfields and islands located in different natural areas of the open world. Additionally, Creative mode provides endless resources for gamers interested in building and testing without being tied to tasks.
