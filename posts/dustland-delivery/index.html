Game Description#

Dustland Delivery puts users in the post -apocalyptic world, where they have to build an empire of delivery. The center of the game is profit, survival and strategic decisions. Gamers start with a truck and gain a team, each of which has unique skills. The purpose of the game? Build cities by successful delivery of goods, competing with rivals and overcoming dangers. The world is inexorable. Users move in an insidious area, control resources and make a critical choice. Each solution affects the result. There are no tips in Title, users must adapt to survive. This title is a sandbox that encourages experiments and independence of gamers.

Success depends on the creation of a strong team and the effectiveness of operations. Users must measure risk and remuneration, taking into account factors such as the capacity of the crew, the value of the cargo and the safety of the route. The game encourages strategic thinking and adaptability. Dustland Delivery is a unique task: prosperity in a hostile environment. Users should become savvy entrepreneurs, skillful survivors and strategic thinkers. The path from a lone trucker to the magnate building a city requires effort, but brings satisfaction. Here you will have to constantly make decisions that will influence.
