Game Description#

theHunter: Primal is an open-world survival game that you can play online with a friend to fight bloodthirsty dinosaurs for free on a pirate. You can survive on the Internet online with random players or play in solo mode. Each participant takes on the role of a Trailblazer. The action takes place on the planet Primal Eden. TheHunter: Primal online game allows you to explore a huge area of ​​24 square kilometers.

The uninhabited planet places the player in the Cretaceous period. Nature challenges anyone who finds itself in its territories. The possibility of free online play allows you to join forces with partners to challenge the primitive world. The Vast Galaxies Corporation sends people to this planet as punishment. If the hero manages to survive, then his entire criminal past will be completely erased from the databases. If he becomes food for predators, then the whole family will follow in his footsteps.

Each character has a personal droppod. Supplies and various useful items are stored here. To resist predators, you need to look for weapons, supplies, ammunition, etc. Nature is inhabited by poisonous plants, dinosaurs and various wild animals. You will have to fight with 5 unique species of dinosaurs, including Tyrannosaurus Rex, Quetzalcoatlus and others. There is a huge variety of PvP matches available. A feature of the gameplay is a permanent inventory. All collected items are transferred to another character in the next session. If the hero dies, then other players can pick up his loot.
