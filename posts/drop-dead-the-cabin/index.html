Game Description#

Drop Dead The Cabin is a co-op shooter with horror and roguelike elements, in which players will have to survive a night in an abandoned cabin in the forest. Set in a 1980s horror film setting, two lost travelers find a hideout formerly owned by a mad scientist named Doctor Monday. His monstrous experiments have created hordes of the undead, which the brave survivors must face.

Gameplay focuses on working together to defend against waves of zombies and complete various tasks to escape. To survive, you must constantly maintain electricity, search for fuel for the generator and fight off constantly arriving enemies. The heroes have a variety of weapons in their arsenal: from revolvers and knives to baseball bats, but ammunition is strictly limited, and melee weapons have a margin of safety. A successful confrontation requires coordinated actions and competent distribution of resources between participants.

Roguelike elements make every playthrough of Drop Dead: The Cabin unique. Key items, targets, and enemy spawn locations are constantly changing, forcing players to adapt their tactics and re-explore the environment. The project is available both in standard PC mode and using virtual reality technologies, providing maximum immersion in what is happening. The ability to choose between single and co-op modes allows you to experience this nightmare both in the company of a friend and on your own.
