Game Description#

Artis Impact throws the player under the control of Akan, a person traveling around the future world, populated people and artificial intelligence. The action takes place in the post-apocalyptic universe, the heroine being studied along with its AI-Companion Boat. The gameplay is built on step -by -step battles and the completion of side tasks from local inhabitants. The narrative presented through the pixel graphics and the style of comics combines humor with gloomy topics that open as it passes.

What to do in the world Artis impact after the end of the world:

Participate in step -by -step battles with enemies and bosses. Explore locations in search of resources and hidden stories. Perform the tasks of local inhabitants, revealing the secrets of the world. Equip and decorate your own cozy home. Make your life with part -time jobs and enjoy everyday matters.

The combat system in this title offers a step -by -step structure for testing and clashes with bosses. Gamers develop AKACA abilities with training and improve her weapons for battles with stronger enemies. Success in battles depends on the development of the heroine and her equipment, and not on complex tactical schemes. In addition to the battles, the fantasy world keeps secrets and secrets, and their detection opens access to different pathways and interactions with the environment.

Outside the main storyline, the game proposes to expand and equip personal housing. Various household classes are available, including food cooking, physical exercises and bathing. To ensure these classes, Akane takes on part -time jobs, earning money and managing his finances. Such mechanics create alternative goals in the retro-world of this project: from communication with robots and other characters to finding potato crockete for dinner.
