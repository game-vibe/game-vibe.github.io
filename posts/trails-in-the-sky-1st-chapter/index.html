Game Description#

Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter follows Estelle Bright and her half-brother Joshua as they begin their journey in the peacekeeping guild. Their initial task is to earn recognition by completing various tasks. A seemingly simple task in the Kingdom of Liberl gradually reveals a difficult political situation. The pair soon discover that their personal ambitions are intertwined with the stability of the state, forcing the heroes to face challenges beyond their training.

Gameplay in Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter offers two different combat systems to fight your opponents. One option is traditional turn-based tactics, with an emphasis on the strategic placement of characters and the use of abilities on the battlefield. An alternative is real-time battles, which offer a more dynamic approach. All interactions in the world depend on the player’s progress: the lines of minor characters and townspeople change depending on completed tasks and story events.

The narrative begins as a personal story, but grows into a sweeping tale of intrigue and danger. Estelle and Joshua’s investigation uncovers an underground conspiracy originating in the highest echelons of power in the kingdom. This discovery changes the essence of the main conflict, forcing the squad to confront the forces that undermine the country’s security from within. The plot combines political maneuvers and personal relationships between the characters against the backdrop of a growing threat to the entire nation.
