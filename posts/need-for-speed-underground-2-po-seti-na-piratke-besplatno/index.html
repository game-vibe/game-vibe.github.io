Game Description#

InNeed For Speed ​​Underground 2 you can compete online with racers from all over the world. For free, you can play online with a friend on pirates, taking part in all modes of NFSU2. Multiplayer includes all types of races from the single player campaign. Events take place in the city of Bayview. According to the plot, the main character must defeat the best racer - Caleb.

A huge city divided into districts is available for exploration. The possibility of free online play includes joint exploration of the streets of the metropolis. To take part in the race, you need to drive up to a special color marker. The NFS Underground 2 campaign consists of 5 stages that unlock as you progress. Car tuning has expanded significantly due to a huge number of vinyls, spoilers, tires, hoods, etc.

Competitions consist of standard circuit racing, sprinting, drag racing, drifting and tournaments called Street X. A Professional League is also available. In addition, there are Jeep Racing, Quick Drive and Test Drive. Need For Speed ​​Underground 2 features cars from manufacturers such as Nissan, Toyota, Ford and others. Jeeps include the following brands: Cadillac Escalade, Hummer H2 and Lincoln Navigator.

The main characters are Rachel Teller, Caleb Callan Race, Tommy, Nikki Morris and the Street Reapers. The first car is a Nissan 350Z, which awaits players upon arrival at the airport. The metropolis consists of several districts with their own characteristics and types of buildings. The areas consist of Downtown, Old District, Northern Highlands, East Port, and West Port. During a free race, random racers can challenge you to a duel.
