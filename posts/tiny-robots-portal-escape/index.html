Game Description#

Tiny Robots: Portal Escape is a 3D puzzle game whose plot begins with the kidnapping of the protagonist’s grandfather. A young and smart robot named Telly witnesses this incident on his way to his relative’s house. On the spot, he discovers only a devastated workshop, broken inventions, and the only link connecting him with the kidnapped man - a working radio station. The protagonist will have to find out who is behind this crime and what are the motives of the attackers. The investigation will take him through many unusual worlds filled with complex mysteries and powerful opponents.

The journey to save grandfather unfolds over 60 levels, each of which is a colorful location full of secrets. Successful completion of Tiny Robots: Portal Escape requires gamers to pay attention to detail and use logical thinking to interact with the environment. To overcome all obstacles and get closer to solving the mystery, you need to perform a number of actions. To successfully advance through the story, the gamer will have to:

carefully examine locations in search of evidence and hidden objects; solve a variety of mechanical puzzles to advance; collect fragments to later create useful artifacts.

In addition to the main adventure, the project offers a large number of mini-games, as well as the ability to change the appearance of the protagonist, combining hundreds of parts, even installing a shark head or a jet engine instead of legs. Artifacts collected according to the drawings will become an indispensable aid in the confrontation with enemies, which will culminate in battles with epic bosses at the end of each chapter. It is the victory over these powerful opponents that will allow the robot Telly to move step by step closer to saving his grandfather and revealing all the secrets.
