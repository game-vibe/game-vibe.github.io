Game Description#

Total War SHOGUN 2 combines elements of turn-based strategy and RTS, allowing you to play online as a pirate, fight alone, or compete with a friend on numerous interactive maps. All online content is available for free, including single content and addons. The opportunity to play online online with both friends and players from all over the world is also available. The gameplay of Total War SHOGUN 2 is aimed at large-scale battles, where only the tactics and abilities of the commander are of key importance.

The edition includes such additions as The Ikko Ikki Clan, Sengoku Jidai Unit, Rise of the Samurai, The Hattori Clan, Blood Pack and Fall of the Samurai, as well as the possibility of free online play. When governing a state, it is necessary to engage in the development of new technologies, training of troops, intelligence, economics, religion and other issues important for the development of the state. Network play gives you the opportunity to both team up with friends against a common enemy, and compete against each other in PvP mode.

The gameplay is divided into two modes: tactical and strategic. In the first case, with the help of ground and sea units, it is necessary to take part in full-scale battles of up to 6,000 people. Generals and admirals play an important role in the morale and strength of troops. The units themselves are divided into units of up to 20 units. Monks study Christianity and Shinto Buddhism, allowing them to receive certain buffs and improvements.

The strategic part is devoted to exploring the map of Japan, consisting of 15 provinces that need to be conquered. You can achieve the status of a legendary diplomat if you use an additional skill in negotiations with enemies. You can trade with an Ainu village for iron, Jurchen tribes for horses, Korea for cotton, China for silk, Vietnam and Indonesia for blessings.
