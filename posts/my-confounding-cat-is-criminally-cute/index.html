Game Description#

My Confounding Cat is Criminally Cute! is a touching visual novel whose story introduces a reclusive magician and his unexpected guest. The story centers on a young man named Blaze, who, due to a curse placed on him, is forced to spend daylight hours in the guise of a black cat. His fate takes a sharp turn after meeting Aster, a gifted but completely unsociable wizard. This sorcerer prefers solitary work on magic potions in his forest hut to communicating with people. The habitual and measured life of the hermit is disrupted when a mysterious guest enters his home through the chimney.

Removing an ancient curse:

Young Blaze in cat form. The hermit magician Aster and his potions. Events in a secluded forest hut. The goal is to find a way to break the spell. Aster overcoming his isolation. The risk of remaining an animal forever.

Soon Aster reveals the whole truth about the unusual animal and the difficult situation in which Blaze finds himself. A difficult task falls on the shoulders of the magician: he needs to find a way to break the ancient spell in order to save his new acquaintance. The process of removing the curse in My Confounding Cat is Criminally Cute! will require the protagonist not only to have deep knowledge of alchemy, but also to overcome his own isolation. Any delay or mistake in finding a solution can lead to irreversible consequences, because Blaze faces the prospect of remaining forever in animal form.
