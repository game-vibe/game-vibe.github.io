Game Description#

Don’T Messes with Bober begins with a character from the city to the city to a country house for the relaxation period. The arrival of the protagonist is conceived as an escape from the hustle and bustle, but one careless act against the local fauna completely changes the situation. This action awakens a beaver that begins the campaign of revenge against a person. The narrative was created in the spirit of horror films of the 1980s, turning a secluded location into a hostile territory. Search for calm is replaced by a state of constant danger.

The basis of the game process of the Doon Mess with Bober is to move the first -person surroundings under an ongoing threat. The player uses secrecy and attentiveness to avoid detection of ruthless animals. Shelter is the main mechanics for maintaining life, since a clash with beavers leads to inevitable death. The detection of the location of the character by the creature launches pursuit of the forest and near the river.

This experience is a cycle of research, hide and hide from a decisive pursuer. This game contains outright scenes with blood, which become the result of unsuccessful attempts to get away from the attacks of Beaver. The area available for research includes a dam - a location related to the behavior of the beast. In the moments of the lull, the protagonist can do river fishing, which contrasts with the main task of survival. The gameplay in the framework of the horror for survival makes you rely on evasion, and not on the battle in order to survive.
