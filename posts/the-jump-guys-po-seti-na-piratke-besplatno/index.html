Game Description#

The Jump Guys provides free online play and allows you to play online for free, including pirates. You can enjoy the game with a friend or take part in exciting competitions online. This online game features 40 levels, each offering unique physics challenges and obstacles.

This platformer features challenges ranging from jumping on a huge trampoline to slippery ice that requires your ability to dodge snowballs and maneuver a sled. You will be tested with ropes and floating logs, where you need to show dexterity and balance. And, of course, one of the levels will offer you the opportunity to fly using a jetpack.

In all these levels, a sweet challenge awaits you: collect the candy before reaching the finish line. Although not required, collecting candy and completing a level will quickly earn you stars. These stars unlock even more levels that can be explored alone or with up to three friends in local or online multiplayer mode.

But the fun doesn’t end there. The Jump Guys also features 12 exciting arenas for a change of pace. They offer a variety of team and solo challenges, including basketball, football, volleyball, and even the battle for the title of last man standing. Other arenas are about collecting items, holding a flag, or completing an obstacle course. And for a truly wild experience, some arenas allow you to use jetpacks, which completely changes the approach to the game.
