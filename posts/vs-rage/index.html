Game Description#

In V’s Rage, an exhausted mother is awakened by a mysterious voice, transporting her against her will to the cursed game world. Trapped in a surreal arcade nightmare, the heroine has a single goal - to find a way out of a bizarre low-poly dimension. The protagonist V will have to defeat any strange creature and absurd enemy encountered along the way. The main means of defense and attack is the frying pan, supplemented by parries and throwing attacks.

Survival depends on mastering these basic mechanics without any initial instructions or clues. The gameplay in V’s Rage changes with each stage, creating a series of different challenges. Each level offers a new experience, changing the main genre from beat ‘em up brawls to shoot ‘em up battles. Events also unfold in the form of run-and-gun shootouts and classic side-scrolling, ensuring that no segment repeats the previous one. This structure forces players to constantly adapt their approach to new mechanics.

In the absence of learning, progress is achieved through a cycle of trial, error and repetition. This arcade project is built on the spirit of the classics from the arcades of the 80s and 90s, including titles such as Final Fight and Parodius. The visuals pay homage to the anime’s ’80s aesthetic, while the hand-drawn approach captures its comic book energy. The surreal world is filled with furious combat and parodies of established archetypes of characters and situations. The main plot focuses on V, who is forced to fight her way through a cursed game to escape.
