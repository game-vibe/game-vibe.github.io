Game Description#

Survival Nation: Lost Horizon opens up the opportunity to play online with a friend, even on a pirate, and completely free. This online network game takes you to a world where every day is a struggle for survival among the zombie apocalypse. There is no place for stories about the past, only your determination and ability to survive in the open world matters.

In this role-playing game, you don’t just craft items, but also develop your character’s unique abilities by choosing skills in three categories: combat, survival, and physical endurance. In addition, you will have to face the realities of hunger and thirst, search for food in abandoned buildings, hunt, fish and farm to provide yourself and your comrades with fresh food.

Of course, a zombie apocalypse is unthinkable without the risen dead. Your actions are inextricably linked with these hungry crowds. Working as a team, protect your base, exterminate powerful mutants, or, if you are not averse to risk, go hunting for zombie leaders for a valuable reward. However, do not forget that the real danger may come not from the dead, but from the living themselves.

Survival Nation: Lost Horizon offers play in both online and offline modes. Join friends for cooperative PvE adventures or go solo on a private server. Explore the open world at your own pace, find the best resources and develop survival tactics.
