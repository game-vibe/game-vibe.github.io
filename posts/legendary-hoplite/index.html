Game Description#

Legendary Hoplite uses a setting inspired by Greek mythology. The project has its own history, lore and secrets that are revealed as you earn achievements. In the story, Odysseus, who returned from Troy, did not rule Ithaca for long, because he was killed by his own illegitimate son. After this, the city-state was besieged by legions of the dead. Dio volunteered to defend the policy. The young commander can build objects, summon units, use ordinary equipment and use artifacts blessed by the ancient gods themselves. As he progresses, he gradually awakens his true power. The story campaign is divided into 100 levels.

The legendary hoplite crosses 2 genres. It is a hybrid of Tower Defense and Action RPG. In this game, the user not only places defensive structures along the path of enemies, but also takes direct part in the battle with them. The user is allowed to move his character left and right, deal damage to adversaries with basic attacks and activate special abilities. These skills cool down for a while after being used. The opponents here are varied. Among them there are bosses. These are creatures from ancient myths: Cerberus, Hydra, Minotaur, Gorgon and so on. In total there are 60 basic types of enemies and hundreds of their variations. The video game allows you to create various builds for them. There are many items for this: helmets, spears, swords, shields. There is also a leveling tree.
