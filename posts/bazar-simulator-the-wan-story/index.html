Game Description#

Bazar Simulator: The Wan Story will throw players in the role of Wang, who arrived in Bokeron City to control the bazaar of his uncle. While his uncle, Van Lun, is passionate about slot machines, Van’s father, Yussef, acts as a mentor in business. However, the outlet itself stores secrets leading to cooperation with the local mafia and a hidden conspiracy. This situation forces the protagonist to combine daily commerce with investigations regarding his own family, combining the management of the shop full of intrigues.

The main gameplay in Bazar Simulator: The Wan Story is focused on the detailed management of the commercial space. The player will have to engage in the interior of the store, selecting furniture, wall colors and flooring to create a unique environment. The growth of the enterprise is achieved by the purchase of improvements that expand its capacity and attract more visitors. Everyday tasks include replenishment of stocks of goods, restoring order in the trading floor and satisfying the needs of customers to maintain profitability.

Bokeron City is an open world for research outside the bazaar. You can move around the environment on a scooter, also available for personalization. The appearance of the character is allowed to change using various clothes and hairstyles. As the plot develops, access to the combat system using unlocked weapons opens. Additional classes include the theft of transport for the sake of money, the use of parkour for movement and search on a map of collection songs that complement the world.
