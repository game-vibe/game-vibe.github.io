Game Description#

Will You Wheel? is a title that combines life simulation with racing mechanics, offering a variety of activities and opportunities to personalize and upgrade your cars. From the very beginning, gamers are immersed in a world where fixing broken cars is just the beginning. The title offers a variety of tasks that help users earn money, which can then be used to buy spare parts, upgrade cars, and participate in various aspects of life, such as food delivery or property management.

The goal is clear: make your car race-ready by collecting parts, customizing it, and competing in various events. To get started, players need to fix a broken car. This process is not just a renovation, but a way to immerse yourself in the world. Gamers can collect junk, haul it from the junkyard, and begin the painstaking work of restoring it to functionality. This may include replacing old components with new ones, such as installing turbo engines, air filters or exhaust systems.

For many in Will You Wheel? the thrill of turning a decrepit car into a performance machine is a major part of the experience. When the machine is ready, the next step is to modernize it. Users can customize their cars for various types of competitions, such as street racing or rallying. There’s no one-size-fits-all approach here: tuning a car for racing requires attention to detail. Adding the right details at the right time can make a big difference.
