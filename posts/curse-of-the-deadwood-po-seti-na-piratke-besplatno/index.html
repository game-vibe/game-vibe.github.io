Game Description#

Curse of the Deadwood is an adventure with the ability to play online for free as a pirate with a friend, exploring the world of Nottington. Alone or online online, you will have to fight numerous enemies and go explore the Dead Forest. The online game Curse of the Deadwood tells the story of two friends who decide to fight back against an ancient evil by fighting hordes of the living dead.

A wide arsenal of weapons, melee attacks and magical abilities are available. You can arm yourself with Saw Mauler, Twig Snapper, Amber Blammer and other weapons. The possibility of free online play allows you to go through the story campaign in co-op. Some game mechanics include elements of randomness: generation of waves of enemies, placement of objects, etc. You will have to fight with Splinters, Thorns, Knots and other enemies. The levels contain various puzzles. Deadwood contains many dangers, including powerful bosses. While exploring the territory, you need to collect auxiliary items and replenish ammunition.

Skills and weapons can be upgraded. A skill tree is available to determine your battle strategy. The style of passing depends on the leveling of the character. You can make the protagonist melee or use ranged attacks. Throughout the story, a Woodling named Late must save the world from destruction. The stone giant Rogard helps him in his adventures.
