Game Description#

Organized Inside is a meditative puzzle game in which the process of cleaning becomes a metaphor for personal growth and transformation. The project invites you to immerse yourself in the leisurely systematization of everyday objects, being completely devoid of timers, points or health bars. The main emphasis is on relaxation and finding harmony in the chaos of everyday life. The narrative unfolds through the stories of five strangers whose secrets must be unraveled by putting their things in order. On this journey, players are accompanied by a cat mentor, providing useful advice and creating a cozy atmosphere. The gameplay allows you not only to find the perfect place for each thing, but also to gradually discover the joy and meaning of life.

Players will encounter cluttered spaces such as kitchen countertops, cabinets, or car interiors. The challenge is to place all the items neatly, which sometimes feels like solving a spatial puzzle. Some objects can be stacked or stacked, while others require pre-washing before taking their place. The Organized Inside project offers over a thousand familiar items for organization. Control is implemented entirely using the mouse, and the isometric perspective provides a convenient overview for putting things in perfect order. Finding hidden items within others adds an extra layer of exploration to the gameplay.
