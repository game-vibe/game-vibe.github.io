Game Description#

High On Life is a humorous first -person shooter with metroid elements. It is made in a science fiction setting. Moments with the action alternate with more calm episodes, where you need to chat with hasty characters, fulfill their instructions and solve puzzles. The rich arsenal includes unusual and diverse weapons with two types of shooting - the main and alternative. There are analogues of pistols, shotgun, machine guns and so on. The game from other representatives of the FPS genre differs in that guns here are able to talk here. The trunks often comment on the actions of the player and joke. They have many prepared replicas. For those who do not like this feature, the developers have added the opportunity to adjust the frequency of phrases in the settings. This project is divided into levels. At the same time, the video game encourages the relay of already passed locations. There are temporarily inaccessible places on the maps. In order to get into them, you need to get an upgrade like that allows you to cling to the ledges of the whip or reactive satchel.

Having received new abilities, the user can return to any already seen stage and collect all missing into chests. They usually store a currency that is spent on the purchase of improvements, or collection items. The story in Hol tells about the adventures of the unsuccessful teenager. After graduation, he did not find a decent job, did not enter the college and did not achieve any significant success. But suddenly he had to become a hero, because the gang of Garmanteosis attacked Earth. Aliens want to use people as a drug. The future defender of mankind finds a talking weapon from a real bustle hunter and instead goes to save the world from an alien cartel G3. Justin Royland was responsible for the comedy plot, who wrote the script for Rick and Morty and other animated series designed for an adult audience.
