Game Description#

Warhammer 40,000: Darktide is a co-op first-person shooter. Events take place in the huge city of Tertium on the planet Atom Prime. The cult of insane heretics Admonition is trying to capture him. Four heroes, together with the forces of the Inquisition, must resist Chaos and stop the bloodthirsty horde of the living dead and save the inhabitants from extermination.

Since this is a classic co-op shooter, the gameplay is primarily focused on shooting and killing demonic enemies using melee weapons. Players must cooperate with each other to explore locations and destroy the horde.

The characters you train have different equipment and skills that make them stand out on the battlefield. The variety of enemies is also impressive - from undead zombies to huge, voracious monsters summoned through magic and science. The arsenal of weapons allows you to use not only standard options, such as machine guns, shotguns and sniper rifles, but also heavy futuristic weapons, such as a plasma gun. You can also use cover and various traps to destroy the enemy as quickly as possible and avoid losses.

The game modes were created for multiplayer (up to four people), but the creators also made it possible to play in a single player mode, in which artificial intelligence takes control of other characters. The game is powered by a significantly improved proprietary engine and offers an atmospheric visual setting with dark tones befitting the world of Warhammer. Locations, made with attention to detail, fascinate with their thoughtfulness and detail.
