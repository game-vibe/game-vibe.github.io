Game Description#

SeaOrama: World of Shipping is a game that puts you in the captain’s chair of your own growing maritime empire. Forget romantic notions of rolling waves and salty sunsets; it’s a game of hard hats and spreadsheets where strategic instinct reigns supreme. Start with the only reliable tug - your gateway to a global network of 90 ports, each a microcosm of supply and demand. Analyze the ebb and flow of trade winds, not only marine, but also economic and political. Will you stake unstable war zones for quick profits or establish reliable routes through stable countries? Every decision affects your income.

Your nascent fleet awaits you - a motley crew of 17 types of ships, each hungry for cargo. Tankers gulp tons of raw materials, and nimble container ships ply between bustling metropolises. Optimize their schedule so they are the right tool for the job and not a rusty anchor dragging you down. Contracts, the lifeblood of any shipping magnate, dangle like carrots on a stick. Make profitable deals, but watch out for hidden clauses and fickle customers. Unexpected storms, political unrest, or even an illegal container stuck in the Suez Canal can turn profit into danger in the blink of an eye. SeaOrama: World of Shipping is not a leisurely cruise. This is a journey through the stormiest waters of commerce, where only the cunning and adaptable survive. So polish your brass binoculars, strap on your metaphorical life jacket, and get ready to chart your course to shipping supremacy.
