Game Description#

Tom Clancy’s Rainbow Six Lockdown is a tactical shooter with a first-person view. In addition to the single, there is multiplayer, which is available online online. You can also play Pirate with a friend online. In single player mode, the plot revolves around the Legion virus. The Rainbow 6 fighters will have to figure out how various terrorist groups are connected to it.

Compared to its predecessors, Tom Clancy’s Rainbow Six Lockdown has improved graphics. The creators have improved the lighting, as well as increased the number of polygons and increased the resolution of textures. In addition, the developers completely redesigned the interface. In addition to firearms, the title includes viral grenades, laser tripwires, gadgets such as lock fuses, hammers and other items. Before going on a mission, you can customize the equipment for each of the 3 team members. They are allowed to give simple orders to their wards. The gameplay has become more arcade-like, and the barrier to entry for beginners has decreased. For example: planning operations as before is no longer required. Monitoring bots is also no longer necessary, since fallen soldiers will be replaced by new people on the next mission.

The possibility of free online play allows users to choose 4 classes. You can be a commando, an engineer, a special forces soldier and a combat medic. Each specialization has its own strengths and weaknesses. And professions also differ in their arsenal and skills. The same doctor is not able to use heavy weapons, but he can restore the health of his allies. Multiplayer includes 9 maps. At the request of fans, 3 classic locations migrated to this sequel from previous parts.
