Game Description#

Kulebra and the Souls of Limbo puts the gamer in the role of Kulebra, an entity that helps the inhabitants of Limbo. Walking without legs, Culebra explores this dimension to collect clues, special items and evidence. The collected elements play an important role for the inhabitants of Limbo to find peace and move beyond their current situation. The process involves interacting with characters and exploring locations to understand their difficulties.

In Kulebra and the Souls of Limbo, exploration reveals a place influenced by Latin American lore, where creatures are trapped, living their days in cycles. Culebra uses a range of abilities, including acts of kindness and sometimes direct intervention, to help these creatures face their unresolved loss and emotional wounds. Finding out the reasons for their stagnation requires close observation and study of the events that chain them to this repetitive existence; this game offers challenges that require a person’s ingenuity and perseverance.

A Path Through Limbo also contains episodes with humorous touches alongside its more serious themes. Detective work forms a central element of the experience, requiring attention to detail and logical analysis to solve problems. Information gained through dialogue, environmental inspection, and problem solving contributes to understanding the overall situation in Limbo and among its population. The notebook system provides the ability to refer to the collected facts at any time, providing support in drawing up a single narrative line.
