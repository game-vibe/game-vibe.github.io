Game Description#

Knock on the Coffin Lid is a role-playing game with strategy elements where you need to help the main character fulfill his destiny. However, there is one caveat - the protagonist is already dead. In order to complete the journey, he had to rise from the dead. The plot of Knock on the Coffin Lid is confusing and offers two different paths: blindly follow your destiny or try to understand the intricacies of history.

The game offers you to choose the side you want to help. For example, on the one hand there are gnomes who are eager to destroy everything connected with elven history. Or make a deal with the ogre, who offers a generous reward. Future events that will not go unnoticed depend on the player’s choice. There are about 50 such forks in KOTCL. One path can make further adventures much easier, while the other can make it more difficult.

Equipment includes about 150 units of ammunition and various equipment. Weapons and armor affect the gameplay and characteristics of the hero. Available for collecting 6 sets of armor. As you progress, the protagonist will encounter 50 types of enemies and 20 enhanced monsters, requiring an individual approach and tactics.

In four unique biomes, you must fight 12 challenging bosses and 2 special leaders, ready to challenge only the most daring players. The gameplay contains elements of a card game, offering to build a deck of 150 cards, allowing you to create unique battle tactics.
