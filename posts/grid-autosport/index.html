Game Description#

GRID Autosport develops a career mode and free driving in a huge number of cars, which can also be upgraded depending on your preferences. The new part of GRID is a car simulator with several game modes. Career passage is a single, story-based scenario part - this is about upgrading your driver, team of mechanics and building a reputation at the level of your driving. Here we upgrade the car and drive on 100 routes in 22 game locations, distributed according to difficulty and prestige.

Multiplayer mode allows players to compete with each other. Career mode is an innovation in the gaming industry. Players themselves choose their driving style, car, upgrade methods and micro-management of the game. If you want to drive super-racing cars, then be prepared that you will have to enter into the most profitable, not expensive, contracts. If you want to build a racing career and perform in public for money, then the development path will be appropriate.

All tracks have been developed from the previous version of GRID Autosport. Therefore, tight turns, sharp climbs and incredible drifts are the norm in the new part. The new level of car customization has been expanded by two levels. The first is pumping up the technical part of the car - engines, changing the crankshaft, tires, brakes, gearbox, suspension. The second is the appearance of the car. Races in multiplayer mode are a struggle between several players from the gaming community. Take on the best, keep up with the worst!
