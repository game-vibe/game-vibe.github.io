Game Description#

We Heist Too takes place in Rivadorium, a city where nothing ever stays the same. Users get used to the role of a thief, move silently through the streets, plan and carry out high-stakes robberies. This world doesn’t require much care, and from the very beginning you will notice that every corner of the city is fraught with opportunity. But the tricky part is knowing which opportunities are worth pursuing. Here’s the kicker: Whether you’re browsing a jewelry store or making your way through a crowded market, the title constantly makes you wonder: Is it worth the risk? And, of course, there is always risk.

Strategy isn’t just a suggestion here - it’s the heart of the game. Each gamer’s move triggers a chain of possible consequences. One wrong step and a carefully planned heist can turn into chaos. But on the other hand, when you make the perfect move, the reward seems even sweeter. For example, navigating a luxury mansion may seem simple at first, but with patrolling guards and security systems lurking in every corner, it becomes a delicate dance. Sometimes all it takes is a well-placed red herring or a convincing lie to slip by undetected. And if you prefer a subtle approach, then Rivadorium is your playground. However, stealth is not the only way to play We Heist Too. Users can dive headfirst into bolder tactics if they choose.
