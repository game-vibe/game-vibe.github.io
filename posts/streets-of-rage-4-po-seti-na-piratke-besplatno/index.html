Game Description#

Streets of Rage 4 is a cooperative action game that you can play online with a friend, fighting for free on a pirate ship with a variety of enemies and bosses. You can play through the campaign both online and on the public screen. Events begin 10 years after the end of the third part. The gameplay of Bare Knuckle IV has not changed much since the previous games, but it has acquired many new mechanics. Combat is a combination of various strikes, as well as the ability to grab or use surrounding objects for your own purposes.

The main difference from its predecessors is that the possibility of free online play allows up to four people to team up on one device! The mechanics of Streets of Rage 4 are built on constant confrontation with different enemies who have unique patterns and characteristics of attacks. The animations are completely handmade. The online game Streets of Rage 4 invites you to go solo or with friends to clean up the streets of Wood Oak City. At the end of each level there is a battle with a strong boss. You need to find his weaknesses in order to deliver effective attacks.

The adventure retains all the features of the classic games, but introduces many new characters and significantly expands the gameplay possibilities. There are 10 charismatic heroes to choose from, each with their own distinctive features. In a company or in solo mode, you will have to go through 12 action-packed levels. Yuzo Koshiro, Motohiro Kawashima, Yoko Shimomura and others. The Boss Rush mode is available, offering to fight all the leaders in a row.
