Game Description#

Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War - Soulstorm is an addition to the original game and offers users to try out several new innovations, including playing online with a friend on a pirate. 2 new armies have been added here - Sisters of Battle and Dark Eldar. Thus, there are 9 playable sides, and each can be used to play on the Internet online. In addition, users now have the ability to travel through the star system to fight in different objects and territories.

The gameplay aspects of Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War - Soulstorm are no different from those in previous parts of the series. Each race has 1 special skill that allows them to gain an advantage in certain aspects of battle. However, if the enemy captures key points, this skill is lost. For the Sisters of Battle, this is especially important, because among all the available armies they have the lowest combat potential. This deficiency is compensated by the faith that each fighter in the squad develops. However, having lost faith, the fighters will have no chance in a skirmish in equal numbers.

In addition to tactical variability, online play encourages gamers to constantly monitor resources and improve the skills of individual classes. For example, the same Sisters of Battle can turn the tide of a battle using the “Emperor’s Touch” skill. With its help, the weapon becomes more effective. As for the Dark Eldar, they have hand-to-hand combat skills, and their builders only need to create an imitation of the building’s frame, after which construction will immediately begin. The possibility of free online play allows you to fully unleash the potential of these races, turning your army into a truly deadly weapon.
