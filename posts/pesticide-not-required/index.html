Game Description#

Pesticide Not Required combines two unlikely worlds: farming and bug control. At first glance, it may seem a little strange to manage crops while fighting swarms of insects, but it’s this bizarre combination that makes the game stand out. In the title you have to take the place of a frog who is tasked with managing a farm and at the same time resisting various insects. There’s a certain charm to the idea of ​​balancing these seemingly unrelated tasks, and it’s this combination that gives the title its freshness.

Every day here brings new challenges, from planting seeds to fighting off the onslaught of creatures, requiring you to be on your guard. From the very beginning, Pesticide Not Required throws you into the thick of farming and survival. The project involves not just caring for the crops, but surviving long enough to reap the benefits. You’ll need to collect resources such as wood, fish, and minerals while keeping an eye on the skies for the next wave of insects.

The farming mechanics are quite simple at first, but the intensity increases as you progress. The battles with insects are getting tougher, the days are getting shorter, and the time is running out. You’ll find yourself in a constant cycle of planting, harvesting, and fighting, all to keep your farm thriving. And yes, it’s all about balance. If you focus too much on one aspect, another part of your farm may fall behind, so multitasking becomes key.
