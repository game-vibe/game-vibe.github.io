Game Description#

Ace Combat 7 Skies Unknown is a hybrid of a flight simulator and an arcade action game. You can play it alone or with a friend online on a pirate. Multiplayer is only available online online. In this sequel, the creators have improved the physics. Air flows now have a more realistic effect on aircraft behavior. Aircraft and missiles can become icy if they move through clouds for long periods of time. A lightning strike in rainy weather can briefly disable devices.

The action of the project “Battle of the Aces 7: Unknown Skies” takes place in the Strangereal dimension. And although the world of the game is fictional (the names of the countries are also fictitious), the planes here are real. The title gives you the opportunity to fly and fight in jet fighters. Single and multiplayer games offer 2 types of controls. One is intended for more experienced gamers, and the second is suitable for beginners. Takeoff, landing and mid-air refueling are presented in the form of mini-games. You can skip these steps if you wish. The plot is dedicated to the conflict between Erusia and Useya. The prototypes of these states were Russia and Great Britain, respectively. To understand the story, it is not necessary to first become familiar with past titles in this series. The story has one ending.

In the single player, players take on the role of Useya Air Force pilots. They are asked to complete 20 different tasks. For completing them, points are awarded. They are the in-game currency. You can and should use it to buy new combat units or improvements for them. The store is made in the shape of a tree. There is also easy customization.

In the seventh part, the developers returned checkpoints. Therefore, if a mission fails, users begin the operation not anew, but from the last checkpoint. The possibility of free online play allows gamers to participate in the royal battle and arrange team fights. Each match accommodates a maximum of 8 people.
