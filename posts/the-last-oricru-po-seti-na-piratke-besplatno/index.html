Game Description#

InThe Last Oricru you can play online with a friend, fighting as a pirate online against fantastic creatures. Co-op involves playing on one screen or allows you to join forces online online. The latest Orikru mixes fast-paced third-person action with RPG elements. The possibility of free online play offers the opportunity to go through a rich storyline together with a huge number of forks and unexpected turns of events.

The fantasy world of The Last Oricru combines the Middle Ages with high technology. You have to explore spacious locations, fight enemies, raise the hero’s level, improve abilities and engage in battles with powerful bosses. Any action and decision made affects both the world around us and the global storyline. There are several factions that will need help during the passage. Each side pursues personal interests.

Network play opens up additional opportunities in the passage. For example, new ways to kill bosses become available, or previously inaccessible passages open to hidden areas on the map where interaction between two players is required. There are several pumping branches. You can become a powerful tank, an agile archer or a devastating mage. Together with your partner you will have to combine the abilities of your characters.

The gameplay is quite hardcore, and every enemy is dangerous. All opponents have a unique set of attacks. Death is one of the main mechanics of the adventure. The combat is built on dodges, rolls, parries and counterattacks. The arsenal includes a huge variety of melee weapons, ranged weapons and various magical attacks. Each piece of equipment has individual characteristics.
