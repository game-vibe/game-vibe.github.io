Game Description#

Theres Poop In My Soup is a humorous simulator in which the main goal is to organize large-scale riots on city streets. The protagonist, being on a hill, carries out targeted discharges of excrement on unsuspecting pedestrians and passing vehicles. Each successful throw earns points, and creating consecutive hits generates multipliers for the final score. The geography of hooligan antics covers several megacities, from New York to Beijing. The entire process is based on a physical model that takes into account the flight path and the behavior of objects after a collision. The simple graphic design is complemented by a thematic soundtrack.

Advancement through the three available levels provides access to new capabilities and weapons. Theres Poop In My Soup requires the gamer to have some skill in order to complete complex tasks. Mastering all the mechanics allows you to unlock upgrades and more powerful projectiles designed for total chaos. To achieve maximum results and gain access to the entire arsenal, you must complete certain tasks:

hit specific types of targets, be they pedestrians or animals. make difficult combination throws to increase points. use special projectiles, like bombs, for mass destruction. complete all items from a special list of tasks at each stage.
