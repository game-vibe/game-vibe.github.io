Game Description#

Backrooms: Last Room allows online play for free, immersing players in a world of creepy backrooms seen through the lens of a VHS camera. This chilling adventure can be completed with a friend on a pirate, which makes the gameplay even more exciting. Multiplayer online gaming taps into the primal fear of isolation and the unknown. As you delve deeper into these mysterious back rooms, the tension increases with every step.

The game is based on a fateful shopping trip. One minute you’re wandering around the shops, and the next you’re hearing whimsical music that transports you into a labyrinth of endless corridors. Talk about a bad shopping experience! Now you have to navigate this creepy kingdom, armed only with a flashlight and ingenuity.

While exploring ready-made locations, keep an eye out for dangers lurking in the shadows. You never know what creepy creatures might be waiting around the next corner. But not everything is so gloomy - there is a glimmer of hope in the form of portal people who are ready to lend a helping hand. Just don’t count on them to do all the hard work.

The game is currently under development. There’s no way to save progress yet, so you’ll have to complete this otherworldly odyssey in one go. Multiplayer via Steam is also in development and promises to add a new dimension to the back room gaming experience. Who knows? Maybe misery really does love company.
