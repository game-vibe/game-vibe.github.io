Game Description#

The immersion in Urban Tale is made in the form of a role-playing adventure, where you have to go to conquer a big city. The game creates a life simulation where the main character tries to succeed in life. The user’s choices and decisions made along the way will determine what area the protagonist will go into to develop. You have to start from scratch, trying to find your way from the possible options.

The atmosphere in Urban Tale is filled with realism, allowing you to feel the difficulties and obstacles that arise for careerists. The player needs to decide on a job choice in order to start working in the field. By gaining experience and skills, the hero will develop and grow as a professional, increasing his income level and job position. As you achieve success, you can improve your quality of life by changing the way you do things.

The user can save up money and create his own business or change his occupation. The game allows you to fully simulate the life of an average resident in order to get a chance to experience different specialties and life paths. Each story and passage becomes unique thanks to the user’s interactions with the outside world and other inhabitants living in an urban environment. There is a wide variety to choose from, from a manager to criminal affairs, which also allow you to stay afloat and survive.
