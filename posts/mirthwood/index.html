Game Description#

Mirthwood is a fun medieval fantasy sandbox game that combines the charm of a life simulation with the thrill of an RPG. It offers gamers the opportunity to create their own adventure in a new country, escaping their war-torn homeland. The game offers two main paths: settle down and build a farm, trade with other cities and start a family; or explore a huge open world and fight bandits, monsters and other dangers. One of the main features is the rich and detailed setting, which includes six different regions and three cities, each with its own unique culture, history and secrets.

A Sims-style interaction system has been implemented, allowing gamers to make friends, romance, or antagonize city residents and NPCs. Completing tasks, dynamic events and solving mysteries determine the plot and progress of the gamer. Mirthwood features real-time combat using a variety of weapons, armor, and skills. Players have the opportunity to customize their character and specialize in one of three combat classes: warrior, archer, or mage.

Survival challenges such as storm, winter and disease add an extra layer of challenge, testing the gamer’s mettle. The beautiful hand-drawn art style, immersive sound effects and original musical score contribute to the overall magical atmosphere of the world. While exploring the vast landscape, users can discover rare and epic items that improve gameplay and open up new opportunities.
