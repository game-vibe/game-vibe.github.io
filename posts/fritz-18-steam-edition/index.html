Game Description#

Fritz 18 presents a chess program that is considered one of the most popular in its segment. This is the eighteenth part of the series. The project boasts sophisticated artificial intelligence, which in the past has defeated such famous grandmasters as Vladimir Kramnik and Garry Kasparov. This is more than a game. This title can be classified as educational software and simulators. It contains content for both casuals and experienced chess players. Beginner players may want to consider the Easy Game mode, where each user gets an opponent roughly equal to themselves in terms of skill level.

This chess helps to develop skills. They have the latest AI-based teaching method. The bot first evaluates the skill of its opponent, and then constantly changes its tactics depending on its playing style. This allows you to maintain a gentle balance of difficulty and make each game interesting. You can also test the effectiveness of different combinations here. The release contains an improved version called the Steam Edition. In the re-release of Fritz 18, the developers improved the engine. They also improved the animation and created a new chessboard with Direct2D technology. Intelligent tips appeared. In difficult situations, the video game gives gamers subtle hints. If users do not notice or ignore the warnings, then on the next turn they will be told what exactly they missed.
