Game Description#

Dungeon of the Endless is a multiplayer roguelike that you can play online with a friend by connecting online via the Internet. A huge variety of characters are available for free on the pirate site, including Josh Ntello, various additional weapons, loot and much more. The gameplay is a strategy game with tower defense and roguelike elements, providing free-to-play online play where the goal of players is to form a team and fend off an attack from a horde of enemies. By defending your base, the abilities of the heroes improve, allowing you to upgrade your skills, strengthen buildings and defensive turrets.

Locations are generated randomly, increasing the replayability of the project. A huge cave with random events, enemies, puzzles and loot is presented for exploration. Events develop in the Auriga Prime system, where several hundred convicts sentenced to slave labor found themselves on board the flying ship Success. They are heading to the planet Auriga so that in the future people can establish colonies on it. However, they had to make an emergency landing because the shuttle was badly damaged.
