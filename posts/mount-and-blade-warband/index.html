Game Description#

Mount & Blade: Warband is a standalone add-on that brings many improvements and innovations to the game. New textures, object and character models, as well as sophisticated visualization settings. Change FSAA anti-aliasing, tone mapping and other effects in the settings. Modernized animations, improved combat system and the spirit of the heroes. People will flee if they are in imminent danger or, conversely, fight to the last if their morality is high.

Take part in multiplayer battles for up to 64 players. Play on custom or standard maps. Earn money and buy new equipment with it. Ranged weapons can now be used up close in battle. All projectiles, such as arrows, can be picked up and reused. There is marriage to obtain lands of other rulers or other privileges. Woo the ladies with poetry or romance. Allies can be rewarded and promoted, giving them access to lands or warriors. Explore the open game world, visit huge castles and villages, capture territories, hire warriors and increase your chances of winning the race for the throne. Gather your own squad of brave warriors and fight enemy settlements and armies for the right to obtain the throne of Calradia. Expand your territories, increase the size of your army, learn new tactics, or come up with your own battle strategy to get a reward.
