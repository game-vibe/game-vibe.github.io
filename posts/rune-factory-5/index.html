Game Description#

Rune Factory 5, like previous parts of the series, crosses a role-playing game with a farm simulator. The events take place in the border town of Rigbart and its surroundings. At the very beginning, you can choose a girl named Alice or a guy named Ares to play the role of the main character. This sequel uses the amnesia plot twist. The protagonist loses his memory and joins the SEED organization. He becomes a ranger and begins to protect the settlement from monsters, as well as help residents with their errands. He will have to restore the balance between people and nature, which was disrupted due to a series of strange events. In this video game, there is the opportunity to form friendships or romantic relationships with townspeople, as well as get married and start a family (including having a child). There are 12 bachelors and the same number of bachelorettes with different appearances and characters to choose from. They all have many unique storylines.

Completing the main story takes about 35 hours. In this case, the transit time can be increased. There are many third party activities in Rune Factory 5. You can complete side quests, explore the area, go down to dungeons, fight monsters, tame and breed monsters, participate in festivals, dig in the garden, and also engage in cooking, fishing and crafting. Gradually, the hero or heroine discovers new combat skills. Tamed creatures can provide assistance in battle and help obtain resources. The world consists of various biomes. Among them there is a ghost town, an ancient volcano and other locations. The map along with the marking system makes it easier to navigate. The video game has a cycle of day and night, as well as weather conditions. The seasons also change in it. There are 4 of them in total. Seasons affect the growth rate of crops and the behavior of NPCs.
