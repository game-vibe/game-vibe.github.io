Game Description#

ActionR.I.P.D. The Game is based on the film of the same name RIPD: Phantom Patrol. You can play the pirate game online for free with a friend, fighting together against otherworldly forces online online. The plot covers the events of the original film. The game begins with Nick Walker arriving in the afterlife and becoming a ghost agent.

The visual style is stylized as a comic book. The online game allows you to take control of Nick and his partner Roy Pulsifer, an experienced police officer of the 19th century. The heroes work together to destroy monsters called deadoes. Gameplay RIPD The Game is a third-person shooter game. The possibility of free online play is aimed at co-operative completion of the campaign. The storyline and Horde mode are presented, in which you need to hold back the onslaught of a huge crowd of enemies.

The campaign consists of 7 extensive chapters. At the end of each mission, a fight with a powerful boss awaits. Each chapter includes 5 rounds, the difficulty of which increases with each new level. By collecting currency, you can buy or upgrade guns. The arsenal consists of shotguns and assault rifles. There is a banana and a hairdryer, just like in the original film. Opponents can be killed or arrested. After completing all 5 chapters, rewards are collected. To earn more points, you need to complete secondary tasks. You are allowed to place bets, competing with your partner for the maximum number of points.
