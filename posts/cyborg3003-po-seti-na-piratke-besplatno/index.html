Game Description#

Cyborg3003 takes players to a futuristic universe where Earth no longer exists. Here you can play online with a friend, fighting for survival and purification in alien worlds. Six cyborgs, controlled by either humans or artificially intelligent bots, land on unknown planets. In this online game, you can play pirate online for free, fighting in five vast, non-linear worlds. These worlds are full of hostile creatures, ready to tear apart anyone who gets in their way.

Players must work together, using their unique strengths and an arsenal of nine weapons to fend off waves of monsters. Venontime adds a cinematic feel to the game by slowing down those critical moments when the grotesque alien dies. This slow-motion spectacle persists even in the heat of multiplayer chaos.

Survival isn’t just about firepower. Teams can equip themselves with welding machines, medical instruments and body armor to improve their chances. There are seven different types of monsters in the game, each of which is terrible in its own way. For lone wolves, there is a single mode that allows you to destroy aliens offline.

Cyborg3003 paints a bleak picture of the future of humanity, where our brain remains the last remnant of our biological essence. What about everything else? Clean car. It’s a stark reminder of the lengths we can go to to survive as a species. When players search for a new home on planets like Eruption, they’re not just fighting aliens - they’re fighting for the future of humanity.
