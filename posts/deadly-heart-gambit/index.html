Game Description#

Deadly Heart Gambit is an intense card competition, where each card drawn can either extend the allotted time or bring the fatal ending closer. Participants in this fight will have to rely on memory and strategic thinking to avoid a fatal meeting with the Joker, which means an instant loss in the round. Defeat is awarded to the one who draws this single card. To win, you must carefully monitor the cards already played and wisely use the opportunity to skip your turn. The stakes in this duel are incredibly high, because nothing less than your own heart and the remaining time of your life is at stake.

The organizer and main opponent in this risky adventure is a mysterious girl known as Alice-sama. It is she who offers the desperate protagonist a chance to win extra days, months or even years from fate. However, her motives are not so simple: if the protagonist loses completely, she will not hesitate to take his heart for herself. The story in Deadly Heart Gambit is divided into four chapters and offers several different endings depending on your success in the card game and decisions made.

The story is based on a desperate situation: the main character is diagnosed with a disease that leaves him only three months to live. It is this circumstance that pushes him to participate in the gambling game proposed by Alice. Each numbered card successfully drawn from the deck replenishes his life supply, while special items like a knife to eliminate the next card add tactical depth. The project offers two main endings and one hidden secret, which the most attentive players can reveal.
