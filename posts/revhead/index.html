Game Description#

In the REVHEAD car racing simulator, the user will have to design his own car. The protagonist has a friend named Charlie who is well versed in creating cars. He invites the central character to Australia to help him with something. It will be necessary to win the competition and get recognition from Charlie, then it will create improved transport.

There is an opportunity to sell and buy cars, as well as trade in various details and customization items. The setting of the machine is also important, as it will provide a better characteristic, more speed, handling and much more. Players here will be able to create a car of their dreams. The car can be made from scratch, adding unique components and various details. All components can be moved, rotated, removed, replaced, painted and modified. There is a configuration of coloring, appearance, controllability and performance of your car.

It will often be necessary to monitor the performance of the machine, since after a long drive or when collisions, the car is able to break. This can be seen from the smoke that falls out of the hood, or on dents on various parts of the transport. You can also purchase broken cars, repair them, and then participate in races. For example, for 4x4 races, cars are very expensive, and the function of acquiring a broken car simplifies the economy. Victory in races is determined not only by driving skills, but also by a car assembled in the garage. An incorrectly inserted part can be detected in the middle of the race and poorly affect the result.
