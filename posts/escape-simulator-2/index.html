Game Description#

Escape Simulator 2 is a continuation of the popular quest room simulator, offering immersion in solving puzzles from a first-person view. The project provides the opportunity to explore various locations both alone and in the company of friends. Locked in themed rooms, participants must use their wits and powers of observation to find a way out. The environment is fully interactive: almost any item can be picked up, examined, or used to interact with other objects, which creates the feeling of actually being in an escape room.

Solving clever riddles becomes even more fun thanks to the cooperative mode, designed for up to 8 people. Collaboration and communication via built-in chat help you find solutions faster and open locked doors. In addition to ready-made scenarios, Escape Simulator 2 introduces a significantly redesigned Room Editor 2.0. This powerful tool allows gamers to create their own unique challenges by constructing multi-level rooms and filling them with complex mechanisms.

At launch, the title offers three unique sets of rooms, each of which includes four levels. Adventurers will have to uncover the secrets of Dracula’s castle, try to save the Starship EOS research ship and find ancient treasures on the pirate island. Although the atmosphere of the project has become darker and more mysterious, it is not horror and does not contain frightening elements. Each location is filled with hidden secrets and additional tasks, encouraging careful exploration of every corner.
