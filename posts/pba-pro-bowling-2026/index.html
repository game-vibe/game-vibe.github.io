Game Description#

PBA Pro Bowling 2026 is a detailed bowling simulation with gameplay based on an advanced physics model that takes into account the material, weight and shape of each ball’s core. A key success factor is adapting to the dynamic change in the oil surface on the track, which wears out during the match. Competitions take place on 13 detailed venues, from replicas of real stadiums to fictional locations. To select the optimal tactics for any conditions, an arsenal of 272 unique balls with different characteristics is provided. In addition to the classic rules, alternative competition formats are available.

Available gaming disciplines:

Candlepin Bowling is an option with thin cylindrical pins. Duckpin Bowling is a variety with squat and wide pins. Strike Derbies is a competition for the maximum number of strikes in the allotted time. Spare Pickup Challenges - challenges where you need to knock out the remaining pins after the first throw.

The centerpiece is the career mode, which takes you from beginner to sports legend. The path to the pinnacle of fame begins with performances in hometown leagues, gradually opening access to national championships. In PBA Pro Bowling 2026, you’ll be competing against 32 real-life pro bowl stars, requiring your utmost concentration. The money you earn allows you to purchase new equipment and expand your ball collection to match the level of prestigious competitions, including the US Open and Tournament of Champions.
