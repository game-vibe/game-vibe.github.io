Game Description#

The economic simulator Timeflow – Life Sim serves as a financial simulator. The Time-Money project is reminiscent of the turn-based board game Monopoly, where you also need to get rich in various ways.

The project does not have a specific storyline; the gamer himself is free to determine the development of his character. There are a total of 16 character stories available. The player is asked to choose a protagonist, as well as determine his abilities, skills and type of future activity through which he will earn money. The choice is wide: from Internet blogging to investing in real estate. It’s not enough just to collect money and acquire profitable assets, you need to constantly improve yourself through useful contacts and obtaining additional education. You also need to rely on time management, the importance of which is emphasized by the creators of the game. You need to constantly think through your moves in advance, invest in promising industries and spend money thoughtfully, otherwise you can quickly find yourself bankrupt. During the passage, the developers suggest relying on 3 strategies:

accumulation of savings; organization of passive income; choosing a dream or goal at the beginning of the game.

The creators also list all the features that need to be used to achieve your goals.

Among the main ones are:

78 ways to make money: from freelancing to real estate; during the game you can get 3 higher and 3 special educations, as well as master 10 time management techniques and 67 seminars; making 19 useful contacts for career advancement; 96 quests that will teach you how to cope with problems in real life.

Thanks to fresh ideas from players, the Timeflow – Time and Money Simulator simulator is constantly being improved, and new realistic paths to financial independence are being added to the game. The gameplay is accompanied by 15 unique musical compositions and hundreds of high-quality illustrations.
