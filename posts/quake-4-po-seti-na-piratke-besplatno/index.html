Game Description#

Quake 4, in addition to the storyline, provides users with the opportunity to play online with a friend on a pirate. In addition to the story campaign, gamers can create servers for arena battles. Unlike previous parts of the series, a more advanced engine is used here, making online battles look more impressive. Every element of the environment, including objects for interaction, looks more detailed, and character models no longer seem angular.

As for the plot component, Quake 4 continues the motif begun by the second project in the series. The conflict between people and the Strogg reaches a new level, and the central event is the counterattack of earthlings on the planet serving as the capital. In total, the narrative is divided into 3 parts, which in turn have several smaller levels. Battles take place inside the base, in trenches, at field points and near artillery installations. Each time, the system updates the list of combat missions, and depending on the presence of third-party buildings on the territory, the gamer is able to diversify the passage. In addition, the list of barrels is quite large, thanks to which you can quickly destroy classes of creatures that differ in size and damage caused.

The possibility of free online play is implemented through arena battles. Their sizes may vary depending on which card format is used. Individual fragments hovering in space or a closed multi-level arena - each time the gamer will have to adapt to the proposed conditions. There are guns, cartridges and first aid kits for replenishing health scattered around the territory, as well as springboards and accelerators. The rhythm of battles is high, so online play will require better skill than when completing a single-player campaign.
