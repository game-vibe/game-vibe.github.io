Game Description#

Hytale is a sandbox role-playing game with a procedurally generated world that invites gamers to explore and create. Travelers will find the Orbis global universe, consisting of many unique climatic zones with their characteristic flora and fauna. The planet’s landscape is represented not only by vast plains, but also by high mountain peaks and deep gorges. In the depths of this world hide dangerous dungeons, inhabited by hostile creatures and guarded by powerful bosses. Each adventure promises valuable rewards and reveals pieces of local history through the artifacts they discover.

In addition to exploring the environment, Hytale provides powerful tools for creativity. Enthusiasts have at their disposal not only the means for block-by-block construction of monumental structures, but also systems for creating their own objects and full-fledged scenarios. Navigating vertically oriented locations is made easier by having a glider. There are several main formats for gamers to interact with the world, access to which is available through the central hub of the Capital:

immersion in a story campaign with base building and dialogues with NPCs. free survival with an emphasis on crafting in randomly generated locations. unlimited construction with endless resources to realize your creative ideas. participation in competitive mini-games on specialized servers.
