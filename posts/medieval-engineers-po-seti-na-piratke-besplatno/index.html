Game Description#

Medieval Engineers is an open-world sandbox in which you can play for free online as a pirate with a friend, designing and building various architectural structures. The setting is represented by the medieval world. Openworld can be explored alone or online, teaming up with other players. You have to study new technologies and build fortresses that will help protect you from enemies. It is necessary to extract resources, as well as engage in mining operations in order to replenish warehouses with valuable materials.

The possibility of free online play allows you to join groups to develop technologies of the Middle Ages and be at the forefront of technological progress. The online game is aimed at interaction, but no one forbids organizing bloody fights between the participants in the adventure. For effective combat, you need to improve your characters' abilities, build combat vehicles, fortifications, etc. The main focus of Medieval Engineers is on engineering skills and construction. The environment is subject to destruction and destruction. Almost any object on the map can be interacted with.

There is an opportunity to use your imagination to build advanced siege weapons, castles and various types of transport. The lands are subject to terraforming, allowing the soil to be molded into any shape. Using a pickaxe or shovel, you can mine different types of resources. You can cut down trees, hunt animals, collect plants and much more. Construction is based on physics. If you do not follow its simplest laws, the building may collapse at any moment. The Structural Integrity View feature allows you to find weak points in structures. It helps to avoid unwanted destruction, and also shows the most vulnerable areas during attacks.
