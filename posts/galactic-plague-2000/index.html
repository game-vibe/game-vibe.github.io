Game Description#

Galactic Plague 2000 is a large-scale space war against ruthless Martian invaders who have threatened the existence of the entire galaxy. Survival in this chaos directly depends on the constant improvement of the manned starship and unlocking legendary weapons. Each encounter with enemy squadrons becomes a real test of strength, forcing you to adapt tactics and make the most efficient use of the ship’s resources to repel the alien plague. Confrontation requires courage, because the enemy knows no mercy and strives for complete destruction.

The central figure of this intergalactic conflict is a boy named Jack, who accidentally finds himself drawn into the war. Having discovered a mysterious machine gun in an abandoned hall, he was transported from the simulation to the epicenter of real combat. Now it is he who will have to carry out an important mission in the Galactic Plague 2000 project. His main task is to return nine stolen magic cubes, which are a source of enormous energy and scattered in different parts of space.

The journey for the artifacts will take the hero through nine unique worlds, each of which consists of 14 intense stages and ends with a battle with a powerful boss. Gamers can choose the visual design to suit their tastes, from an authentic retro style with 80s effects to a modern, clean look. For those looking for the ultimate challenge, there is an arcade survival mode where you can compete for the top spot on the global high score table.
