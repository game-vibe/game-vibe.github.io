Game Description#

Concealed begins with the fact that Maine Jiannan returns to the secondary school of Diinlin after the student exchange program. Her return to school life is interrupted when classmates begin to disappear without explanation. These events are correlated with old, unsolved matters and city legends circulating in the territory of the educational institution for years. Maine Jiannan has to face incidents and find a way to reveal the secrets hidden behind the facade of the school.

The plot of ConceAled develops for 7 chapters, as time in history in history. Players direct the actions of Meng Jiannan, and each decision directly changes the course of the narrative. The game contains a script with a volume of over 200,000 words and represents 20 actors. The choice made leads to different endings for the heroine, with the outcomes from salvation to complete destruction. The plot path depends on the decisions made during dialogs.

The project provides more than 10 hours of playing time, presenting a story in the format of a black and white comic book. The scenes are demonstrated using a rental throughout the gameplay. As part of the Parallel Horror series, this title expands the primary source universe. It includes a crossover with the advent of I RU from the Ghost in the Pool novel. She owns the information necessary to expose school secrets.
