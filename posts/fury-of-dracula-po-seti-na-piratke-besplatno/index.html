Game Description#

Fury of Dracula is a video game adaptation of the board game of the same name. You can play it alone or with a friend online, including pirate. Multiplayer is available not only online. Users can sit in a group at one computer. The maximum number of participants is 5 people.

“Wrath of Dracula” is based on the fourth edition of the rules of the tabletop project. For those who are not familiar with the original source, the creators have built in tutorials. The developers transferred the style of the original and added animation with music. In an indie title, there is more than just the possibility of free online play. You can create sessions with bots and go through everything alone.

Single and online play is built on the interaction of vampire hunters. Lord Arthur Godalming, John Seward, Dr. Abraham van Helsing and Mina Harker try to stop the evil. They travel through Europe, which the main antagonist seeks to conquer. Count Dracula himself is also a playable character. Therefore, users are allowed to organize asymmetrical gameplay. People can use a variety of equipment and coordinate their actions. And the legendary bloodsucker is able to become a bat, turn into fog, confuse his pursuers and use other tricks. When the opposing sides collide, a duel begins. The battles are based on a card system. Participants in battles can develop tactics and even bluff.
