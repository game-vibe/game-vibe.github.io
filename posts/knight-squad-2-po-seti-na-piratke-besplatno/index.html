Game Description#

On the pirateKnight Squad 2 you can play online for free with a friend or in a company of up to 8 people. By forming a team online, you can fight against other players as a knight with fancy weapons. The online game is aimed at confrontations between players from all over the world. The goal is to win glory and become the best warrior in the kingdom. Cross platform supported.

The gameplay is distinguished by humor and funny physics of the characters. You need to run across a three-dimensional battlefield, swinging your weapon. Knight Squad 2 characters can attack, defend, shoot an RPG, ride a unicorn and much more. The possibility of free online play offers you to fight in deathmatch mode and take part in team battles. Modes available for searching for the Holy Grail, tracking targets, hunting for souls and many others. The levels require you to perform a variety of tasks. For example, kill 1000 enemies, hold a minigun for more than two minutes, etc. Additional modes include sports competitions such as football.

The arsenal of weapons includes various medieval weapons, including swords and bows. Heroes can be given exotic boomerangs, rocket launchers, proximity mines and other types of weapons. There is real chaos on the screen: heroes slide across the floor, run across exploding floors, jump over moving platforms and much more. By defeating opponents, experience points are awarded. At locations you can pick up boosts and power-ups that will help you gain an advantage over your enemies. Between levels they must be spent on leveling up characters and purchasing various upgrades.
