Game Description#

Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising is an improved sequel to its predecessor, designed to satisfy both seasoned veterans and curious newcomers. In this updated version of the game you will not only find improved controls and an exciting story campaign, but also the possibility of free online play. Now you can enjoy online gaming with friends or random opponents online - and all this is absolutely free. Also note that the game provides the opportunity to play as a pirate without any restrictions. Thus, Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising will be your ideal choice for online battles and a great time in online duels.

Whether you’re a seasoned fighter or just starting out in the genre, Rising has your needs covered. Streamlined controls let you unleash devastating skills at the touch of a button, removing the barrier to entry so you can focus on the thrilling combat. But don’t confuse simplicity with shallowness: Rising’s combat retains its depth, offering veterans a playground for complex combos and mind games. And when you need a break from the steely battles, Rising’s Grand Bruise Legends mode offers a change of pace. This vibrant island lobby throws you into a delightful mix of party games and online challenges, from frantic obstacle courses to heart-pounding survival matches. It’s the perfect way to relax with friends or test your skills against other players in a more casual environment.

The Ascension doesn’t end there. An epic story campaign awaits you, inviting you to soar across the vast skies of Granblue Fantasy and hone your skills in an action-RPG inspired adventure. As you complete quests and fight against formidable opponents, you will not only unravel a fascinating story, but also strengthen your character, preparing for even more fierce battles. But Rising is not only about battles and competitions. It is a platform for self-expression where you can personalize your experience down to the very core. Dress up your favorite characters in vibrant colors and stylish weapon skins, choose a loyal teammate to cheer for you, and even customize your lobby avatar to reflect your unique personality.
