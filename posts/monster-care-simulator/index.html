Game Description#

Monster Care Simulator offers users to manage an establishment focused on the well-being of creatures. You will treat clients' pets, hatch eggs and raise your own companions inside this place. The goal is to transform the location into a haven through management and expansion. There is a path to go from the initial organization to increasing its scale. Development in Monster Care Simulator starts from a basic point, which gamers improve using earned resources.

Providing assistance in treatment areas generates funds to invest in upgrades, tools and extensions. This process allows us to accept more creatures over time and offer quality services. Staff can be hired to handle the cash register and maintain cleanliness, assigning tasks to keep things running smoothly. Finding specific eggs leads to the appearance of companions, which must be raised and trained in this simulator. These mentees gain strength and levels through interactions and activities under your guidance.

Activities with monsters, such as throwing a ball, playing music, or using a swing, keep them happy. Happy creatures may produce eggs more often and have fewer conflicts with other residents. Income allows you to expand the territory of the establishment and buy equipment for procedures for caring for monsters. You can decorate the space shared between clients and creatures using the available options. The top floor apartment provides a private space exclusively for your character. This area can be furnished and furnished according to preference, offering a place to relax.
