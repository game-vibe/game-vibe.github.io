Game Description#

Perimeter: Legate Edition can be played online for free, as well as online online. This online game throws you into the meat grinder of real-time strategy with a twist: the world itself can be sculpted and remade. Forget weak resource gathering, here you are literally terraforming the environment to fuel your base and create defenses against enemies. These are not just useful ditches; consider creating a path for your troops or manipulating the terrain to create barriers for enemies. You can also play Pirate with a friend.

The story unfolds over the centuries and players take on the roles of various legates leading various factions. Consider it a historical epic where you get to play key figures from different perspectives. Centuries after humanity left Earth, new factions have risen to challenge the established order. Some long for a return to Earth, others believe that humanity must conquer a new plane of existence. Perimeter’s narrative is complex and ambitious, but some material was cut from the international release. Fear not, fearless commander, a community of dedicated people has taken on the task of translating and clarifying the plot. Ready to rewrite history? Perimeter: Legate Edition is waiting for you.
