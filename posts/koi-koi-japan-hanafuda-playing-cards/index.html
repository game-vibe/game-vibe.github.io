Game Description#

Koi-Koi Japan (Hanafuda playing cards) is based on card duels, where success depends on speed and tactical thinking. The key is to beat your opponent to form winning combinations, called Yaku, by collecting cards with floral motifs. Each game is a turn-based confrontation in an authentic Japanese atmosphere. This digital title brings classic tabletop entertainment to the screen while maintaining its competitive spirit and depth.

Having collected one of these sets, a gamer in Koi-Koi Japan (Hanafuda playing cards) faces a dilemma: secure a victory or take a risk. Ad Koi-Koi allows you to continue the game in the hope of increasing the final score by creating new sets. However, this bold move could result in a loss in the round. There is a possibility that the opponent will take advantage of the given time and manage to be the first to complete his winning set, taking all the points for himself.

This game is accessible to a wide range of people due to its flexibility. For beginners unfamiliar with the rules of Hanafuda, there is a detailed tutorial mode that explains the basics of gameplay and the value of combinations. The knowledge gained can be immediately put into practice when fighting against a computer opponent. For more experienced players, there are multiplayer battles that allow you to test your skills against real opponents online.
