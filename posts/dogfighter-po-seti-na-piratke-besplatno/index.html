Game Description#

DogFighter is an aviation arcade game in which participants take control of various air transport modes. On Pirates you can play for free online with a friend or compete with 15 other players, fighting online online in 4 multiplayer modes. A network game and a single-player arcade game are presented, including 7 different modes. You can play online online or locally, competing in battles such as Deathmatch, Team DM and Capture the Flag. Free online play includes a special Wolf Pack Co-Op match, allowing you to team up with a partner.

You can use advanced server options such as Insta-Gib, Turbo Mode and Black Death. The vehicle can perform complex maneuvers, such as the Barrel Roll or the Immelmann Turn. 8 unique abilities and 20 types of weapons are available. There are offensive abilities and defensive skills. Online tables are presented that track personal achievements and the successes of other players. Battles take place over mines, ships, active volcanoes, pyramids, etc. DogFighter content and mechanics: confrontations for up to 15 players; 3 multiplayer modes and 7 solo matches; cooperative confrontation against enemies; advanced features and modifiers; huge map of the Ancient Gods; unique craft Mongoose; training level and tournament tables; 20 types weapons and about 10 different abilities.
