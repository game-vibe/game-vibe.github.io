Game Description#

Dragon Ruins presents players with a dungeon crawl with an intriguing combination of strategy and teamwork. Developed by the Graverobber Foundation, this microgame allows you to assemble a party of four heroes from eight different classes. Each class has its own set of skills that gamers must consider when forming the perfect team. The main goal is to go through dark, twisted dungeons and eventually fight the dragon that lies within.

The mechanics of Dragon Ruins are made in an auto-action format and is a turn-based combat system that places a special emphasis on strategy. Users can control their heroes during battles, using each character’s unique abilities to overcome challenges that arise throughout the adventure. Each encounter presents its own challenges, and the gamer must think critically about how to effectively use the strengths of their heroes.

This level of strategy encourages thoughtful planning and collaborative efforts, making it essential for teams to communicate and strategize to achieve success. Research is key. As users progress through dungeons, they discover various paths, secrets, and treasures that can influence their path. Each dungeon is like a puzzle waiting to be solved, prompting gamers to carefully explore the surrounding area.
