Game Description#

Junk Jack is a 2D sandbox that allows you to play as a pirate online with a friend to collect resources, upgrade tools, and build buildings together. You can compete online online with friends or players around the world. Junk Jack gives you the opportunity to tame or breed pets, cook food, travel with animals, engage in collecting and much more. Multiplayer allows you to travel with your partners across 12 unique planets. Each environment is represented by a variety of biomes with their own individual characteristics.

Portals are available that allow you to instantly travel between locations. Worlds are generated randomly. Junk Jack features over a hundred devices and equipment to collect, providing a variety of abilities and skills. The opportunity for free online play offers the opportunity to create your own world together in creative mode. The Deep Electronics system is available, allowing you to create not only various devices, but even entire systems.

Crafting is divided into two styles: grid and easy mode. The first one is more advanced, and the second one creates items with one click. The world is built using hundreds of blocks, objects and accessories. The environment is interactive, and the world is represented by a dynamic system of daylight and weather conditions. Having built your farm, you can go fishing, tame pets, herd cows, breed fish in an aquarium, etc. The selection mechanics allow you to create thousands of flower combinations, decorating your home with colorful plants and various furniture to suit every taste. It is also necessary to engage in battle with mobs. To do this, a wide arsenal of weapons is available, including firearms, signs and explosives.
