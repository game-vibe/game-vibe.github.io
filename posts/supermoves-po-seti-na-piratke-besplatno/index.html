Game Description#

Supermoves invites you to play online, immersing you in the world of parkour racing and freestyle acrobatics. In this exciting game you can compete with a friend online or challenge other players in online matches. The game offers eight unique modes, including a Career mode where you can enjoy solo runs or join multiplayer battles for free. The option to play as a pirate is also available, allowing you and your friends to compete in fast-paced races full of acrobatic stunts and adrenaline.

The game’s parkour mechanics operate in both first- and third-person perspectives, allowing players to traverse rails, swing on wires, and perform mind-bending flips with ease. Whether you’re climbing walls or jumping off trampolines, Supermoves convey the feeling of smooth, fast motion. This is the perfect combination of arcade fun and exciting parkour simulator that will not let you get bored.

When it comes to multiplayer, Supermoves really kicks things up a notch. You can take on your friends in thrilling challenges like Rising Tide, where you’ll have to outrun a rising ball pit, or test your reflexes in the heart-pounding Bomb Tag. The game also allows you to combine different modes into custom tournaments, giving you endless ways to determine the best freerunner among your friends.

For those who like to flex their creative muscles, Supermoves offers a powerful level editor. The Makea Editor lets you create levels ranging from twisty race tracks to mind-blowing obstacle courses. You can even team up with friends and create levels together in real time, then share your creations with the world. This feature adds virtually endless replayability to an already action-packed game.
