Game Description#

Evil Tonight is an action-adventure game with role-playing game elements. Take control of a brave heroine who is not afraid to look Evil in the eye. As an experienced exorcist, the girl Sylvia encounters numerous horrors of ancient estates and crypts. She has the unusual ability to materialize evil and fight it in open battle. The visual side is represented by pixel style.

Face ghosts and other evil spirits as you unravel the mystery of a huge mansion. Explore rooms and solve exciting puzzles. The gameplay is inspired by classic horror games of the last century. The gameplay of Evil Tonight is quite hardcore. The heroine does not have a map or advice with her. You need to explore the premises yourself and remember the location of quest items.

Discover hidden passages, solve estate mysteries and save ammo. The enemies are strong and will require significant resources to kill. Sometimes it is better to run away than to engage in open battle. The action takes place in a long-abandoned Drama School. Sylvia decides to rid this place of an ancient curse. But to do this, she will have to face powerful bosses guarding the walls of a once cheerful place. Tonight evil will be defeated!
