Game Description#

ILA A Frosty Glide is the journey of a young witch named Ila, unfolding in the vastness of a mysterious snow-covered island. The search for the missing kitten Coco, the last reminder of her mother, forces the heroine to ride her skatebroom - an unusual means of transportation that combines the properties of a broom and a skateboard. Moving around the world with the help of this magical item, the girl explores snow-capped mountains and enchanted forests, discovering hidden corners and solving puzzles.

Mastering the skatebroom becomes the key to overcoming obstacles in ILA: A Frosty Glide. The gameplay is based on the gradual improvement of magical flight abilities. To do this, you need to collect special charges of stardust, allowing you to soar higher and longer. One of the unique techniques is the ability to thrust the skatebroom into ice walls, then push off and soar to new heights. The environment itself helps with movement: on the island you can find jumping mushrooms that throw the heroine to previously inaccessible ledges and secrets.

As you explore the world, new possibilities for personalization open up. Items found in chests or purchased from the store allow you to customize Eela’s appearance, giving her unique outfits such as the Ash Explorer’s Set or Winter’s Cold Robes. The collected coins are spent not only on clothes, but also on useful improvements, such as a magic shield. Each discovery enriches the journey, revealing new details of the story and allowing you to dive deeper into the atmosphere of this adventure with its voxel graphics and cozy mood.
