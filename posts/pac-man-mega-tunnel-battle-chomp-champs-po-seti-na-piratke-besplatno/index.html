Game Description#

PAC-MAN Mega Tunnel Battle: Chomp Champs brings the classic Pac-Man experience to a new 64-player Battle Royale multiplayer format. Play on the Internet online with a friend or for free on a pirate site. Forget the usual spots and predictable ghosts - now this is a real battle for PAC supremacy. Compete in interconnected mazes, devouring everything in your path, and then build tunnels to invade other Peckman’s territory, steal their points and win.

Want a random fight? Enter Assassination mode for an arcade-style battle with players of all skill levels. Earn tokens here to exchange them for cool cosmetic items and strike your opponent. Feeling the competition? In Ranked Mode, you’ll take on similarly ranked players in a battle for glory on the leaderboard. Complete certain missions during matches to get bonus points and dominate the stage.

Remember, it’s not just about ghosts anymore. Click on the dots and fruits to get a boost of energy, then give yourself a free hand! Destroy those pesky ghosts and, more importantly, other characters to eliminate the competition. Powerful items add another layer of strategy. Shields, increased speed - this arsenal will allow you to bypass both ghosts and rivals. But be careful, some Power Items even benefit ghosts! Don’t freeze, because as the match progresses, entire mazes begin to disappear. Hurry up to swallow everything and escape before you are destroyed.

Don’t forget about style! Decorate your character with a variety of body, head and face customization options. Stand out from the crowd and show off your unique kolobok personality. Team up with friends, follow their progress, watch their matches, or even invite them to your game! Don’t feel like fighting? Watch the matches unfold and watch the PAC-MAN chaos unfold. In Assassination mode, viewers can even vote on items of power that will be unleashed on players, adding to the chaotic nature of the competition.
