Game Description#

Vaulting Over It is a challenging 2D platformer where the main goal is to conquer heights using a single tool. The protagonist has at his disposal a branch that serves both as a support for jumping and as a source of constant problems. Character control is completely subject to the laws of physics, which turns every movement into an unpredictable test of precision and calculation. The entire ascension process takes place within a single vertical location, absolutely devoid of any checkpoints to save progress. Any mistake made leads to irreversible consequences, instantly throwing the gamer back to the very beginning of the journey.

Climbing mechanics and passage conditions:

Using a branch to jump and push off surfaces. Control subordinated to a realistic physical model. A single vertical location without division into levels. Complete absence of checkpoints to save progress. A fall resulting in a reset to the starting position. Moving obstacles complicating the ascent route. Supports single and co-op modes.

The process of passing is built on a cycle of constant falls, where full responsibility for each failure lies entirely with the person himself. Only through repeated attempts and detailed analysis of one’s own mistakes can one find a way to move forward. You can overcome all the challenges in Vaulting Over It either alone or in cooperative mode, sharing the hardships of the climb with a friend. The reward for perseverance is a mysterious transformation that awaits the most persistent at the peak of this vertical world.
