Game Description#

Terraria is a simulator of construction, life, survival, an interesting storyline and everything that could be combined into a game with the Sand Box style. Simple 8-bit graphics take us into a randomly generated world generated by the game itself. The open map is almost endless if you dig deep into the world, but you will be stopped by armies of monsters or underground knights. Terraria is not as simple as it seems.

We start with a simple traveler who is looking for clues and wood to build his first home. Before starting the game, we create a character, completely changing his appearance. This is not so important for the future player, because after 2 hours you will put on armor, after 3 you will destroy underground monsters with the help of magic, which is learned in a special book, which also needs to be found or created. The game can be played by several people, so at the depths of the dug mine you can find money, jewelry and rare resources. You can create something from everything in the game, and the workbench will help you with this, which has a huge number of recipes for various items.

Go deep into the ground, into the very depths of your location, to find valuable items; build your hero with the best weapons to find and not lose with opponents who are not at all like the first zombies at the very top - these monsters will cast spells, summon a mass of black spiders, revive enemies, making them skeletons in armor. Gradually, while you are building a house, different people will come to you. They can come and ask for shelter, or they can sell you valuable items for gold that you find in the depths of the earth! Don’t miss the opportunity to explore ancient caves in Terraria - a platformer, sandbox and simulator of everything that is accessible to the common man.
