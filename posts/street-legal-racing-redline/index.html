Game Description#

Street Legal Racing: Redline is a car mechanic simulator that allows you to assemble a car completely from scratch. At the same time, street racing with assembled vehicles is available. New parts can affect the characteristics of the car, which are responsible for acceleration, handling and maximum speed. Street Legal Racing does not provide package upgrades. The rider must install each individual part himself.

The game has many features:

25 different cars, presented by models, prices and descriptions; 17 developed routes; 7 game modes, including drifting, circuit racing and drag racing; DTM racing versions of Nonus, Baiern and Einvagen; more than 100 types of wheels, tires, sports body kits and spoilers; over 50 random events; legendary tracks including Laguna Seca, Oulton Park and Nurburgring.

Despite the fact that there are no licensed vehicles in the game, the models are represented by prototypes of well-known concerns such as Honda, Mitsubishi, BMW, Nissan and others. DTM modifications complement the variety of cars, providing several modification options, consisting of increased engine size, power, etc.

The main character of the story is aspiring racer Vince Polanski. He starts with a small capital of 25,000. With this money you can buy a used car and minimally customize it. With the help of the first car, he can take part in illegal competitions. The streets are controlled by three factions: green, blue and red. Taking part in the first races, he gradually rises in the standings, earning money, buying new parts for the car and gaining a reputation as one of the best racers in the city.
