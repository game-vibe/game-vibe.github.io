Game Description#

Shadows of Forbidden Gods invites the player to become a Dark Elder God and lead skilled agents. The task of the subjects is to cause an apocalypse in a fantasy world. You have to lead your own minions and help them with your own powerful abilities. Working in the shadows will require you to avoid heroes trying to stop the oncoming threat.

The gameplay is presented in the form of a strategy, and the user receives the only task - to arrange an apocalypse on a mysterious planet. To achieve the goal, a wide range of possibilities, conspiracies and rituals are presented. The passage begins with the main character bribing guards, infiltrating small farming organizations and gradually increasing his strength. Having earned the required amount of resources, you will have access to the creation of a plague that will begin the Ice Age.

Diseases and other disasters caused by the user will destroy entire nations, cause volcanoes and send snake gods to the cities. Agents are opposed to good. The peaceful race has unique heroes and Chosen Ones who can stop the dark times. The only advantage a gamer has is secrecy and the ability to act unexpectedly. There are dozens of options for winning. The player can start a civil war, find new allies, purposefully fight the empire, or split it into hostile pieces.
