Game Description#

Dreadhunter is a real hellish mixture of shooter and RPG. In it you will have to fight against swarms of dangerous monsters, as well as fight brutal bosses in order to achieve high rewards. You will have to get into an advanced mechanical suit and use all your skills to become a brave space monster hunter. Slay hordes of screaming monsters and defeat sinister horrors to discover that the universe’s greatest fears have no claws or rocket launchers…

An incredible battle awaits you, where the change of weapons between long-range and melee occurs quickly and unpredictably. Don’t stop there, as the monsters are fast and deadly. You’ll hunt bosses for rewards in each level and fight a powerful final boss at the end of each storyline. Each environment in the campaign is uniquely generated each time you enter it, and you can adjust the difficulty level to suit your skill level. Collect loot that changes your game. Each item you find has a unique and meaningful upgrade to your mech, weapon, or abilities. Find interesting item combinations that suit your play style. Feel the destructive power of a weapon that already feels like a special ability. Upgrade it by equipping armor and add abilities with their own cool variants and ability trees to become invincible!
