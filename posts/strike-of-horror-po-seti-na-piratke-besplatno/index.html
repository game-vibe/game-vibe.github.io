Game Description#

Strike of Horror is a multiplayer horror game that allows you to play online for free on a pirate, connect with a friend, or take part in a confrontation with up to 10 people online online. Players are required to fight in a confrontation between people and ghosts. Each participant takes on the role of one of the parties. While controlling a person, you need to collect 3 sleeping potions. The disembodied spirit must capture people, preventing them from falling asleep. A network game contains a competitive element, so co-op involves the interaction of participants only within a team.

The sides in Strike of Horror swap targets and protagonists every round. Playing as humans, you will need to move in the shadows and try to avoid being seen by your opponents. The latter need to scare the living, catching up with them and capturing them in their cold embrace. People move twice as fast, but monsters have more abilities. The free multiplayer option offers you to take control of 5 female ghosts and 5 human males.

As soon as a dead woman touches a living man, the victory is credited to the spirit faction. An asymmetrical mode is available, allowing you to select an uneven number of characters. For example, a match can involve 5 ghosts and 2 humans, or vice versa. The gameplay is inspired by modern horror games such as Slenderman and other similar projects.
