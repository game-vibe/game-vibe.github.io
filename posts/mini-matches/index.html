Game Description#

Mini Matches will feature many characters, as well as unusual and somewhat crazy locations that will definitely surprise the gamer. This is a bacchanalian game where you can have fun. But don’t relax, because danger can come from the most unexpected places. You need to competently and correctly assess your own capabilities. And also give a tough rebuff to your opponents, taking dominance in this world.

Mini Matches offers multiplayer co-op mode. This allows you to invite fellow gamers or just go through levels with random people from all over the world. In total, up to 4 players can play simultaneously. But there is also a single-player option if you want to spend time alone. In this case, artificial intelligence will be used.

In the game you will need to move, control strange protagonists and perform various actions, complete mini-games, play golf. There is a simple control system and mechanics for this. There will still be many territories and locations, and all of them are designed in a minimalist style. You can upgrade your character, and also completely personalize it by adding bright designs and patterns.
