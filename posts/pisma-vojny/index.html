Game Description#

In the center of the plot of Letters of War is the fate of the Briton Liam, who volunteered for the front during the Second World War. The main goal is to preserve the connection with the little daughter of Rosie through correspondence, overcoming the distance between the battlefields and a quiet rear. Gamers have to go the path of a fighter of the 8th British Army, whose activities are covered in a project with historical accuracy. The story is based on genuine family memories, giving what is personal and dramatic.

The story in Letters of War is divided into two parallel lines that reveal events from different angles. By managing Liam, the player will be at the epicenter of iconic operations on the Western Front-from Monte Cassino mountain heights to landing in Normandy. At the same time, episodes for Rosie are transferred to England, where the girl is waiting for news from her father. Her world consists of everyday worries, maternal custody and letters that have become a single thread connecting her with a distant parent.

The gameplay is built on the mechanics of a two-dimensional site-scroll, where the study of locations is closely intertwined with a solution to simple puzzles. Interaction with the environment occurs in Point-And-Click format, allowing you to find key items and advance by the plot. Thus, the title proposes to consistently reveal the integral picture of events, observing them from two different prospects. This is a strictly directed adventure leading through a personal drama against the background of a global disaster.
