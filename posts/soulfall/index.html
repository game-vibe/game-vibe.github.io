Game Description#

Soulfall is a gloomy fantasy adventure that immerses the hero in an ongoing battle for survival against the inconspicuous hordes of evil spirits. The space around the protagonist is constantly filled with demons, skeletons and other monsters, forcing him to constantly move and use the entire arsenal of abilities to reflect deadly waves. Each sortie into these damned lands becomes a unique test where the procedural generation of the world guarantees the absence of two identical passages. At the end of each stage of this desperate resistance of the hero, powerful bosses are waiting for special combat mechanics, the victory over which requires tactical thinking and a quick reaction.

The character whose appearance from face traits to hair color is tuned in by the gamer himself is the central element of the narrative. Its development is determined by the choice of one of the three classes, each of which has three unique specializations with its own talents and ultimatic skills. As the Soulfall storyline moves, access to a huge amount of equipment, divided into five levels of rarity, opens. Gathering enchanted weapons, armor and valuable resources, the hero becomes stronger, preparing for decisive fights. This process of collecting trophies and strengthening is an integral part of the trip to hellish depths.

The story campaign is divided into four main acts, each of which is a twenty -minute test for survival in unique biomes. Moving through these lands, the protagonist performs tasks, driven by a thirst for revenge and atonement. The decisions taken during the narrative have key consequences that directly affect the outcome of the final battle, in which the main task is solved - the return of the lost soul from the very heart of the underworld.
