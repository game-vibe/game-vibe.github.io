Game Description#

MegaFactory Titan allows players to try their hand at designing their own robotic factory, located on Saturn’s moon Titan. Users will create production chains, work out logistics and influence the economy and diplomatic relations with various factions (terrorists, rebels, colonists, pirates, etc.). The game is a factory management simulator where you can pump up and improve a colonial settlement in the vastness of space.

When setting up a base in MegaFactory Titan, users can expect:

Development of technologically advanced conveyor belts, construction of smart Maglev pipes, as well as sorting and processing of loops during production. Creation of goods, which will include various types of military equipment: tanks, jets, space combat ships, etc. Management of transport types of equipment that transport resources, goods and other elements necessary for the development of the factory. Procedural generation of the universe, which randomly forms mines, landscapes on the planet, etc. The ability to modify and terraform the environment, leveling mountains or filling up seas. Build relationships with states by selling nuclear weapons, gun turrets or aircraft. Arrangement of protection around the perimeter of the plant to repel enemy attacks.
