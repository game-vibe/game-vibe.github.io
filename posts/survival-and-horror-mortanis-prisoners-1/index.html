Game Description#

Survival and Horror: Mortanis Prisoners #1 occurs in the Nazi concentration camp, where the main characters are prisoners who are going to execute for participation in the conspiracy of the rebels. However, they soon find out that death is not the worst thing that can happen to them, because they will find an even more terrible fate. They must find a way to escape from the nightmare that awaits them in the camp.

The game contains an atmosphere of horror that will keep you in suspense while you explore the gloomy and terrible atmosphere of the camp. You will encounter various enemies, from Nazi soldiers to perverted experimenters who will try to stop or kill you. To fight back from them, you will have to use realistic shooting mechanics, as well as carefully control the inventory and ammunition. In addition, you will have to solve puzzles and find tips to advance around the game and reveal the secrets of the camp. Mortanis Prisoners will give you a call and scare you, because you will try to survive and get out of the hellish place. Mortanis Prisoners is part of the Survival & Horror Games - a collection of games that have one genre and mechanics, but are distinguished by a plot, atmosphere, enemies, puzzles and weapons.
