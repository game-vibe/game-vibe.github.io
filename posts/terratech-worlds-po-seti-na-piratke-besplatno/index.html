Game Description#

TerraTech Worlds offers a unique free online gaming experience. You can play online and explore a huge alien world with a friend for free. Discover a network game on a 300 km² planet, where you will find many biomes and unique weather conditions online. On the pirate version or in the official version, an adventure awaits you, where you can team up with five friends to jointly conquer this world.

The game features an intuitive modular construction system that allows you to create unique vehicles, bases and factories. Collect resources, mine them, or free them from others. Craft, design and upgrade your creations to your liking. Harsh wild lands with various challenges await you.

Build planetary infrastructure by extracting resources, processing them, and creating advanced materials. Automate production lines to optimize operations. Specialize your equipment in manual resource extraction or switch to automated systems. Rearrange the landscape as you see fit with free-form tools.

As you develop, encounter enemy technologies and defeat them. Collect and research new blocks to increase your combat power. Experiment with specialized combat designs and team up with other prospectors to raid enemy bases and retreat overseas.

Working together increases your chances of survival in this unforgiving alien world. Determine your role in a team of up to six prospectors. Create a unique build that suits your playstyle and combine block abilities for maximum effect. Fight with your team to defeat powerful enemies and build complex automated bases together. Can you survive and thrive on this alien frontier? It’s up to you.
