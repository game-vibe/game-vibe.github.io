Game Description#

InTools Up! Garden Party, teaming up with a friend or playing alone, requires you to show off your talent as a repairman. You can play pirate for free online using an online connection or participate in local competitions on one screen. Garden Party - Tree House includes free online play for up to four people. It is required to assemble a team of repairmen to move sofas, carry equipment, build walls and much more. It is important to complete the task on time and adhere to the director’s instructions.

Gameplay Tools Up! Garden Party - Episode 1: The Tree House introduces new mechanics. For example, by collecting leaves or completing repairs within the allotted time, additional levels and skins for characters are unlocked. Working as a team, you will need to paint walls, tear off or glue wallpaper, move furniture and engage in full-fledged interior design. There is an opportunity to both join forces and compete for the title of best employee of the month. Basic mechanics: single playthrough or online play; gathering a team of up to 4 people; many tasks as a loader, developer, designer, etc.; 15 levels of an exciting campaign; a huge number of requirements from the boss; funny ragdoll and physics; total fun and continuous humor in every frame.
