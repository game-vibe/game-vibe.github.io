Game Description#

StarRupture places players on a planet constantly changed by recurring disasters. The open world contains resources and unexplored locations throughout its territory. Survival depends on navigating this environment, which is reborn after each destructive event. You can explore it alone or as part of a team, facing the challenges of a hostile environment. The terrain serves as the basis for base construction and resource gathering, requiring its inhabitants to constantly adapt.

StarRupture’s central goal is to create a complex industrial system. You have to search, extract and process materials scattered throughout the landscape. These components directly support production lines and enhance the outpost’s operational capabilities. Subsequently, industrial growth opens up access to research into new technologies. These specific discoveries are tools for survival in the harsh conditions of the planet, including extreme temperatures and other immediate dangers that threaten any operation.

The world has prepared not only climatic difficulties for any expedition. Gamers will have to organize the defense of their base and themselves from hordes of local alien creatures attacking their position. You can resist threats alone, engaging in battle with the elements and its inhabitants. Alternatively, a group of up to 4 people can cooperate, building, exploring and defending their common foothold from any opponents.
