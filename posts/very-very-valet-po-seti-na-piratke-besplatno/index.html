Game Description#

On the pirate shipVery Very Valet you can team up with up to 8 people to form an elite team of parking attendants. You can play online for free with a friend or with players from all over the world, overcoming a variety of challenges related to your driving skills. Via the Internet, online or locally, you will need to do the incredible - save the world, which is on the verge of transport collapse. Pathetic people always leave their cars anywhere, and the main characters of Very Very Valet will need to park their cars in the designated areas. This must be done as quickly as possible and with minimal damage. Online play allows you to both compete and play on the same team.

There are a variety of tests available aimed at testing your vehicle control skills and many mini-games of varying degrees of difficulty. The possibility of free online play allows you to fight with two teams of 4 people. You can personalize the cars. For example, attach a super-powerful bumper that allows you to push your opponent off the map. There are several competitive modes, including Dodgeball and Rally.

There are a variety of hidden mechanics that can be discovered directly in the midst of dynamic confrontations. There are 20 unique maps to choose from with their own terrain and biomes. You can compete on city streets, on cliffs, in a train depot, at an airport, and even on a bowling alley. There is a standings that tracks the results of each player and global statistics of in-game achievements are kept.
