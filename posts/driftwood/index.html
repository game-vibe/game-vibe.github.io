Game Description#

Driftwood is a fascinating journey into the world of high -speed descent on Longboard. The main character is Eddie, a cool sliven who loves to ride in beautiful landscapes. This game provides an ideal balance of speed and control, bringing a feeling of relaxation and calm, despite a high -speed action.

The project offers minimalistic experience, focusing on skating on magnificent landscapes, and not on building a story campaign. This is a game in which rest prevails over the competition, inviting you to enjoy skiing, and not to focus on the results. Nevertheless, the game is not a walk in the park, and speed management may be a rather difficult task. If you can’t cope with management, you will quickly find yourself in a cuvette. But do not worry, because the game allows you to revive near the crash site. The test in Driftwood depends not only on speed. The number and complexity of the tricks that you perform, the proximity of collisions with cars and even the participation of leaves in your tricks - all this affects the overall result. Drifting is the most important movement that can serve as an excellent start for a combined trick.

Driftwood skating seems to be free, especially if you decide to turn on your own music. However, the soundtrack is also very pleasant. The mechanics are simple and emphasizes the importance of tilt forward to increase speed and raise the hands for slowing down. Powerslaiding and the implementation of some tricks are left at the discretion of gamers. Although sliding mechanics requires dexterity, it is very important to understand how it works. Perhaps Driftwood does not offer many poses, but the general gameplay is impressive. Despite a few small shortcomings, such as a slightly uncomfortable camera, Driftwood is a refreshing and pleasant look at games about skating skating. This game is based on the pleasure of skating and resting on the background of beautiful landscapes, which makes Driftwood a unique addition to the genre.
