Game Description#

Majesty 2 may please those who like to fight online or locally over the Internet. You can play Pirate with a friend online. Multiplayer is designed for a maximum of 4 users. This is a strategy with indirect controls.

The project has an alternative name – Majesty 2: The Fantasy Kingdom Sim. It is impossible to highlight units with a frame and give them clear orders. The user can only give general instructions to his wards. This is done with the help of motivating flags. For example: a banner with a monetary reward is placed on a location or on a monster. If the fighters are interested in the amount, they will go to explore the territory or attack the monster. But they can also ignore the request.

The title does not just contain the possibility of free online play. The product was originally designed for multiplayer battles. The construction of buildings occurs as in classic RTS games. The user selects the type of building and terrain. After this, workers come to the site and begin to build the house. Money is obtained from the market and trading posts. As in the first part, units are represented by heroes from several guilds. There are warriors, magicians, rangers, thieves and so on. Each class has its own preferences. The novice likes to defend the castle, while the archer prefers to wander around the local open spaces. These adventurers buy armor and put on artifacts, drink bottles of potions, and level up.

Not only online play is available here. There is also a campaign. The plot is dedicated to saving Ardania from demons. Ruler Leonard was very bored because all evil had been defeated. Then he ordered his subjects to get him evil opponents. They brought him a monster from hell, but this creature eventually defeated the king and seized power. The main character will have to correct the situation.
