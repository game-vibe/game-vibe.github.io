Game Description#

KeeperRL is a simulator with roguelike elements in which you have to build an underground base and develop a society. You play as a powerful wizard learning black magic. Train minions in military affairs, forcing them to attack civilians in settlements, taking their belongings and burning houses to the ground.

Build dungeons, set traps and build up your military power to attack the pitiful people. Be prepared for human heroes to decide to attack back. Prepare your catacombs for the rainbow meeting in advance by setting up obstacles and deadly traps. As you control your henchmen, the game becomes a classic roguelike with turn-based combat. You can also take the side of the heroes by first creating a character or downloading a ready-made one from the Internet, and then go to storm the goblin dungeons.

Build dozens of corridors and rooms, equipping them with traps. Train minions to produce weapons and armor. Having captured prisoners, you can torture them. Develop new technologies, alchemy, witchcraft and turn animals into deadly monsters. Allows you to collect dozens of special items around the world, learn many spells, special attacks, etc.

The game is quite cruel. Here you can cut off heads and limbs, blind or poison enemies. Fire is the most dangerous element in the game. With its help, you can not only burn villages, but also inadvertently burn down your base. Locations in KeeperRL are represented by castles, villages, dungeons and special places. Kill heroes, dragons and witches to get a huge amount of coins. Everything that is done in the game is for the sake of money!
