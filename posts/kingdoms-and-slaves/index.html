Game Description#

Set in a dark tale of power, addiction and ruin, Kingdoms And Slaves offers a journey into the ruins of a fallen civilization. The combat system is built on constant pressure and an aggressive fighting style, rewarding quick reactions. The player needs to exploit enemy vulnerabilities that arise after their unsuccessful attacks. The creation of effective combinations is achieved by interweaving strikes with bladed weapons with magic spells and special skills. A key role in survival is played by competent positioning and timely evasion of enemy attacks, turning every collision into a dangerous dance on the verge of defeat.

The protagonist is created from scratch, which allows you to create a unique style of passage. The development process in Kingdoms And Slaves is closely related to the exploration of the destroyed world. Exploring optional locations and hidden areas is rewarded with valuable upgrades for the character and his equipment. One of the central mechanics is the rescue of representatives of the Greenin race, enslaved by the fallen king. Each rescued ally returns to base, providing long-term benefits and opening access to the creation of new weapons or strengthening of existing abilities.

Events take place in the ruins of the once great Kingdom of Fire, whose prosperity was based on living crystals. These shining stones turned out to be a curse that plunged the inhabitants into madness and caused the catastrophic collapse of civilization. You can go through this dark path either alone or in cooperative mode to overcome difficulties together. For those who appreciate the competitive aspect, there are battles in the player versus player format, where the outcome depends solely on skill and tactical calculation.
