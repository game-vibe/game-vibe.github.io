Game Description#

Classic Racers Elite takes place during the golden era of racing - the 1960s in America. Back then, racers were truly considered pilots with cool leather jackets, virtuoso helmets and goggles. It was a wonderful era of rock stars, rock and roll and iconic cars that still delight the hearts of millions. This period is commonly called the time of “gentleman drivers” who drove sports cars with graceful curved lines.

In Classic Racers Elite, the entire fleet is divided into 5 categories - from vehicles that became the ancestors of the famous F1 to micro-cars. Each such group contains about 10 cars, each of which has a unique appearance and a set of tactical and technical characteristics. The controls in the race are simplified as much as possible and are closer to an arcade than to a simulator. Pilots just need to press the gas, brake, periodically slow down and take turns. No need to worry about traction and gear shifting.

As part of the passage, the user gets the opportunity to demonstrate his own strengths in 12 championships. The action takes place both on dirt roads and on professional tracks. At the same time, the type of road surface does not affect the gameplay at all. In addition to single-player championships, users can participate in international competitions, the results of which are displayed in constantly updated online rankings.
