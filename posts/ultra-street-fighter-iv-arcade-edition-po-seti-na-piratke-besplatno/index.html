Game Description#

Ultra Street Fighter IV is a 3D fighting game that can be played as a pirate with a friend online or alone with bots. Both regular matches and rating battles are available online on the Internet. The system tries to select living opponents with approximately the same skill. The Arcade Edition is posted here. It differs from the usual one in the changed balance, the introduction of new movements and settings for old heroes.

The re-release also boasts the addition of additional characters: the Lee brothers (Yun and Yang), Oni and Evil Ryu. There are about 25 fighters in Ultra Street Fighter IV. Among them, 6 turned out to be new: El Fuerte, Goken, Abel, Seth, Crimson Viper, Rufus. Heroes and heroines have different costumes. Some skins noticeably change their appearance. You can control the actions of the selected martial artist using the keyboard or gamepad. The possibility of free online play allows you to arrange fights with your comrades on the same computer.

In the fourth part, the developers removed parrying, but transferred improved versions of special moves to the sequel. They also added ultra combos and focus attacks. The control became six-button again and was improved. And the online game now has built-in voice chat. In addition to the health bar, the screen now displays 2 scales: super combo and revenge. They are needed to perform various powerful types of attacks. In terms of plot, this is a prequel to Troika. The action takes place between the sequel and the threequel. S.I.N Corporation organizes a tournament in which the strongest warriors and warriors take part. The organization is trying to use them to create BLECE - weapons of mass destruction.
