Game Description#

Carnival Massacre is the story of a desperate journey to save a missing little sister. An endless series of nightmares associated with her disappearance pushes the main character into the epicenter of events. His path runs through the territory of a cursed amusement park, where reality is distorted under the onslaught of unimaginable horrors. Every corner of this ominous location is fraught with deadly threats and intellectual obstacles that require a non-standard approach to overcome them. The search for answers will force you to face the creations of a sick imagination hiding behind the bright attractions.

Carnival survival mechanics:

Detailed exploration of locations to find useful items. Rational management of limited inventory and resources. Solving puzzles integrated into the attraction environment. Hunt down four key opponents to eliminate them. Confront hostile creatures lurking in the dark. Avoiding traps and deadly zones on the territory.

Survival will require a scrupulous examination of the area and rational use of the limited resources found in the inventory. Passing Carnival Massacre requires constant concentration from the gamer, because any mistake can lead to fatal consequences. The main task becomes the methodical elimination of four key figures who control this carnival of madness. The project makes full use of classic elements of the horror genre, placing the protagonist in conditions where every step could be the last, and the atmosphere of hopelessness is felt almost physically.
