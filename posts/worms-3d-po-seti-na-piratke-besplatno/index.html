Game Description#

On the pirateWorms 3D you can compete with a friend locally or play online for free, fighting with a team of fighting worms. Free online play, single player campaign, quick match, competitions and training are available. The online game allows you to take control of worms armed with large-caliber guns. The arsenal includes firearms, bombs and numerous auxiliary items.

You can use jetpacks, freezing, parachute, teleport, pigeons, sheep, grandmothers, cows and many other abilities to destroy the player. In Worms 3D, you can customize your heroes, giving each character individual weapons or equipment. You can also set voice acting and assign names to your units. Gameplay Worms 3D is a dynamic arcade game with an active tactical pause.

You must complete a move within a certain time. Locations are fully interactive and prone to destruction. You can blow up landscapes, thereby gaining a strategic advantage over your opponent. As soon as the worm’s vitality reaches zero, the hero dies. There are three camera modes to choose from: first-person, third-person, and top view. Weather conditions affect gameplay. It may rain and snow. Day or night affects visibility. Worms have a variety of death animations and a variety of facial expressions when injured. Multiplayer allows up to 16 players to compete on one map. You can divide into teams or take part in a classic death match.
