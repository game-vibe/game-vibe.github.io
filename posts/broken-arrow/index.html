Game Description#

Broken Arrow is a large-scale military strategy in which the player will have to take into account many details, including logistics, the presence or absence of intelligence, terrain features, etc. The game offers an environment as close to reality as possible, forcing the user to constantly adapt to the unexpected and form new squads to get a chance to win. Artificial intelligence is thoughtful and can also formulate its own plans and tactics, disrupting the original course of events.

The action takes place in the period of modernity and the clash between the armies of the United States and Russia. You can play for either side, but the weapons and equipment will be different, since the military branches of these states are very different from each other, trying to stand out. The locations can be very different: from the spacious steppe to city streets. The entire environment is destructible, so the consequences of the battle are visible to the naked eye. Categories of military units of Broken Arrov:

Reconnaissance - includes infantry, as well as unmanned vehicles that can determine the location of the enemy from a height and at the same time remain undetected due to their size Military equipment - tanks, missile and anti-aircraft guns Helicopters - provide air cover, most often they can only use various machine guns, it is better not to use them in fire areas Aviation - bombers and paratrooper drops Individual - customized by users at will and can include several types of troops at once

The most effective way to defeat an enemy is to disrupt his logistics. Since if the troops have no one to give orders and provide them with the necessary resources, then their strength will quickly dry up. But this territory is the most heavily protected, so breaking through to it will be difficult and requires not only tactics, but also cunning on the part of the commander.
