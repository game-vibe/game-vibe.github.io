Game Description#

Blacksmith Master is an exciting simulator in which you can control your own blacksmiths, starting with mining and precious stones to the design and sale of finished products. Your task will be tortured everything - from weapons and armor to instruments and kitchen utensils - to earn on your craft and become a real master of blacksmithing.

Here you can lead your own medieval blacksmith and you will control the entire process: the extraction of resources, their processing and production and sale of finished products. Take the best workers at each stage of production and supply them with the necessary tools to optimize the business. Develop a store design for the best productivity and look for new opportunities in the market, as customers will look for a variety of goods inspired by historical objects - from weapons and armor to instruments and kitchen utensils. You will improve your skill in order to become a real master of blacksmithing.

Your blacksmith is in narrow alleys of a small medieval city - initially it is a modest place for a modest blacksmith, but you have potential for greatness. Here you control the entire production chain - from the extraction of ores to melting them into ingots, and then the creation of weapons, armor, tools and jewelry to put them up for sale. You will develop the design of your store to maximize the space and ensure the production and sale of goods, as well as add personal strokes to revive a boring place.
