Game Description#

DIRT 5 is a racing simulator with the ability to play online online with a friend, competing for free on a pirate in various competitions both locally and online. Single player campaign and online play available. You can compete in disciplines such as rallycross, ice racing, super truck competitions, buggy championships and off-road racing. The possibility of free online play allows you to participate in one team or compete for yourself.

Races take place in countries such as the USA, Brazil, China, Italy and Norway. DIRT 5 introduces a dynamic system for changing weather conditions. Precipitation affects maneuverability and vehicle control. The seasons also change, so ice competitions are only available during the winter and are held exclusively in New York. Local multiplayer allows up to four players to compete in splitscreen mode.

Career mode has a full story. The main rival in Dirt 5 is a racer, voiced by Nolan North. Troy Baker gave the voice to the mentor. You will have to listen to his advice and comments throughout the entire passage. More than 70 unique tracks in 10 different biomes are available for racing. The fleet consists of 900 cars, including classic rally cars, GT cars, SUVs and legendary muscle cars. Multiplayer races allow up to 12 players to participate simultaneously.
