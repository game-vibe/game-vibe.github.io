Game Description#

A Way Out is an action-adventure game designed to be played with a friend using an Internet connection online. On the pirate site you can play together online for free. Solo mode is not provided. The gameplay is a third-person action-adventure game with constant splitscreen. The two players constantly interact with each other and work together to escape from prison. Only online play is available, without the ability to complete the campaign alone.

It stars Leo and Vincent as two convicted men trying to devise an escape plan. Since the characters are constantly on the same screen, all player actions are synchronized. No one stands aside and always performs a certain function. The possibility of free online play allows you to come to the rescue, perform certain tasks together, overcome obstacles and much more. Often one must distract the guard while the other sneaks into the service area. Or the first beret gets into a fight, and the second one tries to do everything possible to help both comrades get out of the conflict.

The gameplay of A Way Out is plot-oriented, so the adventure strictly follows the script, acting as a corridor action game with a minimum of branches. However, some situations can be resolved in several ways. It is noteworthy that the roles in a given situation are not fixed and the players themselves decide who will perform certain actions. Also available are dialogue options and the ability to replay this or that episode in a slightly different way in a replay.
