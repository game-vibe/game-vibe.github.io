Game Description#

STAR WARS: Battlefront Classic Collection brings back the beloved online shooters, remastered for modern consoles and PC. Players can now relive classic battles from the Star Wars galaxy, both online and offline, in this new collection. The collection includes the original game, as well as a bonus map: Jabba’s Palace. The second game, Battlefront 2, includes four bonus cards and two bonus heroes: Asajj Ventress and Kit Fisto.

In addition to multiplayer battles, the collection offers extensive single-player campaigns. In Star Wars Battlefront, players will relive iconic moments from Episodes I-VI, and in Battlefront 2 they will join Darth Vader’s elite 501st Legion. Online multiplayer is where the collection really shines, supporting up to 64 players across huge locations. Gamers can fight on the ground as iconic characters like Wookiee Warriors and Jet Troopers, pilot iconic starships like TIE Fighters and X-wings, or control vehicles like Speeder Bikes and AT-STs.

The collection also expands the “Assault of Heroes” mode: for the first time it is available on all land maps. You can fight alongside characters like Mace Windu and Yoda, or against villains like Darth Maul and General Grievous. Whether you’re a seasoned Star Wars veteran or new to the galaxy, the collection offers exciting, action-packed action that will let you experience the saga like never before.
