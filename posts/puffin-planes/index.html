Game Description#

Puffin Planes, a casual adventure game about airline management. In this strategic sandbox, gamers build their aviation empire from the ground up, navigating the skies with a fleet of varied aircraft. From Tokyo to Los Angeles and even to the terraformed surface of Mars, Puffin Planes offers an exciting journey for aviation enthusiasts. As your airline grows, you will need to continually expand your fleet. New aircraft are constantly appearing on the horizon, each with their own unique combination of speed, range, fuel efficiency and capacity. There is no one-size-fits-all solution: adapt your fleet to your network requirements.

As you progress, your airports will also develop. Random bonuses will get you started, but soon you will be faced with the need to make important decisions. Build hubs, lounges and more to optimize your network. Will you adopt a hub and spoke model or explore point-to-point routes? If you’re feeling socially active, invite friends via Steam to join your global airline network. While the adventure is fun to play alone, it’s even more addictive when played with other aviation enthusiasts. Overall, the title promises a complex simulation where profit charts, passenger routes, and strategic choices intersect. This is a game that will keep you coming back again and again, flight after flight.
