Game Description#

Just Cause 2 takes users into a familiar universe, where now, in addition to single-player gameplay, it is now possible to play online with a friend on a pirate. Here, unlike the first part of the series, many innovations have been added, which are especially noticeable with the participation of several users. As before, the main location is the fictional island state of Panau. Only now, when it is possible to play online on the Internet, the level of chaos here is noticeably increasing, and this can be felt from the first minutes of entering the server.

The main feature that makes Just Cause 2 stand out from a number of other projects in a similar genre is its vertical gameplay. Thanks to the presence of a special device for the main character (a grappling hook), it becomes possible to cling to almost any objects. Buildings, cars, helicopters, planes or boats - the character will grab hold of all of the above. And even if, after uncoupling, a controlled fall at speed begins, you can easily stop the process by releasing the parachute. At the same time, there are no quests, tasks for gaining progress or any similar activities. The possibility of free online play gives complete freedom of action, with access to vehicles, weapons and everything that allows you to create chaos. The 400 square kilometer map contains many interesting places that you can reach and cause real mayhem. In addition, the online game includes a chat, so you can coordinate with other users and indulge in madness together.
