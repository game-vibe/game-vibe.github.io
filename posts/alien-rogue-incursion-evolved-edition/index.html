Game Description#

In Alien: Rogue Incursion Evolved Edition, a distress call brings retired Colonial Marine Zula Hendricks to the remote planet Purdan. Her investigation uncovers a secret Weyland-Yutani complex that has been completely overrun by deadly creatures. The gloomy corridors of the station become a battlefield for survival. This title is the first chapter of a duology, the events of which take place between the first two films of the franchise. The plot centers on Zula’s efforts to eliminate an unexpected biological threat.

Gameplay in Alien: Rogue Incursion Evolved Edition is built from a first-person perspective, focusing on combat skills and reflexes. Fierce xenomorphs stalk the player from all sides, displaying unpredictable behavior and launching surprise attacks. To fight back against the ruthless alien threat, gamers must use an arsenal of weapons and personal abilities. A synthetic companion named Davis 01 assists throughout the mission, providing support in the single-player campaign.

This project is built around a story that creates a dark, depressing atmosphere. Encounters with monsters form the core of the gameplay, combining stealth maneuvering with direct and brutal combat. The narrative develops through a series of cinematic events that immerse the gamer right in the center of a military-sci-fi conflict. Zula Hendricks must rely on her determination to survive the constant threat of xenomorphs and the hostile environment of the captured complex.
