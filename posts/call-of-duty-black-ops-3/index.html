Game Description#

The first-person shooterCall of Duty: Black Ops III demonstrates the blurred relationship between ordinary people and technology. In it you will forever forget that the new generation of soldiers is not a fiction, but a reality that will overtake us in the near future. Humanity has created technology to be leaders in the coming war, but will it be? The game shows this in three modes: story campaign, multiplayer shooter at scenario locations, and also in zombie mode.

The game takes place in 2070, 40 years after the events of the second part. Aviation became completely useless as technology was developed to take control of any airborne device. Now that ordinary soldiers can completely control their standard of living with the help of PNI technology, physiological death has become a fiction. Our Special Forces heroes travel to Egypt to rescue the country’s president from the clutches of a rebel group.

We are offered 9 characters with unique abilities and weapons to choose from. These are warriors with spikes that destroy everything around; and a warrior with a bow who shoots special explosive arrows; also fighters who control electricity, shoot energy charges and simple, armored warriors who cannot be taken even with a rocket launcher. When creating a character, his gender is taken into account - you can choose yourself, as well as completely customize his appearance in a well-thought-out appearance editor. The massacre takes place in different locations. The player will definitely not be bored going through different maps in Egypt, Switzerland and Singapore. Specialist. Operations require you not only to strictly control the fighter, but also brain activity. The multiplayer mode is divided into 2 ways of playing: the first is a fight between fighters at a location in the DeathMatch mode. The second is to complete the game in cooperative mode - up to 4 people.
