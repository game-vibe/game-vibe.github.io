Game Description#

Drake Hollow is a third-person adventure game that you can play over the Internet online with a friend. The pirate site offers all game content for free, including multiplayer mode. You can play online with a partner, or team up with different players from around the world. Events take place in a world called Hollow. Darkness has set in and all living things begin to die. The free online game features players teaming up to take on the evil creatures that threaten the Drakes.

The world is inhabited by herbivorous peaceful drakes. With the arrival of darkness, they suffer from hunger, thirst and have nowhere to sleep. You’ll need to grow gardens, feed them, dig wells for water, and even give them yoga balls to keep them entertained. The online game Drake Hollow even allows you to set up a puppet theater. It is possible to buy them treadmills and install solar panels to supply the energy needed for the fence so that monsters cannot enter the animals’ territories. A Tesla coil will help in this matter.

A wide arsenal of weapons is available, from a tennis racket to a pneumatic hammer - you can fight with anything. You need to team up with friends to defend the territories of small drakes together. Allows you to explore different regions, the area of ​​which is 2.5 square kilometers. Areas are generated randomly. It is necessary to obtain wood, metal, crystals and supplies.
