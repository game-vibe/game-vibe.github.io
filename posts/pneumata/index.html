Game Description#

InPneumata, residents disappear without a trace in the dimly lit corridors of Clover Hill. Their screams echo through the walls and blood seeps into the cracks. Your task as a detective is twofold: unravel the hidden truth within and reconstruct your fragmented memories, or succumb to the hidden horrors. Welcome to a game that defies reality and plunges you into a cosmic nightmare. Psychological and cosmic horror: Pneumata blurs the line between reality and alternate worlds. You’ll question what’s real and what’s not as you navigate the winding corridors. The horror is palpable, and survival depends on your wits. Gather weapons and supplies - each resource is scarce - and run or hide to survive.

But this is not just about physical threats, but about unraveling a series of terrible murders. Crime Scenes and Evidence: As a detective, you will thoroughly investigate crime scenes. Piece together clues and evidence to solve the dark mystery. The residential complex is fraught with secrets, secret places and paths. Explore its grim realism, but beware - the very walls seem to whisper malice. Unpredictable Creatures: AI-controlled creatures cannot be predicted. Fight or hide, use the environment to distract them, and pray to survive. The tension never lets up, with every corner fraught with eerie horror. And perhaps in this complex you will find the key to your missing memories.
