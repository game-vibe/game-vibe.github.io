Game Description#

Visco Collection presents 7 arcade titles from the 1980s and 1990s. This collection contains projects of several genres, including Shoot ‘Em Up, fighting, sports and racing. The presented software supports single and local multiplayer modes for competitive or joint passage. Each Title demonstrates the special style of the arcade gameplay of that time with unique goals and mechanics that players have to study.

The Visco Collection contains Andro Dunos-Side-Squash Shoot ‘Em Up, which encounters you with elaboration ships and large-scale bosses. The selection also contains Bang Bead, one-on-one game-fiting, where opponents use a variety of techniques characteristic of the characters. Captain Tomaday is a shooter in which the protagonist takes various forms for moving by locations and entering into battle with a series of original antagonists. The Flipshot project offers a futuristic competition in the style of a pinball against another person or opponent under the control of AI.

The set continues with the Ganryu action-platform, where the player chooses Musashi or Suzum to confront the resurrected Kodzir and his ninja legion. For sports lovers goal! Goal! Goal! Provides arcade football, focused on dynamic matches and the ability to score goals between the teams. The Neo Driftout collection is completed - races with a view of the top, offering control of rally vehicles on tracks through the development of drift mechanics in competitions with other drivers.
