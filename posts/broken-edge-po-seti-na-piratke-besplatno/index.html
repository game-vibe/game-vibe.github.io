Game Description#

Broken Edge is a unique free-to-play virtual reality online gaming experience where you can play online with friends or random opponents. Choose one of the legendary warriors and fight to the death in different arenas in an online pirate game. Each character has their own weapon and fighting style that you must master to win. You can always create your own team with a friend or join an existing one.

Broken Edge offers a truly immersive combat experience. Gamers move freely and swing their swords, using their tactical skills to block incoming blows and break opponents' swords at the point of impact. Thrusting, cutting, parrying and smashing are just some of the techniques you can use to defeat your enemy. Users can hone their skills in the game’s dojo or single player mode to prepare for global competition. Taking part online, participants compete against other gamers from around the world to climb the ranks and prove that they are the world’s greatest sword fighters.
