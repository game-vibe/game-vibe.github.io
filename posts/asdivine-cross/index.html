Game Description#

Asdivine Cross is a Japanese role-playing game with 2D anime-style pixel graphics. The project was ported to computers from smartphones. In the PC version, the developers adapted the controls for a keyboard and mouse and added an achievement system. The title emulates an old school JRPG. In keeping with the tradition of the genre, this video game features random encounters and turn-based combat. The order of moves of all participants in the battle is displayed on the screen, and you can influence it. Team members can use physical and magical abilities, perform a series of attacks, fill a special scale for special attacks, and use objects. Gamers are allowed to create their own combos. For victory they give loot and gold. Money can be spent on purchasing equipment from merchants. Equipment can also be found in locations.

In between battles, adventurers explore the area, chat with each other and communicate with NPCs. As the levels increase, heroes and heroines increase their characteristics and also learn new skills. Asdivine Cross is considered the third installment in the franchise. The plot tells about the adventures of the thief Harvey, who robs the rich and shares the gold with the poor. After a botched robbery, he ends up in prison, where he learns of a conspiracy against the royal family. The anti-hero is trying to save the kingdom by preventing war on the continent. He is helped by Princess Amelia and other allies who join the party as they progress. Events take place in a fantasy world. It is ruled by two gods of Light and Darkness, who draw their power from the faith of people. If the population stops believing, the balance will be disrupted and everything will end.
