Game Description#

Beat Hazard 2 - continuation of the bright representative of the Shoot ‘Em Up genre. You have to plunge into dizzying shootings on flying ships, and destroy the hordes of enemies to drive music. The latter occupies most in the video game, and the help of it in each battle can find its tone and rhythm, on which even the outcome of the battle can depend. Or connect your tracks to destroy the hordes of enemies to your favorite compositions much more fun.

The game presents 7 various spaceships - from fast, but low -power, to destructive with a low speed of movement. Also, there are special modules that will allow deadly rays to emit all directions, which is very effective against simple, but numerous groups of enemies. The use of all aircraft is to be used on various tasks that require a quick reaction, and the ability to navigate in the situation with lightning speed.

Periodically, it is necessary to perform missions for adventures during a certain song, a set of a given number of points, or receiving a new rank. All the Lefts will provide complete freedom in the manner of battaling, and set the tone of reiglable, because even gameplay depends on the variable modules. Previously, unprecedented mobs, in whom artificial intelligence had “wiser”, so that they compete on the field were added. And also, all the bosses have been processed: now these are huge randomly generated collars, creating large challenges even with ardent fans of this series.
