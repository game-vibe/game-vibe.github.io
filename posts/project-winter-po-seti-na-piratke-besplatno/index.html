Game Description#

Project Winter is a multiplayer survival game that you can play online with a friend, competing or playing in co-op on a pirate for free. Game mechanics are based on social camouflage and survival. On the Internet online, you can jointly fight back against wild animals or fight with other groups, exploring the vast expanses of frosty territories. The online game Project Winter encourages constant interaction between participants. Only a well-coordinated game helps you survive and get to the rescue helicopter.

Teams are required to collect resources, construct and repair buildings, fight off predators, and also keep a close eye on who is the traitor in the group. The possibility of free online play contains elements of the classic “mafia”. One or more people in the group must create a sabotage and kill the remaining survivors as quietly as possible. Who to trust? This question remains open until the very end of the match. The stronger the storm becomes, the more power the traitors gain. Killers act together and can think through a plan of action.

It is important to prevent survivors from reaching rescue vehicles. You can use melee weapons, firearms, etc. Each player decides for himself how not to give himself away and how to deal with his opponents. One way is to track down a member who has strayed from the team. Or you can arrange a bloody massacre right next to the helicopter. The traitors, although in the minority, are becoming stronger every minute, so there is a chance that the longer the survivors are unable to track down the killers, the worse they will be closer to the end of the match.
