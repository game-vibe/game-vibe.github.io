Game Description#

Just Cause 3 is a continuation of the famous franchise about the adventures of a specially trained agent named Rico Rodriguez. This part of the series did not deviate from the style and genre of previous games in the universe where the mercenary nicknamed “Scorpio” lives. The end of the trilogy is made with the same driving action, open world, a large number of tricks and devices available in the protagonist’s arsenal, and an adventure plot in which the fate of an entire state will be at stake. The fictional Medici power is located in the Mediterranean Sea, covers an area of ​​1000 square kilometers and is filled with all kinds of posts, camps and enemy units of the evil dictator who has seized power in the region.

The signature, distinctive sign of the series remained in place - the grappling hook located on the hand of the main character. Using this mechanism, Rico quickly moves, grabs enemies, cars, objects (including explosive barrels for throwing at enemy positions), creates obstacles and much more. Also, with the help of a cable in his hand, Rodriguez can soar into the air, and the parachute in his backpack will allow him to land safely. This maneuver allows you to hide from enemies by dramatically changing the protagonist’s position, or helps you climb into a hard-to-reach place. The gameplay largely consists of shooting with crowds of enemies, as well as watching cutscenes between missions. You need to deal with enemies both simply on the way to errands or while moving freely, and on missions in ports, prisons, police stations, military camps, factories, and so on.
