Game Description#

D-Corp is a vibrant action platformer with the ability to play online with a friend, going through a story campaign both online and locally. On a free pirate you need to solve various spatial puzzles, interact with turrets, collect resources and much more. Multiplayer allows up to 4 players to participate. You have to fight with various alien cacti and other funny creatures. With each subsequent level the complexity of the puzzles increases.

You need to control a variety of robots, which each participant configures individually. The free co-op feature allows you to assemble a team of androids to fight your enemies. The gameplay of D-Corp is distinguished by its dynamics, humor and exciting puzzles. Each team member has his own place in the company. It is very important to help, protect and interact in every possible way with your partners.

Moving through locations, you need to avoid dangerous cacti and protect your own base. Enemies rush towards the players in droves to capture the base and resources. Careful planning is required to effectively repel opponents. When collecting resources, you need to pass them through a processor. Friends can be thrown so that partners can climb onto various platforms. At locations you can find dozens of hats that give heroes different bonuses. It is important to keep an eye on the turrets to fill them with ammunition.
