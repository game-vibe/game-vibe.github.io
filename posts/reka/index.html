Game Description#

REKA is an atmospheric adventure game that takes you on a journey through the breathtaking nature of central Europe. Embarking on an adventure as a witch, you’ll wander through forests and swamps, collecting plants and creating magical items for the locals. Experience rural Europe in the late 19th century as you play as a herbalist witch traveling through the countryside with her homemade chicken-legged house. With an extensive witch’s cottage base building system, you can build and furnish your own witch’s house, making it unique with a variety of furniture and decorative elements.

Explore the ever-changing landscape and discover numerous plants and ingredients to create medicines and magical remedies to help and heal the locals. As you progress, you can expand your fairy home by adding new craft stations, furniture, and decor to make it a true reflection of your personal style. Experience a new aesthetic inspired by the enchanting countryside of 19th century Europe, where you can lose yourself in the beauty of the natural world and create a magical haven for yourself and those around you. Join REKA and unleash your inner witch!
