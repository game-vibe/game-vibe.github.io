Game Description#

ClueCluedo The Classic Mystery Game is an adventure game that is a quest, a puzzle and an analogue of a board game in which you can play for free online with a friend. There is a murder in the mansion. Having connected online via the Internet, players need to use evidence to find the killer. You need to find out who killed, where and with what weapon. You can play alone against the computer or compete with friends. The gameplay is completely adapted from the board game of the same name.

Several unique heroes are presented: Scarlett, Mustard, Peacock, Green, Orchid and Plum. Each name carries symbolism and is responsible for the abilities and skills of the hero. All the protagonists of ClueCluedo The Classic Mystery Game were at the Tudor mansion that night when a terrible tragedy occurred. Using the free online game, you need to perform actions by rolling dice. They determine the moves and questions that can be asked of a particular character.

A single player game is introduced that allows you to set the difficulty level. Multiplayer is designed for 6 participants, where each will take on the role of a unique character. Network play supports cross-platform, allowing you to play from different devices. Global leaderboards track the best results and show them in a table. The speed of passage, the number of evidence found, etc. are recorded. There are tips that allow you to act in the right direction. Using the points you receive, you can buy new backgrounds, heroes, skins, etc. You can also open access to two episodes: a trip to Hollywood or become a passenger on the killer express.
