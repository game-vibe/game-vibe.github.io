Game Description#

HoloCafe is a management simulator in which the player takes on the role of a manager and saves a small establishment from ruin. The plot takes place in an alternate universe, where instead of a VTuber agency, a cafe was opened, but the business quickly fell into disrepair. When the situation began to seem hopeless, virtual idols appear from a mysterious portal, ready to help restore business. The gamer has at his disposal a staff of 17 talents representing the Japanese, English and Indonesian divisions of Hololive. Each girl has unique skills, and the team is replenished through the gacha mechanics.

The main gameplay process is focused on direct customer service and operational management. In HoloCafe, it is necessary to personally supervise all stages of work, from taking orders to preparing dishes and serving them to customers. The profit received can be invested in modernizing the premises, purchasing new furniture and expanding the space. The ultimate goal is not only to pay off all debts, but also to create a thriving business with an impeccable reputation. Adding to the unpredictability are random events at the end of shifts, which open up new interactions between characters. To prosper the establishment you will need:

strategically use the abilities of each employee; maintain a high level of customer satisfaction; wisely reinvest funds into improving the cafe.
