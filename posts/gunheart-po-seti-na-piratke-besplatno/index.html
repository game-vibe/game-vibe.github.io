Game Description#

Gunheart is a cooperative shooter with the ability to play online as a pirate with a friend or a whole team of players, controlling robotic bounty hunters and fighting online online with aliens on the very edge of the galaxy. Multiplayer allows you to customize your character with 80 cosmetic items and 4 social skills that determine your character’s style. Mechanics is a fast-paced action game with elements of role-playing adventure. You need to earn money to upgrade your character and buy new equipment. During battles you can strafe, jump, accelerate and teleport. In multiplayer, 4 players can cooperate together.

10 unique barrels available. Guns must be unlocked by reaching a certain level. There are perks that allow you to modify weapons. In total there are more than 40 modifiers available. By combining them, you can create an individual hero build. For example, you can create a rocket launcher with guided projectiles. The possibility of free online play offers you to play in 9 different campaigns or test your skills in 11 challenge missions. An elite mode is available for the most experienced players. The planet Fortuna is available for exploration. On it you need to eliminate the threat in the face of the Antromites. Enemies are represented by both ordinary mobs and strong bosses. You can play both cooperatively and take part in intense PVP battles.
