Game Description#

Your Only Move Is HUSTLE is a 2D fighting game that you can play online with a friend or random pirate players for free, fighting in intense matches and showing off your skills as a deadly killer. You can play online online or compete locally. Controlling your character, you need to perform spectacular combos. Gameplay Your Only Move Is HUSTLE is a turn-based simulator that allows you to plan your actions, creating truly exciting fight scenes. The possibility of free online play offers bloody competitions for the title of martial artist. Sandbox mode, training and online play are available.

It is important to be able to outsmart your opponent. It is necessary to create complex combinations of movements and strikes. Attack patterns are planned in advance. Players make moves one by one. It is possible to fight with fists or use deadly blades. The gameplay relies on creating a cinematic experience inspired by martial arts films. TAS elements allow you to make controlling your hero as spectacular and effective as possible. You also need to think about protection. There are many different variations of both attacks and defenses available. By predicting the actions of your opponent, you can easily emerge victorious from a deadly duel.
