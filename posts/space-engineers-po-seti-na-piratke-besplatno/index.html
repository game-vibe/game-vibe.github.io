Game Description#

On the pirateSpace Engineers you can play with a friend, using all the network features for free. You can play online in various modes, but the main emphasis is on online confrontations over the Internet. Game mechanics revolve around exploration and construction, placing players in a real sandbox where they have to collect resources, build a base and upgrade weapons. You can build spaceships, stations and outposts. The gameplay is also aimed at battles between participants who need to demonstrate their superiority over other players.

The possibility of a free online game includes survival - you need to arrange a race for resources by exploring the surfaces of generated planets. The gameplay allows you to parse absolutely every element into its components. All items are damaged, destroyed, etc. Solo play is available in the style of a lone engineer. There are 2 modes: Creative and Survival. In the first, it is impossible to die and the entire process is aimed exclusively at construction. Space Engineers multiplayer includes: creative mode and survival; co-op and confrontation; battles for up to 16 players at the same time; the ability to build an entire settlement together.

Various scenarios offer a storyline where the engineer finds himself in a unique environment ready to be explored. You can also play on various custom maps. At the beginning of the adventure, the protagonist is completely customized: gender, skin color, hairstyle, etc. Planets have their own volumetric, atmospheric, gravitational, and climatic zones. You can switch from first to third person at any time. For the construction of buildings or ships, more than 200 unique blocks are allocated, consisting of gravity generators, jump drives, turrets, doors, chassis, rotors, wheels, engines, pistons, wind turbines, etc.
