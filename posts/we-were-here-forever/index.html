Game Description#

Waking up in We Were Here Forever in the dangerous Castle Rock, the hero must figure out how to get out of an ominous place from which there is no way out. In an adventure game with puzzle elements, the protagonist and a friend will have to face dangerous dark forces in order to get out of the trap, or they will remain here forever. The game features a cooperative system that is designed for 2 players, where each puzzle needs to be solved together, performing separate roles, communicating through walkie-talkies and trying to convey to a partner all kinds of information about what they saw and heard. The indie horror game belongs to a series of games of the same name, but acts as a standalone adventure with a separate plot and story.

The hero and his friend find themselves in a kind of trap in Castle Rock, from which there is no way out. The guys realize that they have been deceived, and they must join forces to explore a vast territory together and solve puzzles while operating in the distant Antarctic. Here you have to beware of everything that lurks in the shadows, and you can’t trust everything you can see. Comrades must act one at a time, communicating via walkie-talkie, trying to explain the things they saw to their partner in case of danger, in order to quickly solve a logical problem. Soon the heroes encounter ancient forces that are particularly cruel and evil, and are capable of destroying them. Within the walls of the castle there live a lot of entities that you should beware of, for example, the Jester, but he is truly the most powerful creature - the user will have to test it when faced with powerful evil.
