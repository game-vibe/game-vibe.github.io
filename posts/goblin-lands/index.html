Game Description#

Goblin Lands invites you to become the ruler of the seas in a world created using colorful 2D graphics. The journey spans 80 unique levels, distributed across 4 expansive maps, where you will encounter 24 types of islands. The seas are not empty: they are patrolled by wandering transports, and along the way there are various obstacles that require adapting tactics. For the most persistent explorers, hidden locations are provided, and the epic orchestral accompaniment emphasizes the scale of the events taking place and gives each battle a special atmosphere.

The core gameplay is a real-time strategy game focused on conquering archipelagos. Victory at each stage is achieved through the defeat of enemy forces with the help of your own armies, consisting of ships and aircraft. The Goblin Lands project sets the commander the task of not only seizing lands, but also defeating powerful bosses who control entire regions. Each battle is a test of strategic thinking, where it is necessary to wisely manage resources and units to achieve superiority over the enemy.

The development system is closely linked to victories over key enemies. For each defeated boss, skill points are awarded, which can be invested in one of the 20 available upgrades. There are 8 types of transport at your disposal, and for each of them there are 10 unlockable appearances. The total number of achievements exceeds 200, encouraging exploration of all aspects of this tactical adventure and offering high replay value even after completing the main campaign.
