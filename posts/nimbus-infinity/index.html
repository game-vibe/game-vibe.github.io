Game Description#

Nimbus INFINITY depicts an alternate future where humanity lives on a war-torn Earth. There is a struggle in the world between space factions that use flying robots in battles. Events take place in 2100, where some people moved to other planets, and the central government of the CFN created an association of warring factions. Battle Frame technology is falling into disuse as elite fighters begin to create neural links with powerful ‘Mechs, enhancing their abilities during battle. The main character in Nimbus INFINITY is a young courier named Tayo, who is carried to the stars due to a fallen “Battle Frame”.

The storyline will tell about the adventures of the protagonist, who will travel through huge cities, endless deserts and visit various alien planets to find a solution to save the Earth. During the gameplay, players will control a flying “mech robot” that can rise into the air and shoot at opponents using various weapons. Users will decide on a combat stance and select weapons for battle: rockets, cannons, rapid-fire machine guns, rifles, pistols or weapons for close combat. The protagonist will be able to create decoys and use camouflage, temporarily hiding from enemies. You also have to upgrade the hero, gradually acquiring new abilities and combat skills.
