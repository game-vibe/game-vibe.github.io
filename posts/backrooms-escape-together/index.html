Game Description#

Backrooms: Escape Together is an atmospheric horror film designed for cooperative play, where in a group of 4 real users you need to find a way out of another dimension and return to the normal world. The scientists who were sent into these mysterious labyrinths almost completed their mission and were supposed to return back, but the mechanism broke down and they lost the opportunity to return. They know about one more exit, but it lies through the most separated levels, through which only a few managed to get through.

As part of the passage of Backrooms: Escape Together, users can try the classic team mode, as well as a unique format of 1 versus 4. The four act as researchers and must, according to the tradition of the genre, get out to freedom, and one user becomes an antagonist and is obliged to infect the others with deadly bacteria, after which the little people quickly and painfully die. It is worth noting the fact that the protagonists cannot kill the villain.

Users will not have any weapons with them, and therefore will not be able to withstand the threat. Their only option is to solve all the puzzles and escape. Players are required to write down all the most interesting and inexplicable things for later study. Locations are procedurally generated and are more like labyrinths. For ease of communication, there is a voice chat so that the team can quickly convey orders and advice.
