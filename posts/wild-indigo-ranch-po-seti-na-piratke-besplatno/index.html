Game Description#

Wild Indigo Ranch is an exciting role-playing game with a Wild West atmosphere, which you can play online with a friend or for free on the pirated version. Immerse yourself in a new life on the prairies, explore uncharted territories and create the best ranch in the West, enjoying network play over the Internet.

Hello cowboy! You inherited the Wild Indigo Ranch from your uncle, and a new life awaits you in the West! But the border is not always as idyllic as fairy tales tell you. Grit Corp has sinister plans to turn the prairies into a profit-making machine. Work with the residents of nearby Tumbleville to right the wrongs and restore Wild Indigo Ranch to its former glory.

For the prudent cowboy, resources on the prairie are not so scarce. Collect straw to make rope - now you can lasso your first mustang, although you might want to start slowly and catch Rosie the Donkey. A true bull needs a six-shooter to dispense justice and defend himself against the local wildlife. If only you knew how to make it.

A cowboy like you needs a place to hang your spurs after a long day of adventure. Roll up your sleeves, grab your tools and start building the ranch of your dreams. Grow crops and cultivate the land, equip your farm with stables, chicken coops and workshops. Finally, furnish and decorate your new home to your liking - after all, the hearth is where the hearth is.
