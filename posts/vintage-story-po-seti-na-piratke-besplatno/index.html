Game Description#

Vintage Story is a cubic sandbox designed for both solo play and online play with a friend. You can join clans for free on the pirate site, play co-op with a friend and fight with other participants. Vintage Story is inspired by various Minecraft mods, shaping its own gameplay experience and significantly expanding the capabilities of the popular sandbox.

The opportunity for free online play invites a group of survivors to team up to explore the voxel universe. You need to look for food, useful items, tools and much more. You can do crafting, cook food, build your base, etc. The online game places a huge emphasis on search and exploration. The world keeps many secrets and mysteries. Vintage Story takes place in a fictional universe with aggressive flora and fauna.

Players will visit a huge number of biomes where they will encounter bizarre and unique inhabitants. The plot revolves around how the soul of the heroes tries to escape from the locked shell. Going in search of an answer, you will need to get acquainted with the remnants of a past civilization. Participants must solve many puzzles as they descend into mythical dungeons. The world presents temporary instability and temporal storms that affect the environment. Locations are generated randomly. To get answers to questions, you need to go through a huge number of difficult tests. This world does not accept the weak in spirit!
