Game Description#

On the pirateFilthy Animals: Heist Simulator you can play online for free, engaging in debauchery and robbery with a friend. Locally or online, players control mutant animals as they try to rob banks. The leader of the team is the eccentric Tony, a crazy driver and scientist. The possibility of free online play offers the choice of one of 8 adorable animals, each of which has its own individual abilities. Access to new heroes is gradually opening up.

There are many different levels available to complete with different conditions, challenges and much more. The online game Filthy Animals: Heist Simulator invites you to fight with your partners against a huge number of enemies, commit robberies, earn money and increase the experience of your characters. In addition to new heroes, you can unlock additional weapons, abilities, cosmetic items and much more. Alone or together with partners, you need to not only fight opponents, but also achieve the main goal. For example, clean out a safe in a bank. Content, mechanics and features: Co-op for up to 4 people; Vivid battles and various physics-based puzzles; Funny ragdoll of the main characters and non-player characters; Wide arsenal of weapons: guns, machine guns, grenade launchers and others; Various mini-games, bonus levels and difficult challenges.
