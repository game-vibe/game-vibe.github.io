Game Description#

According to the plot of Signs of Life, the United Nations has developed a plan in case of the end of the world due to protracted hostilities on Earth. Based on the plan, two ships were launched into space - the large Hephaestus, responsible for colonization, and the more nimble Hermes, created for reconnaissance. Astronomers discovered a planet whose climatic conditions were similar to those on Earth with great potential for creating a human colony. But the flight to it, as it turned out, is 18 light years.

The fast Hermes was the first to reach its destination. Hephaestus reached the planet 4 years later, and was distracted by a signal from a nearby asteroid. The team wanted to investigate what led to the death of the entire spacecraft. The main character, under the player’s control, managed to stay alive - he managed to use a small space capsule at the last moment. But he has nothing and no one left except clothes, artificial intelligence AIGS and a hand-held laser with many different functions.

In Signs of Life, the gamer will have to explore available locations and meet various creatures, most of which will be aggressive. And also extract resources (rocks, wood, metals, leather, bones and even animal organs), from which you can make armor, weapons and additional devices that help you survive. The range of technologies is great - from a simple bow and arrow to plasma shotguns and Tesla guns.

The game features multiplayer co-op. You can generate your own world (or several with different names), where you can set its size and the number of creatures in it. You can also create an empty world that looks like a simple strip of earth and use it as a training or creative one.
