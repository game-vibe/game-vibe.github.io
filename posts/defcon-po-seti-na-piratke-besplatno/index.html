Game Description#

Defcon, although it does not stand out due to its detailed graphics or similar details, its format is optimal for playing online with a friend on a pirate. The project is inspired by the 1983 film of the same name and conveys all the experiences, moods and atmosphere that was felt in the world during the Cold War. Here, the user is given the role of a general, who is based in a well-hidden underground bunker. His task is to destroy the civilian population of the enemy state without putting his own at risk. And given the fact that there are several sides to the conflict, gamers can fight each other online online.

The world map in Defcon is divided into territories: North America, Europe, Russia, Asia, South America and Africa. Each segment is highlighted with a specific color, and when users make a choice, they can immediately determine which color their area is under. After the choice is made, gamers need to place weapons (including nuclear warheads), air defense systems, and a fleet on the territory. This occurs within 18 minutes of game time, and combat is prohibited during this period. They begin when entering DEFCON 3 mode, when the first row of restrictions is removed. Further, at the second level, the option to launch bombers appears (the network game accompanies all the corresponding models). The last level allows you to use warheads capable of pulverizing cities in the blink of an eye. Moreover, when a player uses this option, other participants in the session see this and can begin to respond. The same applies to launching missiles from submarines - when they begin active operations, they immediately reveal themselves. The more enemy population is destroyed, the more points a particular user receives. But at the same time, the possibility of a free online game does not exclude the outcome of the outcome in which there is no winner, and everyone perishes in nuclear madness.
