Game Description#

Dying Light: The Beast invites gamers to return to the chaotic world of Harran, but this time the story unfolds in the rural area of ​​the Castorsky forest. This new environment is a different kind. If Harran was an overgrown urban venue with high buildings and destroying infrastructure, then Castor Woods is more calm, with small towns and extensive natural spaces. This is the same dangerous world, but now with a more extensive space, in the open air, which offers new ways to combat the usual dangers.

In the center of all this is Kyle Crane, the main character of the Dying Light: The Beast series. After more than ten years of experimentation and suffering, Crane gained freedom, but with her a new battle for survival came. He has to navigate in the forest, to help those whom he meets, and to track down those who are guilty of his torment. Dying Light: The Beast is largely based on the day -night cycle - the main feature that was improved from the time of the first part.

In the afternoon, users can slowly explore the area, collect resources and plan their further actions. The environment is extensive and offers many opportunities for secrecy and strategies. However, with the onset of night, the atmosphere changes. The undead becomes more aggressive, so it is important either to escape from it or join the battle. This transition to gameplay maintains voltage, since players are never too far from danger, even in seemingly safe places.
