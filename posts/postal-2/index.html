Game Description#

POSTAL 2 is a first-person shooter full of dark humor, references, blood and other hardcore stuff like desecrating graves or putting cats on a machine gun as a silencer. The plot develops in a strange town - Paradise. The main character of Postal 2 will be a character named Dude. He tries to live an ordinary life without doing anything illegal.

At the beginning of the story, he gets a job at the computer game development office Running with Scissors. That’s why he moved to Paradise with his wife and dog Champ. The story is divided into 5 days, from Monday to Friday. Every morning, Junior Dude Courier (the protagonist’s full name) has a to-do list that he needs to complete. This could be either running errands from your wife or getting an autograph from Gary Coleman.

The simplest and most obvious ways to solve the proposed quests can turn out to be much more complex in the end. After all, at the end of each day, some event will occur that will entail complete madness. For example, Dude is collecting signatures to allow cruel and crazy games. This will not please activists fighting to ban this type of entertainment and ban gaming communities. They will begin to attack the main character, which will lead to total destruction and many deaths.

There are many locations in the game: a library, a supermarket, a napalm factory, a post office, a butcher shop, a police station, and so on. All of them are located inside the town, because it is open and the transition between territories is carried out by loading. There is a multi-stage system for adjusting difficulty levels, the maximum of which is called “Crazy”.
