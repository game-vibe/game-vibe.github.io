Game Description#

Godumas is a horror game with the ability to play online on a pirate for free, fighting monsters with a friend or random players. Using the Internet online, you can assemble a team of up to four people to go to a mysterious island, where you will need to engage in battle with the possessed locals, interrupting the ritual of sacrifice. The online game Godumas is designed for interaction and cooperative play.

There is a huge variety of puzzles on the island. The heroes of the adventure find themselves trapped on a distant continent. To escape, you need to solve numerous puzzles and engage in battle with the local inhabitants. You need to behave as quietly as possible, not allowing enemies to detect you. The protagonists are being hunted, so every wrong action can be fatal for the team.

There are always two routes on the map. Participants themselves determine the style of passing. At locations you need to collect items, look for clues and much more. The possibility of free online play allows you to join forces to escape. For example, some information is only visible to certain characters. If one player reads the message or hint, then only he has the information. You need to fight off monsters with a flashlight or special amulets. You can throw stones to distract enemies and hide in the bushes. When wounded, the character moves much slower. Treatment occurs with a syringe. There are 500 unique locations on the map where items and treasures can appear. There are a total of 4 types of enemies available with their own unique characteristics.
