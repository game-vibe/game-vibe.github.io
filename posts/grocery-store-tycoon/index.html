Game Description#

Grocery Store Tycoon allows you to build and manage a retail business starting from scratch. The player can switch between a first-person view for direct interaction and a free camera view to guide the store from above. The gameplay makes it possible to independently work at the checkout or arrange products on shelves. You can carry boxes from the warehouse one at a time or use a cart to transport large quantities to replenish inventory and keep shelves from emptying.

The development of a retail outlet in Grocery Store Tycoon occurs through the purchase and installation of equipment. The gamer purchases shelving, refrigerators, freezers and cash registers to furnish the room. Experience gained by working, passively accumulating or completing tasks opens up access to new types of products, management tools and the chance to buy out other stores. Human resource management involves hiring workers, assigning them to positions and controlling their mood, which depends on the work assigned to them and the salary; employees may resign if these conditions are not met.

For a successful gaming experience, customer satisfaction is important - an indicator that depends on the availability of the goods they need and the time spent inside. Maintaining order in the establishment, including returning carts, general cleaning and equipment maintenance, impacts the operation of the establishment. The interactive interface provides data on visitor statistics, supply and demand for specific products, and prices. This system allows you to view the status of equipment and manage worker tasks, which is a key element of the entire game.
