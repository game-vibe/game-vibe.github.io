Game Description#

Create your own board game in Tabletop Simulator. The gamer will have to develop his own project for an interesting “board game” according to his own rules. Come up with scenarios, gameplay conditions, the final goal and interaction mechanisms. Share your projects with other users and, of course, play your creations.

In addition, Tabletop Simulator comes preloaded with 15 classic tabletop experiences. Among the initial collection of games you can find checkers and chess, mahjong and dominoes, poker and many other entertainments. The cooperative mode allows you to play a game with another player via the Internet or LAN, or recruit an entire raid campaign of 10 living people at the same time. What interesting things the game provides the user with:

A real sandbox with a focus on tabletop entertainment. Collect a party in one of the classic games or make a completely new game. Well-developed physics allows figures and objects to interact with each other according to the laws of the real world. A powerful editor with which players can develop mods and additions to existing projects. Communication between gamers is represented by both text and voice communication. Co-op mode supports Internet, LAN and HotSeat competition modes. The RPG generator will allow you to create a real dungeon with a role-playing component. Administrator rights allow the owner of the room to flexibly configure tokens, rules and access for each individual user.
