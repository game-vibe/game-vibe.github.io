Game Description#

The shooterFirescout can be played online with a friend for free, using all the multiplayer capabilities of the project on a pirate. On the Internet online you will have to fight with random players around the world and with friends, joining forces in co-op or performing in intense PvP matches. A single mode is available against bots. Free online play allows you to take part in modes such as Team Deathmatch, Kill Confirmed, Capture the Flag, Conquest and Rush.

Each player takes on the role of an assassin. Mercenaries are professional killers who receive orders to kill difficult targets. Matches are played over several rounds. All modes include a variety of goals: hold a position, quickly decide the outcome of a match, get as many kills as possible, and much more. There are many unique interactive maps available. By defeating enemies, the fighter’s skills are upgraded, affecting the gameplay. Firescout gameplay includes: single and multiplayer play; the ability to train with bots; a variety of online modes that allow you to join teams or fight only for yourself; a wide selection of weapons and equipment; leveling up and improving abilities; tactical shootings and using the environment in for your own purposes; unique maps and different conditions for victories.
