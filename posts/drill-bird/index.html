Game Description#

Drill Bird invites you to immerse yourself in a world where every foray underground is critical. As darkness falls, the depths become filled with danger, forcing you to rush back to the safety of your nest. This story of survival and care is told without a single word, creating a special atmosphere in which comfort is mixed with constant tension. Every decision made directly affects the outcome, because time is running out inexorably, and the creatures waiting in the darkness do not forgive mistakes, turning each expedition into a risky undertaking.

The main character is a bird that fell from the sky and is armed with a drill beak. The main goal of this brave creature is to save its future offspring, for which it is necessary to save and raise a single egg. In the Drill Bird project, the protagonist will have to explore underground caves in search of valuable ores. The collected resources are used to strengthen the egg and improve your own abilities, allowing access to deeper and riskier areas of the dungeons, where the most valuable materials and greatest threats lie hidden.

The passage process is built on cycles of immersion and return to the surface. Drilling through the thickness of the earth, the character finds not only ore, but also hidden treasures. The resulting materials allow you to improve the drill, increase your health and load capacity. Monster encounters are small puzzles that require cleverness to overcome rather than brute force. This repeating cycle of worrying about the future and surviving in the present forms the basis of the entire adventure, making it rich and memorable.
