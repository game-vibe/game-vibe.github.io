Game Description#

The Lord of the Rings: War in the North - Legacy Edition is an updated re-release of the dark role-playing action game, where events unfold on the northern fronts of Middle-earth, far from the path of the Guardians. The storyline focuses on the confrontation with the black Numenorean Agandaur, who is gathering an army of Sauron’s minions in the ruins of Fornost. The journey through Mirkwood, Rivendell and the Lonely Mountain will bring the heroes together with legendary figures like Aragorn, Gandalf and Elrond.

The combat system is based on team interaction of 3 unique heroes in online or local co-op. The re-release of The Lord of the Rings: War in the North - Legacy Edition controls the ranger Eradan, the elven sorceress Andriel and the dwarven warrior Farin with the ability to instantly switch roles.
