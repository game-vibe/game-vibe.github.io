Game Description#

On the pirateQUAKE you can play online with a friend for free or compete with other players online or locally. The possibility of network play is aimed at competition between participants, offering to fight in a variety of arenas, which are multi-story complexes and labyrinths. Online play is available and a full-fledged story campaign is presented.

The single player mode in QUAKE consists of 28 levels, which are combined into 4 episodes. In each of them you can find special sublevels that allow you to find certain bonuses and secrets. Each episode is represented by a unique dimension, which can be reached through special portals. Before starting the passage, one of 4 difficulty levels is selected, ranging from easy to nightmare. The latter is secret and must be opened during the first playthrough. The gameplay is tailored to the player’s skill. The higher the difficulty level, the more aggressive the monsters attack.

The adventure begins from a military base. There is a Slipgate portal here, which allows you to get to a specific location. You will have to fight with different enemies with different patterns of movement and attacks. At the end of the level, statistics are provided including time spent, number of kills, secrets discovered, etc. Monsters can attack other adversaries. You will play as a nameless soldier looking for a dangerous enemy named Quake. The hero leads the Counterstrike operation, designed to destroy monsters from another dimension.
