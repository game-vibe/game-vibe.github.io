Game Description#

Hunters Inc places groups of 1 to 4 players in the role of orc hunters, engaging in first-person hand-to-hand combat. Gamers form a cooperative squad to hunt down and destroy giant monsters in a procedurally generated world for each mission. These locations are filled with hostile flora and fauna, creating constant natural threats in addition to the main targets. Gigantic creatures have enough strength to destroy elements of the landscape during battle, dynamically changing the battlefield.

In Hunters Inc, teamwork is built around 4 different classes, each with a specific function. The Berserker cuts through the enemy hordes, the Pathfinder is engaged in reconnaissance and detection of the main target for the group. The Blacksmith collects natural resources to build tactical combat vehicles, and the Shaman supports the squad with protective totems and spells. Hunting success depends on these specialized roles working together to overcome obstacles and subdue their prey.

Character development is directly related to hunting, as various components are obtained from defeated creatures. Each monster drops unique parts that are used in the item creation system to give weapons and armor the attributes of a specific beast. Completing quests also earns you gold and experience points, a system that encourages gradual game progression. Accumulated resources allow you to level up, giving you access to an expanded range of equipment and new weapons for future forays.
