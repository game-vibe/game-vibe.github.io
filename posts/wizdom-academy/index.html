Game Description#

Wizdom Academy puts the player in the shoes of the director, responsible for the construction and operation of a school for wizards. The process begins with the construction of the institution’s buildings, creating the foundation for learning. The key task is to attract people as students, as well as recruit staff to become teachers in the academy, shaping the composition of the school from the very beginning. The initial stage of construction prepares the ground for subsequent expansion and choice of specialization.

Management tasks at Wizdom Academy extend well beyond normal construction, focusing on monitoring student progress and communicating with staff. Interacting with students and faculty allows you to learn their backgrounds and personalities as they learn. This game includes objectives related to meeting the demands of the board of trustees and exploring the mysteries surrounding the institute itself. The personal growth of the students is directly related to the time spent and the sequence of classes within the walls of the institution, which affects their abilities and character traits in the future.

Inventory control is built around Mana, an element needed for many school processes, the amount of which constantly changes during playthrough. It is necessary to actively collect this resource, come up with ways to store it and carefully distribute it to ensure the operation of different systems of the academy and for magic. The gameplay offers situations where Mana is scarce, requiring the leader to take thoughtful action and careful leadership to survive periods of shortage. Strategic decisions become necessary when faced with a lack of resources and other difficulties in the life of the academy.
