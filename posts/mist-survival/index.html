Game Description#

Another survival game among zombies is Mist Survival. A completely open world, a unique system of characteristics, new types of weapons, shelters and the ability to create their own gang, attracted new players to fight on an open map against the crowd of enraged people absorbed in the epidemic. The main character, like all real players, will be protected from the epidemic, because in their body there is a special hormone that needs to be nourished every day. In the temporary space of the game there is night, day; Weather conditions are also present. The character will get tired, want to drink, feel bad and be treated from blows. The realism of the game is that on the territory of the location, not only zombies are concluded, but also bandits, animals, natural disasters.

The united players will have to build refuge, seize the territories, defend themselves from the misfortunes of other players, and especially be afraid of bandits - they should not just burn your refuge, and then take everything that is there. For animals, it will not be difficult to get to your base at night, if you do not have a fence, and kill you right in the bed. Also with zombies. If he can go to the house unnoticed, then after half an hour you will hear hundreds of zombies around your house, which is about to break through the door.

The craft system in Mist Survival is unique. Firstly, weapons and its modernization are a key moment in the game. You can build your own shelters, sell the best protective structures, build cars and much more. Remember the fog. The game has an analogue of “emissions”. This is a clot of air, which forms over the players for a few minutes (game hours), during which the monsters around will go to you over rivers, lakes, fences, etc. Such an outbreak of aggression distracts monsters from one base, and leads them to another.
