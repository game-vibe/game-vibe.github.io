Game Description#

Siegecraft Commander is a strategy game that allows you to take part in dynamic confrontations online over the Internet. In Siegecraft Commander, you can play online as a pirate for free, fighting with a friend or random players. You can compete on one device. The gameplay includes turn-based mechanics and RTS elements. Depending on the matches, strategic mechanics change. The gameplay includes elements of tower defense.

For example, in turn-based matches, you need to plan how towers, units, walls and fortresses will be built. Here you need to set the vector of attacks and choose battle tactics. Moving into real-time mode, the online game invites participants to build bases and launch full-scale attacks on the opponent. The possibility of free online play allows you to take part in confrontations with up to three people, showing your tactical skills against opponents.

Destructions are built like dominoes. If the main building is destroyed, all child buildings also explode. Cluster mines, missiles, TNT barrels and other types of weapons are available. Towers have their own unique characteristics and provide different benefits. In the barracks you can create ground troops, including infantry and ballistas. Each squad is represented by individual abilities. The confrontation is between two factions: the Freema knights and the tribal lizardmen. The former hunt for treasures, while the latter live far from civilization and worship the Shaman.
