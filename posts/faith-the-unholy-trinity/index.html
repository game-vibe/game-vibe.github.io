Game Description#

Faith: The Unholy Trinity is an indie chorror with an unusual presentation. The project has a minimalistic pixel graphics in retro style. At the same time, there are full-fledged KAT scenes using rotoscopy. The creators also added synthesized computer speech. The plot is dedicated to the confrontation between the minister of the church and the satanic cult, trying to call a powerful demon. The action takes place in the 80s in Connecticut. John Ward tried using exorcism to save Amy’s obsessed girl. As a result, he provoked a series of terrible events and turned out to be the only survivor.

Faith: The Unholy Trinity is divided into 3 chapters:

The first talks about the return of the churchman to the house with a terrible past; The second tells about the further life of the miracle of the surviving priest and his new nightmare; The third is dedicated to his journey in the wilderness and struggle with crazy sectarians.

The horror movie is inspired by old eight -bit games and religious paranoia, who reigned in the United States at the end of the last century. Gamer will explore the temple, sanctuaries, abandoned churches and forests, faced with cruel violence, bloody deaths and paranormal phenomena. Its protege can use the sacred crucifix to repel infernal evil spirits and firearms, like rifles. The game has several endings, including secret endings.
