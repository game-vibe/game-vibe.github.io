Game Description#

Wheel World represents the story of Kat, a cyclist chosen by ancient spirits for a key task. The setting is on the verge of collapse, and only Glavheroin can prevent it, having made a great shift ceremony. Her journey runs through the world, saturated with a bicycle culture, where she has to resist the elite teams in competitions.

The central mission is to return the abducted legendary details, which are the main components to complete the ritual and save its world from oblivion. The gameplay in Wheel World is based on the study of the half -open world and participation in races. The player has to lead Kat through a variety of landscapes filled with secret regions and rare components for her bike. These parts open up wide opportunities for customization, allowing you to construct bicycles for different types of terrain and types of races.

The tasks facing the Savior on two wheels:

Participation in tense races against elite teams and eccentric rivals. The study of a half -open setting for the search for rare components and hidden locations. Search and return of stolen legendary parts to restore bike. Creating a unique vehicle through a deep customization system. The ritual is a great shift to prevent the collapse of the universe.

Progress is directly related to the narrative on the fulfillment of the purpose of the heroine and the salvation of the spirits of cycling. Each start won and every part of the found close the opportunity to conduct a ceremony. The original soundtrack from Italians do it Better accompanies what is happening on the screen, setting the rhythm of both competitions and moments of movement between locations. The whole game is focused on this single goal: to pedal to keep the universe on the right course.
