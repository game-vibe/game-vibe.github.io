Game Description#

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy introduces a backstory set 15 years before the events of A Plague Tale: Requiem. The story follows Sophia, a young robber on the run who arrives on Minotaur Island in search of answers about her own past. There she will have to elude the pursuing army and face the creature that caused the curse. The narrative combines elements of combat and mythology, exploring the theme of doom in a familiar universe.

The gameplay of Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is based on close encounters with numerous enemies drawn to the island for the treasure. The heroine fights opponents using a system of parries and attacks, where reflexes and tactical maneuvers are required to survive. In addition to combat, gamers will have to solve puzzles by using a stolen Minoan orb to control light, a force that Daedalus wove into the very core of the island. The storyline connects the two heroes through Makula’s heritage, and the action switches between medieval Sofia and ancient Minoan times, where events of the past influence the conflicts of the present.

Exploring the world in this game takes place with the help of the main character’s notebook, which documents the route of her team and the secrets of the location. The haunting creature stalks the player throughout various parts of the island, seemingly anticipating their every move as it hunts from the shadows. Every step carries risk, and every pause could be the last. The only way out is to hide, outwit the pursuer and move on through all the trials of this place, so as not to repeat the fate of those who perished here.
