Game Description#

Chronicon has a large amount of content, despite its indie-production. There are about 900 skills, abyles and perks and 700 items. The main campaign is divided into 4 acts: a fallen temple, an elven war, Clerburg Castle and dangerous wastelands. Of the classes, the choice is available: Templar, berserker, guard and sorcerer. These are a kind of analogue of the paladin, warrior, archer and magician. Each has various talent branches with active and passive skills.

Gameplain is a diaploid and a pungent in fantasy and medium-medium setting. At first, Chronicon has a slow pace. But gradually the game accelerates, and in real time the battles become more and more dynamic and fast. The emphasis here is made on grind and farm. In the process of pumping out the hordes of monsters, the heroes increase two parameters at once. The character level traditionally improves and reveals abilities, and skill increases numbers in them. The hardcore regime includes permanent death.

Crystals are used as a currency. With their help, you can buy things from a merchant and revive in case of death. A wide variety of everything and all allows you to experiment with the creation of builds. Objects can be improved using precious stones, enchanting and transmutation. The collection of sets from the armor is also available. Lut is divided into ordinary, rare, unique and legendary.

Cards are generated randomly. On the levels there are shrines, gifting the protagonist with very useful bonuses. When reaching 100 levela, the game does not end. For high -level characters, special dungeons are available, where the complexity and rewards with each passage are increased.
