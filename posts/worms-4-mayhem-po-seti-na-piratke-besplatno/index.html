Game Description#

Worms 4: Mayhem is an arcade game with the ability to play online with a friend, fighting for free on a pirate in a variety of cooperative modes and confrontation matches, both on the same screen and online online. Worms 4: Mayhem features interactive maps that can be completely destroyed. The online game allows you to join teams or control an army of worms alone to destroy enemy units.

The arsenal of weapons consists of a huge number of different caliber guns and explosives, including pistols, rifles, grenade launchers, etc. Worms 4: Mayhem also features more exotic weapons. Free online play allows you to customize your character, choosing from a wide range of hairstyles, hats, glasses, etc. The weapons factory allows you to create a unique and inimitable gun. Victory points earned during matches can be used to purchase upgrades, which are available in a special store. Players are awarded trophies for setting records.

Explosives come in three different types: regular, fragmentation, and toxic. Explosive barrels at levels also have poisonous properties. Teleports are scattered throughout the locations. In addition to the weapon box, special surprise boxes are also available on the maps. They can either help or cause harm when opened. There is a story mode in which you will need to take control of a worm student carrying out orders from Professor Cherwinkle. Events take place in the Wild West, where it is necessary to confront the cowboy Boggy Baby, on Arab lands with an antagonist in the form of Ali Babun and in the age of dinosaurs.
