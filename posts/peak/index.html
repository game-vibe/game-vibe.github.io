Game Description#

Peak puts players in the role of lost scouts on the lost island for the only purpose - salvation. Survival depends on the conquest of the mountain in the center of the island, this test can be passed alone or in the team up to 4 people. The landscape is changing with every new attempt, demanding adaptation from climos. Gamers can help each other climb to ledges or place ropes and rocky hooks to pave the way for the detachment.

The path to the top in the PEAK Title runs through 4 different biomes, each of which creates special natural obstacles. To withstand the rise, the characters have to get food, eating any of the 30 varieties of food of dubious quality. This game requires to carefully monitor physical injuries, since each miss reduces the supply of strength. The disadvantage of endurance directly affects the ability to climb further, which makes each fall a serious incident.

In the process, you can find various equipment, including energy for a temporary rush of vigor and rocky hooks for laying new routes. Heroes can also find more unusual tools, for example, an object called anti-Vervka. Successful orientation on the ground and survival actions open access to dozens of badges and cosmetic things for the external setting up of the character. The project supports a joint gaming mode by invitation or offers a single passage for other experience.
