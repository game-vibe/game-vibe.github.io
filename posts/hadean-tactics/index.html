Game Description#

A strategy with elements of the card game Hadean Tactics invites you to choose one of the Heroes and go fight the creatures of darkness, descending into mystical dungeons. Dungeons in Hadean Tactics are randomly generated. The combat system is represented by turn-based elements, where the assembled deck plays the main role in building tactics. Not a single fight will be similar to the previous one.

Enemies are represented by a huge variety of monsters, ranging from weak units to demon lords. Each new battlefield offers a unique combination of opponents and a different battle strategy. When placing your party of characters on the field, you need to use cards to deal damage. Each hero has its own attributes and characteristics. As you progress through the game, new fighters will open up, whose abilities can be upgraded.

All rooms contain many traps, obstacles, enemies or various useful items, so it is recommended to carefully explore the locations. By receiving new cards and characters, not only your power grows, but also new tactical possibilities open up, building deadly combinations that can crush any enemy. There are many mini-games and moral choices available as you progress through the story. The game world is fraught with many mysteries that the Heroes have to solve.
