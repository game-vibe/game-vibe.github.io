Game Description#

Crashday is an action and arcade racing game. You can play Pirate with a friend online. They also allow you to do this online. The release contains an improved version of the Redline Edition. The re-release added improved graphics, improved controls, full gamepad support and 10 new tracks.

The project “Day of the Crash” has a single and the possibility of free online play. There is a campaign and individual races. There are also mini-games and a map editor. In addition, there is support for modifications. There are 36 tracks in total. The fleet consists of 12 vehicles: Ironhorse V8, The Judge 5000SV, Incubator V12, Firespitter Evolution ‘89 and others. The cars are not licensed and the brand names are fictitious. However, in appearance the cars are very similar to real-life models. Vehicles can be broken and smashed. They deform in real time. Single and multiplayer games consist of 7 modes: Holding the flag - you need to pick up the banner and carry it through all the checkpoints; Test run - free rides in different cars; Transferring the bomb - users try to get rid of the explosives by passing them on to their opponents; Stunt show – users score points by jumping, spinning, flipping and other techniques; Race – a classic fight for first place on a circular track; Destruction – players ram their opponents to the bitter end or shoot at them with machine guns and rocket launchers; Dynamite Race – gamers are offered a ride with a bomb on the roof that will explode at low speed.
