Game Description#

Lightyear Frontier offers farming in a completely open world. In the farmer simulator, you are asked to explore the planet, create your own home, upgrade your transport, growing various non-standard types of plants in order to provide yourself with resources. Multiplayer is implemented here with a capacity of up to 4 gamers. The game has a well-developed storyline, where you will learn about the mysterious past of the world by studying the ruins of buildings created by unknown creatures.

The player, finding himself on an unknown planet, must acquire the resources necessary for life, so he needs to create a farm, adapting to weather conditions that are constantly changing, as well as preparing for all kinds of natural disasters in order to protect the future harvest. At the start, a farm is created, and in the process the character discovers the resources that he plans to grow. Thanks to the extensive toolkit, the building itself can be arranged as desired. Harvesting occurs with the help of various non-standard agricultural equipment - special fur, which is pumped in the process. Here you can also get pets by taming wild ones.

In the farming simulator, it is proposed to explore the territory in which the protagonist finds himself in order to find out its secrets and receive a message from the former inhabitants, deciphering it. As the hero progresses, he breeds new species of exotic plants that provide him with food for a long time. It also features a breeding and genetics system that allows you to breed fancy animals. The protagonist finds himself in a remote corner of the Universe, not alone, but together with a companion-guide who helps him, and here you can meet aliens, who are relatively friendly.
