Game Description#

Sunrises Order is a farm simulator where the main task is to transform an uninhabited island into a profitable agricultural enterprise. The process of developing a farm is directly related to the accumulation of skill points, which provide access to useful improvements, such as increasing the length of daylight hours, expanding inventory, or improving the quality of the crop. Financial well-being and reputation are achieved through a system of contracts. Completing orders not only brings in income, but also opens up blueprints for the construction of new buildings on the farm. An additional complication is introduced by unpredictable weather, which can either help or harm the farmer’s plans. The gradual development of the island makes it possible to open up new types of plots for gardening or livestock farming.

The action takes place on a personal island acquired by the main character under the influence of his passion for video games. As the only resident, the protagonist is completely dependent on the Animazon delivery service to purchase everything he needs. It is through this service that Sunrises Order receives seeds, tools and other goods for farming. The territory of the property can be gradually expanded by clearing new areas for fields, gardens or animal pens. In addition to the main activity, the project offers other activities.

Additional features on the farm:

Fishing is the process of catching fish, implemented in the format of a rhythmic mini-game. A pond is a special body of water where fish can be released in anticipation of rare finds. The kitchen is a place for preparing dishes that restore the character’s energy points.
