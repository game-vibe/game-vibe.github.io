Game Description#

Farmer’s Market Simulator is a project in which the central place is given to managing your own cozy market in the countryside. The customer service process includes scanning products, packaging them and providing a high level of service to build a loyal customer base. Financial transactions at the cash register require precision when accepting payments and issuing change. Successful trade directly depends on the competent organization of display cases and attractive display of collected products. The source of goods is not only the vegetable garden, but also livestock farming, which involves caring for chickens, cows and sheep to obtain fresh eggs, milk and wool. Caring for animals and keeping their pens clean become an integral part of a comfortable farm routine.

The basis of the entire enterprise is agriculture, starting with plowing the land, planting seeds and regular watering. The entire agricultural cycle in Farmer’s Market Simulator is built on the gradual ripening of the crop, which requires attention and patience. To maintain the efficiency of the farm, it is possible to improve the inventory. Watching crops grow from seeds to full-fledged fruits gives a feeling of satisfaction from the work done. The gradual expansion of farmland and the improvement of working tools make it possible to build a prosperous country life step by step, turning a small farm into a thriving business.
