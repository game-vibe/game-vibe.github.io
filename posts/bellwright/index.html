Game Description#

Bellwright throws you into a world of rebellion and intrigue. Finding yourself in prison for a heinous crime, you must not only clear your name, but also organize a revolution against a tyrannical ruler. Build and manage settlements, collect resources and recruit villagers into your ranks. Each new adept brings valuable skills, strengthening your rebellion and unlocking new technologies for your cities.

Combat is based on skill and focus. Choose weapons and armor from a variety of medieval devices, master fencing, archery, or any other fighting style that suits you. Lead your growing army into battle, issuing orders and employing tactics to defeat the crown’s forces. The path to victory lies not only through military power, but also through strategic management of the city. As you liberate villages, you will create and expand your outposts, organize workers and train elite soldiers. The project offers deep progression.

Hone your combat and survival skills as you watch your soldiers and artisans develop. Every recruit is potential for growth, knowledge that can improve your weapons, strengthen your cities and push the rebellion forward. Unravel the mysteries surrounding the prince’s death and your family’s past as you fight for freedom. Bellwright promises an exciting combination of city management, strategic warfare and an exciting storyline.
