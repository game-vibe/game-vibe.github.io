Game Description#

PowerWash Simulator 2 is a continuation of the story of a professional car wash, set three years after the eruption of the Mount Rushless volcano was prevented. Together with his companion Harper Shaw, the protagonist establishes a new business in Mackingham, expanding its activities throughout the entire territory of Caldera County. The mud-fighting arsenal has been significantly improved, including climbing equipment for working on sheer walls and a hydraulic lift for difficult-to-reach heights. Now not only familiar streets are subject to cleaning, but also new cities, including Labry City and Pampton.

The process of eliminating pollution has become more thoughtful thanks to innovations. In PowerWash Simulator 2, soap is no longer a limited resource and now visibly lingers on surfaces, making it easier to tackle stubborn stains. For large flat areas there is a special disc cleaner. Between missions, you can relax at your own base, which serves as a central hub for selecting missions. This hideaway can be furnished, decorated with souvenirs, and even interacted with the house cats, creating a cozy atmosphere after a hard day’s work.

Tasks have acquired a multi-stage structure, where complete cleansing of one zone opens access to the next. For example, work on a public toilet starts from the outside, and once completed, the door opens to the inside, introducing a new front of work. This approach turns routine washing into a kind of puzzle that requires a consistent solution. The co-op mode has also been redesigned, now supporting not only online co-op with shared campaign progress, but also a local split-screen mode that allows two washers to join forces.
