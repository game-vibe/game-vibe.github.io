Game Description#

Users in Catizens have to develop the city of cats. Players will be able to watch the activities of cute and fluffy animals, helping the kittens build a big and beautiful city. During the passage, users will have to create working groups of different characters that can argue or come into conflict with each other. Misunderstandings will have to be cleared up to increase productivity and improve the quality of life for the entire settlement.

Gamers in Catizens get the opportunity to:

Influence the expansion of the site, construction and increase the efficiency of subordinates. To achieve a good mood among the heroes so that they become friendlier and willingly take on their obligations. Create and customize cats, giving them unique personalities using the built-in editor.

The main task for the user is to arrange locations where it will be possible to place houses, outbuildings, beds with plants, indoor furniture and other elements that serve as decor or useful structures. The quality of the interior and the improvement of the living area will directly determine the cat’s motivation to work and his level of life satisfaction. Gamers will be able to customize the choice of profession for each resident (farming or blacksmithing). Also, some cats need to take the oath of knights in order to protect civilians from enemy attacks.
