Game Description#

Peglin is a side-scrolling RPG adventure with a 2D world. The player will play the role of a fictional peglin creature trying to fight dragons that steal and eat representatives of his race. The main character is a small green creature with big ears. He decided to go to the forest, where the faction’s longtime enemies live. There he will meet his enemies and take back what was stolen by the dragons.

If the ward dies, then the passage ends forever. After death, you can start over, and many gameplay elements are procedurally generated. Every enemy you encounter is strong, and to win you will need to come up with different tactics. The central character has magical abilities and skills that help dodge enemy attacks or climb into hard-to-reach places, taking a better position.

As the user progresses, he acquires magic spheres. Typically, you will need to choose between two or three magic balls, which provide passive skills and active abilities. Found materials are used to make a critical hit potion, upgrade, or craft bombs. Consumables can be found in chests or secret places. The Deadly Forest creates random enemy locations, orbs with unique traits, surprises, and boss stats.
