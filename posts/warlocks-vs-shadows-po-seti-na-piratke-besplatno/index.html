Game Description#

Warlocks vs Shadows can be played online for free as a pirate with a friend, going through the storyline in co-op or online online. A versus mode has been introduced that allows you to fight among yourself. The gameplay of Warlocks vs Shadows is inspired by old-school sidescrollers. Features action, RPG, platformer and puzzle elements. The online game invites warlocks to join forces, who must save the universe from monsters and fight powerful bosses.

The world has been overrun by shadow monsters. The Darklings take the situation into their own hands and try to resist the invaders. You can play alone or use the free online play option to complete the campaign with a partner. Confrontation modes are presented, including deathmatch, which allows up to four people to team up. You can fight in two teams of 2 participants per group. Playing in co-op or solo mode, you need to go through 5 dimensions, fighting strong bosses. Upgrade and level up available. Mechanics and features: co-op and versus mode; procedural generation of enemies and loot; 5 unique universes to explore; 11 characters with their own individual abilities; cross-platform multiplayer; stylish pixel art.
