Game Description#

Dungeon Lords is an action role-playing game that allows you to play online with a friend for free. You can play online online, fighting with players from all over the world. The online game is designed for 8 people. Free online play includes completing the story campaign for up to four participants. There is a PvP mode. The plot part is represented by the main quest and many side quests.

The gameplay places a huge emphasis on action and battles. Heroes fight with swords and shields. Ranged weapons such as bows or crossbows are available. Allows you to open various combo attacks, upgrade, and also carry weapons in two hands. Regardless of race, the protagonists are prone to magic. There is a wide selection of spells that vary in strength and effects. Magic belongs to the highest schools of witchcraft. They specialize in different elements. The higher the character’s level, the stronger the spells he casts. 4 schools of magic in Dungeon Lords: Arcana - combat spells aimed at damage; Heavenly - healing and beneficial; Runic - the use of stones and artifacts; Lower - black magic of summoning creatures.

By completing tasks and fighting enemies, the hero upgrades his abilities. The hero’s level also increases. Experience points can be distributed at any time, without waiting for a level up. There is no clear division into classes in the game. Having started the path of a warrior, you can master the profession of a magician or take the path of a robber. Having discovered a new specialization, other ways of developing the hero appear. In addition, the protagonists are able to fight with any weapon without any restrictions.
