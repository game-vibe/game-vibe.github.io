Game Description#

Ratopia is an original strategy that challenges genres thanks to a unique combination of survival, sandbox and a city construction simulator. Get ready to plunge into a huge and exciting world that will captivate you with its endless possibilities. Here you have to create your ideal city and manage it, but be careful, because every resident has its own characteristics and wealth that you must take into account. No one said that it would be easy, but with the right selection of jobs and services, you can make your residents happy, and your economy is prosperous.

And let’s not forget about the taxes - be sure to develop a detailed policy to keep everyone in the UES. In the end, the pursuit of profit never ends, and in the ratopia every rat for itself. But keep in mind: when you create your utopia, disasters can occur. You have to use your ingenuity and policy to prevent or create them. And who knows, maybe with the right expedition you will even find the remains of the great old rats and bring prosperity to your city. Of course, several predators can hide there, but why worry about them if the fate of your city is in your hands? Get ready to go to the adventure of your whole life with Ratopia!
