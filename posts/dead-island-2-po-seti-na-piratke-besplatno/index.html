Game Description#

InDead Island 2 you can team up with a friend to play online as a pirate for free, jointly exploring Los Angeles infected with the zombie virus and online online to complete many side quests and go through the main storyline. Several people turned out to be resistant to the pathogen and they have to clear the city of the living dead. Multiplayer allows you to play as characters such as Carla, Jacob, Ryan, Dani, Bruno and Amy. Each of the 6 protagonists has its own character and special skills. The possibility of free online play is based on mutual assistance, exchange of resources and joint shredding of an army of zombies. There is brutal and detailed dismemberment.

Characters have a number of general parameters available for leveling: strength, stamina, health regeneration, critical damage, agility, peak health and resilience. At the start of the game, each hero has its own unique performance indicator. In Dead Island 2 you can explore the city. It consists of several districts. Each area contains a special type of infected. There are strong zombie mini-bosses with a large supply of health. For example, zombie bodybuilders or dead bloggers live on the beach.

Secondary tasks consist of a wide variety of objectives for accompanying characters, clearing the territory or missions from the category of go and fetch. Some side games contain unique story threads. The Autograph mechanic allows you to collect cards with unique skills that provide various abilities. The arsenal of weapons includes hammers, axes, clubs, machetes, knives, pitchforks, bats and many other bladed weapons. Both cannons and melee weapons can be upgraded and upgraded using fire, spikes, electricity and other devices.
