Game Description#

Old Market Simulator immerses you and a friend in the delightful chaos of running your own market. The main thing is to buy low, sell high, so that buyers are happy - but don’t forget to clean up after them! Imagine how exciting it would be to haggle with sellers and then strategically display your items to get maximum sales. Watch as shoppers stream through the virtual doors, their digital wallets ready to lighten. But a dirty market is a bad market, so keep the aisles clean or watch your reputation fall.

The beauty of Old Market Simulator is its depth. You can build your market empire brick by brick by literally expanding your store and adding shelves to attract a wider customer base. Do you feel like a gardener? Grow your own crops to sell along with other goods. Need help managing? Hire staff to keep things running smoothly - just remember that salaries eat into profits! This is not a general market simulator. Special events like Halloween throw up surprises. What if everyone wants eggs and beer? Adapt your strategy, adjust your prices and watch your profits. Old Market Simulator is a charming co-op game where you’ll test your business acumen while also working as a janitor.
