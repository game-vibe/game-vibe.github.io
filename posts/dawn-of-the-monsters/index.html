Game Description#

Dawn of the Monsters takes place in the distant future. According to it, humanity has achieved a certain technological progress. But everything turned out to be not so rosy. Evil and cruel invaders have arrived on Earth and are ready to do anything to enslave humanity. They are called Nephilim. True, the little people also have aces up their sleeves. The global network of the defense alliance decides to engage them in battle, and is even ready to sacrifice lives. They created special robotic creatures that will help in the confrontation.

There are 4 creatures in Dawn of the Monsters - Megadon, Aegis Prime, Tempest Galahad, Ganira. All of them are so huge that they can break half a city with one left hand. They can also be strengthened and upgraded. You can use the unique abilities of fighters for your own purposes. It must be taken into account that everyone has their own system of fighting, so someone may be ineffective in a long-range battle, or, conversely, in a close-range battle. There is a mode for 2 players to control several defenders at once.

There is a special Rage mode. According to this, the Persian becomes stronger through exhausting struggle. It will cause more damage, and will also allow you to crush your enemy once or twice. There are 35 missions available to complete, and each of them will be more difficult than the previous one. The gamer is also able to customize the DNA of the characters, this will help during battles with bosses. It should be remembered that these entities are humanity’s only hope for existence!
