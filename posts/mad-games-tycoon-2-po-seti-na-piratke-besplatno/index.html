Game Description#

Mad Games Tycoon 2 is a tycoon game that allows you and a friend to create your own game development company. You can play online, together building a business plan on how to distribute your video games: online online, release a batch of discs, etc. You can distribute demo products for free or produce a high-quality product with a full price tag. Using the opportunity of free online play, you and your companion can make a real revolution in game development.

Events start in 1976. It is necessary to rent premises, install equipment, create offices, server rooms, etc. There is only one goal - to create a next generation game. From a small garage team you will need to reach the global level, becoming trendsetters and changing the world forever. By hiring competent specialists, you need to improve your product and enhance your reputation. The network game Mad Games Tycoon 2 allows you to team up, sharing responsibilities, or go into confrontation, trying to overtake a competing company.

Allows you to create single-player shooters, races, strategies or MMOs. While earning money, it is necessary to improve the office, increase the prestige of the company through new furniture, expensive equipment, decor, etc. It is important to monitor employee fatigue and morale. You should do your best to avoid overwork and crunch, which can lead to a toxic atmosphere within the office. Having developed a real video game hit, you need to support it with add-ons, release sequels or products that will only consolidate the company’s status.
