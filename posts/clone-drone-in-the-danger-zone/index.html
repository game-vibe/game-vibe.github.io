Game Description#

Clone Drone in the Danger Zone is an exciting arcade game in which the player will play as a person whose mind has been transferred into an iron robot! The player enters the arena with the only task - to survive. It is worth considering that each opponent is endowed with individual characteristics and skills, so winning will not be easy. Each blow is under the close attention of a robot commentator, who will cover the course of the battle and your actions throughout it.

At the initial stage of the game, your combat arsenal will only have a sword, but after each successful battle you will be able to improve the characteristics of your robot, as well as choose a more effective weapon for battles. In case of defeat, the player is given a one-time chance to use his own clone, who will fight to the last in the arena.

The game features several game modes: the story mode reveals the game’s plot, in which the player will have to escape from the arena and find freedom in a new body. Endless mode is designed to sharpen the player’s skills and also features difficulty levels. Challenge mode opens up access to new skills and trophies for successfully completing the challenge. The level editor is suitable for creative players, it allows you to create your own levels and maps! Experience this vibrant and dynamic arcade game first-hand by participating in deadly battles. Grab your ruthless swords and see you in the arena!
