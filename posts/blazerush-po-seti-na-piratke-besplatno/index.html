Game Description#

BlazeRush is an arcade and combat racing game. In them you can fight for first place with computer opponents or compete online with other users on the Internet. On Pirate it is possible to play online with one friend or several friends at once. At the same time, users are allowed to connect at any time to an already ongoing race.

The gameplay in BlazeRush is reminiscent of Rock n' Roll Racing. It also uses an isometric view, and some of the cars have similar designs. Even races are also held on different planets. There are three of them: the industrial giant LAVA-3, the organic BG-13 and the heavily forested Green Giant. All routes are circular. Bonuses such as acceleration and weapons are scattered along the roads. The arsenal is represented by a sound wave, a saw, a machine gun, a snot, and a rocket. 16 types of cars do not have a health scale.

There is no damage or money involved in the project. To destroy your opponent, you need to push him off the track using a ram or cannons. At the same time, the opponent who flew into the ditch will soon be restored and will continue to fight. There is no pumping here. The single contains various championships. The possibility of free online play includes a hotsit (up to 4 people) and online multiplayer (the maximum number of participants is 8). It should be noted that a full-fledged online game is available even to local participants. 3 modes are available to players: Race - a classic ride, where the winner is the one who gets to the finish line first on the third lap; Survival - all drivers escape from the boss pursuing them, and victory is awarded to the one who died least often during the chase; King of the Hill is a fight for leadership, where the winner is the car that has been ahead of everyone else for the longest time.
