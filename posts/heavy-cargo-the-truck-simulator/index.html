Game Description#

Heavy Cargo - The Truck Simulator is an opportunity to feel like a real trucker who is forced to transport goods through the huge open world. It is necessary to search for orders, loading, unloading and accompanying a variety of cars in convoys. It will be possible to test several types of trailers that can transport the tractor. Juzerov is waiting for the gameplay of the classic driver’s simulator, which controls a big truck.

In Heavy Cargo - The Truck Simulator, gamers need:

To transport unique types of cargo, such as heavy and bulky taps that need to be correctly fixed on the trailer. Work out the optimal route, taking into account the distance and quality of roads, as well as the area (asphalt, crossed, etc.). Pass about 30 missions presented in a single campaign, moving around the world on overall cars.

During the passage, the users will have to ride on European roads and towns, where the area of the entire map is about 200 square kilometers. It will be possible to explore new locations, starting from motorways to narrow mountain serpentines and passes that are on the outlet of the village. Users will have to ride behind the wheel of SCANIA and MAN machine models, carrying trailers/semi -trailers based on real models (boiler bridges, rotary or low -grade platforms, etc.).
