Game Description#

Plunging players into a world of danger, death and despair, Black Forest offers a unique twist on the city-building genre. As the headman of an ill-fated village, your only goal is to survive. The journey begins with gathering resources and fortifying the village to prepare for the terrible night ahead. Will you emerge victorious, enduring all horrors until salvation comes, or will you give in in the face of insurmountable challenges?

In the dark heart of the Black Forest, your village’s original harvest plans have attracted the unwanted attention of the unforgiving darkness that lurks in the forests. As these threats descend upon the village, your role as the village chief becomes pivotal in guiding the villagers to fight the menacing creatures that stalk the night. Sinister secrets of the forest, best left undisturbed, are awakening, and their malevolent attention is focused on you.

Unlike traditional city planners who emphasize expansion and growth, Black Forest focuses on protecting its territory. The strategic distribution of inhabitants, resource management and the construction of fortifications become of paramount importance. Every choice matters, every loss is irreversible, and as night falls, the unforgiving inhabitants of the forest will test your leadership and survival skills. Until the royal knights arrive, you and your villagers will stand alone against the looming threat.

Unique features of Black Forest include a focus on survival rather than conquest, complex artificial intelligence that questions your decisions, and dynamic weather that affects gameplay. Rain, snow or drought are not just window dressing, they affect the needs and priorities of your village. The game is set in the vast world of Dragon Eye, a fantasy role-playing setting, weaving the campaign into a rich tapestry of lore and an overarching storyline.
