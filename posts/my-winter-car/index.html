Game Description#

My Winter Car is a survival simulator in which harsh winter conditions become the main obstacle to restoring an old sports car. The need to constantly monitor body temperature and select appropriate clothing is the key to survival in the world of the project. Any mistake that results in freezing is fatal and entails an irreversible loss of all progress made. The vehicle restoration process is highly detailed, with each of the nearly 200 unique parts requiring the correct tools and specific hardware for installation. The financial side of the issue is resolved by fulfilling orders from local residents, which opens up access to the market for new and used spare parts.

Exploration of the snowy expanses takes place in the first person. The open world of My Winter Car is filled not only with new buildings, but also with dangers, such as thin ice on frozen lakes that can not support the weight of the car. Interaction with the environment involves a variety of winter activities, and control of the rear window ventilation and defogger systems becomes critical to preventing icing. The ultimate goal depends solely on the preferences of the enthusiast himself. Once assembled, the car can be turned into a daily driver, prepared for rally racing, or brought into showroom condition to achieve historic car status.
