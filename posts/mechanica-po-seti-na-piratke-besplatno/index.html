Game Description#

Mechanica is a survival game where you can team up with a friend to play online for free. By connecting online via the Internet, players need to build their own world together in the vastness of an unknown planet. The pirates have the opportunity to jointly create a settlement, build defensive structures, design production systems, and much more. The map is presented by openworld. It can be freely explored to find additional resources to help you build more complex and complex creations.

You will play as an intelligent robot. The possibility of free online play in Mechanica gives additional scope for imagination, since all decisions are made not by one, but by several players. The gameplay allows you to work together to build a village and automate it. In total, up to 8 people can work on the creation of defensive towers. Construction robots improve the defense of the territory with the help of numerous devices that allow them to hold back the onslaught from a hostile world and aggressive robots.

The gameplay allows you to use a visual programming system to create a chain of interaction between buildings. Allows you to create a special trigger to activate the flamethrower. You can also use hotkeys to quickly activate a specific element or quickly lock a base. The use of logic and creativity is welcome!Mechanica multiplayer allows you to: build your own base and strengthen existing buildings; create advanced defenses to resist robots; set up automated production systems; use advanced visual programming mechanics; play in a company of up to 8 people.
