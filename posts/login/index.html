Game Description#

Log.in is about diving into the mind of a criminal who has to face the consequences of his own actions. The narrative suggests taking on the role of an antagonist directly involved in the case of illegally detaining a person in complete isolation from the outside world. The central mystery becomes the identity of the woman being held captive. We have to find answers to questions about the reasons for her imprisonment, as well as understand the motives that prompt the protagonist to try to eliminate her. All events begin to develop rapidly from the moment you first enter the operating system, and the ending of the story promises to radically change the fate of both the protagonist and his captive.

The entire process of immersing yourself in the history of Log.in is based solely on interaction with the personal computer interface. Assembling a complete picture of what is happening is carried out through a scrupulous study of scattered files hidden in the depths of sterile and cold system folders. Among the sources of information are not only images, videos and email correspondence, but also various applications with text notes. In Login, the main emphasis is on gradually building up suspense. The feeling of anxiety is enhanced not by abrupt moments, but by a quiet and methodical analysis of the content. Hidden inside these catalogs are disturbing records and documents that serve as direct evidence of the crime committed.
