Game Description#

Webstore Mogul is an opportunity to build your own commercial empire in the field of online trading from scratch. The project invites you to dive into the intricacies of running an e-business, where every aspect of activity directly affects the final result. One of the central mechanics is price formation, which requires constant analysis of market conditions and consumer demand. Incorrectly set costs lead to either loss of customers or lack of profit, forcing a constant search for the ideal balance between profitability and customer loyalty. Initial steps include choosing a name for the store and determining its product category, which lays the foundation for future development.

An equally important task is the effective management of warehouse inventory, including timely ordering of goods and optimization of storage space. As the turnover at Webstore Mogul grows, there is a need to hire staff capable of taking on routine operations. An interactive order packaging system adds extra depth to the shopping experience. The visual design of the site also plays a significant role, allowing you to customize its elements to increase its attractiveness in the eyes of potential customers.

Management tasks in business:

Pricing - analysis of the market and demand to set the optimal cost of goods. Warehouse logistics - organizing the storage of products and timely expansion of space. Recruitment - hiring employees to automate operational processes. Web design is the visual customization of an online store to attract customers.
