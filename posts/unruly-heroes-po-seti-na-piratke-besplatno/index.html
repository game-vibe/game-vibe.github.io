Game Description#

Unruly Heroes demonstrates a non-standard setting to users, allowing them to play online with a friend on a pirate for one of several characters. Here, the harmony of the world was disrupted when the sacred scroll was torn and scattered throughout the world. Dangerous creatures have scattered across the world, wreaking havoc and intimidating civilians. And only brave heroes are able to restore balance by defeating their enemies. So, gamers can choose the monkey king, the monk Sanzang, the pig Kihong or the thug Sandmonk. Each of them knows kung fu and is ready to crush opponents in any number, and users can also do this online online.

In terms of gameplay, Unruly Heroes is a classic platformer and has all the specific features. Characters move through two-dimensional locations, designed in different settings. These could be dungeons, highlands, forests, or even other worlds. The pace of passage is constantly changing, because the circumstances that an online game throws at users are always different. At one moment it is monotonous battles with opponents, and after 5 minutes it is an escape from a huge sea monster that devours everything that falls into its mouth. As for the combat system, it is quite varied and allows you to use combinations of more than 20 movements. Combo attacks have a unique animation, and depending on which character the user controls, the fighting style will also change. The possibility of free online play allows you not only to go through the story campaign, but also to arrange battles in PvP mode.
