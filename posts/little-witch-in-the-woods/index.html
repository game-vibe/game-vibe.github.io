Game Description#

Little Witch in the Woods allows you to plunge into the life of a girl - an apprentice witch who goes to explore a strange forest, helping its inhabitants. The adventure game has a deep storyline, pixel graphics, a variety of activities and the opportunity to spend interesting leisure time.

The heroine is Ellie, who learns magic. The young protagonist lives in the Witch House, in the world of LUCEREIN ORTU. Here, future sorcerers have various Houses and receive various tasks. The girl must help the residents of the nearby village, and after completing training she will become a real witch. The user’s task is to help the ward pass all the tests in order to achieve the goal. In the process, the witch practices the knowledge she has acquired, helps people by doing good deeds. The witch makes friends with various characters who live in the village, learning the history of each of them. Ellie also needs to collect various resources and work with materials, thanks to which she can create potions needed to solve problems.

As the player progresses, he explores a fantasy world, discovers different areas, meets local residents, interacts with them and bizarre creatures, and for certain achievements receives items necessary for advancement. Here you can also make friends, and by giving them a gift, you can get closer and even learn secrets. The protagonist moves with the cat and is able to communicate with other representatives of the feline species that live here. It soon turns out that the other cats are also separate individuals, and differ not only in appearance. From time to time, the character is able to relax by going fishing to gain strength before a new adventure.
