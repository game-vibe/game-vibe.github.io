Game Description#

No Straight Roads Encore Edition is a rhythm game where you can create a group with a friend and other players. For free on the pirate site, you need to assemble your own rock band in order to displace electronic music from the world charts. Multiplayer is available online online. The network game is aimed at cooperation and confrontation, allowing you to challenge members of other teams.

Events take place in a city called Vinyl City. Local rock musicians are fighting the EDM empire under the auspices of NSR. No Straight Roads Encore Edition is an expanded edition of the original game, containing more tracks, more fan art, and much more.

Featuring a variety of dynamic tracks from the likes of Falk Au Yeong, Andy Tunstall, Funk Fiction, Masahiro “Godspeed” Aoki, Az Samad, Clyde Rabatel and Video Game Orchestra. You can challenge musicians of different genres: boy band, club DJ, pianist, jazzman and many others. At the top there should be only rock!Bonus edition content: new remixes and additional tracks; redrawn introductory video; 500 art and posters for collection; New Year’s special in the form of music and costumes; the possibility of free online play; improved visual effects, sound and much more.
