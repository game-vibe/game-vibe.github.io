Game Description#

WW2 Rebuilder is a first-person simulator that allows you to appreciate the destructive scale of World War II. You have to take on the role of someone who is trying to put a European city in order after the fighting is over. The streets are in ruins. It’s even difficult to say that beauty and grandeur once reigned here.

However, using physical strength, heavy equipment and many other tools, you can completely restore your hometown. It is necessary to collect bricks, clear rubble, restore house facades, restore walls and do everything possible to return people to their normal lives. The gameplay is a real noble act, carrying in itself a love of peace and humanism.

Rebuilding Europe from the ashes of chaos is very difficult. But with due effort and perseverance, you can not only return European streets to their former appearance, but also make them better! Now is the time for new heroes - restorers. By joining forces with civilians, megacities are gradually acquiring their usual features. Houses, streets, factories, stadiums, etc. are being restored.

The events of WW2 Rebuilder will unfold in several cities, one of which is London, which was terribly damaged during the bombing of The Blitz. By clearing debris, undestroyed objects can be reused. For example, if the brick turns out to be intact, then there is no point in purchasing a new one, because you can reuse it. By clearing the city of garbage and clearing away the rubble, you can find many valuable things. By laying the railways, connections between cities are restored, allowing tourists to come and visit. Streets, roofs, sidewalks and parks - all of this needs to be restored to its former beauty and grandeur.
