Game Description#

The team-tactical shooterRavenfield will allow you to plunge into furious battles, where you have everything - from tanks to fleets. There is a complete lack of storyline and even multiplayer. The latter fact is extremely unusual for projects of this genre. It would seem that it is interesting to play against bots and when there are no real users on the map. This omission at first glance is more than compensated for by a fairly developed artificial intelligence - opponents and allies behave extremely intelligently and together with them you can perform a variety of tactics using numerous commands and options.

At the very beginning, anyone can configure the number of bots, the landing point, select a map, and it’s time to join the battle. And this is where the fun begins in Ravenfield. There are two modes in total - capture points and battalions. In the first, it all comes down to controlling areas, and in the second, there is simply a total slaughter.

Initially, everyone starts as infantrymen, but later there is the opportunity to transfer to such equipment as a tank, aircraft carrier, helicopter, mortar boat, airplane and much more. The developers also took a very responsible approach to working on weapons. Each firearm is unique in its own way. For example, there are faster-firing guns, but they do not have such high damage and poor accuracy of fire. More damaging and deadly accurate ones, in turn, take a long time to recharge.
