Game Description#

Of Life and Land provides users with the opportunity to try themselves in the colonization of lands, creating small settlements. Gamers will be engaged in expanding their own regions and trading with neighboring factions. A full-fledged simulation is taking place in the world, where it is necessary to erect buildings and provide the villagers with work, gradually putting the town in order. It is necessary to extract valuable resources in order to erect the desired buildings and improve the quality of life for the subordinate people.

When engaging in urban planning in the Church of Kerzoven, players need to:

Take care of citizens, as well as create production lines for collecting and extracting resources. Work through the economy, acquiring and distributing funds for the development of the city. Trade your own goods, sending cargo to neighboring villages. Encounter wildlife and interact with the environment.

Each character in the game will live in a house, go to work and have needs for food, water and rest. Some people will form social groups to find themselves and identify with similar individuals. Users will be able to achieve a balance between nature and human activity, which will gradually destroy it or use it for their own benefit. The seasons will change in the game, which will affect the natural world and the difficulty of survival for the nascent civilization.
