Game Description#

EvoCreo is a tactical role-playing story set in the vast world of Zenith, filled with diverse biomes and powerful creatures. The path to becoming a true Master Spellcaster lies through confronting elite rivals, ancient legends, and even entire criminal organizations. The protagonist’s main goal is to find his missing father, but this investigation quickly draws him into uncovering a sinister conspiracy associated with the mysterious organization Shadow Hive. Traveling across the continent will require not only strength, but also strategic thinking to overcome all challenges.

The core of the gameplay is collecting and developing more than 170 unique creatures called Creos. Each creature has multiple evolution paths, which allows you to form invincible squads with unique sets of skills. Battles in EvoCreo are turn-based battles where success depends on many factors. In addition to the single-player campaign, which lasts for dozens of hours, there is an opportunity to test your skills in online battles with other players. To achieve victory you must:

competently manage the recovery time of abilities. use status effects to control the battlefield. consider the strengths and weaknesses of different types of creatures. Monitor the stamina level of each team member.
