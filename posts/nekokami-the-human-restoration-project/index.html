Game Description#

Nekokami - The Human Restoration Project places the player in a future space where cats rule the universe. Here you will play the role of a Planetary Architect, an employee of Nekokami Co., tasked with constructing entire worlds and managing them in numerous star systems. The key mechanic is the strategic placement of tiles to form different environments, from pristine natural biomes to urban settlements needed for a reborn humanity.

Such actions further the narrative of Nekokami - The Human Restoration Project, which focuses on the dynamics between the ruling felines and humans, restored after a long period of extinction. Each new star system offers unique rules and conditions, requiring adaptation to successfully develop territories. Progress is not limited to just construction, requiring interaction with the fundamental forces of the universe through entities called Kami.

This game sets the gamer the task of finding and winning the favor of various spirits that personify nature and the cosmos itself. It is these beings who provide the Creative Energy that fuels any creation, so their approval becomes a necessary component for further development. By building these relationships, you can reveal the secret laws of the universe and gain access to new opportunities. However, Kami bring an element of surprise to the gameplay, triggering events that can either help or complicate the architect’s job, from small miracles to large-scale disasters.
