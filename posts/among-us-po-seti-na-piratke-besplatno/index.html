Game Description#

You can playAmong Us online as a pirate either with a friend or in a large group of people to jointly identify the traitor. An online Internet connection is available with the participation of 4 to 10 players simultaneously joining the match for free. The gameplay is based on the Mafia principle. The free online game Among Us features pit crew members against traitors. Among the entire team there are impostors who set up frames and sabotage, as well as kill other players. The network game lasts until the Impostors, who are also traitors, eliminate the entire squad, or the team identifies the opponents.

Events take place in space, where a huge spaceship has to be explored. The crew performs a list of various tasks in parallel. If they manage to solve all the puzzles, the team of traitors loses. After each round, you can hold a vote to eliminate one of the squad members. Victory is counted if all Impostors are eliminated. If the corpse of one of the participants is discovered, an urgent competition is held, where possible criminals are identified. Everyone must convince his group that he did not commit the murder. The person being expelled is thrown into space, lava, or thrown off a cliff.

The deceased can continue the game as a ghost. He has access to all functions except voting and communicating with other players. Traitors are capable of causing mischief and sabotage: turning off the lights, locking the cabins, disrupting the communication system, triggering the destruction of the reactor, reducing the amount of oxygen, etc. If some breakdowns are not eliminated within a minute, then the Impostors are awarded victory.
