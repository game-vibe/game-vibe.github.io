Game Description#

Sea Of Craft - sea battles with sandbox elements that allow you to fight with a friend or players from all over the world, upgrading your ship and installing advanced weapons on it. You can play online in co-op, collecting a whole fleet. All online content is available for free on the pirate site, giving you the opportunity to fight both online and locally on the Internet.

The gameplay of Sea Of Craft is based on physical laws. Each object has its own weight and properties. More than a hundred materials for construction and crafting are available. Multiplayer allows you to explore many different levels with your partners, fighting against opponents. The free online play option includes leaderboards, recording statistics and achievements of all participants. You can challenge other ships, becoming a pirate of the entire planet Bira.

Each player takes on the role of captain, designing their own unique vessel. The locations are represented by three biomes: a desert oasis, a huge archipelago and an endless ocean. A wide variety of accessories and materials are available, such as decks, pipes, weapons, paint and more. Participants can exchange drawings and express compliments, as well as share experiences. Competitions are represented by Sea Drift, Hook Control, Homeless Crocodile, etc. Depending on the competition, the gameplay and victory conditions change. You must take part in treasure hunts, ship building, sea battles, races, fishing and many other competitions both on land and in the ocean.
