Game Description#

Mad Games Tycoon 2 is a tycoon that takes you back to 1976, when the first game studios began to appear. Build your own studio and start developing games. Rent offices, install server rooms and improve production facilities. Try to develop the game of your dreams or create a next-generation console. Start your journey from a small garage company and grow into one of the most powerful corporations on the planet. Improve the lives of your employees by decorating your offices with decorative items and purchasing new equipment. Attract the most experienced and talented employees in the industry. Beware of heat and pollution, which will significantly affect the speed and pace of development of your masterpieces.

Your task is extremely simple: you need to become the best computer game developer in the world. Mad Games Tycoon 2 gives you unlimited opportunities to develop your interactive masterpieces. Create simple text adventures and complex AAA-class MMOs. Choose the platform you want to release on: exclusive to consoles or PC? Or do you want to reach all users at once and release a multi-platform? The choice is yours. To establish your dominance in the market, you can develop a console that becomes the best-selling device in the market. Develop a game engine, selling it to other developers and even your competitors. Influence the industry and survive economic crises. Will you be able to take a leading position in and establish your position in the market?
