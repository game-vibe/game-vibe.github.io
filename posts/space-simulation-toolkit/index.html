Game Description#

Space Simulation Toolkit is a physics sandbox that offers a unique approach to simulating worlds using your graphics card as a supercomputer. This game goes beyond the traditional experience, allowing users to manipulate the laws of physics and create complex worlds with living creatures. This is a platform for experiments - from the development of artificial intelligence to the design of mechanisms and simulation of various physical environments.

With the right hardware, the title turns your system into a powerhouse, making gameplay more dynamic and challenging as the graphics card’s power increases. The title relies on a fundamental building block - atoms. These atoms can form bonds, creating spatial structures that determine the physical properties of the world. Thanks to the flexibility of material properties, you can model everything from gases and liquids to soft and solid bodies.

This wide range of physical behavior opens up endless possibilities for creating realistic or completely fantastical environments. As a result, the Space Simulation Toolkit offers plenty of room to experiment with chemistry, states of matter, and more. One of the intriguing features is the Protobiome simulator, which allows users to create a living virtual world from scratch. It is a system based on evolutionary principles, where players manipulate elements such as light, water and earth to shape the development of life.
