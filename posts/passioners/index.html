Game Description#

PASSIONERS is a visual novel, the action of which takes place on board a spaceship setting off to colonize a distant planet. Having woken up prematurely from cryosleep, the main character finds himself isolated on a ship where women are kept in numerous cryopods. The mission has gone off course, and now the main character must decide which women to awaken and how to interact with them. The dynamics of the emerging relationship and the direction of plot development will depend on the choice made.

PASSIONERS is a series of solutions, with each woman having unique characteristics and biography. Users will have to choose who to awaken from cryosleep, because each interaction has its own consequences. As the main character builds a relationship, they will take different paths, exploring themes of romance, trust and intimacy. Each woman has her own desires, and the gamer has to decide how these desires will be fulfilled.

There is also a pregnancy system included, where every intimate encounter carries with it the possibility of pregnancy. This adds an extra layer of complexity to the choice, as the consequences of each action can have long-term consequences. Managing these relationships and subsequent pregnancies becomes a central aspect of the gameplay. As they progress, users will encounter explicit scenes that can be viewed in the video gallery.
