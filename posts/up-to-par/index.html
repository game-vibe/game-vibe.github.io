Game Description#

Up to Par is more than just minigolf! Take on procedurally generated levels with up to 16 friends or alone, but remember that every hit counts. Stay below par to avoid being eliminated from the game! You will have to show your skills and planning to progress as far as possible and unlock the next course. Can you beat your record? Will you be able to defeat your friends?

Each level is procedurally generated, so you’ll have to face new challenges every time you try. Buy new features to improve your chances of success. Unlock abilities to start your next attempts with new advantages. The further you progress in the game, the more courses you unlock. Each course is unique, with new challenges for you to overcome. Play in co-op mode with up to 4 players, sharing your resources and working together to get as far as possible on each attempt. Compete in competitive mode with up to 16 participants and fight for the title of champion!
