Game Description#

Horror adventureEscape The Ayuwoki Horror Night includes co-op play with a friend, allowing you to play online for free as a pirate to take on the monster together. Alone or together with partners, you will explore procedurally generated locations, solving puzzles, finding key objects and hiding from the monster.

The network game is represented by an asymmetric type of multiplayer. A group of survivors must face one strong enemy. You can play from first and third person. Escape The Ayuwoki Horror Night takes place in an old, gloomy mansion. The possibility of free online play allows you to customize your character, choosing the hero’s hairstyle, skin color, outfit and much more. Several participants take on the role of teenage survivors, while another player controls a monster called Ayuvoki.

The monster tracks people with the help of unique abilities. For example, you can use sonar or echolocation. You will also need to scatter the biomass, creating a sticky substance that the teenagers will stick to. Using the ultimate skill, it is necessary to execute the humans. Possessing enormous strength, you will need to knock down doors or send an army of bloodthirsty rats. Playing as children, you need to complete various tasks that will help you resist the monster and escape from the mansion. Using the experience points you receive, you need to unlock new abilities, skins, and various cosmetic items.
