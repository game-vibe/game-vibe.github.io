Game Description#

Wild Growth is an incremental action game in which the main goal is to maintain the combat effectiveness of the squad in the harsh world of Scandinavian myths. All responsibility for the survival of the allies falls on the shoulders of the protagonist, acting as a healer. The process of restoring the health of teammates is carried out through the use of healing spells activated by mouse clicks. The outcome of any clash with the enemy directly depends on the timeliness of these actions. An important aspect is the ability to experiment with different combat formations on the battlefield, and to strengthen the group, it is possible to hire new warriors.

Squad development elements:

Investing earned resources in the improvement tree to unlock bonuses and abilities. Gradual replenishment of the squad with fighters such as fire mages and protected warriors. Selecting combat formations to gain a tactical advantage on the battlefield. Returning to base after a defeat to regroup and prepare for a new battle.

Defeat in a single battle does not mean the final loss of the war, providing a chance to return to base to regroup. Wild Growth has an extensive development system that allows you to invest earned resources into strengthening your abilities. Exploring the upgrade tree opens up access to new features and hidden bonuses, which motivates further battles. Over time, the squad can be replenished with specialized fighters such as fire mages and well-protected warriors who can withstand significant damage. This repeating cycle of combat and subsequent improvement forms the basis of all gameplay.
