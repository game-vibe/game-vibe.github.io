Game Description#

Witchfire suggests using not only weapons in the process, but also various devices in order to survive after an encounter with monsters. An arsenal consisting of dozens of different items and a variety of enemies will help gamers realize their own shooting skills. Challenge the most dangerous monsters that live in this world.

Here it is important to select the right type of weapon in accordance with the situation and type of enemy. The game environment was created on the principle of photogrammetry, where the objects in the shooter were scanned from real ones. The mechanics of scanning 3D objects made it possible to achieve maximum realism. The horror is linear, but with the ability to explore the game’s expanses at any time, which allows you to find a variety of options for killing the enemy.

Dark fantasy shootouts invite the user to use more than 10 types of animated weapons with which to destroy the enemy controlled by AI. The gameplay corresponds to a first-person shooter, but with an emphasis on constantly improving the skills of the gamer himself as a shooter who learns to overcome difficult obstacles. The story here is relegated to the background, and the task can be completed in one of the many available ways that the user finds himself in the process.
