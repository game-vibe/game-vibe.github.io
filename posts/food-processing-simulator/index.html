Game Description#

Food Processing Simulator is an economic strategy, the central task of which is the construction and subsequent administration of your own food processing plant. Achieving success will require finding the optimal balance between equipment productivity, logical layout of premises and scrupulous inventory control. The basis of the gameplay is the establishment of conveyor lines and the gradual automation of all work processes, from the processing of initial ingredients to the final stage of packaging of finished products. The gamer will need strategic thinking to optimize all stages of the production cycle.

The whole journey begins with a modest workshop, which is destined to transform into a large-scale industrial complex. To ensure smooth functioning in Food Processing Simulator, it is necessary not only to modernize machines and master advanced technologies, but also to competently coordinate the actions of hired employees. Constant physical expansion of production facilities is becoming an urgent need to handle increasingly larger orders and increase capacity. Thus, every management task, from the purchase of raw materials to the shipment of batches, becomes part of a single mechanism for building a prosperous food empire.
