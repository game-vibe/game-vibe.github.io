Game Description#

Emperator Rome is a strategy game that gives you the opportunity to lead your people in a very turbulent time for Europe. You can fight alone against AI, or play online online, or connect locally. You can play online with a friend in the versus mode or random battles. On a pirate, it is possible to form alliances to exchange resources or receive useful items from a companion for free. Together with the great commanders, you will have to follow the path of Alexander the Great, found the Roman Empire and bring peace to the lands.

The strategy includes many mechanics: collecting resources, building a base, training units, tactical battles with the enemy army, etc. Free online play available and Punic Wars content added. Many historical figures appear: Alexander the Great, Hannibal, Caesar and all those who decided the fate of Europe BC. Everyone living then, be it powerful kings, clever generals and self-proclaimed gods, all left their mark on the formation of the Mediterranean. Players have the chance to change history, or stick to the pages of history books. Features of Imperator Rome: many characters with their own skills and characteristics; command of the army and navy; a huge variety of cultures and religions; tactical and large-scale battles; trade with neighboring powers; network play and single player mode.

All settlers, whether citizens, free peoples, slaves, etc., adhere to their traditions and worship their god. It is necessary to satisfy their requests in order to allow the empire to flourish. Each culture has its own approach to warfare and a unique battle strategy. You should be wary of barbarian nomads - wild warriors who bring confusion and chaos. They can brutally kill citizens and steal from homes. The economic side is represented by the management of the Republican Senate, which makes it possible to find allies and create entire clans.
