Game Description#

InEnemy Territory: Quake Wars it is possible to play online with a friend for free on a pirate account. You can connect online over the Internet or fight over a local network. The gameplay is based on the class system. Free online play allows two opposing sides to take control of the human army of the GDF and the Strogg, an alien race that has invaded Earth.

Each faction has fighters represented by 5 classes. For humans, these are the Soldier, Medic, Engineer, Artilleryman and Scout, while the Stroggs fight with the participation of the Aggressor, Technician, Constructor, Destructor and Infiltrator. Depending on the choice of fighter, the style of play and battle tactics change. Each character in Enemy Territory: Quake Wars not only fights with an individual weapon, but also performs a unique function: fighting his way forward, healing teammates, setting up turrets, laying mines, etc. The online game is aimed at confronting two races, where fighters fight with explosives, machine guns, shotguns, machine guns, grenade launchers and many other types of guns.

The essence of the confrontation comes down to the destruction or protection of the main goals. In some modes there is a timer. Often the parties switch tasks and alternate between attacking and defending. There are secondary missions that bring additional experience points not only to the individual player, but to the entire team. Bonus points can be spent on leveling up and improving your character, as well as on purchasing new weapons and additional equipment. Improvements are available throughout one campaign, consisting of three unique maps. This applies to both multiplayer and single-player campaigns.
