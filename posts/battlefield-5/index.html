Game Description#

Battlefield 5 is a first-person shooter, the fifteenth game in the Battlefield series. Unlike previous games in the series, Battlefield 5 sacrifices historical accuracy for the sake of the player: weapon handling, for example, has been noticeably improved, making the game more like an arcade shooter. Most of the changes made to BFV are solid improvements over its predecessor.

War Stories is a series of single-player missions introduced in Battlefield V, where the player is invited to become an eyewitness to tragic events. You’ll be introduced to three stories set in lesser-known corners of World War II. Missions are open-ended, with multiple objectives that can be completed in any order.

Detailed changes not only bring excitement to the gameplay, but also add to the mix an appropriately brutal depiction of war. Depleting or limited resources to operate adds a new dynamic to the franchise. Each of the four available classes - Recon, Assault, Medic and Support - plays a different role to ensure squad members stay alive and ammo never runs out.

Sandbox mode surprises you with unexpected moments of player freedom: Battlefield is a series known for its destruction, but now you can build. Any class can build without you having to gather resources, but the process is slow and leaves you vulnerable to attack.
