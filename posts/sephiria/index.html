Game Description#

Sephiria is a combination of equipment management, battle and research. Users manage a rabbit who is instructed to change the fate of the mystical tower standing on the verge of destruction. As they pass the procedured levels, gamers face creatures with unpredictable motives. These animals can offer help, look for it or pose a threat. The decision about who to trust and how to interact with these characters will be able to significantly affect the course of the game.

In the battle in Sephiria, special attention is paid to time, accuracy and adaptation. The user can break through the waves of enemies, block attacks and strategically use artifacts and tablets to enhance abilities. They give passive effects during acquisition, and tablets enhance the power of artifacts nearby, which contributes to their thoughtful placement. Such a multi -level approach to passing encourages experiments and planning, making each race special. The isometric view and the 2D tetle crime create compact and focused combat experience.

The equipment management plays an important role, demanding from the gamers to think about what and when to carry with you. Balancing between offensive and defensive means, the gamer should overcome dynamic clashes and unexpected difficulties. The combination of battle, collecting and customization allows you to diversify the gameplay. The multiplayer adds a social dimension, allowing users to unite in commands, exchange objects or fight in PVE scenarios.
