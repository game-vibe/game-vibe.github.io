Game Description#

Metro 2033 Redux is a first-person shooter with a deep story and horror elements. The developers questioned not the simple existence of people underground, but the relationships between them. Has the main threat always been with us, in people? In the world of the post-apocalypse, we play as a character named Artem. He grew up underground, in the Moscow metro. In 2013, the world was hit by the terrible consequences of the latest epidemic. Until 2033, the powers that be raised a new generation that must cope with the danger threatening the entire planet, bring humanity to the surface and free large cities from mutants.

The new Metro is augmented reality physics, graphics, sound and dialogues. The more we dive into the intricacies of the plot, the more it becomes clear that Artyom is a real person, a survivor; a character trying to put a nation of people on top. He does not forget that enemies can become friends if everyone unites against one problem - quarrels, evil, mutants.

The pack includes two games: Metro 2033 and Metro Last Light. Both parts were supplemented with new weapons and graphics. The developers did not touch the plot, and the addition is rightfully considered the author’s work. If in the first part we got to know this world, looked for ways to resolve conflicts and killed a lot, mercilessly, with all types of weapons, then Last Light is rather a deep, story-driven saga about people who do not want to make contact, just to remain in a world where survival I became comfortable among the monsters.

A gas mask is still available in the game in case of a gas attack, you can take a rifle and completely upgrade it, because there will be enough quests where long-range killing is the goal. The atmosphere was supplemented with the old difficulty level - ranger. Playing in a low ammo environment where there is no game interface is a real challenge!

Metro 2033, one of the most popular survival-horror games of recent years, has received a global graphics update. The Redux version received support for 4K resolution, high-quality textures, animation and lighting. For gamers familiar with the franchise, this will be a new exciting journey into an already explored world, which has become much more reminiscent of the real one. For newcomers, the remaster is a great way to get acquainted with this epic FPS.

According to the plot, humanity did not reach 2013, and was almost completely destroyed. The survivors hid in the Moscow metro, and only a few colonies remained on the surface, constantly repelling attacks from unfriendly mutants. Artyom, the protagonist of the game, witnessed an unusual event that threatens the remnants of civilization, and went on a dangerous expedition to warn others.

The gamer will have many hours of survival in a post-apocalyptic environment. He will have to use stealth and shooting skills, as well as communicate with many colorful characters. Metro 2033 is a unique project created by domestic developers, showing the Russian version of the end of the world, with its own heroes and consequences.
