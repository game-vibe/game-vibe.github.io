Game Description#

Nether: Resurrected is a multiplayer shooter set in a post-apocalyptic world. On the pirate Nether: Resurrected you can play online for free with a friend. Players fight in co-op against hordes of monsters, or confront other survivors online. The gameplay is a survival game in the open world, inviting you to explore a gloomy industrial city. Ammunition is strictly limited, and each monster poses a serious threat.

Online play and single player mode available. The fighters need to survive in the harsh world inhabited by the Beasts. The streets of the metropolis have long been in ruins, and there are almost no living people left. However, the biggest threat is not the monsters, but the survivors themselves. Free online play allows you to form factions and fight against other teams, trying to take all their valuable loot. You won’t be able to sit out at the base, since each character has hunger and thirst parameters, so the game forces you to go out into the gloomy streets to look for provisions and ammunition. TO

In addition to the city, the Wasteland is represented - a large dead zone. In Nether: Resurrected, a huge variety of tasks are available to find resources, protect shelters, clear areas, etc. By completing missions, you accumulate experience points that can be spent on improvements and leveling up. You can engage in a variety of different types of crafts, be it trading, crafting, etc. By discovering new types of guns, they can be completely customized.

Before starting the game, you create your character by choosing the parameters of appearance and skills. A huge variety of monsters are presented, including a boss nicknamed the Reaper. All heroes have anti-Nether devices that need to be repaired. In total, up to 64 people can participate in multiplayer battles on one map. Who to trust and who is the enemy? Everyone decides for themselves.
