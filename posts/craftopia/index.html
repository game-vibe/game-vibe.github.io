Game Description#

Craftopia is a multiplayer action game with survival and role-playing game elements. The gameplay is based on hunting, farming, exploring territories, collecting resources, crafting, building and fighting a large number of enemies. The creators of Craftopia have combined many mechanics from a variety of video games to maximize gameplay variety.

You have a large open world to explore. Craftopia consists of many diverse biomes: green meadows and plains, mountains, rivers and lakes, dense forests and much more. Chop trees, mine stones and build a base. The basis of survival is water and drink. Agriculture helps not only to obtain huge amounts of food, but also to sell crops profitably. With the money you earn, you can buy new weapons and equipment. You can grow 20 different types of vegetables.

You can automate your harvesting processes by purchasing a variety of equipment. You can engage in full-fledged hunting for both wild animals and dangerous monsters. Tame the local fauna using a special device with which you can catch the animal. The main character has the ability to use magic and various spells.

If you approach a dangerous place, the detector will begin to vibrate. This will make it clear that enemies and other dangers lie ahead. When you go down to clear the dungeons, you will not only fight opponents, but collect treasures. At the end of each dungeon there is a boss fight. Among the third-party activities, fishing is represented. You can fish in any body of water, but to do this you will need to get a fishing rod. From the collected materials you can craft hoverboards, motorcycles, helicopters, cars, biplanes, tanks, balloons and other vehicles. The skill tree includes about 100 skills. Before the adventure begins, a character editor is available, where you can select gender, race and appearance. Multiplayer is available for fans of cooperative adventures.
