Game Description#

Otherworld Legends is a roguelike that you can play online with a friend, fighting a huge number of enemies for free on a pirate. Over the Internet, online or locally, partners will have to descend into procedurally generated dungeons. The possibility of free online play is built on the fact that players, controlling heroes, explore the mysterious world of Asura and try to unravel all its ancient secrets. The gameplay combines RPG elements with complex beat-em-up combat.

The setting is a mixture of the dark Middle Ages and frosty Scandinavia. There are elements of Persian exoticism and fantasy. For example, in Otherworld Legends you can encounter dragons. The network game offers each participant in the adventure to individually upgrade their character. You can be a kung fu master, an experienced swordsman, or a tough Viking. There is a charming elf and other races of heroes. Each protagonist has their own backstory and a special set of skills. To fight opponents more effectively, you need to combine collected items and abilities. At levels you can find power-ups and special equipment. By combining the abilities of their partners, the squad fights Asuradian demons and undead more effectively. Many gameplay elements, including locations, enemy locations and loot, are randomly generated.
