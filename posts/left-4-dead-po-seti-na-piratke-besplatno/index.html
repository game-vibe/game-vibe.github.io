Game Description#

Left 4 Dead is a multiplayer zombie shooter that allows you to play pirate games online with a friend, teaming up to four people online for free. The survivors fight against the horde of the Infected. Several campaigns are presented, consisting of several levels. The main task is to get to the evacuation zone. Between levels you need to barricade yourself in shelters to replenish your health and ammo. It is necessary to treat with painkillers and first aid kits.

Left 4 Dead’s gameplay stands out because it randomizes enemy locations and randomly generates crowds of zombies. There are 4 modes available: single player campaign, multiplayer, battle and survival. The possibility of free online play allows you to take on the role of one of four heroes: William Overbeck (aka Bill), Francis, Zoe and Louis. Before the adventure begins, a difficulty level is selected, including easy, normal, master and expert. Friendly fire is disabled on high difficulties. The battle divides the participants into two teams of 4 people and compete with each other. One faction takes on the role of people, and the second - special infected.

The heroes fight with the usual walking dead, as well as unique monsters such as the Fat Man, the Hunter, the Smoker, the Tank and the Witch. Survival mode relies on panic events that require you to survive. If you hold out for four to ten minutes, you can get 3 medals: bronze, silver and gold. Story campaigns feature scenarios such as No Mercy, Doom Flight, Death Knell, Death in the Air, Bloody Harvest, Sacrifice and the Last Stand. Events take place in different locations. In the final stages, you must perform a special action to evacuate the doomed city.
