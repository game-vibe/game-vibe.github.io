Game Description#

Anode Heart is a monster taming RPG set in a futuristic digital environment. This universe is characterized by the interweaving of physical and virtual reality, where the boundaries between them have become almost indistinguishable. Gamers travel through this semi-open space, immersing themselves in its plot and mechanics. The gameplay is focused on exploring its vastness. The story in Anode Heart is about Sika, a robot who wakes up unconscious near a metal capsule on a foggy coast. While exploring Stack Island, Sik enters into an alliance with the Tama, creatures that originated on the Network and have recently begun to appear in the Overworld. The main goal is to establish the identity of Sika and, with the support of Tam, prevent the approaching disaster.

The title offers about 140 creatures for the collection, capable of mastering techniques from an arsenal of 300 techniques for use in a battle system focused on fast-paced duels. Trading card fun serves as an alternative form of entertainment. The universe includes 20 Overworld areas for travel and approximately 40 characters available for recruitment. The restart mechanic promotes the development of creatures. Tama have a limit level; their rollback to Level 1 increases the pumping ceiling and their total Potential. When they reach the previous peak of development again, the parameters of the wards will increase relative to the previous state, which creates a cycle for the improvement of these creatures.
