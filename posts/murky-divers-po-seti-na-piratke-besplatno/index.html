Game Description#

PlayMurky Divers online, diving into the murky depths of co-op diving horror for free. With a pirate friend, you can explore underwater laboratories online online. In this multiplayer game, you become elite cleanup specialists tasked with erasing the remains of Pharma Corps' disastrous underwater experiments.

Your submarine serves as a base camp, taking you to various abandoned laboratories scattered along the ocean floor. Teamwork is extremely important. One team controls the submarine using sonar and onboard stations, navigating the treacherous depths blind. Meanwhile, a team of divers descends on the sunken ships and, using their boats, recovers the bodies and any evidence.

But be careful, these wrecks are far from peaceful resting places. Oxygen is running out faster than expected, and in the abyss lurks unprecedented horrors, ready to extinguish your light and your life. Each corpse or piece of evidence you bring back will earn you credits, which you can use to upgrade your equipment and improve your chances of survival on subsequent dives. Just remember that in the murky depths, death is a constant companion, and the only way out is to rise. Think you have what it takes? Dive into Murky Divers and find out, but don’t forget to take courage - thalassophobia (fear of the deep sea) is included with the game for free.
