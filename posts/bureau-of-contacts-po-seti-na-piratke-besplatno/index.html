Game Description#

Play online inBureau of Contacts immersing you in the chilling world of ghost hunting, but with a twist. On the Internet online you will encounter not only creepy ghosts, but also the haunted habitats themselves - creepy environments with their own dangers. Teamwork is key here, with up to four players teaming up to overcome these dual threats. You can play with a friend even on a pirated platform and enjoy this online game completely free of charge.

Gone are the days of predictable ghost behavior. Bureau of Contacts uses creepy artificial intelligence that adapts the ghost’s actions and tactics based on your words and actions. Tease him at your own risk - he may become unpleasant. Remember, these aren’t your average video game ghosts - they’re smart and they listen.

The fun doesn’t end there. Each playthrough of the game offers a unique environment to explore, and various cursed locations keep you on your toes. There are many ghostly enemies in the game, each with their own character. Want to play the game alone? Make no mistake, if you go into battle with a team, you can join forces and develop a common strategy. But be careful - ghosts aren’t the only ones you’ll have to worry about. The game features several clever traps set by both the ghosts and the haunted environment itself.

Bureau of Contacts is not only a jump scare, but also a deep game for those who love to strategize. By studying ghosts and other paranormal phenomena, you will learn their weaknesses and how to exploit them. The game gives you a variety of tools to work with, from mystical spells and exorcism rituals to good old-fashioned investigation and protective equipment. Each playthrough of the game promises a unique experience thanks to the ever-changing behavior of the ghosts and a collection system that keeps things fresh.
