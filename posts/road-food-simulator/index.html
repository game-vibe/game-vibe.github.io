Game Description#

Road Food Simulator puts the player directly in charge of running a roadside pizzeria - a task that can be completed independently or with partners in online co-op mode. Activities are focused on preparing pizza and processing customer orders within tight deadlines; The retention of visitors and the volume of new contracts depend on this. The funds earned go directly to the development and improvement of the establishment, including its equipment.

The results of work are determined by the debugging of processes and the level of service, which is reflected in the prosperity of the business. This guide covers all stages of delivery. In the Road Food Simulator title, gamers can join ongoing gaming sessions with others or start a new pizzeria franchise from the very beginning, gradually expanding it. Creating a pizza begins with preparing the dough, on which the required ingredients are then placed according to the customers' instructions.

The essence of this project is initially designed for joint actions, allowing different team members to take on clearly defined roles: for example, preparing the dish itself, packaging it, or delivering the food to the client. Such a division of responsibilities can optimize the entire service cycle, from receiving an application to delivering it. This catering establishment operates on the principle of a fast food establishment, so the speed of pizza preparation becomes the main task for everyone involved in the process. If cooking takes longer than the established time, visitors will express dissatisfaction and leave the premises without food, which will affect profits.
