Game Description#

Fickle Card Legend is a role-playing project whose gameplay is based on an unusual fusion of card strategy and side-scrolling battles in real time. The protagonist is invited to master one of three classic archetypes: Warrior, Mage or Taoist. Leveling up gives you access to powerful skills, while collecting equipment and upgrading your deck allows you to create your own personal fighting style to face greater threats. The character development process is reminiscent of multiplayer projects, but without their disadvantages. All rewards for defeating bosses go exclusively to the hero, and the absence of hostile interaction with other users guarantees a safe adventure.

Battles in Fickle Card Legend take place in 2D arenas and eschew the traditional turn-based system. Instead of directly casting spells, cards here materialize into full-fledged fighters who independently engage in battle with opponents. Success depends on the right choice of moment and proper positioning, because to defeat enemies you need to:

summon warriors to the battlefield in a timely manner. activate special abilities to seize the initiative. effectively manage the tempo of the collision. think through the placement of units to gain tactical superiority.
