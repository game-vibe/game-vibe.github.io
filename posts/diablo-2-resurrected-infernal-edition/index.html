Game Description#

Diablo 2: Resurrected Infernal Edition is a massive expansion of the iconic universe, combining the classic story with all-new content from the Reign of the Warlock expansion. Central to the narrative is the appearance of the Warlock, capable of turning the dark forces of the underworld against the demons themselves. This hero’s arsenal allows him not only to exterminate evil spirits, but also to subdue hellish creatures, turning them into obedient puppets during battles. Traveling through the lands of Sanctuary now supports cooperative interaction for eight characters, where competent synergy of skills becomes the only guarantee of survival in conditions of eternal conflict.

The technical component of the project has undergone significant changes aimed at increasing comfort when managing inventory and sorting production. The introduction of a custom item filter and specialized stash tabs for runes and materials eliminates routine micromanagement. The original Diablo II: Resurrected – Infernal Edition also introduced a Chronicle system that tracks all artifacts found at the account level and rewards collectors with unique bonuses. For veterans, there are more difficult challenges in the updated Terror Zones and a final battle with the Colossal Ancients boss.

Magic specializations of the new class:

Demon - summoning and enslaving creatures of hell to create a personal army of minions. Eldritch - enchant physical weapons with mental magic to cause devastating damage. Chaos - remote control of the elements of fire and darkness to destroy clusters of enemies.
