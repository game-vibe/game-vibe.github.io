Game Description#

Sun Haven is a farming simulator that you can play online with a friend or compete with random people online. The gameplay allows you to build relationships with townspeople, explore territories for magic, monsters and dragons. For free, up to 8 people can team up at the same time on the pirate Sun Haven. Events take place in the medieval town of San Haven.

Each player can become whoever they want. Many professions are represented: farmer, miner, fisherman, warrior, artisan, magician, etc. By collecting resources, you need to upgrade your farm and make new friends in the neighborhood. Not only is there the possibility of free online play, but also a story quest is available that will introduce you to the inhabitants of the elven city of Nel’Vari and the monster city Withergate. In each city you will face a powerful boss. Features and capabilities of the project: network play for up to 8 people; exploration of three unique worlds; 7 races: Human, Demon, Elf, Angel, Elemental, Naga and Amar; many professions: digger, blacksmith, cook, warrior, etc. d.; more than 200 customization options; 15 characters for romantic relationships; 100 home decor items; an extensive tree of skills and upgrades; dozens of main and minor quests; the ability to have pets.

When getting to know the locals, you need to follow their instructions. By fighting monsters, your character’s skills are upgraded. You can fight in close combat with a sword, shoot with a bow at long range and use magic. Each character has a list of unique abilities. At the end of the adventure you will have to enter into a final battle with the most important demon of San Haven.
