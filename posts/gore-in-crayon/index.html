Game Description#

Gore In Crayon is a 2D pixelated survival horror game where the main goal is to avoid mortal danger in an intense game of cat and mouse. The project immerses you in an atmosphere of constant persecution, where the visual style, reminiscent of children’s drawings with colored pencils, creates an ominous contrast with the events taking place on the screen. The narrative unfolds entirely through visual imagery and action, without a single word or dialogue. Every corner of the stylized environment can become either a life-saving shelter or a deadly trap, which forces you to carefully analyze the situation and quickly make decisions to save your life.

At the center of this silent and depressing story is a child’s attempt to survive a night in a house where the only adult poses the main threat. That is why completing Gore In Crayon requires the gamer to have maximum concentration and the ability to predict the next move of his opponent. The outcome of the confrontation directly depends on the attentiveness and actions of the gamer himself, because the ending of the story is not predetermined and can end in one of several options. Any mistake or delay results in brutal and graphic scenes of failure. This entire concentrated experience of horror is packed into about 30 minutes of real time, making you appreciate every moment.
