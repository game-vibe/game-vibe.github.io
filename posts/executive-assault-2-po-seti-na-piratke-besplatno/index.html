Game Description#

Executive Assault 2 is a mixture of RTS and FPS, allowing you to play both alone and online, fighting a pirate with a friend or going through the campaign together. Allows you to build your own fort, choosing from a variety of components and modules available for free. Executive Assault 2 can be played online using cross-platform functionality. The gameplay involves exploring outer space, epic star battles, collecting resources, maneuvering between asteroids, trading with opposing factions, etc.

The main feature is that you can design robots to protect your station. Online play and a single-player campaign are available, represented by a set of various missions. Using the opportunity of a free online game, you need to prove your superiority over the warring factions, becoming the sole ruler of the galaxy. There is a war for technology, creating real chaos and lawlessness. Gameplay features: team or single gameplay; customization of shuttles, bases, customization of names, logos, etc.; construction of dozens of starships and development of a huge station; epic fighter battles in space; more than 60 technologies for research; collection and resource management; creation of superweapons.

You can control the fleet while on the captain’s bridge, or directly take part in hostilities by controlling the space shuttle. On asteroids and planets it is necessary to collect resources that will be required to build new ships or improvements. You should be wary of competing factions that are constantly on your tail. During battles, you can switch the camera between first and third person at any time.
