Game Description#

Terror in the Corn is a first-person survival horror game that immerses you in the nightmarish atmosphere of an endless cornfield. The protagonist comes to his senses without memories of the past, surrounded by tall stems that form a living labyrinth and deprive him of any hope of orientation in space. A supernatural fog spreads across the land, and an ominous chant constantly hovers in the air, serving as a harbinger of trouble. The feeling of constant surveillance does not leave even for a second, because something is hiding in the thickets, which is well aware of the helplessness of its victim. The psychological pressure here prevails over the sudden frightening effects, causing the tension to increase by the minute.

Survival in this cursed place is based on the mechanics of an endless loop. Each death at the hands of one of the many pursuers does not mean the end, but only returns you to the starting point, forcing you to learn lessons from your mistakes. To break the vicious circle, the hero, in the role of a miner, will have to explore not only the fields, but also the abandoned buildings of the town of Vorony Ravine. The process of exploring locations in Terror in the Corn involves searching for artifacts and evidence that can shed light on the mystery of the local curse. Only patience and attention to the details of the environment will allow you to put all the pieces of the puzzle together and gain a chance of salvation.

Gallery of damned stalkers:

Dumitra is a witch whose presence is felt in every corner of this world, and her singing serves as a constant reminder of who is boss here. Jack is the embodiment of pure aggression, appearing at the most unpredictable moments to deliver a swift strike. Ted is a silent and patient hunter who prefers to stalk his targets from the shadows, using endurance as his main weapon. Chris the Creeper is a cold-blooded and unforgiving creature who personifies death itself at its most brutal. Dolly is a source of creepy childish terror, adding an extra layer of unsettling madness to the proceedings.
