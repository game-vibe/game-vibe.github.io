Game Description#

Drop Pockets offers turn-based tactical gameplay based on the principles of billiards and structured like a roguelike. The player’s job is to pocket balls using physics-based strategic interactions against a series of opponents. In addition to standard billiard balls, objects from hockey pucks to the planet Jupiter appear on the cloth.

These unique items have special properties that significantly influence the outcome of the race, both providing an advantage and creating new obstacles. At the end of each Drop Pockets round, the antagonists, called Pool Sharks, introduce new conditions into the match. These boss fights change up the gameplay by changing the physics, adding obstacles, or simply flooding the table with extra orbs. Overcoming such scenarios requires strategic adaptation to changing rules.

Further progress depends on successfully overcoming these special challenges offered by each Pool Shark that does not adhere to the standard rules. Points earned during the game are used to purchase items and upgrades. This project allows you to obtain and combine dozens of Crayons, Triangles and Kievs. This system makes it possible to create many unique builds that offer their own approach to difficulties. The interaction between these various components becomes the key to building an effective strategy to successfully complete each stage of the game.
