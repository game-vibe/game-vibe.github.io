Game Description#

The Case of the Golden Idol will allow you to become detectives, but not in the modern world - but in the past. The events take place in the 18th century and mostly in an aristocratic society. The plot revolves around the murders of 12 people that occurred over a period of 40 years. During this time, law enforcement agencies were unable to find the suspect, and even moreover, they believe that these crimes are in no way connected with each other. The only one who thinks otherwise is the protagonist.

The gameplay of the adventure game The Case of the Golden Idol revolves around complex investigations in which the gamer uses his deduction skills extremely often. It is necessary to collect information literally bit by bit, think about each of them and only then try to put it together into a single picture. To succeed, the user needs to interview potential witnesses, interrogate possible suspects and inspect the places where the murders occurred. Their study is extremely important, because a real maniac could stop some evidence.

The game contains numerous scenes of cruelty - most people were literally mutilated and torn to pieces. Because of this, the age rating is high and the adventure game is only suitable for adults. The virtual world also looks extremely ugly, just like everyone else. As the story progresses, you will also have to participate in numerous dialogues and witness scenes from the life of society at that time - executions, burning of heretics, and so on.
