Game Description#

UFO 50 leads the revival of retro games, bringing together 50 unique projects available for free on the pirate market. This collection offers a wide range of options for those who want to play online with a friend, from exciting platformers to challenging puzzles. Each game features well-developed gameplay, and the development team focuses on full-fledged projects rather than short demos. Network play in UFO 50 online allows you to enjoy real gaming adventures with your friends.

The collection takes players to an alternate timeline where a wild 80s game studio pushed the boundaries of what was possible. The games included in UFO 50 are connected by a common universe: characters appear in different games, and storylines are intertwined throughout the collection. Players can dive straight into the game with all 50 games unlocked from the start, allowing them to explore at their own pace and find hidden gems along the way.

UFO 50’s multiplayer capabilities are a major blow, with half the collection supporting local play. Players can team up or compete against each other, ranging from strategic duels to arcade-style marathons. Take Pingolf for example - it mixes pinball and golf into a strange sport that brings out the competitive spirit in everyone. Meanwhile, Grimstone throws players into a Wild West JRPG where all hell breaks loose, proving that this collection doesn’t shy away from trying to appreciate its scope and ambition.
