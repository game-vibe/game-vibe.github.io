Game Description#

Casual Sport Series: Badminton is a digital reconstruction of rocket sport. Players make a variety of techniques, such as mixes, shortened and high blows, as well as actions at the grid to control the Volan trajectory. The gameplay is focused on reflexes and strategic positioning to move the opponent along the site. The outcome of the confrontations is decided by the exact conduct of various types of attacks in a quick sequence, requiring high accuracy.

Management allows you to instantly move and choose an attack during a draw, providing direct control over what is happening. Gamers choose from the list of stylized athletes with a distinctive appearance and behavior on the court. In Casual Sport Series: Badminton, several modes are available for various forms of competitions. Available formats include single matches one on one, two -way fights of two on two and tournament nets for a series of competitions. All formats support confrontation with computer opponents or with 4 people in a local multiplayer using a common screen.

This allows you to organize direct rivalry in one place. Competitions are held on several open badminton arenas that provide a different background for events. This sports game is built around the main cycle from the filing of Volan, participation in the draw and earning points. Each duel is a check of the reaction time and tactical placement of the blow within the site. The goal is to constantly force the opponent to make mistakes or to land the Volan in an indispensable position on his side of the grid to conquer the point.
