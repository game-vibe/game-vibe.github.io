Game Description#

Elden Ring Nightreign is a separate part of the Universe, where the action takes place in Limgrava, the very first district with the open world, presented in the original series. However, this is not the usual version of Limgrave. The world here is procedurally generated, which means that each passage offers unique experience. Users fall into the game with their team of two people, each of whom has their own abilities. The goal is to survive for three days, each of which ends with a battle with the boss, approaching the gamer to the decisive test.

The Elden Ring Nightreign structure rotates around the teamwork, where each user takes on the role of a night guard. Night wanderers are not just ordinary heroes, they have unique skills that interact with each other. The combination of these skills sets the tone for the whole game. No user should go through the game alone; Each decision made in battle, study or strategy can make or break the session.

To survive, gamers must rely on their teammates, cope with the dangers of the environment and resist the formidable bosses. As each day passes, users are faced with different smaller bosses, and with each victory the card decreases, pushing users to the climax of their adventure. The defining feature is its cooperative character.
