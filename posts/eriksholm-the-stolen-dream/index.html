Game Description#

Eriksholm: The Stolen Dream is a story-driven game that begins with the disappearance of Hanna’s brother, Herman. When law enforcement authorities initiate the pursuit of Herman, the heroine’s personal search sets off a chain of events that will affect her family and the fate of the entire city. The setting is a Scandinavian metropolis, inspired by the urban centers of Northern Europe in the early 1900s.

Each street and alley provides an opportunity for cover, but also contains new obstacles and pursuers. Hannah must work with her allies to uncover the secrets of the place and stay one step ahead of the enemies hunting her. Gameplay in Eriksholm: The Stolen Dream is based on an isometric perspective and stealth.

The gamer will have to control a squad of three characters, switching between them to use their unique skills. These abilities are necessary to gain access to previously inaccessible areas, solve environmental puzzles and successfully overcome encounters with enemies. The main gameplay revolves around observing patrol routes, using shadows and physical cover, and using diversionary maneuvers to evade detection.

Basics for survival in the shady alleys of Eriksholm:

Control a team of three heroes, each of whom has unique abilities to overcome obstacles. Use of stealth tactics: observing opponents, using cover and diversionary maneuvers. Exploring an isometric world to find non-obvious routes and solve puzzles. Dive into a complex plot where you have to uncover a large-scale conspiracy behind the kidnapping. Strategic planning of actions in order to outsmart enemies and remain undetected.
