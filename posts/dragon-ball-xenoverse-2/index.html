Game Description#

DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 2 is a fighting game with RPG and MMO elements. The project is designed for fans of the Dragon Ball universe, consisting of manga, anime, films and other video games. The sequel brought few changes to the series. The developers have improved graphics and network code. Many techniques moved from the first part to the second and were diluted with new options. DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 2 has many familiar characters: Goku, Krillin, Gohan, Vegeta, Trunks, Nappa, Kid Buu, Great Saiyaman, Tien, Piccolo, Goten, Zarbon, Jaco, Yamcha, Hit, Raditz, Lord Slug, Videl, Cell, Broly, Mira, Gogeta, Janemba, Gotenks, Whis, Towa, Pan, Omega Shenron and others.

The game has a full-fledged story campaign. It is divided into two stages: travel in the open world and fights in arenas. The new city of Conton became larger than the previous one. In it you can communicate with NPCs, get equipment, explore shops, take and complete quests. There are also teachers there who allow you to learn new techniques. The hero can walk and fly around the settlement. 4 temporary chasms with additional tasks have appeared. Completing them helps strengthen the protagonist.

It is possible to interact with other users. In addition to standard fights, there are raids with the participation of 6 people, where you need to beat a strong boss together. The game offers you to create your own protagonist. You can customize his appearance, choose his race (there are earthlings, friezes, saiyans, namekians, majins), and a fighting style. The protégé has the following parameters: chi explosions, health, stamina, basic and ranged attacks, super attacks, etc. They can be upgraded. In addition, costumes help improve performance. You can equip your arms, legs and torso, as well as put on shoes and accessories. In battles you can fight not only in the air, but also on the ground. Heroes know how to transform. For example, a Saiyan can become a Super Saiyan, and then change his form twice more to a more powerful version. Such metamorphoses are also reflected externally.
