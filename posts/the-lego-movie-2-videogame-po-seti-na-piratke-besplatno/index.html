Game Description#

InThe LEGO Movie 2 Videogame, players can enjoy an online game where they take on the role of Emmett to rescue friends from the Sistar system. The game allows you to play online and over the Internet, which makes it even more fun, especially if you play with a friend. In addition, it is possible to download it for free on a pirated website, but the best pleasure from the game can be obtained in the legal version. The LEGO Movie 2 Videogame features space travel, new worlds and fun building challenges, including simple quests for young players that reward building blueprints, Layla’s Stones and locked items, with golden chests providing additional rewards.

Fans of Lego Worlds may enjoy this game. For those who prefer linear Lego games with a strong narrative, it’s worth watching the gameplay videos before purchasing. The game offers easy gameplay for young children, helpful guidance, vibrant worlds and opportunities for exploration. Local co-op and a variety of characters and quests add replay value. The extensive text replaces the voiceover that was present in the Early Access version, which is a potential problem. Bugs persist, but do not interfere with play. Repetitive quests and tasks lack complexity. While the graphics are impressive, the game feels like an expansion of Lego Worlds with mechanics being reused. The lack of voice actors from the original film and limited language selection are disappointing. The content feels sparse, requiring replaying to gain additional building options.
