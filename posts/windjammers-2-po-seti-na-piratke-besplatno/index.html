Game Description#

TheWindjammers 2 competition includes the ability to play pirates online for free with a friend, throwing discs at your opponent both online online and in local matches. The continuation of the cult arcade game includes expanded mechanics. additional characters, various techniques and a lot of other content that gives completely new emotions from windjamming. The online game invites participants to dash, throw, jump, deliver powerful blows, etc.

The characters are such heroes as Max Hurricane, Klaus Wessel, Jao Raposa, Steve Miller, Jao, Sammy Ho, Hiromi Mita and others. Each character represents their country and has unique skills that determine the tactics and strategy of the competition. All athletes have their characters written down and the backgrounds of their stories are contained. Depending on the choice of fighter, the type of gameplay changes. For example, one hero has powerful strength, while another unit is able to move quickly. Both the heroes of the first part have returned, and a number of completely new ones have been introduced. Mechanics, content and features of Windjammers 2: Single player and free online play, including local multiplayer;

Expanded capabilities of the original;

Stylish 2D animation with bright effects;

Abilities such as slapshot, dropshot, EX moves, dash, etc.;

Numerous modes including arcade, championships and challenges;

Fast-paced gameplay with many combat tactics.
