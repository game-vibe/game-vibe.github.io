Game Description#

Portal with RTX completely rethifies the visual side of the classic portal by tracing rays, completely updated high -resolution textures, drawn surroundings, etc. Light is reflected from the surface and the shadows from all physical objects are realistically discarded. Rays dissipate fog or smoke. The updated visual significantly rethinks the original game, thereby offering to experience a new experience from passing.

The actions of Portal with RTX are developing in the Half Life universe. Players find themselves in the Laboratory of Research of Portals (Aperture Science). It will need to manage the girl Chelle. The gameplay is presented in the first person. It is necessary to solve a huge amount of spatial puzzles using a special portable device that can create portals. It is required to break matter in two locations, moving between them in space or transferring objects with their help.

Portals differ in color: blue and orange. The holes of the same color can be applied to a friend, and the opposite must be placed on a separate surface. The gameplay is completely built on physics. Any body retains all its values: weight, speed vector, etc. From Half-Life 2, the manipulator of a zero level of a zero level, but in a simplified form, migrated.

The story campaign consists of 19 unique locations. Between them you can move with an elevator. With each new room, the complexity of the puzzles increases, demanding to combine mechanics and previously acquired knowledge. It will be necessary to encounter obstacles in the form of walls, acid, power field, etc. After 19 rooms, you can get to the development center. At the end of the game, you will need to fight the boss.
