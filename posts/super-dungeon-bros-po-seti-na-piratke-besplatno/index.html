Game Description#

Super Dungeon Bros is an adventure arcade game designed to be played online online, allowing you to play together online with a friend on a pirate, completing various daily and weekly tasks for free. The gameplay is presented in a rock style. As four rock bros, the heroes must navigate the numerous infernal dungeons of Rockheim, defeating the forces of evil and hordes of the undead. Online play allows you to fight with heroes such as Axel, Lars, Freddy and Ozzy.

Every character in Super Dungeon Bros is named after a great rock musician. All heroes have unique features and characteristics. The possibility of free online play includes overcoming traps together and constant interaction between participants. For example, you can throw your partners, throw them over a trap so that they get to levers and switches, or climb onto the necessary ledges. Together with the heroes you need to experience the power of heavy metal!

There are several unique worlds to go through: Kriptheim (dungeon), Hladheim (brewery), Bogheim (jungle) and descend into the depths of Rockheim itself. Maps are generated randomly. At each level there are traps, puzzles, obstacles, various enemies, bosses and mini-tasks. Bonuses and rewards are awarded for successful completion of main and secondary missions. Weapons are represented by 4 classes: swords, crossbows, hammers and wands.
