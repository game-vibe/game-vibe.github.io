Game Description#

Priest Simulator is a first-person action game where you take on the role of a priest. The peculiarity of the game is that you yourself choose what kind of priest to be: to forgive sins or to take the side of adultery? Forgive people? Or should I just get rid of them? The priest simulator gives you complete freedom of action.

Many people are familiar with the phrase: “with great power comes great responsibility,” but not all games provide the opportunity to experience this to the fullest. You don’t have to be a superhero to have that kind of power. In a small town, you play the role of a privileged person and many people come to you to confess. Be a mediator between God and man. But remember that reputation is most important, especially in such a hostile society. Features of Priest Simulator:

conduct sermons and people will be drawn to you; share sacraments and knowledge, but at a price; collect toll; upgrade and upgrade your church; cast out devils from those possessed; make alliances and destroy sects; fight your competitors with the help of Divine Power and take part in illegal games.
