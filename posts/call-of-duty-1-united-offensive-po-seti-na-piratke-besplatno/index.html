Game Description#

Call Of Duty 1 + United Offensive is a combination of two projects from the series, and provides the opportunity to play online with a friend as a pirate in the main campaign mission, as well as multiplayer modes. Here, players will be transported to the period of World War II, where they will find themselves in completely different conditions. In total, there are 3 story campaigns - for the USSR, the Allies and the USA, which cover different time periods. For example, one United Offensive mission will send players flying over the English Channel as the Royal Air Force battles the Luftwaffe. Only now, users can take part in these events by coordinating their actions online online. But it’s the individual modes that deserve more attention in multiplayer mode. List of modes and their features in Call Of Duty 1 + United Offensive: Battle against everyone - a classic deathmatch, where the user with the most points wins. The time of rounds is limited, and resurrection after death occurs almost instantly. Team battle - the victory conditions are the same as in the previous mode, but now users are divided into teams. Capture the flag - team flags are located in the designated place on the map. To score points, you need to capture enemy objects and bring them to the allied base. Headquarters - having captured a certain territory, the team must keep it from being captured by enemies for as long as possible. The longer the hold occurs, the more points the team gets. Search and Destroy - One team must find key objects and plant explosives on them. The other side is obliged to prevent this by preventing troops from approaching the objects or defusing bombs. Assault on the base - the first detachment defends the fortifications, and the second tries to destroy it, using all its combat potential. For this purpose, grenade launchers, self-propelled guns, explosives and standard firearms are provided.

As for the combat itself, the possibility of free online play makes it more dynamic than in the single-player campaign. Here, on the map there are many more units, and the speed of battles increases noticeably. You can’t hesitate, because everyone is moving chaotically, intending to kill as many opponents as possible. Therefore, you should not sit in one place, because then you shouldn’t even count on a successful game. The network game keeps track of the points scored for each user, due to which you can be selected as the leader of the rounds. And given the variety of weapons, each player will be able to find or develop their own gaming style.
