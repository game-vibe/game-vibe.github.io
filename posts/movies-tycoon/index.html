Game Description#

Movies Tycoon is an exciting simulator. This ambitious project invites players to immerse themselves in the complex process of creating cinematic masterpieces. Set in the 1990s, you’ll be able to start your own film studio, gather and manage talent, and carefully orchestrate the production of blockbuster films.

At the heart of Movies Tycoon is a dual focus: the business side of running a studio and the creative side of filmmaking. You’ll manage budgets, schedules, and staff while immersing yourself in the art of storytelling. A unique feature is the built-in movie editor, which allows you to create short animated films within the gameplay itself. Whether it’s a heart-warming drama, a thrilling action thriller, or a quirky comedy, you’ll have to think through every aspect, from casting to writing and directing.

PixelCraze s.r.o. seeks to find a balance between the tycoon simulator and the filmmaking process. Will gamers be more drawn to the economics of running a studio, or will they enjoy the creative freedom to create memorable scenes? The studio’s decision to release full content as part of Early Access will allow them to gauge player preferences and tailor them accordingly. As the game progresses, we eagerly await the verdict: will the title be remembered for its shrewd business acumen or cinematic magic?
