Game Description#

Wild West Supermarket Simulator immerses the player at the head of the universal store in 1850. The action takes place in a ghost town, empty after the end of the gold fever and the majority of their inhabitants. The gamer arrives to lead a business, which is only a shadow of former greatness. The main task is to restore the enterprise from scratch, laying out goods on shelves and managing supplies to revive an abandoned location.

Success in Wild West Supermarket Simulator requires direct management of the business. It is necessary to order over 70 products, including food, tools and weapons, as well as bargaining with suppliers in a changeable economic environment. Demand and supply are constantly hesitating, forcing to adjust prices to save profit. The entire game routine, from unpacking the parcels to service at the checkout, is performed independently or with the involvement of hired assistants, allowing to focus on expansion.

The store is a site for development, from physical layout to interior design; The same control applies to a personal house with a view of the city. As the assortment of goods increases, the settlement itself is growing, attracting new customers whose needs are formed under the influence of periodic events. Suppliers from time to time offer mysterious boxes with unknown contents - a risky venture that can bring benefits. This game also includes competitions, for example, by throwing tomahawks, offering additional entertainment and opening new licenses for the shop.
