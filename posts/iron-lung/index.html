Game Description#

Gamers in Iron Lung will plunge into dark adventures aboard the Iron Lung submarine. The game is designed as a horror adventure where players need to make their way through the ocean on the surface of an alien moon. Users will move as a first-person character through various locations, represented by corridors and rooms on the ship. You will have to navigate the environment using the central window, as well as monitoring the data on the dashboard.

Iron Lang players have to study anomalous phenomena in the Bloody Ocean, using a variety of technological equipment. Moving through locations, users will take pictures of mysterious places using a camera. Players will be able to freely explore the map by interacting with interactive objects. In the process of passing, you often have to face puzzles and select alternative ways to solve riddles.

The storyline tells about inhabited planets that randomly begin to disappear. The universe is filled with a large accumulation of asteroids and lifeless moons, on which the remnants of humanity are forced to survive. One of the victims is the main character, who happens to come from a colony that is stationed on space stations or ships. Gamers will be able to find out the reason for the disappearance of stars and planets by unraveling the mystery of the Bloody Ocean.
