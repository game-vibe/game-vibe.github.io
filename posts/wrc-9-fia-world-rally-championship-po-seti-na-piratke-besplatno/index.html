Game Description#

WRC 9 FIA World Rally Championship is a car simulator in which you can team up with a friend to control a car over the network. On the pirate VRS 9, all network functions are available for free, allowing you to cooperate online over the Internet as a driver and navigator. Also, the possibility of free online play includes competition with other players in various championships and tournaments.

The gameplay is distinguished by realistic physics. Each vehicle handles differently, so before you go to the competition, you need to test the car on the training track. The online game WRC 9 FIA World Rally Championship offers you to fight in 3 rally modes and take part in 35 races on special stages. There is a map editor that allows you to create your own tracks, filling them with numerous turns, jumps and obstacles. It is also possible to create custom championships. The main game takes place in the vastness of Kenya, Japan and New Zealand.

15 classic rally cars take part in the competition. The campaign consists of 100 races, the difficulty of which increases dynamically. The gameplay is aimed at complete immersion in the sports discipline, so players will experience very complex and hardcore gameplay. Each track has unique properties, the cars have individual handling and physics. In the ninth part, the graphics component has been improved and the transcript has been updated.
