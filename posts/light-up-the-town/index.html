Game Description#

Light Up The Town is a cozy project, the main goal of which is to create a festive atmosphere in a small town on the eve of winter celebrations. The process of transforming the settlement provides complete freedom to realize creative ideas. Placement of multi-colored garlands is carried out with a simple mouse click, and for each decorative element detailed settings of dimensions and rotation angle are available. The protagonist is a young ferret named Bean. Exploring the snowy streets is rewarded with hidden coins. This in-game currency can be exchanged for even more colorful and varied decorations, expanding the arsenal of available decorative tools. In addition, Bean delivers gifts to other residents of the town.

The plot takes you to the fictional settlement of Bellflower, located in Colorado. The entire story of Light Up The Town revolves around the sudden illness of local electrician Chuck. Due to illness, he is unable to prepare the city for the annual Winter Festival. All responsibility for decorating the streets and saving the holiday from the approaching snowstorm falls on the shoulders of his daughter. It is she who will have to return light and joy to the homes of the anthropomorphic animals that inhabit this world. You can watch what is happening from both the first and third person.

Main activities in Bellflower - what the player will have to do:

Placement of festive garlands and decorations on more than 10 levels. Find and collect hidden coins to unlock new content. Delivery of gifts to residents to enhance the festive mood.
