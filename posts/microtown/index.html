Game Description#

Microtown is a urban planning simulator in which you will control your own settlement. Build the village of your dreams, lead it inhabitants and try to expand your possessions in order to become the most prosperous settlement on all land. Microtown’s gameplay is emphasized on production chains.

You begin your way as a small village, gradually expanding to a huge city. Explore the surrounding territories, collect and process resources to not only improve the city, but also provide a happy life for your settlers. Follow the needs of the inhabitants and try to satisfy all their requests. People are the key to prosperity.

In the center of the main mechanic are knowledge and technology. Open new types of activities, study professions that will help you get unique types of resources and bring new mechanics to the game. Various buildings have individual properties that help build production chains: ore mining, production of materials and tools, agricultural technologies, brewing, etc.

Build at home, sawmill, mines, warehouses, farms, mills and many other structures. Assign the workers to different positions, whether it is the processing of raw materials, the goods of goods to warehouses, the construction of buildings, etc. For successfully completed tasks, glasses and bonuses will be accrued, which can be spent on improving the city.
