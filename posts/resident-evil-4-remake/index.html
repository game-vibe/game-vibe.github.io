Game Description#

Resident Evil 4 Remake is a full-fledged remake of the cult fourth part, which is rightfully considered one of the best in the series. The developers took into account the criticism of fans after the release of the remake of the third game and did not stray far from their roots, adhering to the course taken with Resident Evil 2. The plot has not undergone drastic changes, however, some storylines have been added that were not in the original, the arcs of minor characters have expanded and, Of course, the controls have been improved and the graphics have been significantly updated.

The story centers on Leon Kennedy, who arrives in the Spanish village of Pueblo to save the president’s daughter named Ashley. Here he encounters the religious cult Los Illuminados and their leader Lord Saddler. The latter controls the villagers with the help of the parasite Las Plagas, which is why they unquestioningly obey their leader. Resident Ivl 4 Remake moves away from the usual plot about the living dead, becoming closer to the original concept of “zombies” that appeared on the islands of Haiti. As legends say, Haitian shamans used Voodoo magic to control people like puppets.

During the passage of Resident Evil 4 Remake you will visit three vast locations: a village, a castle and an island. Each of the three chapters features unique monsters and bosses. In the village you will have to fight with the residents of the village and their leader, Bitorez Mendez. The castle is the family estate of the short lord Ramon Salazar, under whose leadership there are cult fanatics. The island offers to engage in battle with real military personnel and Leon’s former partner Jack Krauser, who injected himself with a strain of the Plaga virus. Some characters from past games have returned, such as Albert Wesker and Ada Wong. But they pursue their own personal goals…
