Game Description#

Unbound Eternity is a turn-based card roguelike in which players take on the role of a powerful monster. The protagonist will have to invade the mysterious lands controlled by the mysterious cult of the Eye and challenge its orders. The basis of the combat system is runes, which allow you to play cards and create powerful combinations. With their help, you can multiply damage, poison opponents, or sacrifice your own health for the sake of devastating attacks. This is the only way to resist the army of the empress and defeat the hero chosen by the cult to maintain the eternal cycle.

The main task of the villain is to break this endless chain of battles and establish undivided power over the world. To achieve this goal, Unbound Eternity will have to constantly improve your deck by creating and improving cards. The gameplay invites you to navigate your own route through hostile territories, choosing battles and avoiding unnecessary risks. Each decision affects the final result, opening up access to new opportunities to expand your sinister domain and obtain permanent improvements.

Tools for establishing dominance:

Collect powerful artifacts that provide unique advantages in battle. Combine tiles on the global map to strategically plan your route. Receive a variety of rewards, including permanent combat enhancements or even immortality. Overcoming a series of unpredictable events, each of which affects the further passage.
