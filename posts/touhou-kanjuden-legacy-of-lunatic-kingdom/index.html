Game Description#

Touhou Kanjuden ~ Legacy of lunatic Kingdom opens as city legends become tangible events throughout Gansko. The rumor that the Apollo program never reached the moon is gaining strength against the backdrop of the appearance of an enemy army from the lunar capital. At the same time, metal spiders begin to cross the mountains, moving with an even, leisurely step. These machines have a functional, non -biological form, and the earth under them loses vitality: the fog is dissipated, and the vegetation fades.

The character of Sanae recognizes their design, comparing it with the Curiosity Mars trip. The game process Touhou Kanjuden ~ Legacy of lunatic Kingdom requires the player to maneuver through the intricate patterns of enemy shells. Survival depends on the evasion of collisions while the destruction of opponents for advance forward. Each stage of this game ends with a meeting with the boss - a figure with a significant force that blocks the further path.

This personal conflict unfolds while mechanical spiders continue to purify the lands - an operation designed to turn Gansko into an inanimate world, devoid of all living things. While in their residence of Eierey, Kaguya and Eirin recognize the appearance of a threat, understanding the impossibility of direct intervention without risk to fall into a trap. Airin prepares a special medicine and sends the moon ranger to deliver it to people. In another place, the sorceress Marisa analyzes seven occult balloons, believing that one of them, associated with the lunar capital, is a source of incidents.
