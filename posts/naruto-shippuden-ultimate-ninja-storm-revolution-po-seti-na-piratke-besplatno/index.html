Game Description#

NARUTO SHIPPUDEN: Ultimate Ninja STORM Revolution is a fighting game with beat-em-up elements based on the Naruto universe. It offers battles with bots and fights with live opponents over the Internet online. You can also play pirate online with a friend. It also supports a mode where users sit at the same computer and fight on a single screen.

NARUTO SHIPPUDEN: Ultimate Ninja STORM Revolution features free online play, a single player campaign, championships, individual competitions, free battles, and training. The number of fighters reached 100 units. Among them is Mecha-Naruto. One of the major innovations is the World Ninja Tournament. These are fights for 4 participants in large arenas. Before the start, heroes and heroines sacrifice techniques or mobility (users are given a choice). In this game, gamers and bots fight for yellow balls (analogous to a health scale), trying to knock out as many spheres as possible from their opponents. The winner is the one who managed to score the most points.

The combat has been updated. It had absolute jutsu introduced. Network play allows for joint attacks. This can also be done in a single. There is also an open world. In it, between fights, you can communicate with NPCs and complete their quests, as well as make friends with other heroes. As a reward for these activities, achievements and trophies are given out. The local plot, on the one hand, is filler, and on the other hand, it reveals the secret of the creation of Akatsuki. The story also demonstrates the replenishment of the Uchiha clan.
