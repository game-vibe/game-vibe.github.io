Game Description#

Traveler’s Refrain talks about the wanderings of a protagonist with the name traveler in a forbidden forest who seeks to find his lost lover. It carries a special tool, Buzuki, which begins to vibrate and make sounds when approaching a certain mechanism in this closed area. The tool pronounces the words with a voice devoid of reason, indicating the traveler to find 3 destinies and thereby setting the direction to his subsequent steps.

This Action-RPG Project, Traveler’s Refran, is focused on the task of the Gordeger and its actions in the world, covered by an old conflict. Gamer controls the traveler when he fights with hostile creatures and overcomes the riddles associated with the environment with the help of his tool. The plot includes a clash with creatures called the furs-gods of fate, in order to get to a mechanism that must reunite the protagonist with his lover. The gameplay in the battles involves the use of a special buzuki to activate melodies-coaches against opponents.

These melodies are possible to combine with a nearby weapon with spontaneous damage to obtain a variety of attacking effects. The system contains elements of the calculation of time where the timely execution of melodies-coaches can affect the outcome of the clashes. The study of locations occurs in old forests - areas, which hides secrets, dangers and certain opportunities among trees and ruins. The Glavherero meets other individuals, people and robots who go to their goals within the forest.
