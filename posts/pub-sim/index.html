Game Description#

Pub Sim puts players in charge of their first pub after purchase. Initial tasks include preparing the establishment by cleaning, stocking up on merchandise, and setting drink prices before admitting customers. Customers come for service and require constant attention to ensure drinks are stocked and calculations are correct. Successful work over time can bring funds for improvements and expansion of the business.

Running a business in Pub Sim involves solving problems related to running a drinking establishment. Success opens up opportunities for reinvestment, allowing you to expand space, hire staff and update the environment. Your decisions determine whether the pub will become a popular city place or remain a haven for a narrow circle of people. The cleanliness of the premises greatly influences the opinion of customers, so regular cleaning is necessary.

Investing profits in this project promotes development: you can buy furniture that influences the time of visitor’s stay. Pricing is a key element of the game, directly affecting the establishment’s reputation, speed of service and its overall atmosphere. Maintaining a supply of a variety of beverages, from casual to premium, including wines and spirits, is important to satisfy the public’s needs. In addition, you need to pour the drink accurately, because overflow reduces income. Working with personnel includes the distribution of tasks, but the main business decisions remain with the gamer, as well as salary control to maintain financial stability.
