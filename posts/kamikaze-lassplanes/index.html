Game Description#

Kamikaze Lassplanes is a visual novel that combines the plot of a novel with the action of a shoot-‘em-up game. Set in a steampunk world, the dramatic storyline involves two powerful weapons, Lassplanes, and two girls who become them. Gamers navigate a war-torn kingdom and make choices that affect not only the fate of the kingdom, but also their own hearts.

The project introduces Alba Trosset and Hannah Brandenburg, two girls who transform into deadly warplanes. Alba is a cheerful and curious girl who deeply loves her home village. Hannah, on the other hand, is a decorated war hero with a mysterious past. Together they fly their Lassplanes on various shoot-‘em-up style missions against the Imperial Navy. The gameplay is a mixture of story and arcade, offering players a unique combination of genres.

In addition to the action, Kamikaze Lassplanes offers a rich narrative filled with romance and comedy. There are several story threads, each of which reveals different aspects of the characters and the world in which they live. Users will have to uncover the truth about the origin of the Lasplanes and the dark secrets hidden in the world of steampunk. Thanks to its unique combination of genres, the title will be a fun and exciting entertainment for fans of visual novels and shoot-‘em-up adventure games.
