Game Description#

Eternal Glory is a fantasy visual novel whose narrative focuses on an emotional story and life-changing decisions. The central figure of the plot is a young woman whose existence was destroyed by a mysterious curse. 9 years after the tragedy that resulted in the loss of her family and her usual way of life, the protagonist returns to the ruins of her past. There she encounters three powerful men and learns a truth that radically changes her perception of the world. Realizing that these strangers are leading divine organizations, she decides to confront them in hopes of getting rid of her illness.

Progress through the plot of Eternal Glory is based on sequential passage of chapters, where each dialogue offers two answer options. One choice creates intimacy with the character, while another creates distance. The system provides instant feedback: the interface turns red when sympathy increases and blue when it decreases. Unlocked episodes remain available for replay, and each chapter has a quick save feature. At the center of this story are three powerful men.

Heads of divine organizations:

Uriah Machel is the leader of the Divine Ministry, whose impeccable kindness raises suspicions. Haise Carro is the head of the Reaper Corps, impulsive and prone to creating chaos. Ismay Heren is the leader of the Holy Knights, living by the laws of strict discipline and responsibility.
