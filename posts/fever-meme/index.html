Game Description#

Fever Meme is a journey into the mind where behind the façade of absurdity and parody lies a deep personal story. Surrounded by white walls and checkerboard floors, a path begins that leads far beyond the normal gaming experience. From time to time, fragments of phrases appear on the screen - emotional messages and requests for help, hinting at the author’s inner experiences. The initial guide in this strange world is a skeleton in a fedora named Salvador, whose actions immediately set the tone for the entire subsequent adventure, full of unexpected turns and metaphors.

The narrative is purposefully disrupted, throwing the user from one extreme to the other. A classic platformer that requires extreme concentration can suddenly give way to a surreal game show where the contestant has to face a giant spider. This constant change of genres is not just a satire on established canons, but an artistic device that reflects internal chaos. The Fever Meme project makes you constantly doubt what is happening, offering puzzles whose solutions are beyond the bounds of conventional logic.

The central mechanic is the complete breaking of the fourth wall, creating a unique connection between the gamer and the creator. The title tracks actions and remembers previous mistakes, using this information to further develop the metaplot. Interaction with the environment reaches a new level, where the very nature of the relationship between the author and his audience is explored. This approach puts the project on a par with such works as The Stanley Parable and Undertale, but with its own unique view of interactive art, filled with humor and at the same time tragedy.
