Game Description#

Hellcoming is a co-op horror game that can be played over the Internet online or alone. The gameplay allows you to team up with a friend to play pirate online for free, exploring dark interactive locations. The online game offers you to clear the building of a demonic entity in solo mode or in the company of friends. Each location places you in corrupted rooms inhabited by a demon. You need to find a way to get rid of evil spirits. The possibility of free online play allows you to join a team of up to four players.

To recognize the wicked from the building, you will need to find and destroy an artifact that is tied to a particular place. As you progress, a portal to the demonic world opens. The infernal altar is protected by seals, so you need to find a way to unlock it. Each seal has a different type of corruption. It will be necessary to join forces and act together to achieve success in exorcism. Equipment in Hellcoming: vacuum cleaner - collects small objects; brush - cleans dirt; shocker - damages creatures; flask - extinguishes fire; scanner - shows the progress of cleaning; EMF detector - detects paranormal activity; medical station - treats characters; video cameras - monitor the premises.

Heroes have a stamina parameter. You need to constantly rest and monitor your protagonist’s fatigue. Each location consists of many different rooms and huge spaces. The complexity of the gameplay increases every minute. Demons become more aggressive and begin to attack more often. Traps can be placed to temporarily slow down opponents.
