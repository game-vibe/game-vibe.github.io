Game Description#

On the piratePikuniku you can play for free online with a friend or random players from all over the world. The co-op is available both locally and online. Online play allows you to work together to solve various puzzles, explore a whimsical world, and much more. Together with the main characters, you need to help the residents of the surrounding area and unravel the mysteries of the dystopian country. The visual side stands out with bright graphics and minimalist style.

The project stands out for its absurd and black humor. Carrying out various assignments from local settlers, you will have to get involved in a large-scale conflict and unravel the insidious plans of the main villain to destroy the entire state. It is necessary to lead a real uprising and carry the banner of revolution in an attempt to overthrow the oppressive government. The possibility of free online play allows you, together with one partner or several companions at once, to save the bizarre inhabitants from the machinations of the oppressor!

The world is filled with charming creatures. Almost every creature is available for interaction and can give tasks of varying degrees of difficulty. Basically, quests are aimed at solving various puzzles. Their difficulty varies depending on the level. Each character has their own problems and quirks, but they all know how to charm and be memorable. The complexity of the puzzles increases with each stage. At some levels, even one mistake can lead to catastrophic consequences.
