Game Description#

Star Valor gives many opportunities: to explore the huge expanses of the galaxy on the cosmic shuttle, to extract resources, to trade, to fight and create your own weapons. With the help of flexible tools, you can configure the ship by your own preference. Each decision -making affects relations with fractions and has its consequences. At the start, it is necessary to take the place of the captain of a small ship, to fulfill simple instructions and hire small personnel. Gradually, I master the expanses of the universe, it is necessary to carefully select the allies and determine the enemies. The world in Star Valor is procedurally generated.

The game contains:

simple in the development of the combat system; A vast craft system of weapons; more than 140 units of weapons for shuttle; about 60 ships; The character of the character and many perks; 7 opposing fractions; Additional mode of the arena.

Outting objects have a degree of rarity: gray - the least useful, white - standard, green, blue and purple - are of value. To raise the rarity of armor, weapons or artifact, it is necessary to find special relics. Things can be collected precisely for your style of play, endowing them with various properties and features thanks to a flexible craft system. The combat system is thought out and is based on the characteristics of the dishwasher. For example, smaller shuttles have higher speed and are able to dodge many shells, while giant battleships are less rotary, but can apply more damage. The arsenal of weapons allows you to use both various machine guns and self -assembled missiles.
