Game Description#

Fantasy Maiden Wars DREAM OF THE STRAY DREAMER is a tactical role-playing project, the events of which take place in a world called Genshokyo, where gods, people and supernatural creatures youkai exist together. The story begins with a series of mysterious incidents that upset the fragile balance of this region. To understand the causes of the growing chaos, two heroines, the priestess Reimu Hakurei and the sorceress Marisa Kirisame, set off on a long journey. The choice of one of them as the main character affects some plot details, offering a look at the unfolding drama from different points of view.

The gameplay is based on turn-based battles taking place on maps divided into cells. Success in battle is determined by competent squad positioning and thoughtful use of the unique abilities of each of the more than 70 available allies. The characters' arsenal includes both long-range and melee attacks, as well as powerful Spell Cards, the use of which is accompanied by spectacular animated inserts. Between missions, there is a special training screen where you can equip fighters with new equipment and distribute skill points, customizing the team to your fighting style.

For those who want to dive deeper into the details of the universe, the Fantasy Maiden Wars DREAM OF THE STRAY DREAMER project provides an extensive encyclopedia. It contains information about characters and key terms that explains the intricacies of the local world order. Additionally, there is a music room containing over 200 compositions, which allows you to fully appreciate the atmosphere of this fantasy world. The provided difficulty levels and the presence of training missions make the title accessible even to beginners in the tactical strategy genre.
