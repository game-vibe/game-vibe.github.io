Game Description#

The Legend of Dark Witch Episode 4 is a continuation of a series of two-dimensional action games, the gameplay of which is based on clearing locations and strengthening the character. The visual style of the project is made in pixel graphics format. The basis for the heroine’s development is a resource called Tres, received for eliminating opponents at the level. Each location contains hidden special enemies known as Jammers. Their detection and subsequent destruction is one of the primary tasks on the way to the final boss of the stage. Successfully completing fights with powerful opponents opens up access to new types of magic, which significantly expands the arsenal of available actions and combat techniques.

Character progression and development:

Tres is the main resource for enhancing the heroine’s abilities, which systematically drops from enemies defeated at the level. Jammers are special hidden enemies camouflaged in each location, and their destruction is a prerequisite for advancing to the boss. New types of magic are a valuable reward for defeating powerful bosses, adding to your arsenal of combat techniques and expanding your tactical capabilities.

The central character of the story is the Dark Witch named Zizou. Her mission is to confront the mysterious Verde organization, which uses magical interference to disrupt global communications in order to seize world domination. Walkthrough of The Legend of Dark Witch Episode 4 offers flexible difficulty settings.
