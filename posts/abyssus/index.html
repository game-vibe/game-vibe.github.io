Game Description#

Abyssus is a dynamic adventure through a world created based on the legends of Atlantis. For greater color, the buildings of the Aztecs and their temples were used to develop the architecture. The player will have to explore a vast territory, which is not very happy that its silence has been disturbed, and it is ready to call on all possible forces to help restore peace.

Actions take place in the 19th century. This is a unique period of rapid development of technology and in places where they still do not plan to reach. The main character is a mercenary who is ready to do any job if he is paid well. This time he takes over a rather simple task from some researcher. His goal will be to go down to the ruins indicated on the map and inspect them for dangers, but they are located under water. The protagonist is sure that this is easy money, because not a single creature, except some crabs, will survive in such conditions. But as soon as he descended, the mechanism came into action and the guards and monsters who guard the secrets of the sunken city woke up from their sleep.

The main task of the Abyss player will be to survive in these difficult conditions and find a way out. To do this, he will need to solve various puzzles, try to overcome the maze and fight monsters. Since the ammunition is not infinite, the character should quickly find an analogue for his pistols, because there is nothing to update the cartridges with. Fortunately, as it turned out, the lost civilization was quite developed militarily.
