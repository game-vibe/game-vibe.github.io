Game Description#

OH DEMON! Fix my TV is about how a banal everyday task turns into a surreal adventure beyond the bounds of reason. The main tool for interacting with the world is the protagonist’s gaze, with the help of which the environment is transformed: from discovering hidden clues and turning on lights to activating secret passages. The project is a unique combination of comedic elements and scary moments, where the atmosphere of horror is achieved largely through the sound design. A specially created audio language heightens the tension, and the musical accompaniment dynamically adapts to the action, enhancing both moments of pure fear and epic clashes with opponents.

The plot is extremely simple: an elderly woman calls upon a demonic entity to fix her TV. The protagonist in this unusual narrative is precisely this summoned creature. However, in OH DEMON! Fix my TV this seemingly trivial request becomes the starting point for a journey through impossible spaces. The gamer will have to explore locations that defy logic and overcome sophisticated obstacles. On the way you will encounter otherworldly creatures, confronting which will require ingenuity and quick reaction. Solving numerous puzzles becomes the key to moving through this absurd and dangerous world.
