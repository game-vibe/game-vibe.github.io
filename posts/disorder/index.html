Game Description#

DISORDER invites users to immerse themselves in a department store labyrinth filled with secrets. The main character, a girl experiencing heartache and lost memories, finds herself trapped in a place where every attempt to escape seems to be an endless loop. As they progress, gamers must help her piece together the pieces of her past while battling an ever-changing environment. With each failed escape attempt, the main character gains strength, making progress a constant but evolving challenge.

No two escapes in the world are the same, and each attempt to escape from a store provides a new opportunity for self-discovery. What sets this game apart is its emphasis on replayability. The department store, a central figure in the narrative, is designed to change with each session. This gives the title a fresh feel and ensures that users will never be able to predict what will happen next. The ever-changing store layout presents gamers with new challenges and forces them to think on their feet.

As the user explores different areas, he has to adapt and improvise, which is an important element of the gameplay. The unpredictability of the store creates a sense of urgency and excitement, as no two journeys through the store will be the same. In DISORDER, the gift table plays a key role in helping users improve their character’s abilities.
