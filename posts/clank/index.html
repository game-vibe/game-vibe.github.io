Game Description#

Clank! throws players headlong into an exciting world of dungeons and daring theft. This board game marks their physical debut, offering a unique blend of deck building and push-your-luck mechanics. Main task? Infiltrate the legendary Dragon Vault, steal the most coveted artifacts, and escape with the loot before the dragon turns you into a crunchy beast. Sounds simple enough, right? But no.

Every false step, every clink of metal on stone echoes through the caves, serving as an uninvited call to the slumbering dragon. The deeper you go, the richer the rewards, but the higher the stakes. One wrong move and the legendary Clank! will echo through the tunnels, infuriating the dragon. It’s all about deck building. Players start with a basic deck of weak cards, allowing them to move cautiously and mine scarce loot.

But as players progress through the game, they can upgrade their decks with powerful movement cards that propel them deeper into the dungeon, powerful combat cards that allow them to fend off the dangers that lurk, and superior treasure cards that net them valuable loot. However, you have to pay for each card you receive. Many powerful cards also generate noise, represented by a scary symbol. If there is too much noise, the dragon will awaken and unleash his fiery wrath on all the unlucky ones lurking in the depths.
