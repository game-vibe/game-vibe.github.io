Game Description#

Prison Simulator is an action prison simulator. The main character comes out of this hole after 30 days, but the fewer days remain, the more it seems to him that the whole world is against him. The administration constantly imposes difficult cases and compromising responsibilities on you.

To begin with, it is necessary to maintain a balance between the convicts and the prison administration. Neutral relations will help to ensure agreement between the two parties, but helping only one of them may end badly and will not pay off the efforts at all. As a caretaker, you will have a lot of tasks. You will need to accept new criminals and look for free cells. Confiscate items from prisoners, participate in the treasury, solve their problems and complete other tasks.

You have a certain time to complete all the missions. But still, the gameplay is completely free, do whatever you want. Even the hero’s behavior will affect the relationship between workers and those behind bars. The more bad things you continually do to prisoners, the worse they treat you. Things can escalate to the point where they get out and attack the character.

Play a campaign with limited time and specific objectives, or explore all the gameplay possibilities in Free Mode. Here you will not have tasks or time, do all the things that you would not do in the story. Visit the most remote places of the prison, take a gun, throw cigarette butts inside the cells and do other things. Sometimes you can ignore the law and do a few prohibited things.
