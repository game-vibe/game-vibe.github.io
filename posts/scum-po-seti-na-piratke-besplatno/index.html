Game Description#

SCUM is a multiplayer action game that invites you to team up with a friend or a team of players to play for free online as a pirate, surviving in the open world. On the Internet online you can take part in various competitions, where the main goal is to kill your opponents. Allows you to customize your character by choosing appearance parameters, equipment, abilities and much more. Online play challenges skill and allows you to determine the best warrior by placing everyone involved in the action in wild conditions.

The events take place in a despotic world where the television show SCUM appeared. It is a game of survival. The confrontations involve prisoners who can only get a chance at freedom if they can kill all their opponents in a brutal game. Viewers place bets and choose their favorites. The opportunity for free online play is represented by intense PvP competitions, where everyone plays for themselves. You can divide into teams and join forces, but there is only one goal - only one participant must survive.

While exploring the map, you need to collect various resources and useful materials. You can craft equipment and weapons. Before the start of the match, everyone can fully customize their hero by selecting appearance parameters and setting initial characteristics. It is also necessary to look for food and water. You always need to be on alert, because the hunt does not stop for a second. A stab in the back could follow at any moment. It takes careful strategy and tactics to survive this madness.
