Game Description#

The co-op puzzle gameUnboxing the Cryptic Killer can be played online with a friend while investigating for free on a pirate site. By joining forces online, you will take on the role of two detectives, trying to jointly solve numerous puzzles. You need to communicate with each other, crack codes and try to outwit a dangerous killer.

The online game tells the story of two characters - Ellie and Old Dog. They are on the trail of a dangerous criminal and must unravel the tangle of confusing events and solve all the puzzles that the criminal leaves behind. Unboxing the Cryptic Killer’s free online play experience relies on constant interaction between participants. To open the mysterious box, you need to find out the secret combinations of numbers. To do this, you need to play a variety of mini-games, which are inventive puzzles in a retro style.

There are various non-player characters present. You can communicate with them or try to extract useful information from them that will help in the investigation. It is very important to take notes and record various observations or collect clues. It is advisable to keep a piece of paper and a pen so as not to lose sight of anything and write down every note about the environment or notate the testimony of witnesses in a notebook.
