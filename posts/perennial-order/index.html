Game Description#

Perennial Order is a 2D plant-based horror game with bosses set in a dark, atmospheric world inhabited by nature-inspired horrors. Gamers navigate this eerie environment, encountering unique bosses that present them with various challenges. It uses dual-stick melee controls, emphasizing intense combat where players must quickly learn from their actions due to the one-hit-kill mechanic. Rebirth immediately at the beginning of the battle allows users to focus on mastering each battle without unnecessary delays.

The game offers a rich storytelling experience, with gamers discovering the story through interactions with unusual NPCs. The world of Perennial Order is designed to be played solo or with a friend, and both local and online co-op are provided. The cooperative element allows users to share their journey, making it more fun and dynamic. Scenic 2D art inspired by a dark fantasy theme creates an even more immersive atmosphere, immersing users in a world of ghosts.

In addition to challenging combat and co-op gameplay, there are a variety of Eldric bosses, each with their own characteristics and strategies needed to defeat them. This variety keeps users engaged and constantly challenging themselves as they progress through the game. The combination of a compelling story, co-op play and intense boss fights makes the title a standout in the boss rush genre, offering a unique and memorable experience for players looking for a challenging and atmospheric adventure.
