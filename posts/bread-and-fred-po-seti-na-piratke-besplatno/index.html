Game Description#

The cooperative platformerBread and Fred features the ability to play online with a friend, free on a pirate ship, controlling two adorable penguins named Bread and Fred. Network play is available both locally and online online. The penguins' task is to get to the very top of the snowy mountain. The gameplay is a classic side-scrolling platformer. The main gameplay and mechanics are based on precision jumping. You can cling to walls, jump over chasms and much more. The possibility of free online play in Bread and Fred allows you to go through a variety of levels and difficult challenges together.

To reach distant platforms, you need to swing or use the help of a partner. It is also necessary to use inertia to correctly calculate the distance of the jump. You can grab onto your partner or use another penguin as an additional platform. You can play both in co-op and alone. An additional mode is accelerated passage, where you need to compete with other players in the skill of overcoming obstacles. The challenge was created for fans of speedruns. There is a leaderboard showing the best result.
