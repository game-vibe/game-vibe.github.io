Game Description#

Aethermancer - an adventure in which the player in the role of a powerful Ethermant will have to explore the constantly changing world of the Terast. The main task is to create an indestructible connection with mythical creatures that fell into an endless cycle of death and rebirth. The protagonist needs to assemble a detachment of three monsters and conduct them through dangerous procedurally generated locations of destroyed ruins. Each creature has unique spontaneous abilities that can be developed by creating powerful synergies within the team to overcome all trials. The combat system offers tactical step -by -step battles in the format of three by three, where planning and competent use of resources are the key to victory over fierce enemies and divine bosses.

The central mechanics of Aethermancer is a unique system of rebirth, which allows monsters to become stronger with every new life. This adds an element of a slide to a gameplay, where each passage differs from the previous one, forcing to constantly adapt its strategy. The etherman does not just command creatures from the outside, but takes an active part in the battles with his detachment. To achieve success, it is necessary to masterfully control the four elements of the ether, releasing destructive spells and finding the most effective combinations of skills. This project offers a gloomy fantasy atmosphere, supplemented by pixel graphics and deep customization of creatures.
