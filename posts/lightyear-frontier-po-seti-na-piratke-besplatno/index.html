Game Description#

Lightyear Frontier offers free online play with friends. This is a unique adventure where you can play online, exploring new worlds for free and pirated. Go on a journey through the Internet and enjoy online gaming in the company of a friend. In this game you will immerse yourself in the tranquil atmosphere of a distant planet on the edge of the galaxy. Don your exoskeleton and get busy growing unique alien plants, building homes, and solving the mysteries of the wild. Together with three friends, you can create a thriving exo-farm, where everyone will contribute to the common cause.

Lightyear Frontier emphasizes recreation and discovery. Players can focus on farming and exploration without the pressure of combat or managing basic needs. The soothing soundtrack in the style of cosmic country puts you in a calm mood.

Customization is one of the key features. Players can equip their mechs with a variety of tools, unlock upgrades, and personalize their appearance with paints and details. Environmental friendliness is another important aspect. Players manage the ecosystem by clearing it of pollution, removing invasive plants, and reducing danger. Planting new trees provides a healthy environment for future harvests.

As a reward for research, players receive a variety of seeds that turn into bountiful harvests. Watering and fertilizing fields optimizes growth. Harvested crops can be sold for credits or used to build structures and improvements. Players transform resources into buildings, gradually transforming their homestead into a thriving retro-futuristic farm. Customization options allow you to personalize your color palette and decorative elements.

Unraveling the planet’s secrets adds another layer of intrigue. Players use a friendly scanner companion to decipher clues scattered across a varied landscape, uncovering lost knowledge along the way. Up to three friends can join in on the adventure, pooling resources to create the exo-farm of their dreams and sharing the satisfaction of a successful harvest.
