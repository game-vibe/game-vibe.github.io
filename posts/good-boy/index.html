Game Description#

Good Boy is a co-op horror-comedy in which a team of up to six people must carry out daring heists in a very unusual area. The houses here are filled not only with valuable loot, but also with the terrible results of scientific experiments in the form of mutated pets. The main task is to act as quietly as possible, because any extraneous sound can attract the attention of aggressive creatures defending the territory. The carelessness of burglars risks triggering the appearance of that most menacing creature known as the friendly neighborhood dog. A miscalculation in planning an operation can cost the entire team their lives, turning a fun adventure into a tense struggle for survival.

Robberies and associated risks:

Secretly entering houses to search for prey. The need to avoid noise and other unmasking factors. Confrontation with mutated pets guarding the territory. The risk of provoking the appearance of the most formidable creature. Team coordination for removal of valuables and survival. The likelihood of the entire group dying if the operation fails.

Successful completion of the foray allows you to return to the shelter with the loot for the subsequent distribution of funds among your accomplices. Capital can be invested in upgrading the base, which opens up new opportunities for future raids. The passage of Good Boy is built on a cycle of risky raids aimed at gradually increasing the criminal reputation of the squad. However, no one can guarantee that all members of the group will return from the next raid alive. The project skillfully combines a tense atmosphere with elements of absurd humor, making players simultaneously afraid and laughing at what is happening.
