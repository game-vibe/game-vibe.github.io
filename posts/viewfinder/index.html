Game Description#

More than just a first-person puzzle platformer, Viewfinder is a true immersion into a world of mystery and mystery in a bizarre, surreal environment. You will have to use the camera to change reality and question your perceptions. This single player game will be a real challenge for your intellect and imagination, captivating you for many hours. You’ll bring paintings, screenshots, and postcards to life as you uncover long-forgotten secrets and explore connected worlds. Solve puzzles to discover why your world exists and what creative freedom means.

But be careful, knowledge does not come on its own - you must be prepared for the fact that you will have to fight for every piece of information. But for those who are truly eager to uncover the mysteries of this world, Viewfinder’s plot will unfold gradually, gradually revealing more and more unexpected differences. Get ready to step into a mysterious world and unravel the mystery of the impossible! You will find many hours of exciting gameplay that will not leave any player indifferent.
