Game Description#

Trüberbrook takes place in 1967, at a time when the lunar race between the USA and the USSR was in full swing. Student rallies and protests are taking place from Russia to Europe, carrying their slogans in Paris, Berlin, Budapest and Warsaw. The world is full of chaos, but what do we care about the whole world while the German province is dominated by one big problem? Hans Tannhäuser is an American student. He heard about the conflict in Vietnam, but now he is worried about a random lottery win that can only be claimed in Trüberbrook.

Curiosity fascinates the hero, so he goes to the town, forgetting about his dissertation in physics. However, immediately after arriving, he realizes that strange things are happening in the city. In the room where he is staying, an article on physics is stolen from him, and the residents of the city seem to him to be incredibly strange people. And only one person seems more or less normal to him - Gretchen Lemke - a student vacationing in the city, where she is simultaneously collecting anthropological material. She becomes the only companion of our hero, who must save the world, but he doesn’t know it yet. Experience the adventure from the perspective of the hero of Wagner’s opera, try to come up with the most beneficial solutions to the problem, and don’t go crazy with the characters who so skillfully manipulate your behavior! You have to solve 10 hours of riddles and puzzles, follow the development of characters and their friendship, and also understand which character, who is an American student, should stay in a remote German town, which is quietly located somewhere in the wilderness, far from civilization?
