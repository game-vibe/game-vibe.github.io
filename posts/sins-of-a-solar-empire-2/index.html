Game Description#

Sins of A Solar Empire 2 is a space strategy in which players have to take one of three races under their control in order to fight for dominance over the whole galaxy. It will be necessary to engage in the study of space, expand the sphere of influence, extract resources and fight with representatives of the warring faction. The game presents a lot of diverse mechanics, including military operations, diplomacy, trade, etc.

Gameplay is built on classic elements of 4x strategies. For the study, a whole galaxy is provided with various planets and unique biomes. Sins of a Solar Empire 2 makes it possible to become a commander of one of the three fractions: Byzari, Tes forces and coming. Each warring party has its own individual characteristics, including special buildings, an army, ground or flying equipment. All fractions have a unique set of heroes.

With the help of strategic emperors, it is necessary to calculate the orbit of planets and moon in order to colonize them. The battles occur both on the surface and with the help of spaceships in outer space. There are several types of shuttles, and it is also possible to build defensive turrets for the most effective protection. Ships are subject to upgrade due to numerous blocks, compartments, guns, etc. You can develop heroes with the help of a tree of skills. The battles occur with the participation of thousands of units and military equipment.
