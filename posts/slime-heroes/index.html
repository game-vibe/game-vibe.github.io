Game Description#

Gamers in Slime Heroes will go to the territory of a fantasy universe where colorful slimes live. The main character must prevent the spread of a terrible corruption that is infesting the land of slugs. Users will take control of one of the slime creatures, which will move around a three-dimensional map with a top-down view and fight dangerous monsters. The game is made in the genre of role-playing adventure with soulsliking mechanics, where you need to participate in hardcore battles with bosses. The main goal for the protagonist is to search for the 6 Gods of the kingdom in order to defeat the corrupted version of each of them and drive away the fog of corruption.

In the process of passing Slime Heroes, users will collect various equipment, find weapons and upgrade their character, adding new combat abilities. You will be able to master magical attacks that will help in battles. By destroying enemies, the player will be able to pick up dropped loot. Also on the map there are hidden locations to which you need to find a passage in order to get valuable treasures, magical artifacts or precious stones. Pebbles can be combined with each other and gain new witchcraft skills. Users will find elemental relics, which will allow them to change character builds and their damage. Gamers can play through the single-player campaign on their own or go into co-op mode, allowing you to play with a friend.
