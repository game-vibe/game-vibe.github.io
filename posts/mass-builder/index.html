Game Description#

M.A.S.S. Builder is a dynamic slasher game that allows you to completely customize your combat robot. The game allows you to create a unique Mechanical Assault Skeleton Suit (in the original - Mechanical Assault Skeleton Suit, abbreviated as MASS). You have to lead an army to protect humanity from an alien invasion. Don’t let the human race be destroyed and take the lives of your people into your own hands!

MASS Builder takes place in the distant future. Technologies have developed so much that they have allowed people to build not only flying combat mechs, but also to explore distant planets in the vastness of the galaxy. However, they thereby unleashed a bloody war with an alien race. As a result, many planets became war zones. Your sworn enemies are quarks.

Gameplay Features:

customize the hero according to your own preferences; dynamic and intense gameplay; fight against a whole horde of monsters and monsters; Hundreds of costume parts available; deep plot and thoughtful lore.

Lead an army of combat robots and get into the exoskeleton yourself to prevent humanity from being wiped off the face of the Earth. Customize your mech to your liking by choosing appearance, color, modules, weapons and much more. Using global settings, you choose the type of fighter and his strategy of action. Create a powerful soldier in close combat or an invincible mech at long range.
