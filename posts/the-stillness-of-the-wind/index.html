Game Description#

The Stillness of the Wind is about loneliness and loss. The once noisy village has now turned into a quiet place where only Talma lives. Everyone left to look for a better life in the city. Only she, a woman of advanced years, remains to live in such familiar and familiar walls. Every day the grandmother manages the farm and herds the goats.

It is necessary to monitor the area and animals. Make cheese from goat milk, collect chicken eggs, garden, grow vegetables and cook food. You can buy or sell food to a traveling merchant, who occasionally brings disturbing news beyond the city walls. The traveler will bring letters in which relatives share news and talk about life outside their native village.

Their stories can be filled with joy and sadness. She remembered many of her relatives when they were very small and young, and reading their messages, she understands that their lives, just like her years, are coming to an end. The gameplay of The Stillness of the Wind is a meditative and leisurely action about the most ordinary everyday life of people whose best years are far behind them.

The gameplay allows players to manage Talma’s time themselves. You can distribute the work by the hour, or you can simply go wandering around a deserted village, remembering your youth and nostalgia for the old days. Memorable points are scattered around the location, interacting with which reveals part of the heroine’s memories. Grandmother exchanges food for various resources needed in everyday life: hay for goats, plant seeds, fairy tales and even cartridges.
