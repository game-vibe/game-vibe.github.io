Game Description#

Wandness: Wandcraft Survivor will put in the center of the process the creation of magic guns for survival in collisions with superior forces. The player’s main task is to go down as a procedurally generated dungeons for battles with hordes of monsters. The main tool for survival is a unique rod collected from the found magical components. This craft mechanics allows you to construct unique spells that change the behavior of shells used in battle. Each entry offers new materials and challenges, demanding to adapt to advance deep into darkness.

Fights in Wandness: Wandcraft Survivor are held in an autoboy format, where a designed weapon attacks independently. The promotion of the game is built around the nodal points, on each of which it is necessary to repel opponents' attacks before reloading the portal. The screen is filled with shots in the Bullet-Shell format with a top view. In between the battles, the gameplay offers to engage in the management of production and tuning the character, using RPG elements to strengthen the assembly before the next dangerous location.

The system of creating objects has over 50 sorceresses modifying more than 20 types of shots. Gamers open synergies, combining spontaneous gems with certain classes of wands to obtain powerful attacking combinations. Such experimentation becomes the key to developing tactics to win the bosses, stereo depths. The gameplay of this title consists of individual sessions-self-sufficient action camps last 40-60 minutes, focused on one attempt to overcome the overhanging threat.
