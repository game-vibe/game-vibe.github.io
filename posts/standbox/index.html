Game Description#

Standbox, a bizarre single -user physical sandbox, invites players to indulge their children’s impulses to expand borders and experiments. Imagine a virtual world in which the central place is occupied by an elastic doll, which, on your order, passes all kinds of tests and hardships.

In this interactive digital site, you can interact with ragongollah in a variety of ways. It doesn’t matter what you choose - to lightly push it or throw out your most daring whims on it - you can observe the reaction and stability of Ragdollah. In the process of manipulation, he demonstrates amazing behavior: stands, balances, falls and inevitably tries to rise, even if his physical condition leaves much to be desired. These movements are controlled by complex approximation of the medical system with organs that react to what is happening in their own way.

Standbox returns us in the era of technical demonstrations of video games, when the attractiveness of the game was not so much in the gameplay itself as in technical perfection. This is a modest but intriguing experiment in the genre of “sandbox”, in which special attention is paid to the behavior of a doll named Stanley. Able to maintain balance, tolerate damage, fight a traumatic brain injury, experience fractures and show a stubborn desire to get to your feet, despite the injury, Stanley really becomes a star of this non-standard show. The landscape of the game consists of three separate cards that provide enough space for the implementation of your Ragdoll undead. As this world is studying, various tools become your companions, each of which offers unique ways to interact with Stanley and manipulating his reactions.
