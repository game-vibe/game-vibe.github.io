Game Description#

Splintered brings a fresh twist to the genre. In this game, the setting is not static, it changes unpredictably after each major victory, thanks to the villain’s ability to randomize the environment. What starts out as a traditional RPG with a simple mission to avenge your king soon turns into something much more complex. As you progress, users will encounter new challenges that arise thanks to the randomized world, cities, items and enemies.

Each time the world “splits apart”, new gameplay possibilities open up, requiring gamers to adapt and be strategic with each change. Splintered is based on turn-based combat, which is one of the core principles of the RPG genre. Users can participate in battles using a combination of attacks, spells and items. The title provides the ability to customize equipment, which allows gamers to develop unique strategies based on available resources.

This flexibility is important because the project is ad hoc and creates an ever-changing experience. By defeating opponents, users can unlock new abilities and items, which further affects the gameplay. The title features a variety of class archetypes such as paladin, thief, and mage, allowing for a wide range of playstyles. The classes are highly customizable, allowing gamers to mix and match equipment to gain different tactical advantages. As the world fractures, enemies evolve with new traits and skills, forcing gamers to constantly adjust their tactics.
