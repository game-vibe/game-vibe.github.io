Game Description#

Survival Nation offers free online play in a world where survival is an art. In this game you can play online, team up with a friend to fight zombies together, or try your hand alone. Embark on an exciting adventure as a pirate, where every step you take could be your last. Explore a dangerous world for free, improve your skills and fight for your life online on the Internet. Online play in Survival Nation is not only a struggle for survival, but also an opportunity to test yourself in the unpredictable conditions of a zombie apocalypse.

Food and water are critical. Fishing, hunting, gathering and scavenging in abandoned buildings are all on the menu. In this dangerous world, taking care of basic needs is a constant challenge.

They say collaboration is the key to success, and Survival Nation is no exception. Multiplayer mode allows you to team up with other people for joint adventures and exploration. Chat with people, trade supplies and fight together. If you’re a lone wolf, PVP will allow you to hunt other players for prey. For those who prefer single player play, Solo mode is also available.

Survival Nation isn’t just about shooting zombies. There are RPG elements, various missions, and side activities that will not let you get bored. You’ll develop your character through three different skill trees, focused on survival, combat, endurance, or a combination of the three. There are also many types of weapons that can be purchased and upgraded.

Besides completing quests and collecting food, there is a lot to do in the game. You can do metal detecting, rock climbing, solve riddles to find treasures, and much more. The game features a diverse arsenal of weapons, each with unique characteristics, controls, and reloading mechanics. At your disposal are pistols, shotguns, rifles, melee weapons, explosives and even a bow.
