Game Description#

Rift of the Necrodancer immerses the world of musical battles. Title talks about Kadens, which turned out to be locked in a strange dimension filled with monsters. To survive, she must fight with these creatures, using a mechanics based on the rhythm. Each enemy has its own unique behavior, which makes players adapt their approach to each meeting. As the players move, they will face more and more complex opponents, to win over several strikes, so the accuracy and time become critically important.

The project offers a variety of tests due to various levels of complexity, from easy to impossible. Due to this level of complexity, gamers with any level of training can enjoy the game, while for more experienced gamers it remains complicated. Users can also enjoy constantly developing experience thanks to the Remix Mode mode, which generates fresh bitmaps with each passage of the game. Remix Mode mode gives the Title unpredictability: players compete in global leaders, using the same randomized compositions every day.

For those who want deeper immersion, Rift of the Necrodancer allows you to create their own bitmaps using the developer tools. The ability to create personal levels and share them increases reigree. In addition, there are bizarre, frivolous mini-games that allow you to be distracted from the main rhythm-battalion. Whether it is helping a friend with work or yoga, these mini-games allow you to change the pace compared to the main ones.
