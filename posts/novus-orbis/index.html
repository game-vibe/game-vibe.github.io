Game Description#

Novus orbis is a Roguelike designer of decks, inviting gamers to the world, where selected cards and how you play them determine your path. The essence is to master the combo. Each card has its value, and their effect depends on how you arrange them - play the cards in the correct order to maximize their strength, or mix them to get a potentially different result. This emphasis on the strategy gives Tita depth, forcing you to be on the alert while you are guided in complex mechanics.

While you are traveling through this fragmented world Novus orbis, time is constantly passing. The reverse reference system means that each played card and each completed move bring the enemies closer to the attack. We have to constantly balance - move fast, but not too much. Make sure that your deck has cards that will help you survive long enough to increase the pace and strength. But the matter is not only in rough power. To succeed, you will have to collect synergistic decks, reveal secrets and find ways to become stronger every time.

Several classes are represented in Title, each of which has its own set of cards and a mechanic, which brings a variety to each stage. The possibilities of creating decks are very extensive - more than 100 cards for each class. You will also find powerful blessings - improvements that can turn the course of the battle, and pets, who can provide support in various forms - healing, attack or other useful abilities. This world is full of capabilities, and each solution matters.
