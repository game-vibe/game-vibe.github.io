Game Description#

Greedland is an exciting isometric shooter in which you play for the interstellar mercenary. Fight in constantly changing environmental environments and destroy the waves of enemies. If you like a dynamic gameplay like Crimsonland and Vampire Survivors, then Greedland is an ideal game for you! An online mode is offered for 2-4 people, an endless regime, a number of complex tasks, a system of fur and many roles to choose from. As well as many types of weapons, additional weapons, amplifying chips and much more.

Created on the basis of Unreal Engine 5, “Greedland” will captivate you with qualitatively worked out enemies that will fly on you from all sides, causing an adrenaline explosion that will check your borders of reason. Unlock a huge arsenal of powerful weapons and skills to create satisfactory combinations of battles and survive the sensations of classic games such as “Alien Shooter”. Select from dozens of primary and secondary weapons, as well as a long list of skills that offer endless strategic capabilities. Expect future updates where you can control powerful furs!
