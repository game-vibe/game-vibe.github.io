Game Description#

Rising World - a game in the “sandbox” genre. Your main goal is to survive in a world that you can create yourself. Explore caves, forests, mountains and dungeons. Develop new tools to solve problems and invent your powerful technologies. You can unite with other players to create civilization, trade and fight. This world is in your hands!

Peculiarities:

The modified world - everything you see can be changed. Single and multiplayer modes. At your disposal 200 building materials, 200 furniture, 200 weapons and tools. Load the images and place them in your own world.

The developers did not hesitate to use ready -made, successful developments from Minecraft, Dayz. Crusting stones to make turning machines, saws and other tools. The arrangement of the territory will take place at different times of the day and in different climatic conditions - everything, as in real life. For each completed task you will be a game glasses. You definitely don’t have to miss you idle - you need to build a shelter, even the most primitive, get food, defend yourself from predators day and night, and extract resources for development.
