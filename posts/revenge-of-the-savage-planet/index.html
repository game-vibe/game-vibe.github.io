Game Description#

Revenge of the Savage Planet throws the player into alien locations. The character turns out to be thrown on the edge of the space by the former corporate employer. The central task is to move around alien territories, collecting resources and finding improvements to organize home and revenge the corporation, guilty of its position. The study of the area includes the detection of hidden objects and the interaction with the environment.

In Revenge of the Savage Planet, promotion is associated with visiting several planets. The player applies skills like jumping, shooting, as well as special methods of movement, such as using hook and sliding, to overcome barriers and contacts with the world. Interaction with the environment covers scanning of the flora, the capture of various creatures for studying and working with special substances. The creation of a new equipment is tied to discoveries made during wanderings, which over time expands available opportunities.

The game supports joint passage, allowing two people to act at the same time. The cooperative mode functions both by network between different systems and locally with the separation of the screen on one device. Together, you can examine at least 4 different worlds, each with their own organisms and vegetation. The systematization of finds and the implementation of experiments increase the scientific level, opening access to additional equipment.
