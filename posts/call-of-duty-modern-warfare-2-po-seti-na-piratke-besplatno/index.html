Game Description#

On the pirated version ofCall Of Duty Modern Warfare 2 you can play online for free with a friend. There is both local co-op, and various competitive modes are available online online, allowing you to demonstrate your skill and superiority. The single-player campaign will take players to Russia, Kazakhstan, Brazil, Afghanistan and the USA.

The online game consists of classic competitive modes. Added additional matches: 3rd Person Team Deathmatch, 3rd Person Cagematch, Demolition, Capture the Flag and Mosh Pit. Special modes developed specifically for PC include competitions such as Global Thermonuclear War, V.I.P. and One Flag. There is a level system that gives access to perks, weapons and titles.

By participating in matches, experience points are accumulated, which increase the character’s rank. The maximum level is 70. After reaching it, you can go into prestige to start leveling up again, receiving the emblem opposite your nickname. The ability to play free online in Call Of Duty Modern Warfare 2 allows you to participate in both solo competitions and join forces with friends. Perks are awarded for completing various tasks during matches.

Callsigns personalization consists of two parts: a title and an emblem. It remains possible to indicate the name of the clan. There are rewards for deaths and special achievements for combos - killstreaks. The class system has been preserved, consisting of the main and secondary weapons that the character can use, as well as a set of starting perks. Before starting the game, you can create 5 classes. You can switch between them in real time.
