Game Description#

The Last Campfire is a project from the studio that developed No Man’s Sky. It is made in cartoonish, bright graphics, each of the landscapes is unique and creates new details of the world. The story is presented in the genre of an adventure quest.

The protagonist will be a coal shining with kindness. He got lost in the forest because a strong current knocked his boat off course and washed ashore. The hero does not understand how to get out to his native place and find his destiny, to see his family. Meeting strong characters along the way, you need to help the ember get home. You will be amazed by the detail of the details and the strange world, unlike ours.

Before your eyes there will be more than 10 different locations, lying between your protégé and his home. Strange ruins with previously unseen creatures, settlements of ancient peoples, dense, almost impenetrable forests - this is a list of what you will encounter, and that’s not all.

You have a long and exciting journey ahead, with many mysteries, aggressive creatures, obstacles and changeable weather, which has already almost taken the life of your coal several times. You can fight with opponents, but most often they are much larger than you and a hundred times stronger. It is necessary to demonstrate stealth skills and the ability to look for alternative routes, avoiding unwanted encounters and collisions. Between levels, the hero will swim along the river to the next place in the story.
