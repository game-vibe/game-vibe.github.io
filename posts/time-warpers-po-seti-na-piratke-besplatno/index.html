Game Description#

Time Warpers is a first-person shooter that allows you to play online with a friend on a pirate, competing for free in all multiplayer modes. On the Internet online you can compete in versus modes or take part in cooperative missions. The project is the ideological successor to Time Clickers. A wide range of functions is presented, full customization of weapons is available, and there is character development that allows you to upgrade your skills.

There is a permanent death mechanic. Having died once, the hero loses everything. The visual style is represented by cubic artistic execution. Time Warpers lets you collect gold, cover for a teammate, and use free online play to join pre-made rooms to show off your tactical skills.

When choosing a location, you must specify 3 cards that determine the hero’s bonuses. There are 13 cards in total with different mechanics and boosts. Network play allows you to compete as a team, performing in classic death matches. Allows you to call an air strike, suppressing fire, etc. There is a hoverbike at the levels, which allows you to quickly move around locations. Perks enhance the protagonists' abilities, unlocking skills like click weapons, drone support, or turret deployment. You will face strong bosses, against whom it is important to develop individual tactics.
