Game Description#

I am an Air Traffic Controller 4 is a flight simulator in which air traffic controllers play a vital role in the gameplay. This is the fourth game in the series to be released since 1998. The gameplay is focused on the duties of aircraft controllers. Take on the role of one of the airline’s most important employees. You have to give instructions while in the radio room. Keep track of aircraft routes, follow all instructions provided and thoroughly study the schedules of all airlines.

It is necessary to navigate emergency situations, respond to emergency situations, meet deadlines, follow instructions and be as efficient as possible. You can save at any time and resume progress the next time you start. Every minute the gameplay of I am an Air Traffic Controller 4 adds new stages, mechanics, conditions and tries to increase the dynamics of what is happening.

The settings allow you to set various modifications: change weather conditions, time of day, wind direction and many other parameters. The game will take place at Haneda Airport in Tokyo. It is the largest airport in Japan, with four runways and serving 80 million passengers a year!
