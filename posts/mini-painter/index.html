Game Description#

Mini Painter is an isometric tabletop experience focusing on a miniature crafter painting miniatures. Players observe a character’s daily routine, guiding them through the process of applying color to small figures. The key mechanics require maintaining a balance between work and rest: excessive diligence leads to burnout, and prolonged inactivity leads to loss of motivation. Personal space is an important element of gameplay, offering options for furnishing and arranging a room. The environment directly affects the artist’s creative output.

Progression in Mini Painter links room decor to artist productivity. Adding new items to the environment increases the hero’s creative potential, which affects the outcome of each job. As the activity continues, the character’s skill also increases, allowing him to complete tasks faster. This simulator offers 24 unique figure models to unlock, each of which is a reference to famous images from the popular media environment. Completing these tasks brings us closer to the long-term goal - a well-deserved vacation for the master.

The gameplay loop is designed for long immersion and offers approximately 70 hours of content. Development in the project occurs slowly, encouraging a methodical approach to customizing the environment and career growth. The main challenge is to constantly monitor the hero’s energy and motivation to continue his activities. Everything that happens is presented in pixel style with an isometric perspective. The entire title functions directly on the desktop, integrating into the usual use of the computer.
