Game Description#

InOctodad Dadliest Catch players will have to play pirates for free online, controlling Octodads pretending to be people. You can play with a friend locally or compete with random people online online. The gameplay focuses on funny ragdoll and physics. You will need to perform a variety of tasks, destroying objects along the way. Online play includes competition and co-op modes.

The action takes place in a huge aquarium. Octopus: Father’s Catch offers to take control of a human-like octopus to perform all sorts of bizarre antics. For example, the possibility of a free online game offers to go a certain distance, hitting as few surrounding objects as possible, or vice versa - to destroy everything in your path. There is a huge amount of humor in Octodad Dadliest Catch.

Up to four people can compete. Character customization available. Various hidden ties can be found throughout the levels. Another special feature of multiplayer is that all participants can control one character. Each is responsible for a specific limb. You can create your own levels by constructing a location from a wide list of different objects and materials. With the help of the editor, obstacles and traps are created that complicate the already difficult life of a human octopus. Levels can be shared with friends. There are tournament tables.
