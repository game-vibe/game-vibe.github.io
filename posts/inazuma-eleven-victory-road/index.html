Game Description#

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road opens up the possibility of collecting over 5,400 characters from the history of the franchise under your command, which becomes a reality in Chronicles mode. Together with Victorio, a boy from the future, you can relive iconic matches of the past, forming a dream team of legendary football players capable of crushing any opponent. This mode takes a deep dive into the series' legacy, recreating iconic rivalries and opening up new tactical options using classic lineups.

The events of the new storyline take place 25 years after the original story, and at the center of the story are two completely different characters. Destin Billows enters South Cirrus High School in search of a world without football. He faces off against Harper Evans, a true football monster from the prestigious Raymond School, famous for its championship team. The intersection of their destinies in INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road sets off a chain of incredible events. Their encounter becomes the catalyst for a story of friendship, rivalry and a true passion for the sport.

The path to the football Olympus runs through:

Collecting and then training thousands of available athletes. Teams face off in intense online tournaments. Creation of a unique base city in the Communication Station mode. Revealing a new story full of drama and unexpected twists.

Visual storytelling is taken to a whole new level with numerous cinematic anime cutscenes. These episodes, which have the longest running time of the entire series, deepen the drama and make the story even more exciting.
