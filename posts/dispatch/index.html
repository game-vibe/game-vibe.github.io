Game Description#

Dispatch takes the superhero genre and turns it on its head, placing it in the mundane yet unpredictable world of office life. Instead of fighting evil aliens or saving cities, the title follows Robert Robertson, a former superhero called Mecha Man, who is now forced to find his place in a superhero control center after his trusty mech suit is destroyed in a fight with his nemesis. So what? Now he controls a team of former supervillains.

That’s right, the very villains he once fought are now on his payroll, and Robert must juggle office politics, emergencies and personal drama while trying to keep the city from falling apart. At the heart of Dispatch is a strategy map that acts as a command center. This is where gamers can view active emergencies that require immediate attention. Heroes must be sent to solve these situations, but it’s not just about sending the strongest hero to complete the task.

Decisions made on this map affect how characters interact and develop over time. One wrong move could take Robert’s command, ruin relationships with other heroes, or worse, cause them to reconsider their allegiance. It’s like managing a small dysfunctional family, only the stakes are a little higher - we’re talking about life or death situations.
