Game Description#

The competitive shooterMuffled Warfare invites you to play online with a friend or random players, participating on a pirate for free in blind shootouts where you only need to rely on your ears. You can compete online in teams or play in deathmatch mode. A special feature of Muffled Warfare gameplay is binaural sound. The action takes place in dark rooms. When moving, the outlines of surrounding objects are highlighted. You need to move around the location as quietly as possible in order to prevent the enemy from detecting you. Silence is golden. As soon as a character makes a sound, he gives away his location.

A wide selection of firearms is presented. Heroes are able to crouch, jump, hide behind cover and shoot. Online play is represented by fast-paced matches in which you need to destroy your opponents. Using echolocation, the opponent’s location is calculated. The possibility of free online play forces all participants to move as silently as possible. Stealth helps prevent footsteps from echoing throughout the room. Before the match starts, you can set the color scheme for ambient sounds. A total of 4 colors are available, which stand out brightly against the background of pitch darkness. The gameplay is first-person action. Each participant chooses an individual type of weapon. Pistols, shotguns, rifles and many other guns are available.
