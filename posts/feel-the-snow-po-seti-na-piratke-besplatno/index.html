Game Description#

Feel The Snow can be played online as a pirate with a friend, using all available multiplayer content for free. Online and LAN connections are available. Allows you to choose one of the characters to go on an adventure. It is necessary to collect resources, build a base, do crafting, etc. Featuring plenty of puzzles, tough boss battles, and more. The possibility of free online play allows you to go together into a harsh and cold sandbox world where you need to survive.

A story campaign is available where the only goal is not to die. Along the way you meet various charismatic characters. These can be both NPCs and living people. Strong bosses pose the greatest threat. Each has its own unique properties and attack patterns. The difficulty of battles increases dynamically. The online game Feel The Snow gives you the opportunity to arm yourself with a variety of weapons and equipment. You can study magic.

By collecting resources, you need to build a house, protecting it from external threats. Co-op play allows up to four players to team up. Two players control Annie and Willie, and the rest control snowmen. Events take place during a harsh winter. It is required to use the strengths of the heroes to survive during the blizzard. Locations are generated randomly, as are trees, stones, chests, monsters, and hiding places. Randomization increases the replayability of the project, allowing each new launch to go on a completely unique journey.
