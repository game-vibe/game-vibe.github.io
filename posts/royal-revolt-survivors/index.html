Game Description#

Royal Revolt Survivors is a continuation of the famous franchise, taking gamers into a fantasy world that is being desecrated by hordes of dark forces. In the role of one of the Warriors from the star cast of heroes in the Royal Revolt universe, you will have to repel the invaders and cleanse your native lands of filth. This project combines elements of a slasher film and a simplified roguelike, where the main task is to survive under the onslaught of hundreds of enemies in a variety of dynamic arenas. Each race forces you to adapt to new conditions, improve your skills and experiment with your available arsenal in order to stop the invasion.

The key mechanic is based on constantly strengthening the character during battle. With each new level, powerful upgrades and abilities are unlocked, allowing you to create unique attack combinations. You can combine countless types of Weapons and Trinkets to find the most effective tactics against enemies with different behavior and attack styles. The Royal Revolt Survivors project offers both single-player mode and cooperative playthrough, designed for a group of up to four people, where well-coordinated teamwork becomes the key to victory over the most powerful bosses.

Each Warrior has his own set of unique skills that must be mastered for maximum effectiveness in battle. During forays, you can gain skills such as Monastery Brew, which allows you to throw fireballs at nearby enemies. The gameplay forces you to constantly move, dodge attacks and wisely choose the moment for a counterattack, be it close combat or distance attacks. As you progress, the heroes become stronger, opening up access to new strategic opportunities to cleanse the corrupted lands and return peace to the kingdom.
