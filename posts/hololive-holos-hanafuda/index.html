Game Description#

hololive Holo’s Hanafuda is a card battler game with fully voiced dialogue from the talents of hololive. The story follows Nakiri Ayame, Shirakami Fubuki, Ookami Mio and Sakura Miko, who find themselves in a world based on hanafuda imagery. In this country, unknown individuals challenge the four protagonists, involving them in a series of card battles. The outcome of the fights will decide the fate of the heroines, but along the way they will also meet allies who are ready to provide support.

What awaits in this card world?

Story-driven adventure with famous talents. A unique variation of the Holo Awase rules with special skills. A hint system for quickly mastering the rules of hanafuda. Online matches against gamers from all over the world. Creative mode for creating and sharing bento boxes.

The gameplay of hololive Holo’s Hanafuda is based on the traditional Japanese card game, using a deck of 12 suits corresponding to the months. The title features a variant of the Holo Awase rules, functioning as a variation of the Koi-Koi. Players use a special deck with illustrations, and by combining cards with the same characters, they can activate unique skills. On-screen markers and tips help you understand the mechanics.

In addition to the single-player campaign, the project offers online matches against other gamers. You can choose the type of competition, create a lobby, or send an invitation to a friend for direct confrontation. A separate mode allows you to create custom bento boxes. Points earned in games unlock a variety of ingredients and box decorations. After putting them together for lunch, you can share your culinary creations with others online.
