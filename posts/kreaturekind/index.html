Game Description#

KreatureKind sets players a fateful task - to save magic from complete and irrevocable extinction. To do this, you don’t have to fight, but conduct heated debates with various monsters, using cards from the assembled deck as arguments. Successful completion of a verbal battle convinces your opponent to join your cause, while failure brings the world closer to disaster. Despite their apparent simplicity, the mechanics have depth, offering a serious challenge for fans of tactical planning. The journey takes place across procedurally generated maps, where you need to win over entire factions to your side.

The central element of the gameplay is the formation of a squad of seven available characters. Among them are both the melancholy young man Luca and Riley, whose dance moves become a powerful argument in the argument. Each hero has unique strengths and weaknesses that require the right combination to achieve victory. Completing KreatureKind involves constantly discovering new cards and improving abilities. Even plush toys can help with this by enhancing debating skills. If you fail on the current map, you will have to go through it again, but all previous progress will be saved.

The art of persuasion in details:

Assemble the perfect team by combining the abilities of seven dissimilar heroes. Create unique decks by unlocking and upgrading dozens of cards for different arguing styles. Find and use plush toys to gain an edge in the toughest debates. Gain the trust of magical factions as you advance through ever-changing locations.
