Game Description#

The Long Dark is a cooperative survival game that you can play online for free with a friend or explore the coast of Canada online online with other players. The adventure begins when a group of survivors awaken from a plane crash somewhere near the village of La Ku’alams near Prince Rupert. Multiplayer allows you to explore a huge open world together. You can unite in groups or fight with each other. Players pursue only one global goal - to stay alive in this harsh world. For all survivors, only a long darkness awaits, in which many dangers and darkness lurk. Game mechanics consist of first-person action and simulation. The possibility of free online play allows a team of up to four people to join forces. The following parameters apply: air temperature, hunger, fatigue, wind speed, etc. Every physical aspect directly affects gameplay.

Of particular danger in The Long Dark are wild animals, be it wolves, bears, crows, etc., as well as adverse weather conditions. You need to hide from the cold and storm. More than 50 square kilometers of territory remain to be explored. You can find more than 100 useful items, including equipment, tools, weapons, first aid kits, clothing, etc. The plot part tells about an airplane pilot named Will McKenzie. After the disaster, he tries to find the lost Astrid. It will take 15 hours of real time to complete the single-player campaign. The story is divided into episodes, such as Don’t Go Humblely, Light Fugue, etc. They go under the general name Wintermute. Added challenge modes. For example, Whiteout, where you need to hold out as long as possible during a blizzard, or Victim, where you have to run away from a bloodthirsty bear.
