Game Description#

Tiny Racing is an arcade competition that immerses pixel graphics and high speeds in the nostalgic atmosphere of racing with a view of the world. Gamers have to take a variety of vehicles under the control, from trucks to car car, each of which has unique characteristics. Finding 18 unique tracks scattered along three different settings, participants compete both day and night. The development of intuitive management becomes the key to victory, allowing you to make spectacular drifts and overtake rivals on the way to the championship title. The confrontation unfolds not only with computer opponents, the complexity of which is configured, but also in the local multiplayer mode.

Instead of strict linear passage, a quarry is built through participation in various events. Having taken part in fast races, tests for a while or championships of 12 races, you can earn in -game currency. The funds received in Tiny Racing are spent on the purchase of new cars, expanding the available fleet and opening fresh tactical opportunities for future competitions. This project offers a dynamic gameplay, where each race becomes a test of reflexes and driving skills.

The aesthetics of the project is deeply rooted in the culture of the 80s and 90s, which is reflected in a bright color palette and stylized graphics. The developers offer not just a competition, but a full retro adventure. You can concentrate on honing skills in single modes, such as a race with your own “ghost” to set a circle record, or share excitement with friends. Multipoling battles support from 2 to 4 people, turning races into a stress for the first place. Each victory brings closer to the rank of absolute champion of this pixel world.
