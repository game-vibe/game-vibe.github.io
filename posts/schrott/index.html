Game Description#

SCHROTT is a roguelike racing game where performing crazy stunts and collecting valuable loot provide the basis for deep vehicle customization. Instead of ordinary machines, there are absurd designs that lend themselves to all sorts of improvements to wreak even more chaos. Each race turns into a hunt for resources that allow you to turn your car into a unique weapon of destruction on the road. Arcade controls make the gameplay accessible and intuitive from the first minutes, and high-speed action does not let you relax. The rides take place along procedurally generated tracks designed in the style of JunkPunk, which ensures that each new playthrough is unique and forces you to constantly adapt to changing conditions.

The central character of this dynamic project is a raccoon who gets behind the wheel to create total chaos. The variety in SCHROTT comes not only from the numerous upgrades, but also from the other available drivers. Among them you can choose a penguin or a rabbit, each of which has its own passive skills that affect your driving style. Travel is not limited to one type of terrain, offering 3 maps and a completely new biome to explore. Two additional abilities expand your arsenal of techniques for performing spectacular maneuvers. A random loot system and special bonuses for equipment sets make each session unpredictable.
