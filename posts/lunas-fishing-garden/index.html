Game Description#

Luna’s Fishing Garden is a two -dimensional adventure, the mechanics of which are tied to research, fishing and construction. Traveling through the surrounding waters, you need to catch fish to sell it to the fox. A dream garden is being built with the proceeds. The heroine can plant trees and plants, construct water bodies, as well as populate animals on developing islands.

The game process Luna’s Fishing Garden has no restrictions and timers - everything can be done at its pace, enjoying the pacifying gameplay. A number of plot quests are available, but no one leads players by the handle, allowing you to engage in various secondary activities. Exploring various archipelagoes, it is allowed to decorate their appearance, giving the islands of beauty and greatness. An animals play a special role in the development of continents. The mechanics are aimed at immersion, allowing for a second to forget about the matters of the pressing and simply dissolve in a leisurely peaceful gameplay, without dynamic action and shootings. Exploring the islands, you can get acquainted with spirits and find out the features of each individual continent. Key game mechanics:

the ability to pump a fisherman’s skill; more than 20 species of fish; Immersive approach to fishing: each fish has unique properties; Creating your garden: planting trees, hanging plants, etc.; the settlement of the islands with a variety of fauna; Traveling by boat on different continents.
