Game Description#

In Shrine’s Legacy, two heroes, Rio and Reima, must confront a dark force that threatens the world of Ardemia. Their main task is to restore the Sword of the Holy, a powerful weapon necessary to save all things. To do this, they need to find and combine 8 elemental magic stones that store particles of the necessary power. The narrative focuses on the characters, developing bonds between the protagonists and the residents who receive help from them along the way.

Shrine’s Legacy gameplay features a top-down view and real-time combat. Gamers explore a vast world and descend into dungeons full of monsters and bosses. The title supports single play and local co-op, where in the first case the partner is controlled by AI. A sophisticated equipment system ensures stat development, and exploring hidden corners rewards with valuable finds, including crafting materials and currency.

Magic, secrets and battles of the world of Ardemia:

The search for eight stones to restore the legendary Sword of the Holy. Dynamic battles in real time alone or with a friend in co-op mode. Solve puzzles in dungeons using unique spells. Collect valuable resources and equipment to strengthen your heroes and learn new abilities.

In the dungeons, players will find both battles and puzzles solved with the help of magic. Defeating the bosses guarding these places will give you items to improve your stats and unlock new skills. The storyline confronts the protagonists with difficult choices against the backdrop of the possible destruction of Ardemia, raising the theme of self-sacrifice. The entire game explores how Rio, Reima, and the other inhabitants cope with loss while the main characters determine the price of their victory.
