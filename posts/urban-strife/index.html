Game Description#

Urban Strife takes place in a post-apocalyptic world, and the gameplay is presented in the style of old school TBS. The main character lives in a dangerous environment, where every resident does everything for his own benefit. Resources are running out, there are no jobs, and most kill and rob to survive. The player can enter into alliances by choosing 1 of several factions, automatically becoming an enemy of the remaining factions.

As soon as the central character chooses a side, he will have to carry out assignments for rewards and just like that, increasing the reputation and trust of people. He will have to make forays, get food and materials, and then take them to the base. The resources found will allow you to create weapons and useful consumables. Equipment items gradually wear out and require repair after prolonged use. People within the faction need food and new opportunities, which only the protagonist can obtain.

The combat system is made in a turn-based style. There is a storyline that develops as you complete the main quests. The user has numerous choices that influence the outcome of the entire story. It all starts when the ward awakens from amnesia. They told him that he was in a half-dead state, but kind people helped him out. Having risen to his feet, he must become the leader of a remote urban shelter in the southern United States, and then help people. You can negotiate with other organizations and create alliances.
