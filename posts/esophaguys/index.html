Game Description#

Esophaguys features characters who are elderly people with the ability to elongate their necks. Players control these extending necks to bite, grab, and move around locations, working with or against each other. The title supports from 1 to 4 people depending on the selected activity. The action unfolds to a soundtrack of ringing tones and senile grunting, creating an auditory background for the entire gameplay.

The co-op component of Esophaguys allows 2-4 players to work together to explore the environment and solve puzzles to reactivate ancient monuments. The competitive part invites gamers to engage in direct competition in team competitions or single battles in the arena. A separate masochistic mode removes checkpoints and terra firma, introducing a series of challenges in environments designed for high-speed runs and testing endurance.

The story mode is designed for 1-4 people and invites you to go on a narrative journey with the goal of reuniting a lost people. This adventure is narrated and explores the nature of the characters' physical characteristics. Completing all game modes contributes to the development of the central hub world, where everyone works together to restore the monument. Completing these tasks unlocks the attire of relatives, allowing you to change the appearance of heroes using the outfits of forgotten relatives.
