Game Description#

Clock Tower: Rewind is a modern reimagining of the classic survival horror game that first appeared in Japan in 1995. The plot centers on Jennifer Simpson, an orphan who ends up in the Barrows family mansion, teeming with dark secrets and dangers lurking within them. Taking on the role of Jennifer, gamers navigate the disturbing corridors of the mansion, searching for objects and piecing together the plot, while trying to avoid the infamous Scissorman. This relentless antagonist wields a huge pair of scissors, adding a unique twist to the gameplay.

The core gameplay of Clock Tower: Rewind focuses on running, hiding, and solving various puzzles to survive. The mechanics are simple and at the same time exciting. Users control Jennifer using shoulder buttons for lateral movement, and a joystick or D-pad makes it easier to interact with the environment. This setting emphasizes exploration and strategy, as each action can lead to different results. The tension builds as players face various challenges while trying to stay one step ahead of Scissorman.

Every decision made in the mansion matters, so players must think fast and act even faster. One of the features is multiple endings that add complexity to the gameplay. Thanks to random elements, no playthrough will be the same. Users may receive different results depending on their choices and the paths they take.
