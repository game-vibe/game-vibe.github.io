Game Description#

On the pirateBattlezone Gold Edition you can play online with a friend for free, fighting each other in futuristic tanks. Co-op mode and versus matches are available online online. You will need to control a tank of the future called Cobra. The network game is designed for battles of up to 4 people simultaneously. Transport can be customized using a variety of modifications: weapons, improved body and much more. Battlezone Gold Edition’s free online play allows you to split into two teams to battle it out in a variety of versus modes.

You can install up to 10 unique sirens, including a ship, a clown, a sports car, a train or a motorcycle. There are 15 types of camouflage available, with which you can customize the appearance of the tanks. Vehicle skins are taken from various comics and games, including Judge Dredd and Rogue Trooper. A total of 8 unique buildings are available. Weapons include missiles, lasers, artillery, EMP and protective shields. In the single-player campaign, as in multiplayer battles, some game elements are randomly generated: the environment, enemies or tasks. Additional Classic mode, in which the vehicle is controlled by two tracks. In a special menu you can look at the leaderboards to find out your position in the global ranking.
