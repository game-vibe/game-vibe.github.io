Game Description#

Outpost Infinity Siege is an exciting online game where you can play online with your friends. Take control of a mobile outpost as you venture deep into forbidden territory with the help of your trusty XEN firearm. These weapons pack a powerful punch, but you’ll need to collect unique weapon blocks to truly bolster your defenses. Play online with friends and take advantage of online gaming features. The game is available for free, even if you play on a pirated platform.

In Outpost Infinity Siege, the world is your oyster (or more accurately, your resource reserves). Explore a diverse environment, collect materials, merge and upgrade your outposts. This strategic element will allow you to set up your defenses and adapt to any threats you face. Speaking of threats, you’ll have to master XEN’s firearms. This weapon boasts various Protoslagm attributes, and you will need to choose the appropriate ammo for it.

Dive into different difficulty levels, explore unique maps in exploration mode and conquer new zones full of dangers and potential rewards. Want to team up? Team up with friends and fight for survival in Recovery Day or Endless mode. Will you and your comrades be able to survive endless waves of monsters?
