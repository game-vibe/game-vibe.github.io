Game Description#

Head To Hell begins with the main character waking up on a moving train with no memory of how he got there or what lies ahead. The only thing that gives any semblance of direction is a text message. Not the most pleasant thing when you’re on a train that never seems to stop. The message gives clues, but they are vague and mysterious, leaving the user guessing about what it really is. This uncertainty becomes a constant companion as you move forward, trying to understand what this train is and why you are on it.

The train itself is like a puzzle, and each choice made at each carriage door leads the gamer down a new path. Some of these doors lead to strange and creepy places, but others? Well, let’s just say they don’t make the game any easier. There is always the feeling that you are missing something, a clue or information that could be crucial. This is not a game you can beat without thinking; every decision matters, even if it is not immediately clear how. And this is half the difficulty - to understand the rules of the game as you play through it.

As they progress through Head To Hell, users encounter many oddities. The environment changes, sometimes imperceptibly, sometimes dramatically. At one moment you can find yourself in an ordinary passenger car, and at the next you can find yourself in a place that defies logic. At first glance, none of this makes much sense. There is a feeling that you are being pulled somewhere deep, even if you don’t know what that something is.
