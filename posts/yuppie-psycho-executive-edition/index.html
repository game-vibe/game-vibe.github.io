Game Description#

The story of the hero of Yuppie Psycho: Executive Edition, a surreal pixel adventure game, is familiar to every office worker. A young and inexperienced clerk, Brian Pasternak, dreams of getting a promising position in the large corporation Sintracorp. To do this, he needs to survive the nightmare of his first working day and complete one assignment.

Brian is unlucky with career guidance: he has to become a hunter of the “Witch”. The eccentric lady, whose supernatural powers contributed to the development of the company in the past, has now returned and carried out a bloody reign of terror. The capture of the Witch was entrusted to Pasternak as a baptism of fire. Checklist for the first day:

Find out the office schedule. There is time for business, an hour for fun, a split second for panicked flight from otherworldly entities. Get acquainted with the workplace, ride the elevator and see what is happening on other floors. Take part in discussions near the water cooler, make useful contacts, listen to the latest gossip and learn the dirty secrets of your colleagues. Assess occupational safety. More precisely, its absence. Conduct a situation analysis. Solving riddles and puzzles of varying levels of difficulty opens up new opportunities. Develop a five-year plan that will influence future developments.

Yuppie Psycho skillfully plays with the phobias of people employed in certain fields. Many psychological associations make the player feel like he is in the place of the main character. Surreal nonsense combined with lush pixel art and a realistic setting turn the game into the quintessence of real madness. The plot involves several possible endings, so the ending of the story will depend on the actions of the user.
