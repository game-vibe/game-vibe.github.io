Game Description#

Salt 2: Shores of Gold tells the story of the complex life of a lone pirate who travels on a small sailing ship. Unlike other games on a similar theme, this project does not focus on sea battles or robbing other ships. The player will have to spend the bulk of his time constantly exploring the vast expanses of water and searching for treasures.

The main character feels that the ocean is his real home. Only here can he breathe deeply and enjoy life. But surviving in such confined spaces can be difficult. To feed yourself, you will have to engage in fishing and, if possible, hunting. The action takes place during the period of development of wild lands, so most of the islands have not yet been found by Europeans and are fraught with many secrets. You can get unique items by landing on new islands or even while fishing. Suddenly the hook will catch on an interesting box and catch real gold. But this will not happen often, so you should not rely only on a fishing rod.

Sometimes on the found areas of land in Salt 2: Shores of Gold you can meet other pirates, and they are almost always aggressive and do not intend to share what they have acquired. Therefore, the player can either engage them in a saber fight, or run away, leaving behind a potentially valuable prize. To keep users from getting bored, the map and the loot on it are constantly generated randomly, so no two playthroughs will be the same.
