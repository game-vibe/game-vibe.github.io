Game Description#

Master of Magic (2022) is a remake of the once classic project, which at one time attracted the attention of a large number of people. Users, as before, will have to go a long way through the territory of two neighboring worlds - Arcanus and Mirror - in order to face the strongest sorcerers for the title of the strongest. In total, there are 14 different wizards to choose from with their own unique abilities, who, among other things, are representatives of different schools. Therefore, no matter who the gamer chooses, it will in any case be a unique experience.

It is important to consider that even though Master of Magic (2022) offers you to play as a specific character, you will not be fighting with his hands. This mage plays the role of commander and general, gathering and training entire groups of elves, dragons, minions and other units. They contain all the destructive power, but the character is able to provide support by using spells and destroying the enemy’s formation. Each battle takes place step-by-step on separate sections of the map. Each sector is divided into hexagons, due to which you can immediately understand how the squad moves. Moreover, you can strengthen your squad not only with new fighters, but also with the help of randomly generated items. There can be more than 250 of them in total, and each of these things provides its own advantages on the battlefield. Therefore, it is absolutely not necessary to adapt to a single strategy, but to constantly change your approach to passing, surprising your opponents with your actions.
