Game Description#

The Precint is a police officer single -user simulator that immerses gamers into the lively urban environment of the 1980s. Having entered the place of Officer Nick Cordell Jr., users are traveling as a beginner who recently graduated from the police academy. Against the background of Averno City, an active life is in full swing, a wide range of procedured crimes is presented in it, from minor violations of the rules of the road to large robberies of banks. This city functions almost like a character whose corner opens new problems and stories. Gamers not only patrol the streets, but also encounter the all -encompassing secret of the murder of Father Nick, which gives the gameplay additional depth.

The Precint mechanics combines the elements of a police simulator and action, allowing players to participate in various law enforcement measures. Using a camera with a downward look, gamers can respond to crimes, pursue the suspects and even cause reinforcement if the situation requires that. Each interaction requires a thoughtful approach, because in Advenchur special attention is paid to compliance with the law. The commission of unlawful actions will not do without consequences that can vary from the loss of the title to other intra -game punishments. Such an emphasis on legality prompts users to critically approach their actions in the world.
