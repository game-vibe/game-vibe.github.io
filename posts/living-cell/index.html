Game Description#

Living Cell is a dark adventure game with survival elements that immerses you in the atmosphere of Samosbor - a dystopian world where residents live in endless apartment buildings called “Gigahrushchevkas”. In this exciting story, you will take the place of the liquidator, who is entrusted with the responsibility of maintaining order and security in the quarter. At the beginning of the story, players find themselves the only survivors in block G-326 after a mysterious incident. They embark on a dangerous journey, carefully exploring room by room, dodging poisonous slimes and other dangers. Resource management becomes an important aspect: gas mask filters, flashlight batteries and food supplies require close attention.

Living Cell features a variety of puzzles and riddles that must be solved to progress through the intricate story. In addition to the challenges, the game reveals a fascinating storyline through the environment and interactions with the residents of the block. Getting to know the lives and surroundings of the neighborhood’s residents reveals their dark stories, adding depth to the overall experience. The decisions made by the gamer significantly influence the outcome of the game: depending on his actions and choices, several endings await him. An atmosphere of gloomy hopelessness and decline has been skillfully created here, inspired by the famous network projects “Gigahrushchevka” and “Self-Collection”, depicting a gloomy alternative reality of the post-Soviet space.
