Game Description#

Apes Warfare is a turn-based tactical strategy game that pits squads of armed primates against human armies on compact, caged battlefields. Success in a confrontation depends on the competent management of resources, synergy between different types of troops and the skillful use of landscape features that can provide a tactical advantage. Under the command of the gamer are not just monkeys, but full-fledged combat units with an impressive arsenal, including heavy armored vehicles, aviation and even naval forces. The story campaign reveals the story of the primate uprising against their former overlords, offering a narrative with elements of dark humor and unexpected twists.

The project combines 2D and 3D graphics, creating a unique visual style. In addition to the single-player mode, Apes Warfare offers the opportunity to compete in intense multiplayer battles, where strategic thinking comes to the fore. To achieve victory, gamers will have to:

use unique superpowers to turn the tide of battle. adapt to dynamic changes in weather conditions that affect tactics. Effectively combine over 20 unit types with different strengths. create your own maps using the built-in level editor.
