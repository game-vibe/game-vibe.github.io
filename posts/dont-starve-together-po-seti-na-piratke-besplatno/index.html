Game Description#

Don’t Starve Together is an open-world survival simulator that allows you to join forces with a friend as a pirate for free to fight, farm, build and explore procedurally generated locations, or participate in online confrontations over the Internet. Players find themselves in the vast wilderness, where, as night falls, the local fauna becomes incredibly bloodthirsty. The only thing monsters are afraid of is fire. A torch or fire is an indispensable weapon against night creatures.

The map is represented by a variety of biomes, which contain a huge amount of resources, threats, caves, forests, etc. Multiplayer requires diving into the Ancient Archive or exploring the Lunar Islands, trying to collect as many minerals as possible to build a camp or craft useful items. You will have to fight shadow creatures, evil plants, bosses and other enemies.

Everything in the world of Don’t Starve Together wants to turn the hero into an eerie, shapeless ghost. Alone or using the option of online play, you can grow your own farm. You can plow and sow fields with seeds, harvest crops, etc. It is very important to take care of the crops, water the fruits on time, etc. The action takes place in a strange and mystical world full of secrets and mysteries.

Bloodthirsty creatures inhabit the territories. You never know what danger is hidden in the forest. By collecting resources, you can craft items and create huge structures that protect you from attacks from monsters. It is important to solve the mystery of the Constant, located on these lands. In a team with other players or alone, you will have to survive many nights, warming up by the fire. The only important thing in the game is to avoid starvation.
