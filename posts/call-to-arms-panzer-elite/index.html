Game Description#

Call to Arms: Panzer Elite puts a World War II era tank platoon under the player’s command. In the role of a German officer in 1944, you have to stop the advancing enemy forces on French territory. Players form their own armored units and lead them into battle. Control occurs from both a third and a first person to assess the situation and direct control. Gameplay focuses on platoon-level tactics during historical battles, requiring coordinated action to defeat the enemy.

The combat system in Call to Arms: Panzer Elite is based on realistic ballistics and a well-developed damage model. Battlefields are completely destructible, changing the tactical landscape as clashes progress. The 5-quest single-player campaign, available for co-op play, offers a cohesive story. As an alternative, a Skirmish mode with 9 missions is presented, allowing you to play on the German or American side. The goal is to capture key objectives using powerful weapons like the Tiger tank or StuH 42.

What awaits the commander on the battlefield:

Command an entire platoon with switchable 1st and 3rd person views. Well-developed ballistics physics and a completely destructible environment. Two modes: story and tactical missions in Skirmish. Recreation of real battles and personal fates of crew members.

The campaign recreates the pivotal events of World War II, focusing on major tank battles in the areas of Saint-Lo, Mortain and the Ardennes. A distinctive feature of the narrative is the personal crew, whose stories are revealed over time. The player will have to witness the victories and difficulties of his subordinates as they complete tasks. This project offers 20 controllable combat vehicles, and each faction has 10 historically accurate tanks and armored vehicles.
