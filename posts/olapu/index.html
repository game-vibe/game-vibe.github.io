Game Description#

OLAPU is a test for a well-coordinated team, challenging raid bosses whose mechanics are reminiscent of large-scale battles in MMORPGs. Defeating opponents like Guardian Seraphina or Empress Elisera will require not only perfect knowledge of attack patterns, but also impeccable coordination. Each successful foray into the Valley of Elisera is rewarded, allowing you to unlock permanent upgrades, legendary gear with semi-random stats, and even new characters for future runs. Progress between sessions is the foundation for conquering increasingly difficult peaks.

The project is based on the concept of a high-speed cooperative action game with roguelite elements, designed for a group of up to 4 people. Every playthrough in OLAPU becomes a race for survival, where you need to strengthen yourself as quickly as possible to withstand the growing waves of enemies. The gameplay forces the squad to constantly adapt in order to defeat the powerful entities that inhabit this world.

There is a wide roster of heroes to choose from, including melee fighters, marksmen and support characters such as Chii, Yuvi, Tavi and Kana. By destroying opponents, the team collects stars, which are then exchanged for constellation fragments, which open access to summoning allies or powerful chain reactions. As you progress, the heroes evolve, receiving exclusive upgrades that change their fighting style and tactical capabilities.
