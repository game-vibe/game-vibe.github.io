Game Description#

Ikarus Parkor is transferred to the role of a contestant in a television competition arranged for the entertainment of a chosen group. The contestants voluntarily join this event, after which they are transported to a secret underground place for the test. The goal is to climb the massive 850-meter vertical structure from its base to the top. Successful overcoming the obstacles of the tower means obtaining a monetary prize and personal freedom, which is awarded to the finish.

Basic rules of the cruel show Ikarus Parkour:

Climb to the top of the 850-meter tower. Get a huge amount of money and long -awaited freedom. Avoid the only mistake leading to the fatal fall. Become a sight to entertain the mysterious elite.

Climbing is a situation of life and death for each involved, since one mistake leads to a fatal fall. This game offers two sets of lifting rules: a casual mode with frequent control points and a complex one that removes them completely with the addition of a timer for high -speed passage. Each jump and maneuver is a calculated action with a final outcome, and survival entirely depends on the continuous movement of the gamer forward and its spatial orientation.

The very huge structure is divided into four separate levels. Each of these sections introduces its own aesthetic style along with new types of interference for movement. Difficulties grow as it moves from one zone to another, requiring a gamer over time more honed navigation skills. Such a titla structure allows you to explore the environment, while facing a systematic complication of the gameplay as it is ascended.
