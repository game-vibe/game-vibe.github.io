Game Description#

PlayingLEGO DC Super-Villains online with a friend is a real pleasure, because the plot centers on the villains of the DC Comics universe! On the pirate site, both online play and local split-screen mode are available. All in-game content and add-ons are included for free in this Lego adventure based on comic books about your favorite heroes and villains.

The online game LEGO DC Super-Villains involves a confrontation between two factions. It goes without saying that we are talking about the Justice League and the Injustice Team. The former includes Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, Flash, etc., while the anti-heroes are represented by the charismatic Joker, Harley Quinn, Scarecrow, Riddler and other charismatic maniacs. The Lego adaptation provides the opportunity for free online play in version mode, free adventure, and even allows you to go through the story campaign together.

Unlike other Lego superhero games, this adaptation focuses entirely on the villains, giving them the bulk of the story. You can also create your own characters and modify the skills of existing protagonists. As you progress, new opportunities and settings for the heroes open up. Upgrade and level up available.

The plot begins at the moment when the Justice League leaves Earth, leaving it in the care of themselves from a parallel universe. However, a certain Crime Syndicate, consisting of maniacs and murderers, feels unpunished. Villains team up to destroy Metropolis. The events will also take place in Gotham, where the Fear Toxin was used.
