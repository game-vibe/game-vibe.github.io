Game Description#

RuneScape: Dragonwilds introduces gamers to Ashenfall, the forgotten continent of the RuneScape universe where dragons have awakened. This open world experience challenges 1-4 players to survive together or alone. The gameplay is based on collecting resources, constructing buildings, developing skills and creating things. The main task is to hunt down the dragons and fight their Queen.

Survival in RuneScape: Dragonwilds is constantly complicated by the threat of dragons that roam the lands. Gamers need to level up their character, select weapons and carefully prepare to meet these opponents. Studying traces of dragon activity and exploring the continent reveals ancient knowledge about how to effectively fight and defeat them. Making potions, creating the right equipment, and leveling up your combat skills are necessary steps before the battle with the main forces of Ashenfall.

Interaction with the environment is a key element, with unique mechanics tied to the world. Characters can cut down trees, defend themselves with runic energy, and use special abilities gained from the Anima-rich landscape, such as destroying ore veins or using summoned tools to quickly gather resources. Traveling through different regions of Ashenfall reveals the ruins of ancient civilizations, allowing you to understand the essence of the transformative magic that works on these lands. Such research reveals the source and nature of the power available to adventurers.
