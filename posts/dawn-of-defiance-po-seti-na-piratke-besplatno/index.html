Game Description#

InDawn of Defiance you will play online, controlling Defir - a mortal who finds himself in a mythical world. Inspired by Greek myths, this multiplayer survival game allows you to immerse yourself in exciting adventures across the Internet online. Escaped to a deserted island, you’ll start from scratch and strive to become a god, and the ability to play with a friend makes this journey even more fun. It is important to note that to participate in the game you need a legal version, and playing on a pirated version or for free in this case will not work.

Build structures, create tools and fight creatures. The discontent of the underworld fuels your journey - a test set by Charon. Explore ruined lands, from cliffs to caves, to find secrets and resources. Craft equipment, build impressive buildings, and prepare for the ultimate challenge: challenging the gods. Defeat wild animals and fallen heroes to become stronger. Unlock recipes, upgrade equipment, and build structures that surpass Olympus. Choose wisely as your decisions shape your character. When you’re ready, head to the Forgotten Crossing. Find out the true purpose of your odyssey. Find the shrines of the Olympics, pass the trials and break the grip of the gods. Go it alone or with up to three friends in co-op multiplayer mode. Share resources, overcome challenges together and receive rewards.
