Game Description#

On the pirated version ofDawn of Magic 2 you can play online with a friend for free, going through the story campaign together online. The plot directly continues the original. Players will have to fight phantasmagorical creatures and enter into an unequal battle with the immortal Modo. The balance of power has been disrupted and the Legions of Shadows are eager to take over the world of the living. Online play allows you to team up with other players, protecting humanity from a threat from the other world.

You can fight with blades and also use magic spells. In total, more than 100 unique spells are available, aimed at defense and attack. Magic is based on 12 schools. Each has its own strengths and weaknesses. The opportunity for free online play invites the heroes to go into the labyrinths of Other Worlds, where they will have to engage in the final battle with the Shadows. Dynamic combat mode allows you to use a variety of combos and chains of attacks. The storyline consists of dozens of exciting missions, which take place in different parts of the continent. Game features of Dawn of Magic 2: a large fantasy world open for exploration; a huge number of main and minor quests; 12 magical schools that determine the style of play; over 100 spells aimed at attack and defense; a variety of leveling branches; melee combat and long-range attacks; strong enemies and powerful bosses; the atmosphere of a real adventurous adventure; a bright folk soundtrack.
