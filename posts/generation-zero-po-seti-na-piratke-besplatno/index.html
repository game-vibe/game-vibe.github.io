Game Description#

On the pirateGeneration Zero you can play online for free with a friend, acting in co-op against common enemies. You can connect online via the Internet to go through the campaign together. Events take place in an alternative Sweden in 1989. The Osterton region remains to be explored. He was attacked by robots. It is necessary to fight for survival for the entire human race.

The gameplay provides the opportunity for free online play for three people simultaneously. The mechanics of Generation Zero are tied to stealth, shootings, searching for resources and exploring a huge open-world location. A huge number of different hiding places, secrets and enemies are presented. Each enemy has unique characteristics and attack patterns. The online game invites you to join a professional team hunting for robotic mechanisms. Alone or together you need to explore forests, abandoned cities, fields, military bases, etc.

On your way you will meet other survivors. They are not always allies, so you should be wary of people in front of you. Everyone has their own difficult fate, so it is unknown what goal this or that group of survivors is pursuing. However, it is possible to create coalitions and resist the mechs together. You can create your own outpost that will protect you from the invasion of evil AI. The arsenal of weapons is represented by a huge number of large-caliber guns. Every robot has a weak point. By shooting at him, there is a chance to quickly eliminate the car.
