Game Description#

In Salt and Sanctuary, the player will have to explore a mysterious island that he ended up on as a result of a shipwreck. The setting is dark fantasy, and the mechanics of Dark Souls and Castlevania are taken as a reference point. The gameplay is presented in the form of a 2D side-scroller.

The player explores vast locations and fights difficult enemies. Each movement requires stamina points, be it running, rolling, hitting or blocking. Found weapons, armor, rings and various artifacts affect the hero’s movement speed. The main characteristics are represented by strength and dexterity. Ranged attacks available.

Leveling up is represented by a tree skill, but is tied to specializations. There is no way to view the map, so you should carefully remember the location of the locations. Instead of souls, the player collects salt, which can be spent on upgrading a hero or weapon. If you die, but the accumulated salt is lost. There is a possibility of returning it if you run to the place of death. Otherwise, it all disappears forever. A co-op for two has been implemented, where the second player controls a mercenary.

There is a war going on in the world of Salt and Sanctuary. But there is an island that does not participate in the bloody feud. “Sanctuaries” are places where travelers can feel protected. The protagonist accompanied the princess, who was supposed to get married to end the war. But as a result of the Kriken’s attack, their ship sank. Once on the mystical island, you will have to meet not only the creatures of darkness, but also the powerful ruler of these lands - the Nameless God.
