Game Description#

Metro Awakening is a story-driven first-person adventure created specifically for VR, immersing players in a post-apocalyptic Moscow. The action takes place in 2028 in the shadowy tunnels of the Moscow metro, which serve as a refuge for the last remnants of civilization after a nuclear disaster. Gamers take on the role of Serdar, a doctor navigating a world filled with danger while searching for his wife and the medicine she needs. There is an atmosphere of isolation here, and the remnants of humanity coexist with the ghosts of the past.

As Serdar delves deeper into the Metro, he encounters various problems. The project balances exploration, stealth and combat, requiring the player to think strategically. As you move through the dark tunnels, you feel a sense of urgency as resources dwindle. With limited ammunition and gear, gamers have to prioritize. Every encounter with hostile elements - bandits, mutants or supernatural forces - requires careful planning. The atmosphere remains tense, forcing users to weigh every action.

Metro Awakening’s game mechanics emphasize a slower, more thoughtful approach to combat. Serdar is not depicted as an experienced warrior; instead, he relies on cunning and stealth to overcome obstacles. The title encourages gamers to use the environment to their advantage. Hiding in the shadows, avoiding confrontations whenever possible, and using distraction tactics are key strategies.
