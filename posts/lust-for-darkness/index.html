Game Description#

Lust for Darkness is a horror adventure with elements of erotica and psychological thriller. Immerse yourself in the exciting, twisted worlds of a Victorian mansion and the otherworldly dimension of Lusst’ghaa. The plot begins with the disappearance of the wife of the protagonist, Jonathan Moon, who, having received the letter, immediately goes in search of his loved one.

Arriving at the area, you will find suspicious and half-naked individuals. They give in to lust and engage in obscene activities. It is later revealed that this is an occult ceremony to open portals to a parallel world. The entire area contains scenes of nudity, violence, coitus, and a lot of BDSM trinkets. The locations contain a lot of visually original ideas based on the works of artist Zdzislaw Beksiński. You will sometimes see surreal installations with a mysterious atmosphere. Beware of unknown creatures who strive to torment the main character. And from time to time you need to run away from them, or bypass the monsters using stealth, carefully avoiding the danger.

This project contains quest mechanics. And to advance through the plot, you periodically need to solve puzzles, find matches in objects, and study various notes and diaries. There are also original tasks that include playing the piano to open a secret door, or “connecting lines to get a picture.” Almost every object can be twirled in your hands after carefully examining it. This creates a certain interactivity with the environment. And it will tell you a lot about this mysterious world. And a unique, ideological atmosphere will be created by the soundtrack of the famous composer Draco Nared, who created the multifaceted music for this game.
