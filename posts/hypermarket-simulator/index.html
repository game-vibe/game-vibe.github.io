Game Description#

In Hypermarket Simulator, the player will be tasked with designing and managing a full-scale retail enterprise with departments of electronics, sportswear and home goods. Gameplay focuses on customizing the physical layout, selecting specific product sections, and organizing a vast commercial space. It is necessary to determine the location of shelving and retail equipment to influence customer flows, as well as strategically form storage areas in the utility room.

The main task is to build a multidisciplinary establishment from scratch, controlling every aspect of its presentation. The key mechanics of Hypermarket Simulator is the detailed control of the inventory, consisting of thousands of unique products. An essential element is the handling of perishable products: for example, frozen products must be stored properly to avoid spoilage making them unsuitable for sale.

This system affects the organization of warehouse areas to maintain a stable flow of assortment into the sales area. Additionally, setting prices and monitoring sales are critical to financial management. Strategic decisions about product selection and cost directly impact business profitability. The accumulated profit allows you to expand the outlet through the acquisition of additional space and the introduction of new product categories. As a business grows, new systems become necessary to manage the increased scale and volume of visitors.
