Game Description#

Darkwater gives gamers to command a submarine, which ended up in an ice trap on a foreign planet. This is a cooperative horror, designed to work from 1 to 4 people. Survival implies navigation in a hostile environment, clashes with enemy courts and underwater life forms, as well as an active search for resources necessary for further work of submarine. Success depends on the implementation of the necessary actions for existence in underland conditions.

Darkwater will have to control the customizable underwater apparatus in the frozen depths of the satellite of another planet, acting alone or as part of the team. Up to 4 people can form a crew to confront numerous deep -sea threats that this project presents. Activities include sea battles with other submarines and mooring its own ship to abandoned avantosts to search for supplies. The plot tie is associated with an outbreak of illness on the planet, which is why the authorities froze the surface of the ocean, thereby locating a command under an ice layer.

The reserves in the underwater world are limited, which makes the crews extract fuel and move between the advance. It is necessary to detect and examine the dilapidated underwater complexes on foot, collecting any valuable materials inside these structures. Such sorties are fraught with risk, because the teams can meet various opponents, including infected divers, deep -sea bites and alien crabs lurking in ruins. Arsenal is available to resolve such situations, including sledgehammers, share lamps, pistols and shotgun; Coordinated efforts are required to deliver a canister with fuel back to the submarine.
