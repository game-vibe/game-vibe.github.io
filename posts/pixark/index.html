Game Description#

Pixark is made in the style of the famous Maycraft, but the game has a good plot, which is revealed in different ways for each player. The world of wildlife includes more than 100 different creatures that we can clone, tame and use for our own purposes. Among them will be friendly inhabitants of the jungle, as well as aggressive representatives of the game world.

The mysterious lands made in the form of cubes are destroyed and built every minute. You can build a house in the ground or build a whole castle from available resources. They did not bypass the mine in which there are rare resources. The system of tasks asks the player to perform a number of actions, like building a house, making a workbench, cutting trees, digging the earth. Among the creatures around will be dinosaurs and mystical creatures, so part of the tasks is built on the study of flora and fauna around the character.

Your base will be a salvation for the same as you. On the map, tribes of other views will gradually appear with which you can either fight or unite against another tribe. The developers have foreseen cooperative and multi -user regimes in which you can create a tribe with friends and to be justice in the game world. Fly riding on the dragon, build locks, explore the Pixark world with your friends. Create a weapon, pump the abilities and characteristics of the character, do everything that the Creator in the New World can afford! A multiplayer and cooperative regime is available.
