Game Description#

BULT: Hunting simulator is a waterfowl hunting simulator in which the result is determined not by reaction speed, but by methodical planning. The protagonist finds himself in open spaces inspired by the harsh nature of Yakutia. The atmosphere is formed by misty swamps, wide lakes and quiet shores, where any mistake is instantly noticed. Success depends on the ability to read the landscape, understand bird habits, and quickly respond to changing conditions. Each fishing trip becomes a lesson in attentiveness and control, turning a well-aimed shot into a valuable achievement.

The behavior of birds is subject to complex algorithms: they have natural instincts, flock together and are sensitive to human actions. The ballistic model in BULT: Hunting simulator is highly developed, taking into account air resistance, the weight of the shot and its spread. The process of luring prey requires a strategic approach. Careful placement of decoys and timely use of decoys help direct the flock to the desired area, while excessive activity or careless noise can undo minutes of careful preparation.

One of the gameplay components is the ethical fishing system. The local ecosystem contains rare species listed in the Red Book, and their shooting entails game fines. This aspect encourages thoughtful actions and respectful attitude towards fauna. The project is aimed at connoisseurs of a thoughtful process, where patience is rewarded much more generously than reflexes. There is no place for arcade rush, and each trophy becomes a natural result of the demonstrated endurance and composure.
