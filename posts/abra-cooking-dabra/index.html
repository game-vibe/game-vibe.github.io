Game Description#

Abra-Cooking-Dabra is a cozy card simulator with puzzle and management elements, which takes place in the kitchen of a mysterious castle. The player finds himself in the role of a new chef in a fantasy land who faces severe punishment for accidentally destroying an old recipe book. This book was vital to the coming Royal Feast, and now the only way is to restore the lost formulas of outlandish British cuisine. You have to not only cook, but also serve various guests and even powerful bosses who are trying in every possible way to interfere with the process with their tricks.

The entire gameplay is based on card mechanics. To complete an order, you need to assemble a deck of ingredients, sauces and utensils. Every task, from chopping vegetables to washing plates, takes up precious time while dissatisfied diners wait for their food. A grumpy boss cat with a monocle is watching everything that happens, who only glances disapprovingly at the bustle. Successfully completed orders in Abra-Cooking-Dabra earn money needed to purchase new card packs, the contents of which are always random, or to upgrade kitchen equipment between over 30 levels.

Kitchen Challenges - From Chaos to Mastery:

Building and managing a deck for efficient order fulfillment. Growing fresh ingredients during a busy shift. Satisfying the needs of more than 10 types of picky guests with their own quirks. Experimental recreation of over 100 unique and complex recipes.
