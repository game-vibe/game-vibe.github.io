Game Description#

Smack Studio presents a sandbox fighting game. Players can play online with a friend, battling in both offline and online modes. The game features 16 base characters and 23 stages, plus a hidden arena just waiting to be discovered by eagle-eyed fighters. The network game offers pirate battles for free online. Rollback netcode ensures online battles for up to 4 players run smoothly, without lag and keeping the action intense.

At its core, Smack Studio offers more than just button fighting. The game’s signature feature is its creation tools, allowing players to bring their own pixel art characters to life. Using new 3D pixel art rotation technology, aspiring designers can animate their creations, giving them depth and dimension. The character editor simplifies the process, allowing you to create a fully playable fighter in less than half an hour. Stop-motion animation options add polish, and custom projectiles and effects enhance the visual experience.

Steam Workshop integration opens the floodgates to community-created content and fosters a vibrant ecosystem of shared characters and scenes. The game export feature allows artists to use their Smack Studio creations in other projects without entering into legal relations. Whether you’re a seasoned pixel artist or a newbie with a vision, Smack Studio’s tools are designed to simplify the creative process. It’s a sandbox where imagination meets combat, inviting players to create, fight and share in equal measure.
