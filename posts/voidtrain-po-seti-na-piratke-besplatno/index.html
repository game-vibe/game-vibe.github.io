Game Description#

Voidtrain is a survival game in an open world with the ability to play online with a friend for free on a pirate site. By uniting over the Internet, online players discover new mysterious worlds, meeting a variety of phantasmagoric creatures inhabiting fictional continents. Network play allows you to jointly overcome dangers and engage in battle with numerous enemies. By collecting materials, you can create weapons and craft armor. The possibility of free online play and single play is presented.

In the company of friends or in solo mode, you need to explore a world that lives by its own rules. Once in it, you need to adapt and learn to survive. There are many wild creatures along the way. They are not friendly, so you always need to be on your guard. The world of Voidtrain is represented by a variety of biomes. Each area has its own unique features. There are many territories available for exploration, where a huge number of secrets are hidden. You need to solve inventive puzzles and overcome difficult challenges.

The crafting system allows you to create useful items and build buildings that help you survive. All participants in the adventure start with a small cart of resources. They must be spent wisely in order to settle on a distant continent. Using the mechanic’s abilities, you need to build protective structures or assemble weapons to repel wild creatures. Allows you to construct a train. You can also modify the environment and objects around you. The gameplay is non-linear and provides complete freedom of action.
