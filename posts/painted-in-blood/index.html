Game Description#

Painted In Blood is a dive into the depths of a distorted consciousness, where reality is deformed under the influence of the actions and mental state of the protagonist. In this surreal first-person shooter, the world is a mad artist’s nightmare, constantly changing and reacting to every action. Gameplay is directly dependent on a unique sanity system that forces the player to balance on the brink of madness, as visual effects and even the opponents themselves transform in real time.

This psychological terror forces you not just to shoot accurately, but to constantly adapt to unpredictable events generated by a fevered imagination. Survival in this nightmare requires non-stop movement and lightning-fast reactions. Combat in Painted In Blood is relentlessly brutal, reminiscent of the classic shooters of the past, but with the addition of modern mechanics. You will have to confront hordes of grotesque creatures, from hooded fanatics to creatures woven from flesh and fear. The arsenal for exterminating evil spirits includes both hellish shotguns and cursed bone saws, but the shortage of ammunition makes every shot count.

The maze-like levels, reminiscent of a desecrated cathedral, are full of hidden passages and breakable walls, making exploration a dangerous but necessary endeavor to find resources. The story is deliberately shrouded in mystery, inviting you to piece together the pieces of the narrative yourself. The protagonist is not a hero, but a victim who wakes up in a surreal world of rituals and indescribable entities without understanding what is happening. The only way to get out of this nightmare is not just to destroy monsters, but also to carefully study the environment, finding encrypted clues and fragments of memories that gradually reveal the secrets of the mad creator.
