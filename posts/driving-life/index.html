Game Description#

Driving Life is a detailed simulator that offers you to master more than 40 types of transport in the setting of rural America. Players will explore a vast open world of 16 km², where they can not only complete work tasks, but also buy and sell 16 unique houses. Of particular interest is the mechanics of restoring 6 project cars, parts for which are scattered throughout the island. In addition to managing finances, it is necessary to constantly monitor the basic needs of the character: hunger, thirst, sleep and even boredom, which significantly increases the level of immersion in what is happening.

The basis of the gameplay is economic survival, built on completing various tasks. To earn money, there are 24 types of work, including transporting tourists, delivering goods, removing garbage and replenishing vending machines. Successful completion of Driving Life contracts is directly dependent on your ability to drive manual transmission cars, vans and trucks. Every dollar counts because poor financial decisions, whether it’s not buying fuel on time or missing rent, can quickly lead to bankruptcy and the end of the passage.

The project focuses on realism, offering a complex simulation rather than arcade racing. Advancement up the career ladder is determined by a system of licenses and reputation, and innovations have added a well-developed system of damage to vehicles during collisions to the title. Numerous signs and signs have been added to make road navigation easier, and some tasks now require physically interacting with objects and manually loading them into a vehicle. All of this creates a complex and skill-demanding experience where success depends on patience and strategic planning.
