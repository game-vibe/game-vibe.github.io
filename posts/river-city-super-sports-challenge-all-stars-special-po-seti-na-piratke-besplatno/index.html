Game Description#

River City Super Sports Challenge All Stars Special is a sports competition that offers the opportunity to play online over the Internet. You can take part in four different disciplines for free on Pirate. You can play online with a friend on the same screen or compete with random people. Competitions are presented with survival mechanics. You need to use wooden swords, dumbbells, bombs and many other improvised means. Events take place in the town of River City. Online play allows you to use any means to get to the finish line first. Kick or punch? Always yes!

Each character has two super attacks. You can perform trips, tackles and hit from a turntable. Free mode is available, allowing up to four players to play on one device. A single-player mode and the possibility of free online play for several people are presented. Among the disciplines, a special competition is available: Athletic Combat Meet. The River City Super Sports Challenge All Stars Special features a character editor. Before you go to conquer the podium in all four competitions, you can create your own hero. Allows you to create an entire team. In the options you can adjust the difficulty and speed. 4 disciplines are presented: Yumemchino cross - running along the streets, roofs, tracks, mansions, swimming pools, sewers, etc.; Obstacle relay - a huge number of traps and obstacles on the way; Camphor Ball - a team competition between the Laporians and the Beaters, where you need to climb a pole; Royal Battle - a classic deathmatch where only one champion must survive.
