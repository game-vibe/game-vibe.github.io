Game Description#

Class of ‘09: The Flip Side is the final chapter of the visual novel series that follows the characters’ lives as they navigate their post-high school struggles. This installment puts the spotlight on Jack, a character who was previously seen in a supporting role. The adventure focuses on her experiences and the emotional difficulties she faces, which contrasts with the more light-hearted approach of the previous protagonist, Nicole. The narrative structure of Class of ‘09: The Flip Side allows gamers to make choices that affect the storyline and offers multiple endings depending on the decisions made.

Thanks to this interactive element, each playthrough can lead to a different outcome, increasing replayability. The action takes place in 2009, which creates a nostalgic backdrop that resonates with the experiences of many players. The title is presented as an interactive sitcom, fully voiced to enhance the storytelling experience. Users are immersed in the social dynamics and personal dilemmas of the characters, making the adventure a thrilling journey through the ups and downs of young adulthood.

The inclusion of events and personalities inspired by real life gives the story added authenticity, making it accessible and entertaining to a wide audience. Jackie’s journey is marked by introspection and self-discovery. As she navigates the complexities of her relationships and career aspirations, players will watch her grow and develop. The title touches on themes of friendship, love and finding your place in the world.
