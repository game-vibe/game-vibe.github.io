Game Description#

Vellum throws gamers into a world filled with strategic combat and decision-making. Go it alone or team up with others to overcome challenges and gain control of your journey. The main highlight of Vellum is the innovative mechanics of influencing the enemy’s development. This gives the strategy an extra layer that goes beyond simply winning the next fight.

The heart beats with collaboration. Team up with friends to overcome obstacles together. The game encourages teamwork and problem solving as gamers decide how to not only level up their abilities, but also how to deal with the evolving threats they face. These dynamics create an ever-changing battlefield, requiring users to adapt and strategize on the fly.

The title offers rich content to keep every gameplay fresh. The adventure is built on fast, satisfying passages, so every entry into its depths will be exciting. With an emphasis on synergy and strategy, the project stands out as a unique take on the roguelike genre, challenging users to rethink their approach to this popular genre.
