Game Description#

Little Rocket Lab tells the story of aspiring engineer Morgan, who returns to her home to complete her family dream of building a rocket. The girl discovers that the city of Saint-Ambroise has changed a lot, and now her experience is needed to help local residents and revive the region. Through research and creation of ingenious devices and factories, Morgan directs his efforts to solving the problems of the community.

Her engineering talents become the key to bringing industry back to the city. The central mechanic of Little Rocket Lab is to convert local resources into useful materials through automation. The gameplay involves building vast industrial complexes using drills, furnaces, assemblers and extensive conveyor networks. Such factories solve the difficulties of citizens by introducing electrical elements, water supply systems and cranes.

To extract the necessary components, you will have to explore the coasts, railway junctions, quarries and underground zones, because each location contains unique materials for inventions. In addition to industrial tasks, the game’s plot pays attention to interaction with the residents of Saint-Ambroise. Gamers will get to know local characters, make friends and become an important part of society. The process of revitalizing the town through engineering solutions and social connections gradually brings Morgan closer to her personal goal. The culmination of all efforts is the final assembly of the mother’s rocket - the main project of her entire family.
