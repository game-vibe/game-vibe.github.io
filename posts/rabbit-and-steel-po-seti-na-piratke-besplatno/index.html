Game Description#

Rabbit and Steel lets you play online and with a friend in intense co-op raids. Gather your furry bunny friends, choose your class, and fight for the Moontop together. This online game offers you a roguelike experience with the familiar sensations, but without the extra costs - because you can play for free and online, even on pirates.

Do you think you can handle it alone? Rabbit and Steel will throw at you hellish attacks that will replace your teamwork. But solo playthrough is not the only challenge. Loot is the key to success, and grabbing the perfect upgrade before your allies do is half the battle. Just remember that friendly fire is a very real thing, especially if your friend keeps pumping up gear that you desperately need.

Something has gone wrong in the Moon Kingdom. The once friendly inhabitants have turned into enemies, and a giant white tower reaches for the stars every night. Time is running out, so grab your bunny friends and get to the bottom of this mystery. You can team up with up to four people - online, locally, or both. Bosses, stages and a huge number of bullet patterns await you. There’s even a single-player story campaign where you can chat with enemies - although you may be too busy blowing them up for deep conversation. There are over 100 items to choose from, so you can strengthen your bunny and become the ultimate champion. Just be careful not to kill your friend with a stray hit - although, to be fair, if they keep stealing your gear, they might deserve it. Rabbit and Steel supports a variety of control schemes, so choose your poison - keyboard, mouse and keyboard, or controller - and get ready to raid!
