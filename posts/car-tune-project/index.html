Game Description#

CAR TUNE: Project is a racing simulator that provides the ability to assemble and customize cars for street racing. The project was created for tuning enthusiasts and avid car enthusiasts. Start assembling a car from scratch. Repair, configure, replace spare parts and much more.

The developers offer realistic physics and full car driving simulation. Vehicle customization occurs through both external and internal customization. Get under the hood, install engines, create interior design, as well as appearance: painting, installing additional elements such as spoilers, and also apply various stickers, emblems and vinyls.

The car is painted using a spray gun. You will manually apply the paint over the entire area of ​​the car. Transport can be completely modified. Earn money to install new parts, mechanisms, modules and accessories. Installation of audio systems is available, allowing music to sound powerful and spacious. Once your vehicle is ready to run in, choose a dyno for a test drive. Here you can test the car for power and torque. Accelerate to maximum speed, drift and test your car for skidding and maneuverability. CAR TUNE: Project is a real gift for car-obsessed players!
