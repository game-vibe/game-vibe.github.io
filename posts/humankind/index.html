Game Description#

In the 4X strategy HUMANKIND, the player will have to lead his own civilization through all eras of human development. You have to develop cities, create an army and manage units, participating in battles on land and sea. The fictional planet is procedurally generated and includes multiple regions. At the start of the passage, they are not occupied by anyone, but gradually new rulers appear and territories are captured by other factions.

In each era, the user will select 1 of 10 civilizations based on historical societies. This will present various bonuses and disadvantages for the newly created state. The game has millions of combinations through which the gamer will develop. Gradually, more opportunities open up, new types of buildings, iron weapons, and subsequently developed technologies. To progress, you will need to get food, gold, improve influence, industry and fame, which will allow you to speed up production, trade with other cultures and modernize technology.

Battles take place in the form of a tactical role-playing game. Each battle takes into account the terrain conditions and the special skills of the warriors. Wars can last several turns, and by the standards of the game this is several years. Building wonders of the world or researching new technology will allow you to gain Glory. This branch shows the success of civilization. The only way to win here is by scoring the most points at the end of the match. As the game progresses, the user will encounter historical events or personalities that have influenced humanity in one way or another.
