Game Description#

Colony Survival is a cubic strategy for playing online online, or for co-op play with a friend. By teaming up with partners or joining other participants to play online, you can build your own worlds on the pirate, erecting castles, fortresses and entire settlements. There is a wide selection of building blocks, and characters, building tools, and a huge variety of materials are also available for free.

Using the opportunity of free online play, you and your friends need to build your own voxel colony. You can take command of guards, farms, mines, castles and other colonists - the online game Colony Survival offers an endless variety of mechanics and possibilities, limited only by the imagination of the players themselves. There is a dynamic change of day and night. As the sun sets, there is a greater chance that the settlement will be attacked by enemies and various predators. With each new wave, opponents become many times more aggressive. Walls, moats and forts will help keep monsters at bay. It is necessary to apply a whole scientific system in order to build real masterpieces of architectural thought.

Quests, blocks, upgrades, units, weapons and much more are available to unlock. By going into the mines or exploring the surrounding lands, you can mine copper and other metals, including bronze, iron and steel. It is necessary to acquire fuel, charcoal and coke. Once you have access to crossbows and matchlocks, you can place guards on the walls. Inside the colony, it is necessary to train farmers to grow wheat, bakers to bake bread, tailors to make clothes, and much more. Depending on the biomes, different types of raw materials are mined. You can trade and interact with other colonies owned by players from all over the world.
