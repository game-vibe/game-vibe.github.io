Game Description#

Dummy Dungeon is a hardcore roguelight action game with a first-person view, where sharp reflexes and high concentration become the key to survival in a colorful but dangerous world. The gameplay is based on the choice of one of 24 unique heroes, whose specialization radically changes the approach to battles. For example, the Monk relies on a grappling hook for high maneuverability, while the Witch uses a cauldron to create powerful buffs in combat. The high dynamics of confrontations force you to constantly adapt to the situation, and the abundance of available characters allows you to form your own style of play, experimenting with different tactics and combinations of abilities.

The main goal of wandering through procedurally generated labyrinths is to rescue beautiful princesses. The journey through Dummy Dungeon takes you through many different locations, from dark catacombs to giant flying mechanisms filled with unique enemies. The progression system is built on modifying characteristics and weapons with the help of perks and unique improvements. The Arsenal is capable of completely changing the mechanics of its use - from simply setting it on fire to creating special zones or automatically chasing enemies. Each stage ends with a clash with a powerful boss, which makes the final fights especially intense.
