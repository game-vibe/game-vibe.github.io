Game Description#

Soda Crisis is an incredibly colorful and dynamic action game with runner elements in which the player will have to explore levels and destroy all enemies along the way using advanced weapons. The plot here is not particularly deep and is revealed only superficially, since the gameplay is mainly aimed at mild madness and constant shootings. Enemies attack from all sides, including from the air, so in order not to fall under their bullets, you need to constantly be on the move.

A huge alien ship hovered above the Earth and humanity held its breath. Have these monsters arrived in peace or do they want to enslave the entire population? But suddenly he starts moving again and stops over a factory that produces cola. A huge mechanism begins to move and captures all the reserves of the drink. As it turned out, these creatures are simply addicted to sweet soda and will not calm down until they deplete all the Earth’s reserves at any cost.

The main character, Soda Crisis, is an unknown creature whose identity is not revealed. But his main goal is to completely eliminate extraterrestrial thieves and protect people. He has all the weapons for this and he himself is worth an entire army, so he easily rushes into battle and shoots down drones, as well as armed mechanisms. Perhaps he likes to drink a little soda himself. Along the way, he can open special boxes that will temporarily or permanently give him some improvements or new models of guns.
