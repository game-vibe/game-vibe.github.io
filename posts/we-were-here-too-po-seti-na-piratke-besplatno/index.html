Game Description#

We Were Here Too is a first-person adventure aimed at playing together with a friend, allowing you to play online as a pirate, plunging into an atmosphere of madness and fear. Network functions are available for free, and by connecting online you can go through the story campaign together. Caught in a merciless storm, the heroes were forced to hide in a gloomy castle, which became their trap. To get through the gloomy corridors, you have to solve a huge number of puzzles and constantly act together.

Events directly continue the previous part of the same name, We Were Here Together, showing the dark and sinister location of Castle Rock from a new perspective. The essence has remained unchanged, offering to use the opportunity of a free online game to solve various puzzles and tasks. The point is that the heroes are in different rooms of the castle. They have to communicate via walkie-talkie, describing what they see in front of them. The partner must have spatial thinking in order to draw a plan of the room in his head and do everything possible to ensure that the second participant moves further in the plot. The online game We Were Here Too shows how two people can explain and understand each other. Gameplay features: fully interactive environment available for interaction; each protagonist has his own role in the adventure and a unique narrative, which increases the level of replayability; many randomly generated puzzles; atmospheric musical accompaniment; a dark and confusing story containing a shocking plot turn.
