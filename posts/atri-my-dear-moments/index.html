Game Description#

ATRI -My Dear Moments- is a visual novel with a well-developed plot that leaves food for thought after completing the single-player campaign. Casual control is carried out using the keys on the mouse, allowing you to enjoy reading a work with anime-style slides even for people who are not familiar with games. Recognized masters of this genre worked on the story, which allowed the project to receive many prestigious awards and the love of an audience that loves visual novels.

The storyline takes place in the near future, in which most of the land is under water due to a sharp rise in the level of the world’s oceans by several meters. The story is told from the perspective of a young guy with a difficult fate named Ikagura Natsuki. All other characters involved in the game have voice acting, and the protagonist’s remarks are presented only in text form so that the gamer can identify himself with him. The guy’s main adventures begin after he finds a strange coffin with a living girl named Atri, who turns out to be a robot. During a treasure hunt, such an unusual find is discovered that it could have washed up on the shores after the flood. Plot characters: Ikagura Natsuki is a 17-year-old guy who lost his leg due to an accident. Atri is a robotic creature that previously helped the older generation of the protagonist’s family, but her memory is partially lost. Kamishiro Minamo is a girlfriend of a virtual protégé, that with him from a very early age. Nojima Ryuji is a strong and brave guy with leadership qualities that allow him to lead people. Katerina is a “debt collector” who came to Ikagura to get what Natsuki’s grandmother promised her. Nanami Ririka is an orphan , which is under the care of the hero.
