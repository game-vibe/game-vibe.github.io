Game Description#

Kanon Standard Edition is an interactive visual novel in which users have the opportunity to take control of a boy named Aizawa Yuichi, who is a simple schoolboy. The main character returns home after a long time of absence and spontaneously finds himself drawn into a cycle of romantic adventures, which force the young man to reconsider his usual views on the world and his own life. A young man encounters a girl named Ayu Tsukimiya, which allows the guy to regain old memories of his childhood and immerse himself in atmospheric adventures.

By immersing yourself in Kanon, players will be able to:

Watch the development of the relationship between two young people. Face choices that change the non-linear story narrative. Determine the fate of schoolchildren who communicate with the protagonist. Influence the relationships between minor characters.

Users will have a direct influence on the development and establishment of connections between the participants in the story, which tells the story of first love and hidden secrets kept by the protagonist. The hero will meet new people, make friends or adjust his personal life, going on a romantic adventure with a cute girl who wants to get to know Aizawa Yuichi better. You have to determine the boy’s fate by making responsible decisions for him and changing the endings of the game.
