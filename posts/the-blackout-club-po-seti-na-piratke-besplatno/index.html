Game Description#

The Blackout Club is a cooperative survival horror game that allows you to play as a pirate for free online with a friend, or gives you the opportunity to gather a team of up to four people to complete generated tasks. The team will have to rescue friends from captivity, hang posters to recruit people, look for evidence against cultists, etc. The possibility of free online play allows you to take control of children who find themselves in difficult situations. They enter into a fight with adults who patrol the city at night.

Opponents are divided into two types: sleeping and sighted. The former react to any noise, while the latter peer intently into the darkness. The Blackout Club is made up of child adventurers who crave adventure after the sun goes down. Therefore, they come into conflict with the most ordinary people. There are no lethal weapons in The Blackout Club. Online play allows you to knock adults down, distract them, or put them to sleep. But the more often you enter into confrontation with adults, the faster you can bring into action the Figure - an invisible enemy hunting for children.

The heroes use various gadgets, crossbows with tranquilizers, stun guns, grappling hooks and firecrackers. You can find various secrets and bonuses in the locations. For example, paintball grenades, polyurethane foam, etc. The action takes place in the town of Redijk. The Calm Zone operates here. The fact is that scientific research is taking place nearby, due to which local residents began to suffer from sleepwalking. Nobody believes children, because adults in the morning do not remember their nightly adventures. But after the disappearance of their friend, the teenagers decide to take up the investigation of such a strange phenomenon.
