Game Description#

The Battle of Polytopia is a turn-based strategy game with city builder elements. The events of the game develop in the fictional world of Square. There are 12 factions to choose from, one of which you can start a campaign for. Lead your people to world domination by expanding your sphere of influence and exploring new technologies.

You will explore the surrounding lands, develop various technologies and collect useful materials, on the basis of which your base will develop. Maps in The Battle of Polytopia are randomly generated. There are 5 types of terrains available: small, normal, large, huge and giant.

You can customize the biome type yourself: wasteland, lake, continents, archipelago and water world. The modes are represented by three types of games: excellence, domination and creative path. The last mode implies complete freedom of action without cataclysms and wars, where you can play for your own pleasure and focus exclusively on the development of the empire.

The population is called Pelitopians. They are made up of a dozen different tribes, each with their own strengths and weaknesses. You have to take control of one of the nations. Available tribes include Xin-Xi, Hoodrick, Bardur and others. Each of them are good at fighting at close or long range, or have many other qualities that significantly change the gameplay.
