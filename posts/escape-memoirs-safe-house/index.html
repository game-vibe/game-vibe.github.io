Game Description#

Escape Memoirs: Safe House invites gamers to become members of a shadow organization. The task is to infiltrate the safe house and take away the most important equipment. But here’s the catch? The boss of the safe house has gone missing. No keys - no problem. It’s hacking, but with a corporate twist. Puzzle lovers rejoice. This game is a real bonanza for the brain. Safes, codes and riddles galore. There’s a challenge lurking around every corner. Find collectibles for extra fun. No two puzzles are the same. Each requires focus, logic and creativity.

Adventure seekers alone or in a group - this adventure game is suitable for everyone. Up to five users can join the robbery. Coordinate, communicate and win. Teamwork will help make your dream come true. Or crack a safe. Doesn’t matter. This is not grandma’s escape room. This is a high stakes mission. Will you be able to solve the case? Outwit the guards? Run away with the goods? Time is ticking. A safe house awaits. There is a replay option here. Different paths, new challenges and hidden secrets encourage multiple playthroughs. This is a puzzle for the criminally inclined.

Or the curious. Or both. Expect the unexpected. Each playthrough brings new surprises. Hidden rooms, secret passages and plot twists keep players on their toes. A shelter is not just a building, it is a living organism. The atmosphere of the game is tense and the stakes are high. Users feel the pressure. The ticking clock adds to the thrill. Every wrong step can be fatal. Or at least awkward. Escape Memoirs: Safe House is a test of skill, patience and teamwork.
