Game Description#

Divinity Original Sin Enhanced Edition is a turn-based RPG that can be played online with a friend and several other friends. The full edition includes addons and improvements to the original Divinity for free, and also provides the opportunity for co-op playthroughs online. A cross-platform is available that allows you to play online on different devices. You need to assemble your team to go explore fantasy lands with your comrades, participating in turn-based battles, interacting with partners and the world, and also go through a huge number of single and secondary quests.

The story centers on the Source Hunter. The hero’s task is to rid the universe of those who use forbidden magic. And his comrades will help him with this. With the possibility of free online play, the gameplay of Divinity Original Sin Enhanced Edition allows you to go through the story campaign together, stopping the evil magician in the company of friends. The plot will allow you to conduct a real detective investigation, trying to find out the details of a brutal murder.

The re-release includes a thousand improvements, new game modes, split-screen co-op, several hours of story content, a reworked ending, additional skills, puzzles, enemies, loot and weapons. Multiplayer allows you to play Explorer, Tactical and Classic modes together. There are many different biomes with their own characteristics and enemies to explore.

You need to use a combination of skills and spells while fighting numerous enemies. Using magic requires melting ice to form water, boiling it to create steam, electrifying a cloud to make rain, etc. Making decisions changes the course of the narrative. Depending on the style of playthrough, it is possible to see several options for ending the story.
