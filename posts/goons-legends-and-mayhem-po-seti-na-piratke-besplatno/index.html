Game Description#

PlayGoons: Legends & Mayhem online to immerse yourself in a crazy world where hockey and beat ‘em up fighting collide head-on. Fight with a friend on a pirate for free and experience all the benefits of online play. Thematic levels await you online on the Internet, where each team of heroes has unique abilities. If you think you’ve seen intense sports action, get ready: Goons redefines the genre and delivers an unforgettable experience.

The game features a story mode where you have to fight to save Rondelia, a kingdom obsessed with hockey. You choose from eight characters, each with their own strengths: snipers for offense, enforcers for defense, and wingmen who can switch between support and destruction. Team up with a friend in 2v2 hockey against one goalie, or go for a classic 3v3 to cause pure chaos. Communication is vital and mastering your skills is the difference between winning and losing.

Strategy is the key to success. Choose your characters wisely to create a team that complements each other’s strengths and weaknesses. Sharp shooters can score points, enforcers can disrupt the other team’s games, and wingmen can adapt to the situation. Various abilities and unique character traits make the game very interesting.

Goons is pure arcade player versus player hockey with a twist. It evokes the nostalgia of couch co-ops while offering a new take on the sport. Character classes, a goalkeeper who constantly poses a challenge, and multiple play styles - local co-op, online with friends, or even casual matches - make the game interesting. But be careful, goalkeepers are not fluff. They will do everything they can to stop you, from ranged attacks to vicious lunges. Story mode turns goalies into true bosses, each with unique mechanics that teach you valuable hockey skills for online battles.

Speaking of combat, Goons offers a variety of environments in which to fight. From a sunny fish market to a mythical realm overflowing with magic, each location has its own special mechanics, dangers and, of course, a unique goalkeeper to defeat. So lace up your skates, grab your stick, and get ready to save the Hockeyverse! Goons features a single-player campaign that pits you against mascot villains led by the evil Grand Mascot, who is plotting to ruin hockey forever. As you progress, you’ll discover new characters and delve deeper into the mysteries that shroud this crazy hockey universe.
