Game Description#

In Space Haven, a planetary colony building simulator, you develop your own human colony. Initially, only a spaceship is available, on which you must monitor its inhabitants, fulfill their needs, build new compartments, and, in general, monitor all vital signs. But over time, there will be a shortage of resources, which will push you to explore the in-game galaxy, procedurally generated in each game of the video game.

A key feature of the project is the realistic behavior of your team. All characters have their own personal qualities, traits, and character. And everyone needs to satisfy their own needs, whether it be improving the quality of food, making friends, installing slot machines, etc. It is also important not to forget about parameters such as gas pollution and oxygen levels, maintaining them in every possible way, building special air purifiers and temperature regulators.

This project has the features of strategy and RPG, which opens up previously unprecedented opportunities for exploring different objects with resources and people. Having gathered enough people, you can send them to explore an abandoned spaceship, or other space objects. But on each mission, different challenges and threats await you. Somewhere you will meet aliens, and in another place you will discover a new faction, and you can decide whether to make contact with them or not.

If diplomacy fails to win peaceful relations with various representatives of their races, then there is always the possibility of starting hostilities. Each hero has his own set of special abilities and weapons. And by controlling each member of a small army, you can prove your military abilities. There are also battles between starships, and to emerge victorious, you need tactical control over all weapon systems, and a timely response to damage to your spaceship.
