Game Description#

Mewgenics is a tactical role-playing game whose core mechanic is the process of creating the perfect dynasty of feline warriors to send them on dangerous adventures. The central element is the family house, where the survivors of the forays return. This cycle makes it possible to consolidate valuable characteristics in the offspring, passing on not only combat skills, but also various genetic characteristics or mutations. The main goal is to breed increasingly powerful generations of fighters capable of withstanding growing threats and obtaining valuable resources for the further development of their kind.

Each expedition is a series of turn-based battles in tactical arenas, where the positioning of fighters and interaction with the environment play a decisive role. Success in Mewgenics directly depends on proper planning of actions and synergy between squad members with different class specializations. To win, a team of mustachioed warriors must:

carefully choose a position on the battlefield; use unique abilities at the right time; create powerful combinations of skills from different classes.

The project is distinguished by a colossal amount of content, offering more than 1,000 unique skills to master, distributed among 10 character classes. Gamers have over 900 pieces of equipment at their disposal, and they will face more than 200 types of enemies and bosses. Legacy mechanics and procedural event generation guarantee high replayability, turning each playthrough into a unique experience. This creates a deep strategic system where selection and tactical planning are inextricably linked, opening up scope for numerous experiments.
