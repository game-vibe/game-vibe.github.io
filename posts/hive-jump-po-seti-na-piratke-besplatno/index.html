Game Description#

Hive Jump is an action roguelike in which you can play online for free with a friend or team up with up to four people online to fight in procedurally generated locations with a huge variety of monsters and behemoths. The gameplay includes platforming and run-n-gun elements. You must constantly rush forward and destroy crowds of enemies with powerful weapons.

The main characters of the adventure are jumpers. This is an elite squad from the 24th century that is fighting an alien threat. The soldiers infiltrate the alien hives and destroy their main nests. The online game Hive Jump allows a group of friends to kill crowds of aliens and monsters, collect treasures, various lost relics and avoid numerous traps with obstacles. At the end of each mission, an intense boss fight awaits.

You have to hold positions and be ambushed by aliens, trying to withstand the onslaught of a crowd of enemies. Various level modifiers are available to diversify the gameplay. Locations are never repeated twice. The mechanics of permanent death add to the hardcore aspect. To be resurrected, you need to keep special backpacks with a transponder intact. If the enemies manage to destroy it, the game will be over. Free online play allows you to complete a variety of challenges, including daily and weekly challenges. You can rob treasuries, look for artifacts and much more. You will have to fight with pulse rifles, flamethrowers and other powerful guns.
