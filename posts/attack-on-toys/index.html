Game Description#

Attack on Toys is an indie shooter in 3D graphics, combined with the real-time strategy genre. You have to lead an army of green plastic soldiers who have started a not at all toy war with their yellow opponents. The battles will take place, as in childhood, inside the house, but now everything is for real! Think through strategies and tactics, give orders, and lead your plastic army to victory.

At all costs, protect your headquarters and develop your soldiers, moving them up the career ladder - without this you will not win. Toy soldiers can control toy equipment, committing the most serious acts of sabotage or going into open battle. But try not to improvise - death in battle will be irreversible. You can treat wounded soldiers in a military hospital, take care of medical tents at your headquarters. If you don’t have enough fighting forces, hire fighters using the in-game currency you receive after completing missions.

Progress through the story will send the player into combat in various locations: a children’s room, a dining room, and even a picnic area. Use objects and hide your sniper behind a packet of chips. Beware of cockroaches! Despite the grotesque surroundings, the player will seriously experience the fierce battles between the soldiers of the green and yellow armies.
