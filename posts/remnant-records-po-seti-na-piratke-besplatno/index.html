Game Description#

Remnant Records is a survival horror game with detective elements, which you can play online with a friend for free on a pirate account, exploring murder scenes and locations inhabited by the living dead. By cooperating online, you can assemble a team of up to four people. Each member has a unique role in the squad.

Participants in the adventure act as employees of an agency that captures ghosts and other paranormal creatures. Online play allows you to team up to unravel mystical mysteries and drive out aggressive spirits. Several dark locations are presented where you will need to find clues, solve puzzles, collect auxiliary items and fight monsters. Many game elements are randomly generated.

Evidence includes diaries, newspaper clippings, items that belonged to the deceased, etc. The more objects you collect related to the ghost’s past, the greater the chances of destroying it. It is advisable to hide from monsters, and strictly divide tasks among the team among themselves. You will need to perform exorcism rituals, read prayers and much more. Gameplay mechanics and features of Remnant Records: Procedurally generated content and stories; Possibility of free online play for 4 people; Unique and terrible monsters; Thick and tense atmosphere of horror; 4 different roles in teams: jock, medium, etc. ;Customizable difficulty and various modifications;Survival elements and individual strategies for each demon.
