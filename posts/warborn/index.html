Game Description#

WARBORN is a tactical strategy game with turn-based combat in which you need to put on a combat mech suit and go to fight your opponents. The battle takes place to preserve peace throughout the galaxy. The game features 4 commanders from different factions. Each of the military leaders has individual traits and fighting style. He leads a small army of mechanoids and leads them forward, defeating the enemy army.

At a certain point, it is possible to request help from orbit, choosing between fighters with unique abilities. By collecting units with different skills, you need to build effective tactics that can defeat any enemy army. WARBORN features a huge location through which the warlord moves. Progression occurs along cells (in this particular case, hexagons). The environment is interactive and allows you to use landscapes for your own purposes: as cover or to create a trap.

By capturing various buildings, you obtain resources that are necessary to upgrade your heroes. Exoskeletons have unique characteristics and are equipped with various equipment. Each new armor can affect the tactics and style of battle. The campaign consists of 40 missions that will take place in the Auros system. The opposing sides clashed in a bloody war. The player will have to observe the point of view of each of the factions.

Multiplayer Warborn will allow you to fight one on one with another military leader, where tactics and competent command will win. Single cards allow you to play a game against AI. An extensive toolkit will make it possible to create your own custom map and post it on the network.
