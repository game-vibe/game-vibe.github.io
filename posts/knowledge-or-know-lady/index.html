Game Description#

Knowledge, or know Lady throws you into the heart of a unique dating simulator. As the only guy at an all-girl university, you’ll find yourself caught up in a whirlwind of romance and college life. Will you succumb to the charms of the beauties around you or forge your own path to become a campus legend? Ladies School Prince unfolds through your eyes, offering a first-person perspective. Your choices determine the direction of the story, and there are multiple endings to discover. Play fair, pursue one love interest, juggle two flames, go through a harem scenario, or even defy the odds and end up single. Keen eyes can discover hidden storylines by collecting special items during the game.

Six women await you, each of whom has a distinct character. Will you find love with a mysterious girl, a tender sweetheart, a badass motorcyclist, a mature school doctor, a bubbly foreign student or a proud big sister? The developers of Ladies' School Prince welcome both praise and criticism from players. The game offers a variety of ending options, from classic single-heroine romances to more complex multi-character scenarios. There is even a special ending, “The Limits of Humanity,” for those who decide to remain single and focus solely on their studies.
