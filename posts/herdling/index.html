Game Description#

In Herdling, the player leads a motley oral of creatures called Kalicorn into a grand campaign to the mountains. The journey passes along a winding path full of terrible dangers and sudden barriers. Promotion requires laying the route forward through a complex area to a secret lying on top. The intersection of forgotten lands constantly experiences the strength between the conductor and the wards of animals in the middle of a deserted wild.

Movement in a variety of landscapes requires honed driving skills to overcome numerous threats. The route runs through contrasting locations: from the forests shrouded by fog and lifeless snow plateau to the valleys lost among the peaks. In Herdling, promotion depends on the solution of simple spatial puzzles and confrontation with sudden tests to protect the herd. Successful control of Kalikorn in each situation is a key element of the gameplay and the key to continuing the expedition in harsh lands.

This game is a story without words, focused on the topics of trust, survival and partnership. A deep bonds are formed with a group of Kalikorn, hardened by general adversities during the great wanderings in the fallen world. Some points grow into a breathtaking scene of a swift run, passing on a rush of movement along with the beasts. Ultimately, this title tells about the stamina and relationships poured during the fateful adventure in the wild.
