Game Description#

Action roguelikeNuclear Throne immerses you in a post-apocalyptic world, where you can go for free on a pirate ship with a friend. You can play online, overcoming traps and obstacles together. Using the Internet online, you can interact with a partner, exchanging resources or covering a friend’s back. The gameplay is distinguished by intense shootings and high dynamics of what is happening on the screen.

The online game invites you to team up with a partner to become the last hope of all humanity. People are almost extinct. Instead, terrible monsters and mutants roam the surface of the Earth. In Nuclear Throne you need to break through a crowd of monsters using powerful guns and using various abilities. The possibility of a free online game includes exploring locations, collecting radiation and modifying the limbs of the protagonists.

The main goal is to reach the Nuclear Throne. To do this, you need to go through 15 rich locations, randomly generated. They are represented by 7 unique biomes. On each of them you will have to fight a powerful boss. Having destroyed the final monster, you will need to get into the Loop, where everything must begin again. However, the monsters become more aggressive, new enemies appear, additional levels and much more. Between missions, players can change character mutations to become stronger.
