Game Description#

On the pirateBBQ Simulator: The Squad you can play online for free with a friend on the pirate or get together with a random group online on the Internet to barbecue in your backyard. The gameplay is a real barbecue simulator, where the main task is to feed your friends meat without letting them die of hunger. Online play brings together up to 8 participants, allowing you to both compete in one team and split into groups to show your skills.

There are several modes that set a variety of goals for victory. BBQ Simulator: The Squad has a grill available and a huge amount of meat to fry. The possibility of free online play is aimed at interaction between participants. You will need to share responsibilities among yourself in order to defeat the opposing team and feed your brothers faster. If you delay cooking, people faint from hunger. While one is engaged in grilling, the second should cook steaks, and the third should set the table for guests.

Many external factors will need to be taken into account. For example, weather conditions. It could be wind, rain, snow, etc. It is also necessary to drive away annoying flies from the meat. Each participant can create his own unique character by choosing his appearance parameters and clothes. The backyard allows you to use lawnmowers, a soccer ball, a blowtorch, a fire extinguisher and many other items to maximize gameplay variety. A creative approach to cooking is encouraged. But you need to be careful not to overdo it, otherwise the grill, oven or microwave may explode.
