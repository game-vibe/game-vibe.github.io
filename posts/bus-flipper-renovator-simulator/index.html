Game Description#

Bus Flipper: Renovator Simulator puts the player in the shoes of a restorer who transforms old buses and vans. Available appliances, including models from the 1980s and 1990s, are purchased in poor condition. The initial process involves complete internal disassembly and thorough cleaning of the body. Old components will need to be dismantled and accumulated dirt washed away, preparing the ground for the next phase of the project. This preparation is the foundation for all future work.

The key gameplay in Bus Flipper: Renovator Simulator is remodeling, allowing you to design and create new interiors from scratch. Work is often driven by customer requests, with each customer putting forward a list of unique requirements for a specific build. For example, a car can be converted into a mobile cafe or a functional mobile home. The player is free to make creative decisions to modify both full-size buses and small minivans to meet different operational needs.

Once the transformation is complete, the gamer can test the functionality of his creation to ensure that all systems are working properly. After successful inspection, the restored vehicle is sold to the client or on the open market. Payment is accrued for labor and materials invested in the project. The proceeds are used to purchase further buses, allowing you to continue the cycle of updates and take on more complex projects over time.
