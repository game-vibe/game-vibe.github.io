Game Description#

It’s Only Money will immerse gamers in the realistic story of a resident of the underworld, who, by order of the mayor of Rockhaven, was thrown into a huge pit. But don’t despair! Together with other inhabitants of the underground, you can return to the surface and begin your path to wealth and power. You must steal from the rich, fight tycoons, and regain control of the city block by block. You can buy houses, businesses and anything else you want. It’s Only Money is an open-world third-person game where you can explore the city and find new opportunities. You can steal from the rich, hack cars and drive them. Or maybe you’ll want to fight the local police or sample the food at the Mayor Mart.

You can play alone or with friends - up to 4 people. Together you can steal, fight, complete missions and spend time in clubs and casinos. If you’re feeling competitive, you can indulge in PvP mini-games like Prop Hunt, where you can disguise yourself in various pieces of outdoor furniture. Or take part in street races around the city. Once you start stealing from the rich, you can buy houses and businesses and thus help other inhabitants of the underground. You will be able to decorate the homes of new residents to their liking and receive game bonuses and items that will help you in liberating the city.
