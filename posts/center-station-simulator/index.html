Game Description#

Center Station Simulator immerses users in the heart of a ghost shopping center located in a deserted desert. What is your mission? Breathe life into this abandoned monstrosity and transform it into a vibrant commercial mecca. The game is based on resource management and production autonomy. Beneath the dusty floors of the mall lies a vast underground laboratory - a blank canvas for your production endeavors. Here you will produce a variety of goods, from daily necessities to high-tech gadgets.

The laboratory can be expanded indefinitely. But remember that growth comes at a price. You will need to carefully manage resources, ensuring a steady supply of electricity, water, oil and other raw materials to keep the wheels of your enterprise turning. Building and managing a shopping center is a practical matter. You will need to plan stores, automate payment systems, and implement strategies to increase sales. The title encourages a sense of industrial independence by requiring you to produce the necessary resources yourself.

A well-functioning parking lot is also critical to attracting customers and maximizing revenue. Center Station Simulator offers a sandbox-style experience, allowing gamers to experiment and create their own unique shopping mall layouts. The game encourages creativity and strategic thinking as you balance production, sales, and resource management.
