Game Description#

DRAGON QUEST BUILDERS 2 is a role-playing adventure with construction elements, which you can play for free online as a pirate, exploring the fairy-tale world with a friend. On the Internet, online with a partner or alone, you need to repel the evil cult calling themselves the Children of Hargon. Online play allows participants to explore territories, fight enemies and build various structures. In addition to the campaign, a free mode is available.

At the beginning of the adventure, you can set the parameters of your character. The single-player campaign offers you to go through the story together with your travel partner Malros, a guy who has lost his memory. Free online play in DRAGON QUEST BUILDERS 2 allows you to team up with up to four players. Construction is based on blocks. Various buildings are built from them. Cooking skills available. Dishes can be prepared using the recipes found.

You need to perform a wide variety of tasks. It is necessary to help local residents by rebuilding destroyed buildings and cities. By helping people, the level of respect increases. The higher the protagonist’s level, the more opportunities open to the hero. You can craft equipment and select weapons according to your preference. A huge open world is available for exploration. It consists of islands with their own unique biomes. You can build your own house by planting various plants and harvesting them.
