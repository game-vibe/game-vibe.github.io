Game Description#

Rock of Ages 2 Bigger and Boulder is an absurd competitive game aimed at competing on the Internet online or in splitscreen mode, allowing you to fight a friend on a pirate. There are many locations, spheres, cubes, etc. available for free. In the sequel, paired battles between the two sides appeared. The gameplay contains elements of arcade, platformer, action and tower defense. The online game invites you, alone or in a group, to rush through interactive maps like a huge boulder, demolishing everything in your path. Smash and break is the main motto of Rock of Ages 2 Bigger and Boulder.

The developers are inspired by the humor of Monty Python, bringing a huge amount of British humor into the game, connecting it with the Renaissance, surrealism, the world of Ancient Egypt, late Gothic, etc. Using the possibility of free online play, up to four players can fight in dynamic confrontations. There are powerful bosses in the locations, such as the Thinker or the Great Sphinx. You need to look for a special approach to them, studying the weaknesses of your enemies.

The gameplay is based on realistic environmental physics. Rolling down like a huge boulder, you need to crush everything in your path, doing everything possible to make the buildings crumble into small pieces. Breaking, crushing, crushing - nothing matters before the power of a rushing boulder. Defensive units have been added, the purpose of which is to stop powerful spheres right on the move.
