Game Description#

Bulletstorm: Full Clip Edition is an action-adventure game that features the ability to play online with a friend using a wide arsenal of weapons. On the pirate Bulletstorm: Full Clip Edition, you can go through the story campaign in co-op for free by connecting online via the Internet. In total there are 7 types of weapons and one machine gun. Each gun has unique characteristics and firing mode. With the help of skillshots, intense and furious gameplay is provided, allowing you to get caught in a hail of bullets.

The gameplay is based on a skillshot system. The better you play, the higher the reward. By performing unique skillshots, more bonus points are awarded. They are spent on new guns, upgrades, buying ammunition and much more. The possibility of free online play allows you to fight in intense confrontations, demonstrating your shooting skills. Events take place in the 26th century. The main character is Grayson Hunt. He is the former commander of the Dead Echo squad. Now he is retired. Since he left the service, the protagonist has become a space pirate.

Due to his rash actions, he and his associates end up on the planet Stygia. During the passage you have to make a choice: to escape or take revenge. Depending on the choice, a different ending to the story will be shown. The single player campaign features 30 unique challenges. The campaign includes about 7 hours of intense gameplay. Multiplayer allows you to compete with other players in 12 different multiplayer stages. Each mode is represented by different tasks and includes a variety of mechanics.
