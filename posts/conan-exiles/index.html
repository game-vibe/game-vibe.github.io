Game Description#

Conan Exiles is an amazing multiplayer survival game with RPG and action elements. Employees of the Norwegian company Funcom worked on the project. The project was released at the end of spring 2018. Stunning graphics are a rare quality that developers of various MMORPG games pay attention to. But this time everything happened exactly the opposite. Funcom, 10 years before Conan Exiles, had already released a similar game called Age Of Conan. Despite the fact that the game was warmly received by players and the press, over time, it stopped paying for itself and its main servers were closed.

Like its predecessor, this project is based on the work of the Texas science fiction writer Robert Irwin Howard and his immortal hero - Conan of Cimmeria. Conan is the greatest fantasy character. He alone weighs at least as much as the entire Lord of the Rings brethren combined. Therefore, the creators of the game tried to take a responsible approach to the virtual embodiment of the hero. However, players will not be able to fight on behalf of Conan. At the very beginning, we choose the gender and appearance of our hero. Next, a prologue begins, during which the legendary hero removes our character from the cross (on which he was crucified, in the middle of a lifeless desert). Next, we have to go through a little training. The main point of the game is to create your own fortified settlement, fight with other players, mine minerals and explore the area. We can say that Conan Exiles is a mixture of sandbox, role-playing game and action, where battles are fought on mounts, and you can also create an entire empire within the world of Conan the Barbarian.
