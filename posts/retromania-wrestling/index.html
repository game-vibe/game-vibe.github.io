Game Description#

Retromania Wrestling is a sequel to the cult game Wrestlefest, which was released back in 1991. The gameplay is a dynamic wrestling that is distinguished by detailed two -dimensional sprites of characters and animated backgrounds. In fact, before you a simplified version of the struggle without rules, balancing on the verge of a simulator and arcade.

Roster of wrestlers is 16 unique athletes, each of whom owns an individual battle style and is distinguished by appearance. A single story campaign is available and the opportunity to play in multi -user matches up to eight people. The game presents several modes: one on one, team tags and the royal battle.

The main goal is to hold the opponent on the shoulder blades for three seconds. However, for this you will need to be pretty exhausted by the opponent. Do you think it’s so simple? No matter how! Real legend of breakling always opposes you! Fight on training arenas or arrange a real show in a cage!

As well as in the original wrestling, all fighters in Retromania Wrestling have several options for going on stage, as well as 2-3 types of winning racks. All wrestlers go under individual musical accompaniment. For the game, about 700 animation frames were recorded. The developers paid special attention not only to the arena, but also to the crowd. Fans react to what is happening, sing songs and much more.
