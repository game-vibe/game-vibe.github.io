Game Description#

JR EAST Train Simulator can hardly be called an ordinary video game, since due to the high level of realism it is a full-fledged simulator that will allow you to learn the basics of the profession of a driver. Instead of the usual virtual world drawn in graphics, the developers offer the opportunity to travel through areas recorded in real time - the universe here is revealed as in a high-definition movie. This approach allows you to immerse yourself in the atmosphere as deeply as possible.

JR EAST Train Simulator is about freight trains that are capable of transporting huge amounts of goods over long distances. All actions take place exclusively from the eyes of the driver. He is forced to stay in the cockpit all the time and perform the same actions as a real driver. In front of the user there are many speedometers and various indicators that show the technical condition and performance while driving.

At the right moment, the user must press the levers to make a turn, brake, switch to another track, and so on. The soundtrack adds even more realism to the video game - all sounds were recorded from real trains as they moved.
