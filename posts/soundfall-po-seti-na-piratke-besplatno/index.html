Game Description#

Soundfall introduces the opportunity to explore dungeons with a friend, fighting for free on a pirate ship against numerous enemies and solving various puzzles. You can play online both in the story campaign and in other additional modes. The adventure is designed for four participants who can team up both locally and online online.

Soundfall’s gameplay places a huge emphasis on rhythm. Free online play allows heroes to synchronize their actions to deal devastating damage to their enemies. There are over 500 different items available to collect. Collected artifacts can be combined with each other, creating unique character builds. Online gaming is all about interaction. Both long-range characters and heroes who are most effective in close combat are available.

There are 5 characters in total with their own unique abilities. They are musical geniuses. Each one fights with a unique musical instrument and is represented by a specific genre. Events take place in the world of Symphony, where you will need to fight the brutal forces of Dissonance. The heroes of the adventure are guardians of harmony who go through many difficulties and trials while exploring a world consisting of many colorful biomes. You will need to visit dark dungeons, lush green gardens, etc. In addition to ordinary enemies, you will have to fight powerful bosses.
