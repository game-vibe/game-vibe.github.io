Game Description#

A chamber of Stars tells about the life of the performer Starr - a musician who has passed through decades of cultural shifts and personal transformation. After many stage images, the change of genres and public scandals, the artist remains a significant figure in the public consciousness. The anticipation of the new album is growing, according to rumors, preparing to become his main work. This journey explores the internal universe of personality, asking the question of what part of yourself can be conveyed through creativity in the world, constantly creating your own myths about you.

The narrative a chamber of Stars continues The Endless Empty 2018 events, collecting together the fate of the protagonist through surrealistic experiences. Players have to go through six unique worlds representing different faces of life and Starr’s consciousness. Episodes with the study of locations and the solution of puzzles alternate with rhythmic performances, where the quality of musical performance entails consequences. The decisions made directly affect the development of the plot and change the songs created as a result, forming the hero’s legacy.

The game affects adult topics, considering the path of celebrity through the prism of black comedy and psychological horror. Her plot contrasts the nature of personality and memory with the pressure of public opinion in a stylized anti -utopian situation. Each interaction reveals new layers of complex personal biography, casting doubt on the line between the stage image and the real person. This game experience is a philosophical reflection on glory, self -expression and internal universes built by people around those who were in the spotlight.
