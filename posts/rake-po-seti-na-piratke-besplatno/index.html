Game Description#

Rake is a first-person horror game that allows you to team up with a friend to play online for free on a pirate site. A confrontation mode and a cooperative game are available, offering online survival in the vastness of the wild over the Internet. The main character is researcher Gordon Davis. He travels to various mysterious lands of our planet to study various paranormal features and destroy myths about whether Chupacabras, Sasquatch, Skinwalkers and other monsters exist.

Events take place in the summer of 2003 in the northeastern United States. Complaints began to be received from residents of the town that the light began to go out. In 2006, we managed to get hold of many records from the 12th century that told about 4 continents where paranormal things were observed. The protagonist decides to visit these territories to track down poltergeists, Bigfoot, the Mothman and other mythical legends. Online play Rake allows you to go on a journey with other players.

The most important monster is Rake. It is a humanoid creature that prefers to appear at night. The possibility of free online play allows you to make preparations during the daytime: set up traps, sensors, cameras, etc. It is necessary to cover the entire forest area. You have to switch between cameras and monitor any changes. You can arm yourself with a rifle to be able to fight off the creature. Will you be able to lure the monster into a trap?
