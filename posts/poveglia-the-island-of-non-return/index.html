Game Description#

Poveglia The Island of Non Return is a psychological thriller that plunges into the atmosphere of the abandoned Italian island of Poveglia, where the protagonist will have to face the horrors of the past. Forced to explore the dark ruins of a former mental hospital and plague quarantine, the character must solve puzzles and avoid the dangers that lurk in the darkness. The environment is explored from a third-person perspective, and the gameplay requires the gamer not only to pay attention to detail to find clues, but also to manage limited resources to survive.

The storyline develops non-linearly, revealing the secrets of the island through found documents and sudden visions that blur the line between reality and madness. The atmosphere of oppressive loneliness in Poveglia: The Island of Non Return is enhanced by a dynamic soundtrack, where every rustle can foreshadow a mortal threat. The history of this place, known as Ghost Island, is closely intertwined with the personal drama of the main character - a journalist investigating the disappearance of his partner, who disappeared here under mysterious circumstances.

Moving deeper into the cursed territory, the hero discovers evidence of cruel experiments carried out by the hospital’s chief physician, and realizes that the island itself is a hostile entity. Every decision made affects the character’s mental state, and confronting supernatural forces will require not only the use of found weapons, but also ingenuity to solve complex mysteries left by the souls of hundreds of thousands of dead. This project makes you go through an unforgettable experience, balancing on the brink between investigation and the fight for your own life.
