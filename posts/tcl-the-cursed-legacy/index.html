Game Description#

TCL - The Cursed Legacy is a tribute to the projects of the 90s, where the gameplay is based on the principle of repeated trial and error to learn enemy patterns. Events take place back in 3145 in a world where humanity is on the verge of complete extinction. A young mercenary named Leif falls under control and is forced to survive in the harsh realities of the fallen principality of Dallongeville. In search of power, he sets out on a risky mission to steal the artifact RB209, hidden in one of the oases of the god Cube. His journey is an odyssey through the ruins of a former civilization, filled with dangers and echoes of past atrocities.

Structurally, the passage is divided into two alternating types of stages. TCL - The Cursed Legacy combines run’n gun-inspired segments with exploration phases reminiscent of classic metroidvania genres. To successfully advance through the plot and confront 13 bosses, you will have to master many mechanics.

Hero mechanics and abilities:

Using cover and acrobatic techniques in combat. Exploring locations to find secrets and upgrades. Using wall jumps to overcome obstacles. Using a grappling hook to move quickly. Walkthrough of five built-in retro slot machines. Battle thirteen bosses with unique attacks.
