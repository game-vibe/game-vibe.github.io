Game Description#

InAge Of Empires 3 you can compete online online with players from all over the world or play online with a friend, competing for free on pirates in versus matches. Co-op mode available and cross-platform supported. New additions include two modes: Historical Battles and Art of War. The single player campaign and online play are still available.

Age of Empires 3 allows you to take control of 8 major powers, as well as 16 additional ones, including the Swedes and Incas. The possibility of free online play offers you to fight for one of the nations. Each power has its own unique abilities, a unique type of buildings, units and individual characteristics. Powers of Age Of Empires 3: Great Britain - the main representative - Elizabeth I, instead of ordinary houses they use estates, and London is the metropolis; Prussia - Frederick the Great, the faction creates carters, the capital is Berlin; Holland - Moritz of Nassau, settlers are hired from limit of 50 people, center - Amsterdam; Spain - Isabella I, fighting dogs are trained, metropolis - Seville; Portugal - Henry the Navigator, use a telescope to open the map, capital - Lisbon; Russian Empire - Ivan IV the Terrible, residents and warriors are created in groups , center - St. Petersburg; France - Napoleon I, the power uses trippers instead of settlers, capital - Paris; Ottoman Empire - Suleiman the Magnificent, settlers arrive automatically, center - Istanbul.

The single player campaign consists of three acts: Blood, Ice and Steel. The story tells of the Great Siege of Malta, which took place in 1565. The main character is Morgan Black. He is a member of the Order of Malta, tasked with defending Fort San Elmo against the Ottoman commander Sahin. The gameplay allows you to collect resources, build buildings, train troops and fight enemies. Having accumulated enough resources and combat experience, you can transition between eras, unlocking new abilities and access to advanced technologies.
