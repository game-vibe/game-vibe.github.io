Game Description#

Journey Through the Undead is an uncompromising first-person shooter where the sole goal is to survive in a world twisted by the consequences of a catastrophic scientific miscalculation. The confrontation will unfold in the gloomy scenery of abandoned cities and deadly swamps, where grotesque mutants wait around every corner. The main character, who finds himself at the epicenter of events, is deprived of memories of the causes of the tragedy. His progress is subject to a strict linear sequence of missions leading through a series of dangerous zones. The project is an ideological rethinking of its predecessor called Aftertime, offering improved mechanics and redesigned level designs.

The gameplay is built on the contrast between moments of ominous silence and sudden, chaotic clashes with hordes of the living dead. Successful completion of Journey Through the Undead will require constant adaptation to changing conditions. A wide selection of weapons, from melee weapons to firearms, is offset by a limited amount of ammunition, which forces you to carefully plan every action. To stay alive you must:

competently manage found ammunition and medicines. use grenades to eliminate clusters of enemies. Use interactive environmental elements such as exploding barrels to gain a tactical advantage.
