Game Description#

Finally, the plot ofFar Cry 5 has reached America. Crazed fanatics unleashed their might on the state of Montana. A first-person shooter with action elements brings us to a reality in which even the movements of dogs will be more than real. The modernization of the shooter took several years, so the development studio warns us in advance that the game is based on religious motives. Age limit: 18+.

The plot takes place in the fictional Hole County, Mont. In the reality of the game, the exact actions of Americans, their actions, attitudes towards alcohol and many habits are observed. The main character is a nameless deputy sheriff of the city, who, out of helplessness, turned to a hired killer, that is, to us. The head and ruler of the district is Joseph Sid, a religious fanatic who created a religious sect in the city. According to him, he is the only one who can save people from real devilish punishment. Having convinced a crowd of civilians, a pursuit of dissidents begins in the area.

Our hero will have to craft weapons, collect items and use the skill tree, which pumps up not only his health level, but also the ability to carry more weapons; we can make grenades and Molotov cocktails. Driving cars and auxiliary equipment is provided to you. As you progress through the story, you will find people who will join your mission. You can also play Far Cry 5 with a friend and go through the story on increased difficulty. An eye movement feature has been added to the game. You can help your team spot enemies faster, cooperate in stealth mode, and carry out stealth strikes. The project is designed for several endings, each of which carries a certain meaning and beginning, which you are completely guided by when creating a character.
