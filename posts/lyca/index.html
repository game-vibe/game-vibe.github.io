Game Description#

Lyca is an incremental game where the goal is to restore a barren area and turn it into a grassland. Players control the wolf character Lika, moving him across the landscape and taking actions to transform the world around him. Key gameplay involves exploring the area, using the wolf’s abilities, and collecting materials that appear from the ground. This cycle of actions allows you to turn a desert area into a field suitable for free movement of the character.

The power of the wolf character in Lyca is used to initiate the growth of plants and flowers from the earth’s surface. This emerging flora becomes a source for collecting the necessary resources during the passage. Subsequently, the mined materials are required to unlock nodes in the extensive upgrade tree. This system is a key development mechanism, expanding the wolf’s ability to change his environment and modifying the way he obtains or collects resources during an adventure.

Promotion in the title is structured in stages and is associated with the continuous accumulation of resources. The Branching Tree contains many upgrades to unlock sequentially, guiding the process of rebuilding the world. In addition, this project offers other modes through special unlockable options, including idle mode for background progress and endless mode for unlimited progress. Completing the main campaign is estimated to take approximately 3-4 hours.
