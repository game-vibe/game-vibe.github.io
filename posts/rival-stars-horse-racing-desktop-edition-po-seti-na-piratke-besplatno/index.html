Game Description#

The pirateRival Stars Horse Racing Desktop Edition allows you to play online for free with a friend, exploring the ranch or placing bets on the races. The gameplay is a horse racing simulator, allowing players to bet on their favorite horses, receiving huge amounts of money from their winnings. With the proceeds you need to build your estate and raise a future champion. Network play allows you to both join forces with a partner and enter into confrontation with other players.

Rival of the Stars: Racing delivers a true equestrian experience, allowing each player to raise their own champion and build their own estate. The ability to play online for free opens the door to different styles. Each participant chooses his own horse, training method, etc. Story missions and various multiplayer competitions are available. The main goal in Rival Stars Horse Racing Desktop Edition is to become a horse racing legend. Horses are bred using a sophisticated genetic heritage system. Each mare has its own unique characteristics and character, accompanied by pedigree indicators. You can breed your champion using a horse designer. Caring for the estate and exploring the ranch: hiring professional grooms and various workers; building new stables and training complexes; completing missions and goals for fame and glory; Horseback riding mode, allowing you to freely roam the estate; traveling on horseback through wild forests and fields .
