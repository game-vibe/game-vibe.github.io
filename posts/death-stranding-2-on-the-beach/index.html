Game Description#

Death Stranding 2: On the Beach is a video game that continues the story of Sam Porter Bridges and Fragil, two characters who survived the disasters that occurred in the first game. The game takes place in a world where mysterious creatures called BTs threaten humanity, and people are connected by a network called the Chiral Network. The game is a sequel to Death Stranding, which was released in 2019 and received critical acclaim for its originality and innovation. The author of the game is Hideo Kojima, a famous game designer known for his work on the Metal Gear Solid series and other games.

The game was announced in 2022 at the Game Awards, where a teaser trailer was shown. The trailer features Fragile’s voice saying, “We thought we were the last ones standing. We thought we saved the world. But we were wrong. There’s something else out there. Something that wants to take everything from us. Something we can’t see, but we can feel. Something that’s waiting for us. On the beach.” It is expected that the game will feature new gameplay mechanics, such as the ability to travel to different beaches, which are parallel dimensions reflecting the inner worlds of the characters. The game will also feature a multiplayer mode in which players will be able to cooperate or compete with each other in various missions and tasks.
