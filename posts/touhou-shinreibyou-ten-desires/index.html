Game Description#

Touhou shinreibyou ~ ten desires begins when strange divine perfumes begin to come from people, Yokaev and fairies. In response to this event, female protagonists of the game go to the other world, suggesting that it is the key to unraveling the phenomenon. The gameplay offers to lead the chosen heroine through a series of levels filled with flitches of shells from opponents. The task is to survive these attacks and destroy the entire opposition, completing the passage of the stage with the boss.

In Touhou Shinreibyou ~ Ten Desires, survival depends on the management of several resources, including a limited number of lives and spells for defensive maneuvers. The capacity of the character’s shot is determined by the collection of special objects falling out of enemies. The central mechanics of this Shoot ‘em up is tied on a trance scale represented by the skull and soul icon in the lower part of the screen. This indicator is filled when the player defeats the enemies and collects the divine spirits remaining after them.

The trance scale provides a special reaction to damage. If a shell enters a gamer if there is at least one charge on the indicator, it enters the state of a trance instead of an immediate loss of life. This condition is served as a hallucination on the verge of death, which gives temporary invulnerability and maximum power of attack for a short period of time. The indicator is depleted during this period, and when it is devastated, the heroine dies, performing the final blow. Alternative activation of trance at maximum charge does not lead to a loss of life after the timer, offering a strategic offensive option.
