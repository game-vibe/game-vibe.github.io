Game Description#

Use Your Words is a fun arcade party game that promises endless laughter for both those who are naturally funny and those looking to unleash their inner humor. This fun casual game offers players a variety of mini-games and clues, and their task is simple: come up with the wittiest answers imaginable. A distinctive feature of the project is the ability to use smartphones and tablets as controllers, so everyone can immerse themselves in the fun without any problems. It can bring a lot of fun to any group of 3-6 users. This is a party game that really knows how to scale up the fun!

One of the most exciting mini-games is “Sub The Title”, where you have to hilariously dub foreign films by adding your own subtitles, resulting in moments reminiscent of Mystery Science Theater 3000. In “Blank-o-Matic” you can laugh by adding your own quirky phrases to the sentences. The developers have carefully chosen the humor, so even a grandfather can hit the mark. In “EXTRA, EXTRA!” You have to come up with funny captions or titles for ridiculous photos. Meanwhile, “Survey Says” offers a game show-style lightning round that tests intuition and wits. One thing is for sure: Use Your Words knows how to keep the humorous energy going, making it the perfect choice for an evening of non-stop fun. So gather your friends, grab your smartphones and get ready to dive into the lush world of the game.
