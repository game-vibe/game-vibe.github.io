Game Description#

9 Souls takes you on an atmospheric journey on the edge between madness and reality, where every step requires utmost care. The gameplay is based on constant choice: in each section, three paths open to the protagonist, but only one of them is free from anomalies. The slightest details in the environment, barely noticeable distortions of familiar objects, become the key to advancement. The right decision saves the lost soul, bringing it closer to the solution, but the price of a mistake is immersion in even greater chaos and uncertainty, forcing you to doubt your own perception.

The story begins with an awakening after a car accident, which itself raises doubts about its reality. The world around you seems familiar, but this is a deceptive feeling, as the rooms begin to repeat themselves, creating the feeling of an endless labyrinth. The narrative in 9 Souls is built on fragments received from saved souls. Their whispers gradually form a single picture of what happened, causing the shadows of the protagonist’s own guilt to appear more and more clearly. With each correct choice, the feeling of the fundamental wrongness of what is happening only intensifies, and reality is distorted more and more.

Ultimately, he will have to face the faces of those whom the hero considered long lost. This psychological horror film offers no definitive answers, leaving the ending open to interpretation. Pieces of the puzzle put together may never form a complete picture, leaving one to wonder whether the truth was ever achievable. The oppressive atmosphere and constant tension force you to explore not only distorted locations, but also the depths of your own subconscious, where the frightening truth is hidden.
