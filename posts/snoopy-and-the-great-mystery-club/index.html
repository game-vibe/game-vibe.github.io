Game Description#

Snoopy and the Great Mystery Club is an exciting detective adventure in which the famous dog Snoopy and his friends from the Peanuts gang take on the investigation of mysterious incidents in their city. When things mysteriously begin to disappear, including the main character’s dinner, Snoopy puts on his detective hat and organizes the Great Mystery Club. Together with such characters as Charlie Brown, Lucy, Pepper Patty and others, he will have to get to the bottom of the truth, exploring locations familiar to all comic book fans. The team’s investigations will take them to Charlie Brown’s house, a baseball field, and even the infamous Kite-Eating Tree.

The process of solving mysteries is built on teamwork and the unique abilities of each club member. Gamers will have to form a squad of four characters, where each contributes to the common cause, using special skills to solve puzzles. Snoopy himself can change his images, turning into either a Beagle Scout or a brave pirate, which opens up access to various tools and mechanics for overcoming obstacles. In Snoopy and the Great Mystery Club, players will not only look for clues using a magnifying glass or metal detector, but also participate in a variety of mini-games.

Reveal all the secrets together with your favorite characters:

Take part in numerous activities, from sports like baseball to racing with homemade cars. Help the club solve a variety of cases: from searching for missing sports equipment to investigating the appearance of ghostly music. Enjoy a light and humorous atmosphere full of the charm of classic Peanuts stories and a celebration of friendship. Take control of the legendary Sopwith Camel in an aerial pursuit of the Red Baron.
