Game Description#

Chaos Galaxy 2 is an economic, political and military strategy in which the player will have to command the forces of an entire planet. The people must live not only in prosperity, but also in peace, which is very difficult to achieve against the backdrop of the current war. The peoples grappled with each other, wanting to seize as many territories as possible, some of which would simply become sources of resources, and all citizens would be destroyed.

The great empire fell apart due to constant civil wars. Feeling complete freedom, the former bosses, commanders and other individuals in power decided that they could lead the people and settled on various planets. To establish their borders and prove their independence, many of them started a war and the protagonist is no exception. He will not offend those whom he led and is ready to stand to the end. But before that, you still need to choose the direction in which the country will move.

Types of factions in Chaos Galaxy 2:

Imperial - can compete for the title of various ranks and occupy high positions, which allows it to significantly expand the generally accepted limit of military forces and endow military leaders with unique abilities Pirate - capable of pursuing fugitive opponents, taking away their weapons and dismantling ships for parts Mercenaries and assassins - eliminate unwanted individuals, can accept orders for dirty deeds at a good price Artificial intelligence – with the help of hacking skills allows you to steal other people’s drawings and knowledge, hack databases, and conduct reconnaissance

There are also 4 more types of groups available in the game that you can join. Playing as each sets a different pace in the game and allows you to unlock a variety of random events. This makes it possible to get a unique gaming experience every time and discover the universe from different sides. A large number of tasks and constant struggle will definitely not let fans of dynamics get bored.
