Game Description#

Acre CRISIS transfers players in 1992, placing them in the AKR area in the jungle of the Amazon. The Brazilian Military Police Brigade is going to this area to investigate a series of deaths caused by wild animals, as well as reports of a meeting with cryptids. Upon arrival, the soldiers were in direct confrontation with a group of hungry dinosaurs, which was very different from their initial task. This adventure horror for survival offers players immersion in the world of threats, where salvation becomes the main goal.

The detachment is faced with the struggle for existence in the middle of dense jungle. They have to not only survive, but also resist prehistoric predators in insulation conditions. In Acre CRISIS, the subblinear Thais D. Oliveira moves along a dangerous landscape, trying to survive contrary to numerous threats. Her journey through the Amazon begins to open the gloomy secrets of the past in the region. In the 1960s and 1970s, the Brazilian military dictatorship carried out a secret program for the development of biological weapons here.

This project offers several gameplay formats, including the plot regime for the development of history, an arcade regime for quick survival matches and a multi -user regime that provides cooperative and competitive interaction. Gamers should resist constant dangers from these large creatures, while revealing the truth about former government operations. Survival in such an environment requires a continuous reaction to new threats and active actions.
