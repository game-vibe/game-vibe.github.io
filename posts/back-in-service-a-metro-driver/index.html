Game Description#

Back in Service: A Metro Driver will allow gamers to immerse themselves in transport control in the detailed system of the Prague Metro. By controlling the historical train of the ES series from the cabin from the first person, the players move along the lines of the metropolitan subway. The main task is to navigate on the specified routes, timely and safe transportation of passengers, as well as strict observance of the movement schedule to prevent delays.

It is necessary to constantly monitor the time of arrival and departure, managing the challenges throughout the work shift. This Back in Service: A Metro Driver provides more than just driving a train, adding sandboxes and research elements. The project gives significant freedom of choice of actions within the framework of the virtual world of the Prague subway. You can concentrate on improving the skill of managing the composition, studying the intricacies of driving historical technology and reacting to emergency situations.

It is also possible to leave the cabin in order to explore the extensive underground tunnels on foot or perform a variety of instructions, providing assistance to other employees of the transport system in their work. Such a gameplay structure allows you to engage in various activities in addition to piloting the composition. The successful performance of the operator’s functions, which is confirmed by the completion of flights on time according to the schedule, is rewarded with the intra -game currency. Earned funds are integrated into the Title economic system, allowing you to interact with the environment.
