Game Description#

Le Mans Ultimate - The official game FIA ​​World Enduraance Championship and “24 hours of Le Man”, promises exciting experience to both experienced riders and beginners. Runned in early access, the game offers to try the exciting season of 2023 with official cars, legendary tracks and online media. Sit behind the wheel of ultramodern hypercars from manufacturers such as Toyota, Ferrari and Porsche, and recreate the battles on prestigious tracks.

In addition to hypercars, give up a call in the categories of LMP2 and GTE, driving cars of such cult brands as Aston Martin and Corvette. More than 100 cladding options provide opportunities for personalization. Participate in races on famous tracks such as Monza, spa-francrades and legendary le-man, experiencing acute sensations from the maximum capabilities on these revered tracks. Le Mans Ultimate uses advanced physics and management, which allows you to configure gameplay for different levels of skill and offers assistance to beginners. Language localization and convenient interfaces increase accessibility.

For competitive riders, online races with the Racecontrol system will provide an adrenaline rush. Daily races strengthen your reputation on the network, opening future opportunities as the game develops and the real Fia WeC. In the autonomous Race Weekend mode, you can choose the level of complexity, practice, qualify and take part in the race, adjusting the conditions for your preferences. Evaluate the full structure of the weekend of FIA. The tracks change dynamically during the race, simulating rubber sticking and garbage accumulation. Dynamic weather and car wear even more increase realism.
