Game Description#

The pirated version ofHalo 2 provides the opportunity to play online online for free. Allows you to play online with a friend. Players can work through the story campaign together. Online play includes versus modes. The game directly continues the plot of the original part. The story tells about the Master Chief and the Arbiter Elite. They are involved in a conflict in the 26th century. The war is between the UNSC (United Nations Space Command) and the Covenant, an alliance of alien races.

Gameplay Halo 2 is a first-person shooter game. Compared to the previous part, a more diverse set of cars is available in the sequel. The possibility of free online play allows you to go through the story campaign in splitscreen mode. The protective shield is restored several times faster, which increases the dynamics of firefights. Master Chief can shoot with both hands. The arsenal includes an M7 submachine gun, beam rifle, Covenant carbine and other guns.

All factions use a unique type of transport. The UNSC is represented by a modification of the Warthog. The Spirit shuttle replaced the Phantom. The cars are completely destroyed. You can also board enemy vehicles. All barrels have different characteristics. Covenant guns are powered by batteries, while human weapons use classic ammunition. You can use 8 types of grenades, including fragmentation and plasma. The Chief uses a flashlight, and the Arbiter uses optical camouflage.

Each of the characters represents the opposite side in the conflict. There are 4 difficulty levels available: easy, normal, heroic and legendary. They affect the aggressiveness of enemies and the amount of vitality of the protagonists. A huge number of Easter eggs are only available on Legendary difficulty. If you collect special skulls, you can combine them with each other, changing the gameplay features of the shooter.
