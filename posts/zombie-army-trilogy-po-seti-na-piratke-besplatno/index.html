Game Description#

Zombie Army Trilogy is a multiplayer first-person shooter that you can play online with a friend, or cooperate with players around the world. On the pirate platform, 4 fighters can unite for free by connecting via the Internet online to fight the zombie army of Hitler himself. Events take place in an alternate 1940s, when, after the death of the Fuhrer, his army rose from the graves. Zombie Army: Trilogy not only features free online play, but also allows you to take on the role of all the heroes from the famous zombie franchise Left 4 Dead.

Stealth mechanics are completely absent, giving way to a dynamic corridor shooter. The online game combines the efforts of four players, giving them the opportunity to confront a huge army of the living dead. In Zombie Army Trilogy, there is a huge variety of guns, melee weapons and auxiliary equipment available, such as explosives, grenades, mines, dynamites, etc. In addition to the story campaign, which is a set of various missions, the Horde mode is presented - an endless battle with an ever-growing army of the living dead.

The action takes place in an alternative Germany in 1945. The task is simple - to rid the world of Nazi demons who have risen from their graves. In total, there are 3 intense campaigns to go through, with a total complexity of 15 missions. It is necessary to mow down legions of the undead led by super-soldiers, armored skeletons, zombie snipers, elite fighters with chainsaws and various fiery evil spirits - the variety of enemies is a full car. The gameplay is incredibly brutal, allowing you to dismember zombies and tear out their insides.
