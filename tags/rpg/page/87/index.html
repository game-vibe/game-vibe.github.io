Rune Coliseum

Release Date: 2024/09/23

Game Description Rune Coliseum immerses you in the brutal world of gladiatorial combat. This deck-based roguelike features a unique semi-real-time combat system that departs from traditional turn-based mechanics. Players choose a gladiator, assemble a deck of cards that reflect his movements, and then engage in duels to the death. The basic concept is simple: survive and become a champion. But Rune Coliseum brings the challenge with its innovative queuing system. Both gladiators choose the number of cards they want to play at the same time, creating a fast-paced duel where strategy and reflexes reign supreme....
